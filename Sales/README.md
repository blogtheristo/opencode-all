### Reindustrialize EU Field Tour 2026

**Status:** Draft for review
**Doc:** `Sales/ReindustrializeEU_FieldTour_2026.md`

**Concept:** European counterpart to the Detroit Reindustrialize 3.0 "drive-the-loop" — pair the convening with a physical drive through EU industrial capacity, then sit down with the operators who run it.

**Four-city backbone (= Detroit stops):**

1. **Duisburg, DE** (= Ecorse / US Steel) — Europe's largest steel concentration, mid-transition
2. **Gothenburg, SE** (= Auburn Hills) — Volvo / SKF OEM and tech-campus reinvention
3. **Tampere, FI** (= Fraser) — Metso / Valmet mid-tier heavy machinery
4. **Liège, BE** (= 8 Mile) — John Cockerill green-steel pivot, distributed factories

**Capital & Policy Backbone (Gemini overlay, = Detroit's capital/government layer):**

- **Brussels, BE** — InvestEU / EAIS policy + capital anchor (pairs with Liège, ~100 km)
- **Athens, GR** — EBAN Congress, Southern-Europe angel ecosystem
- **Budapest, HU** — CEE industrial finance (EU Finance Days lineage)
- **Dublin, IE** — Western-European finance & cross-border innovation gateway

**Target Audience:** plant/operations managers, sustainability/ESG officers, procurement heads (CBAM, CSRD, "Made in EU") on the Capacity Backbone; angels/VCs, EC policy staff, founders on the Capital Backbone.

**Next Steps:** build named-account target list → anchor at Industrial Decarbonisation Europe (Amsterdam, May 2026) + European Manufacturing Summit (Düsseldorf, Dec 2026) → pilot Leg A (Duisburg → Gothenburg) Q1 2027.
