**About Lifetime Oy / DWS IQ 6**

Lifetime Oy is building DWS IQ 6 — the industrial climate OS for Europe's 16 most heavily regulated industries. As EU regulation tightens — CBAM, CSRD, the Industrial Accelerator Act — industrial operators need real-time lifecycle tracking, CBAM exposure mapping, and EU-sovereign data infrastructure that does not expose them to CLOUD Act risk. We are the intelligence layer over the European reindustrialization wave.

**The Role**

We are hiring a Sales Associate to drive enterprise outreach for the **Reindustrialize EU Field Tour** — a structured B2B outreach program bringing DWS IQ 6 to the floor of Europe's key industrial hubs: Duisburg (green steel), Liège (green-steel transition), Brussels (InvestEU and EU policy), and Stockholm (Nordic PE/LP capital cluster, November).

Modeled on the Reindustrialize Summit in Detroit, the tour pairs a physical drive through operating industrial sites with curated dinners of 8–14 plant-level and C-suite buyers. You will be the person who fills those seats.

**What you will do**

- Build and manage the named-account target list across the four-city backbone — plant managers, sustainability/ESG officers, and procurement heads at thyssenkrupp Steel, ArcelorMittal Belgium, John Cockerill, Volvo Cars/Group, SKF, Metso, Valmet, and regional heavy-industry suppliers
- Execute structured outreach sequences to warm target accounts before each tour stop
- Qualify inbound interest and convert tour attendees into DWS IQ 6 pilot customers (target: 2–4 signed pilots per leg)
- Coordinate logistics for the 2-day Belgian Anchor pilot (Liège → Brussels, Q1 2027): venue, guest list, dinner format, follow-up cadence
- Support investor outreach touchpoints at the Stockholm PE/LP cluster in November (PE Insights Nordics, Nordics Institutional Forum, Real Deals Nordic Summit)
- Maintain pipeline, account notes, and reporting in the DWS IQ 6 dashboard

**What you bring**

- 2–4 years in B2B enterprise or industrial sales (SaaS, cleantech, heavy industry, or regulatory compliance preferred)
- Demonstrated ability to reach plant-level and C-suite buyers in manufacturing, steel, automotive, or logistics
- Strong written outreach — you write clean, direct cold emails that get replies
- English required; German, Swedish, or Finnish is a strong plus
- Working knowledge of EU industrial regulation (CBAM, CSRD, ETS) is a significant advantage
- Comfortable operating in an early-stage, high-autonomy environment without a large SDR team behind you
- Based in or willing to travel within the EU (Belgium, Germany, Sweden, Finland focus in 2027)

**Why this role, why now**

The EU Industrial Accelerator Act, CBAM, and CSRD are converging into a single compliance event for heavy industry in 2026–2027. Every plant manager and sustainability officer in the Ruhr Valley, Gothenburg, and the Liège basin needs a system to track, predict, and prove their regulatory posture before the audit — not after it. You will be the person who gets DWS IQ 6 in front of them before the competition arrives.

**What you get**

- Base salary + commission on pilot conversions
- Equity participation in Lifetime Oy's SAFE round
- Direct line to the founding team — no three layers of management
- Field travel across Belgium, Germany, Sweden, and Finland
- A front-row seat to the European reindustrialization wave, with a product that belongs in the room
