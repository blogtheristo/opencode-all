## 2. Key Announcements From the Summit Floor

### 2.1 Govini → Air: Corporate Rebrand + "Enterprise Readiness" Category Launch

**Who:** Tara Murphy Dougherty, CEO (formerly Govini, now Air)
**When:** June 16, 2026 — announced from the summit floor

**What happened:**
Govini, a defense AI software company with an existing US Navy contract, rebranded as **Air** and declared "Enterprise Readiness" a new product category. The launch thesis:

- The US spends **~$700 million per day** on military readiness — and readiness is declining
- Development timelines have doubled (6 years → 12 years to reach IOC)
- Production ramps take twice as long (7 → 10 years)
- ~75% of Air Force aircraft entering depot maintenance are delayed
- Root cause: "fragmented systems, disconnected industrial coordination, archaic workflows"

**Air's solution** — three-layer architecture:

1. **Execution** — real-time operational data
2. **Orchestration** — cross-supplier/cross-agency coordination
3. **Activation** — closes the loop from data to action

**EU field tour implication:**
The "Readiness Gap" framing is directly portable to the EU regulatory compliance context. Replace "warfighter needs vs. defense enterprise delivery" with "regulator audit expectations vs. plant-floor compliance infrastructure." Every thyssenkrupp, John Cockerill, and Volvo operations team faces its own readiness gap: CBAM exposure mapping they don't have, CSRD lifecycle data that doesn't exist in the ERP, "Made in EU" provenance tracking not yet built. **DWS IQ 6 closes the EU Regulatory Readiness Gap.** Use this framing at Brussels sovereign data briefing and Liège plant walk.

## 3. Capgemini Research: Reindustrialization of Europe and US 2026

**Source:** Capgemini Research Institute, "Reindustrialization of Europe and US 2026" (released ~June 2026)
**Sample:** Large European and US organizations

### Key findings

| Finding                                                                    | Data point                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Organizations with reindustrialization strategy in place or in development | ~75%                                                                 |
| Primary technology investments                                             | AI/Gen AI, digital twins, automation, robotics, IIoT, edge computing |
| Investment outside strategic sectors (semiconductors, defense)             | Declining — policy/tariff uncertainty, tighter capital               |
| Geographic diversification destination (2026)                              | India #1, then Vietnam, Mexico, Canada                               |
| China posture                                                              | Rebalancing (not full exit)                                          |
| Key constraint                                                             | Skilled industrial + digital talent shortage                         |

### What this means for EU outreach

**75% have a strategy but investment outside critical sectors is declining.** This is the DWS IQ 6 window: organizations that have committed to reindustrialization strategy but face pressure to show ROI on every compliance and operational investment. DWS IQ 6 is the intelligence layer that turns the strategy into a measurable compliance posture — before the CBAM audit, not after.

**Talent shortage is the limiting factor for scale.** Our pitch addresses this directly: DWS IQ 6 reduces the analyst headcount needed to produce CSRD lifecycle reports and CBAM exposure maps. The platform is the team.

## 5. Competitive Intelligence Signal

The Govini → Air rebrand and product category launch (Enterprise Readiness) confirms a structural trend: **defense-grade industrial AI is converging with industrial compliance infrastructure.** The same architecture — Execution + Orchestration + Activation — maps onto DWS IQ 6's design:

- **Execution** = real-time EN 15978 lifecycle tracking per site
- **Orchestration** = CBAM exposure mapping across the supply chain
- **Activation** = pre-audit compliance posture for regulator submissions

No current EU-native competitor is building this category. Air/Govini is US-focused with a defense orientation. DWS IQ 6 has the EU regulatory specificity (CBAM, CSRD, ETS) and the EU-sovereign data hosting advantage. The category is open.
