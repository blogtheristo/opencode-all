## 1. Core Structure and Argumentation Chain (7 steps)

* [ ] **1. Targeting:** Does the text clearly define the industry, enterprise size class, and buyer persona (e.g., CxO / Compliance Officer)?
* [ ] **2. Limitation & Compliance:** Are the regulatory context and the data/process limitation presented voluntarily and proactively from the start?
* [ ] **3. Claim Map & Sources:** Is every claim classified (source, own data, analysis, hypothesis) and anchored to evidence?
* [ ] **4. Decision Impact:** Is the business or legal impact of the limitation described realistically, without exaggeration or understatement?
* [ ] **5. Management Measure:** Is a clear methodological management measure presented for the limitation (bounded use case, audit chain, marking of data gaps)?
* [ ] **6. Residual Uncertainty:** Are the boundaries of the solution expressed explicitly? (E.g., "not a complete compliance automation, but decision support".)
* [ ] **7. Commercial Connection (Demo):** Is the demo or next step presented as a logical continuation for verifying the management measure — not a sales pitch hidden in the text?

## 2. Scientifically and Ethically Protected Argumentation

* [ ] **Reactance prevention (Brehm & Brehm):** Does the text avoid pushy sales language so that it does not generate purchase resistance in the reader?
* [ ] **Managed two-sidedness (Crowley & Hoyer; Eisend):** Is the raised limitation essential but honestly balanced against the presented management measure?
* [ ] **Recognized repetition (Hasher et al.):** Does the text reinforce the DWS core claim in a new context without identical repetition of other publications?

## 3. Technical and Audit Requirements

* [ ] **Reference formatting:** Are all academic and public source references expressed precisely (preferably in Oxford notation or a standardized reference system)?
* [ ] **Information separation:** Does the reader immediately distinguish public data, DWS's own analysis, and the product's functional hypothesis?
* [ ] **Ontology feedback readiness:** Does the text define the data points or questions whose reception and reactions are fed back into the World Model?
