Data Processing Agreement

Lifetime Oy (Data Controller) ↔ Shufti Pro Limited (Data Processor) Tier 1 / Tier 2
Identity Verification Services — EU-Only Processing

1. Parties & Roles

1.1 Data Controller. Lifetime Oy, Business ID 0772407-9, VAT FI07724079, registered at Laidunmaanraitti 2 A 25, 02330 Espoo, Finland (the “Controller”).

1.2 Data Processor. Shufti Pro Limited, registered in the United Kingdom (the “Processor”).

1.3 Roles. The Controller engages the Processor to provide identity verification services (KYC / IDV) in connection with the Controller’s DWS IQ platform. The Processor acts as Data Processor within the meaning of GDPR Article 4(8). Where the Controller’s own customer (the end-customer of the DWS IQ platform) is the underlying Data Controller, the Processor acknowledges its position as Sub-Processor in that chain.

2. Scope & Underlying Framework

2.1 Reference framework. This Agreement is the Lifetime ↔ Shufti instance of the Lifetime Oy Data Processing Agreement template, version 1.2 (April 2026), published at https://dws10.com/legal-hub/dpa.html (the “Template DPA”). Where this Agreement is silent, the Template DPA applies.

2.2 DWS Tier applicability. The Processor's services support the Controller's Tier 1 (DWS IQ Aegis) and Tier 2 (DWS IQ 6 Sovereign Infra) deployments. EU-Only Mode (Clause 4) is mandatory for both tiers. The Processor is not engaged for Tier 3 (DWS IQ Connect) deployments under this Agreement.

2.3 Subject matter. Identity verification (document scan, facial match, liveness check) and the related processing of biometric data (GDPR Article 9 special category data) submitted by the Controller via the Processor's verification API.

2.4 Categories of data subjects. Natural persons whose identity is being verified at the Controller's request, including end-users of the Controller's customers and personnel of the Controller's customers.

2.5 Categories of personal data. Identity document images (passport, national ID, driver’s licence), facial images and liveness video frames (biometric data, GDPR Art. 9), document metadata, verification results, and verification request metadata.

2.6 Duration. For the term of the underlying commercial agreement between the Controller and the Processor, plus the retention period set out in Clause 9.

3. Legal Basis (GDPR Article 9)

3.1 The Parties acknowledge that the processing of biometric data under this Agreement constitutes processing of special categories of personal data under GDPR Article 9.

3.2 The legal basis is GDPR Article 9(2)(a) — explicit consent of the data subject, obtained by the Controller (or by the Controller’s end-customer) prior to initiating each verification request.

3.3 The Controller is solely responsible for obtaining valid explicit consent from each data subject. The Processor is entitled to rely on the Controller’s representation that consent has been obtained for each verification request submitted to the Processor.

4. EU-Only Processing & Storage

4.1 EU-Only Mode (mandatory). All processing and storage of Personal Data under this Agreement shall take place exclusively within the European Economic Area (“EEA”). The Processor shall enable EU-Only Mode on the Controller’s account and shall not disable it without the Controller’s prior written consent.

4.2 No extra-EEA transfer. The Processor shall not transfer, store, process, or make accessible any Personal Data to or from any location outside the EEA. This obligation applies to all Sub-Processors.

4.3 Sub-Processor exclusion. The Sub-Processor Programmers Force (Pvt.) Ltd. (Pakistan) is excluded from the authorised Sub-Processor list for the Controller's account and shall have no access to the Controller's Personal Data under any circumstance.

4.4 Acknowledged trade-offs. The Controller acknowledges, as flagged by the Processor's Compliance team on 2 April 2026, that EU-Only Mode entails the following operational constraints, which the Controller accepts:

  • No model training on the Controller’s verification data (no contribution to the Processor’s global AI models).
  • No manual human-reviewer fallback when an automated check is uncertain; ambiguous cases will return “uncertain”.

5. Sub-Processors

5.1 Authorised list. The Processor's authorised Sub-Processor list for the Controller's account is set out in Schedule A.

5.2 Google Cloud — EEA region lock. All Google Cloud Platform infrastructure used to process the Controller’s data shall be region-locked to the EEA. Replication outside the EEA is prohibited.

5.3 Notice period. The Processor shall give the Controller at least thirty (30) calendar days advance written notice of any intended addition, replacement, or removal of a Sub-Processor. The Controller may object within that window; if the Processor cannot reasonably accommodate the objection, the Controller may terminate this Agreement without penalty.

6. Security of Processing

6.1 The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures per GDPR Article 32, including encryption at rest and in transit, role-based access control, tenant isolation at the record level, and continuous monitoring.

6.2 The Processor shall ensure all personnel accessing Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations and have received data protection training.

7. Personal Data Breach Notification

7.1 Forty-eight-hour notification. The Processor shall notify the Controller within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of any Personal Data Breach affecting the Controller’s data, providing the information needed for GDPR Articles 33 and 34 notifications. This 48-hour processor-to-controller deadline is set so that the Controller can comfortably meet the statutory 72-hour obligation to the Finnish Data Protection Authority (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto) under GDPR Article 33.

7.2 The Processor shall implement, without undue delay, measures to contain the breach and mitigate its effects, and shall provide ongoing updates as additional information becomes available.

8. Data Subject Rights & Audit

8.1 The Processor shall cooperate with the Controller in fulfilling Data Subject rights requests (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) within the timeframes required by GDPR.

8.2 Audit. The Controller may audit the Processor’s compliance with this Agreement once per calendar year with thirty (30) days’ written notice, at the Controller’s expense. Audit results are confidential and subject to non-disclosure.

9. Retention & Return / Deletion

9.1 The Processor shall retain Personal Data for no longer than necessary for the verification purpose, and in any case no longer than thirty (30) days following completion of the verification request, unless a longer retention period is required by law or expressly instructed in writing by the Controller.

9.2 End of Agreement. Upon termination of this Agreement, the Processor shall, at the Controller’s option, return or securely delete all Personal Data within thirty (30) days, and certify deletion in writing.

10. Optional Partner Addendum

10.1 The Parties acknowledge the existence of the KYA Partner Addendum (v1.0, 24 April 2026), which records optional, forward-looking operational practices aligned with the Lifetime Oy Know Your Agent (KYA) Standard v1.5 (Memory Binding). The KYA Partner Addendum is offered separately, is not a precondition of this Agreement, and takes effect only if signed by both Parties.

11. Term, Conflict & Governing Law

11.1 Effective date. This Agreement takes effect on the date of last signature below.

11.2 Conflict. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and the Template DPA in respect of the Lifetime ↔ Shufti relationship, this Agreement prevails.

11.3 Governing law. This Agreement is governed by Finnish law. Espoon käräjäoikeus (Espoo District Court) has exclusive jurisdiction.

11.4 Counterparts. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts (including by qualified electronic signature), each of which is an original and which together constitute one instrument.
