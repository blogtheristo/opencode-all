Recitals

WHEREAS:

A. The Parties are entering into discussions regarding a potential business relationship, including but not limited to a reseller and/or consulting partnership under which Lifetime Oy would resell and/or provide consulting services around Aiven's managed data infrastructure services, and related technology cooperation (the "Purpose");

B. In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose to the other Party certain Confidential Information and Trade Secrets as defined herein;

C. The Parties wish to establish the terms and conditions under which such Confidential Information will be disclosed, received, and protected in accordance with Directive (EU) 2016/943 on the protection of undisclosed know-how and business information (trade secrets) against their unlawful acquisition, use and disclosure (the "Trade Secrets Directive");

D. This Agreement is intended to protect the legitimate business interests of both Parties while facilitating their commercial discussions.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

Article 1: Definitions

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any and all information, whether in written, oral, electronic, visual, or any other form, that is disclosed by or on behalf of either Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), including but not limited to:

  1. Technical Information: software, source code, object code, algorithms, APIs, system architecture, databases, technical specifications, designs, drawings, schematics, prototypes, samples, inventions, discoveries, improvements, research and development activities, testing procedures, and technical data;
  2. Business Information: business plans, strategies, forecasts, projections, financial information, pricing, costs, margins, customer lists, supplier lists, marketing plans, sales data, market research, and competitive analyses;
  3. Commercial Information: contracts, proposals, negotiations, partnerships, joint ventures, mergers and acquisitions, and investment activities;
  4. Personnel Information: names, expertise, compensation, and organizational structure of employees, consultants, and contractors;
  5. Intellectual Property: patents, patent applications, trademarks, trade names, copyrights, trade secrets, know-how, and any other intellectual property rights;
  6. Client Information: information relating to the Disclosing Party's clients, their projects, requirements, and any information received from clients under confidentiality obligations;
  7. Any other information that is marked as "Confidential," "Proprietary," "Trade Secret," or similar designation, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

1.2 "Trade Secret" means information which meets all of the following requirements in accordance with Article 2 of the Trade Secrets Directive:

  1. It is secret in the sense that it is not, as a body or in the precise configuration and assembly of its components, generally known among or readily accessible to persons within the circles that normally deal with the kind of information in question;
  2. It has commercial value because it is secret;
  3. It has been subject to reasonable steps under the circumstances, by the person lawfully in control of the information, to keep it secret.

1.3 "Disclosing Party" means the Party disclosing Confidential Information, including its affiliates, subsidiaries, or authorized representatives.

1.4 "Receiving Party" means the Party receiving Confidential Information and includes its employees, officers, directors, agents, consultants, and subcontractors who have a need to know such information for the Purpose.

1.5 "Purpose" means the evaluation and negotiation of a potential business relationship between the Parties, and any subsequent business relationship that may be established.

1.6 "Authorized Representatives" means employees, officers, directors, professional advisors (including legal counsel and accountants), and consultants of the Receiving Party who:

  1. Have a legitimate need to access the Confidential Information for the Purpose;
  2. Have been informed of the confidential nature of the information; and
  3. Are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than those contained in this Agreement.

Article 2: Obligations of the Receiving Party

2.1 Confidentiality Obligation. The Receiving Party agrees to:

  1. Keep and hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence;
  2. Not disclose, publish, or otherwise reveal any Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party;
  3. Use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever;
  4. Protect the Confidential Information using the same degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of like kind, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care;
  5. Limit access to Confidential Information to Authorized Representatives who have a genuine need to know for the Purpose.

2.2 Prohibited Actions. The Receiving Party shall NOT:

  1. Copy, reproduce, or duplicate any Confidential Information except as reasonably necessary for the Purpose;
  2. Reverse engineer, disassemble, decompile, or attempt to derive the composition, structure, or underlying information of any Confidential Information, including any software, products, or prototypes;
  3. Use any Confidential Information to develop, manufacture, market, or sell any product or service that competes with the Disclosing Party's products or services;
  4. Remove, alter, or obscure any proprietary notices, labels, or marks on any Confidential Information;
  5. Acquire Confidential Information through any unlawful means as defined in Article 4 of the Trade Secrets Directive, including unauthorized access to, appropriation of, or copying of any documents, objects, materials, substances, or electronic files lawfully under the control of the Disclosing Party; or any other conduct which, under the circumstances, is considered contrary to honest commercial practices.

2.3 Security Measures. The Receiving Party shall:

  1. Implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect Confidential Information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction;
  2. Comply with any specific security requirements communicated by the Disclosing Party or its clients;
  3. Promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing of any actual or suspected unauthorized access to, use of, or disclosure of Confidential Information;
  4. Cooperate with the Disclosing Party in investigating and mitigating any security breach.

2.4 Binding of Personnel. The Receiving Party shall ensure that all Authorized Representatives who receive Confidential Information are informed of the confidential nature of the information; are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement; and use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose. The Receiving Party shall be liable for any breach of this Agreement by its Authorized Representatives.

Article 3: Exceptions to Confidentiality

3.1 The obligations set forth in Article 2 shall not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by clear and convincing evidence:

  1. Public Domain: Was in the public domain at the time of disclosure or subsequently became part of the public domain through no fault, act, or omission of the Receiving Party;
  2. Prior Knowledge: Was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure by the Disclosing Party, as evidenced by written records predating such disclosure;
  3. Independent Development: Was independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to any Confidential Information, as evidenced by written records;
  4. Third Party Disclosure: Was lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party who was not under any obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party and who had the right to disclose such information.

3.2 Legally Compelled Disclosure. If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, court order, or governmental authority to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall: (a) provide the Disclosing Party with prompt written notice of such requirement (to the extent legally permitted) to allow the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy; (b) cooperate with the Disclosing Party in seeking such protective order or remedy; (c) disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that is legally required to be disclosed; and (d) use reasonable efforts to obtain assurances that confidential treatment will be accorded to the disclosed information.

Article 4: Intellectual Property Rights

4.1 Ownership. All Confidential Information remains the sole and exclusive property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting or conferring any rights by license or otherwise to any Confidential Information, patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, or any other intellectual property rights of the Disclosing Party.

4.2 No License. The disclosure of Confidential Information under this Agreement does not constitute any grant, option, or license under any patent, trademark, copyright, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of the Disclosing Party, whether by implication, estoppel, or otherwise.

4.3 No Warranty. Neither Party makes any representations or warranties, express or implied, regarding the accuracy, completeness, or fitness for any particular purpose of any Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement. All Confidential Information is provided "AS IS."

Article 5: Return and Destruction of Confidential Information

5.1 Upon the earlier of: (a) the Disclosing Party's written request; (b) termination of this Agreement; or (c) completion or termination of discussions regarding the Purpose; the Receiving Party shall, at the Disclosing Party's option:

  1. Promptly return to the Disclosing Party all Confidential Information and all copies, extracts, and summaries thereof; OR
  2. Destroy all Confidential Information and all copies, extracts, and summaries thereof, and provide the Disclosing Party with written certification of such destruction signed by an authorized officer of the Receiving Party.

5.2 Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Receiving Party may retain: (a) one (1) archival copy of the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of determining its ongoing obligations under this Agreement; and (b) copies required to be retained by applicable law or regulation, provided such copies remain subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement.

5.3 Any Confidential Information retained pursuant to Section 5.2 shall remain subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement for the duration specified in Article 6.

Article 6: Term and Duration

6.1 Agreement Term. This Agreement shall become effective on the date of the last signature below (the "Effective Date") and shall continue in force for a period of five (5) years from the Effective Date, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Agreement.

6.2 Survival of Obligations. The confidentiality obligations set forth in this Agreement shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement and shall continue for a period of: (a) five (5) years from the date of disclosure for general Confidential Information; and (b) indefinitely for Trade Secrets, for as long as such information qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law.

6.3 Termination. Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party. Termination shall not affect any rights or obligations that have accrued prior to termination.

Article 7: Remedies

7.1 Acknowledgment of Harm. Each Party acknowledges that: (a) the Confidential Information of the other Party is valuable and unique; (b) unauthorized disclosure or use of the Confidential Information would cause irreparable harm to the Disclosing Party for which monetary damages would be inadequate; and (c) the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek equitable relief, including injunction and specific performance, in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity.

7.2 Injunctive Relief. In the event of any actual or threatened breach of this Agreement, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief without the necessity of proving actual damages or posting any bond or other security.

7.3 Damages. The Receiving Party shall be liable for all damages, including but not limited to: (a) direct damages suffered by the Disclosing Party as a result of the breach; (b) indirect and consequential damages, including lost profits and business opportunities; and (c) reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in enforcing this Agreement.

7.4 Contractual Penalty. In addition to any other remedies available, in the event of a material breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party, the Receiving Party shall pay to the Disclosing Party a contractual penalty of EUR 100,000 (one hundred thousand euros) (or equivalent in local currency) for each breach. Payment of such penalty shall not limit the Disclosing Party's right to claim additional damages.

Article 8: Data Protection (GDPR Compliance)

8.1 To the extent that any Confidential Information includes personal data as defined by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation — "GDPR"), the Receiving Party shall:

  1. Process such personal data only in accordance with the Disclosing Party's instructions and for the Purpose;
  2. Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk;
  3. Not transfer personal data to any third country or international organization without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent and appropriate safeguards;
  4. Assist the Disclosing Party in responding to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under the GDPR;
  5. Notify the Disclosing Party without undue delay upon becoming aware of any personal data breach.

8.2 The Receiving Party shall ensure that any persons authorized to process personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

8.3 For clarity: this Agreement alone does not constitute a legal basis or instruction for either Party to disclose personal data concerning its own customers or end users to the other Party. Any such disclosure requires a separate, documented GDPR basis (e.g. a data processing agreement and, where applicable, customer consent) agreed in writing before disclosure.

Article 9: General Provisions

9.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, warranties, and agreements between the Parties.

9.2 Amendments. No amendment, modification, or waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

9.3 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. The invalid provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable while preserving the Parties' original intent.

9.4 Waiver. No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right under this Agreement shall constitute a waiver of that right. Any waiver must be in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

9.5 Assignment. Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the other Party. Any attempted assignment without such consent shall be void.

9.6 No Partnership. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as creating a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship between the Parties.

9.7 Notices. All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by: (a) personal delivery; (b) registered mail with return receipt requested; (c) internationally recognized courier service; or (d) email with confirmation of receipt. Notices shall be sent to the addresses set forth above or to such other address as a Party may designate in writing.

9.8 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed valid and binding.

Article 10: Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

10.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Finland, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The Parties acknowledge that this Agreement is subject to and complies with Directive (EU) 2016/943 on the protection of trade secrets.

10.2 Amicable Resolution. The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement through good faith negotiations. Either Party may initiate such negotiations by providing written notice to the other Party.

10.3 Arbitration. Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, or the breach, termination, or invalidity thereof, which cannot be resolved through amicable negotiations within thirty (30) days, shall be finally settled by arbitration in accordance with the Rules of the Arbitration Institute of the Finland Chamber of Commerce. The arbitration shall be conducted as follows: (a) number of arbitrators: one (1) arbitrator appointed by the Arbitration Institute; (b) seat of arbitration: Helsinki, Finland; (c) language: English (or Finnish by mutual agreement); (d) confidentiality: the arbitration proceedings and any award shall be confidential.

10.4 Interim Relief. Notwithstanding the above, either Party may seek interim or injunctive relief from any court of competent jurisdiction to prevent irreparable harm pending the outcome of arbitration.
