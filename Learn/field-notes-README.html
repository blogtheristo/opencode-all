DWS Field Notes

This directory contains processed field notes that capture enterprise knowledge from real-world interactions.

Purpose

Field Notes are the raw sensory input for the Lifetime World Model (LWM). They capture reality that off-the-shelf LLMs cannot know: - Customer conversations - Production incidents - Regulatory changes - Competitive intelligence - Edge cases and failures

Naming Convention

Format: YYYY-MM-DD-[brief-topic].md

Examples: - 2026-01-21-karp-enterprise-ai.md - 2026-01-22-london-customer-cross-border.md - 2026-01-23-production-incident-llm-hallucination.md

When to Create a Field Note

Create a field note when: - ✅ Customer says something unexpected - ✅ LLM/AI fails to handle a situation correctly - ✅ Production incident reveals a knowledge gap - ✅ Regulatory change affects operations - ✅ Competitive intelligence is gathered - ✅ Edge case is discovered

Time requirement: Within 24 hours of the event

Templates

Three primary templates are available in ONTOLOGY.md Section 10:

  1. Customer Interaction Template
  2. For: Sales calls, support tickets, feedback

  3. Focus: Customer quote, problem/insight, business impact

  4. Production Incident Template

  5. For: System failures, AI errors, edge cases

  6. Focus: What happened, root cause, prevention

  7. Regulatory Change Template

  8. For: New laws, compliance updates, policy changes
  9. Focus: Summary, impact, required actions

Required Elements

Every field note MUST include: - Date: ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD) - Author: Your name/email - Location: Geographic location (if applicable) - Context: Customer/project/system name - Ontology Tags: Geography, sector, entities - The Story: What happened (chronological) - The Insight: Why this matters - Next Steps: Follow-up actions

Ontology Tagging Example

## Ontology Tags

- **Geography:** Europe > UK > London
- **Sector:** Financial_Services > Insurance > Underwriting
- **Entities:** Customer ABC Ltd, FCA, GDPR, DWS-IQ6
- **Temporal Context:** 2026-Q1, Post-2025 UK FCA Rules
- **Confidence:** High

From Field Note to Core Artifact

A field note should be elevated to a Core Artifact when: - ✅ Used 3+ times in decision-making - ✅ Validated by 2+ team members - ✅ Contains reusable business logic - ✅ Addresses a recurring pattern or gap - ✅ Has regulatory or compliance importance

Process:

Field Note → Team Review → Draft Artifact → Validate → Publish

Workflow

1. CAPTURE:  Real-time → Database (Supabase)
2. PROCESS:  Daily export → /docs/field-notes/YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md
3. ELEVATE:  Weekly review → /artifacts/CATEGORY/ART-YYYY-NNN.md
