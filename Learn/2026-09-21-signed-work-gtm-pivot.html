1. The line

We ship signed work that releases money. Committees announce compliance.

FI: Toimitamme allekirjoitettua työtä, joka vapauttaa rahaa. Komiteat ilmoittavat compliancea.

That is the whole pivot. Not a new product. Not a new price. A different noun on the first breath.

2. Diagnosis — the Trophy Effect

A government cheque plus a standing task force closes the category they were funded to “solve”. They must announce. They will copy a six-to-eight-month-ahead evangelist to fill the announcement. Payday continues. The issue is politically solved.

The founder already did what a compliance officer believes AI can do to compliance. That is why they can copy the advice. That is also why selling that advice as a product is over. They do not need another vendor to complete a trophy they already hold.

This is the same mechanic in industry: a CSO who hired a Big Four to “do CSRD” also has a trophy. The plant still cannot produce the signed artifact that lets a shipment leave or a customer pay.

June 2026 already said “don’t sell fine-avoidance; sell tender velocity” (docs/field-notes/2026-06-10-offensive-positioning-dws10.md). It still sold the noun compliance. The trophy-holder owns that noun. First-touch must drop it.

5. Two-track founder motion

This is the 6–8 month lead, made operational.

Track A — Evangelist (public, free, copied on purpose). Doctrine, standards, thesis, LinkedIn, rooms. They will copy it. That is how the market learns the words. Do not sell from this track. A task force buying “advice” is them restocking the trophy.

Track B — Engineer (private, billed, uncopyable). DWS IQ running on their data, KYA-gated, Article-12-logged, ontology-bound, emitting signed artifacts. This is the 6–8 month lead as a product: they are still standing up the committee; the artifact has already shipped.

Rule: every public sentence is Track A. Every invoice is Track B. Mixing them is how the trophy gets filled for free.
