KYA Partner Addendum to the Data Processing Agreement

Optional addendum — Lifetime Oy ↔ Shufti Pro Limited Optional

1. Parties & Underlying Agreement

1.1 Parties. This Addendum is entered into between Lifetime Oy, Business ID 0772407-9, registered at Laidunmaanraitti 2 A 25, 02330 Espoo, Finland (the “Data Controller”), and Shufti Pro Limited, registered in the United Kingdom (the “Data Processor”).

1.2 Underlying DPA. This Addendum is supplemental to the Data Processing Agreement of Lifetime Oy (DWS IQ 6 Platform), version 1.1 (April 2026), published at https://dws10.com/legal-hub/dpa.html (the “DPA”).

1.3 Optional nature. This Addendum is optional. The DPA stands fully on its own; entering into this Addendum is not a precondition for the parties’ commercial relationship. Either party may decline to sign without effect on the DPA. If signed, this Addendum applies only between the parties named above and only in respect of Personal Data processed by the Data Processor on behalf of the Data Controller.

2. Recitals

the Data Controller has published the Know Your Agent Standard, version 1.5, at https://dws10.com/kya-standard/ (the “KYA Standard”), Section 3 of which describes the principle of Memory Binding;

the Data Controller wishes to invite trusted processors to align operationally with the Memory Binding principle;

the Data Processor confirmed in writing on 2 April 2026 (compliance@shufti.com) the availability of EU-only processing and storage at the Data Controller’s account level, and acknowledged the absence of model training and non-EU manual review under that mode;

the parties wish to record, on a voluntary basis, the operational practices that follow from those commitments.

3. Definitions

3.1 “Personal Data” has the meaning given in Article 4(1) of the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, “GDPR”) and includes, for the avoidance of doubt, biometric data within the meaning of GDPR Article 9, identity document images, verification metadata, and verification results processed by the Data Processor on behalf of the Data Controller.

3.2 “EU-Only Mode” means the configuration confirmed by the Data Processor on 2 April 2026, under which all processing and storage of Personal Data takes place exclusively within the European Economic Area (“EEA”).

3.3 “Memory-Mutation Event” means any operation that creates, updates, supersedes, or deletes a Personal Data record held by the Data Processor on behalf of the Data Controller.

4. Tenant Isolation

4.1 The Data Processor shall store, index, and retrieve Personal Data processed on behalf of the Data Controller in a manner that prevents co-mingling with data originating from other customers of the Data Processor.

4.2 Access controls shall enforce tenant boundaries at the record level. The Data Processor shall apply the principle of least privilege to internal personnel access.

5. No Shared-Model Promotion

5.1 The Data Processor shall not use Personal Data processed on behalf of the Data Controller to train, fine-tune, or improve any machine learning model, artificial intelligence system, or automated decision-making system intended to serve the Data Processor’s other customers or its general product, except with the Data Controller’s prior written consent and only in respect of anonymised, aggregated patterns that do not permit re-identification of individual data subjects.

5.2 Clause 5.1 records, in contractual form, the practical effect of EU-Only Mode as confirmed by the Data Processor on 2 April 2026.

6. Memory-Mutation Audit Trail

6.1 The Data Processor shall maintain an immutable log of Memory-Mutation Events affecting Personal Data processed on behalf of the Data Controller. Each log entry shall record at least: (a) operation type, (b) timestamp (UTC), (c) actor identifier (system component or human reviewer where applicable), (d) affected record identifier, and (e) reason code where applicable.

6.2 The log shall be retained for the duration of the Underlying DPA plus five (5) years thereafter, consistent with the record-keeping expectation of Article 12 of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) for high-risk AI systems.

6.3 Upon reasonable written request and no more than twice per calendar year, the Data Processor shall make available to the Data Controller, in a machine-readable format (JSON or equivalent), the log entries pertaining to the Data Controller’s Personal Data, within ten (10) business days of the request.

7. Term, Termination & Conflict

7.1 Term. This Addendum takes effect upon signature by both parties and continues for so long as the Underlying DPA remains in force.

7.2 Termination. Either party may terminate this Addendum by giving sixty (60) days’ written notice to the other. Termination of this Addendum does not terminate the Underlying DPA.

7.3 Conflict. In the event of any conflict between this Addendum and the Underlying DPA in respect of the matters expressly addressed herein, this Addendum shall prevail. In all other respects, the Underlying DPA continues to govern.

8. General

8.1 Governing law. This Addendum is governed by Finnish law. The courts of Espoo (Espoon käräjäoikeus) have exclusive jurisdiction.

8.2 Counterparts. This Addendum may be signed in counterparts (including by qualified electronic signature), each of which is an original and which together constitute one instrument.

8.3 Severability. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder of the Addendum continues in full force.
