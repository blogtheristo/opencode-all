KYA-S Certification & D&O Safe Harbor

KYA Standard — Know Your Agent / Know Your Architecture

DWS IQ 6 is governed by the KYA Standard (v1.6), a dual-purpose framework unique in the market:

  • Know Your Agent (KYA-S): Hardware-attested agent identity, capability gating, and forensic audit trails. Every AI agent carries a cryptographic identity, operates within explicit capability bounds, and produces court-defensible logs.
  • Know Your Architecture (KYA-O): Financial optimization and agent self-optimization governance. Agents iteratively improve their own models under safety bounds, delivering 11–30% efficiency gains benchmarked from real autoresearch runs.

Why this matters to investors: KYA is not a feature — it is a standard. Customers build compliance workflows on top of it. Switching costs are structural, not contractual.

D&O Safe Harbor — EU AI Act Article 99

  • Audit trail completeness: Every agent decision is logged with reasoning lineage, providing the evidentiary basis for D&O safe harbor claims.
  • KYA-S certification as safe harbor proof: Organizations deploying KYA-S-certified agent swarms can demonstrate EU AI Act compliance — reducing D&O insurance premiums by an estimated 8–12%.
  • Liability containment: The KYA Standard’s capability gating ensures agents cannot exceed their authorized scope.

Investor relevance: D&O insurance is a €2B+ European market. The 8–12% premium reduction from KYA-S certification is a quantifiable, recurring savings that drives enterprise adoption.
