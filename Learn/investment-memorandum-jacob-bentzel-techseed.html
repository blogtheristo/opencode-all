Executive Summary

TechSeed Nordics is now the GTM and lifecycle-support partner for Siemens Building X, Desigo CC, and Navigator. This partnership puts you in front of a massive pain point: Nordic real-estate CFOs spend €500K–€2M per year per portfolio on manual compliance work.

DWS IQ is the compliance automation layer that turns that spend into a revenue stream for you.

We do not compete with your Siemens systems-integration business. Building X generates telemetry; we operationalize the compliance layer. You remain the integrator and customer relationship owner. We provide the compliance-automation IP.

Revenue opportunity: €15K–€25K annual recurring per portfolio customer × 6–12 customers/year = €90K–€300K annual revenue with 60%+ reseller margin.

The Solution: DWS IQ as Your Reseller Channel

Three core components, all of which you resell under your brand:

Aegis — Regulation-to-Control Mapping

Building X sensor data → CSRD E1 disclosure, Desigo controls → EPBD certificate, facility logs → EU Taxonomy alignment, material assets → EN 15978 carbon. You integrate once; we provide the compliance schema binding. Regulatory updates flow through our platform — you don't rework client implementations.

Sovereign Infra — Jurisdiction-Specific Automation

  • EU standard stack: CSRD + EPBD 2024 + Taxonomy + EN 15978 (all built-in)
  • Data residency: All processing on EU-sovereign compute (zero US routing, GDPR-native)
  • Future-ready: As new regulations land, we update the platform; you inherit the updates

Connect — Audit-Ready Writeback

  • Native integration: Building X API → DWS IQ → submission-ready output
  • KYA-governed control: Approved changes flow back into Desigo CC with full audit trail
  • Signed output: Every metric tied to sensor source, calculation rule, regulatory basis — ready for Big 4 auditor review
