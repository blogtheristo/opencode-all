Event Overview

FIGBC (Green Building Council Finland) has opened recruitment for two strategic working groups focused on circular economy and nature/biodiversity in the construction sector. These working groups bring together member organization experts to define industry-wide standards and influence policy direction.

Source URL: FIGBC Member Portal (subscription required)

  • Define requirements for mainstreaming component reuse

Competitive Analysis: Tuotetieto Hiililaskuri vs DWS IQ 6

Launched: December 4, 2024 Focus: Building-level carbon footprint calculation for Finnish Construction Act compliance

Aspect Details
Primary Use Calculate building carbon footprint/handprint + building product list
Methodology Finnish Construction Act (Rakentamislaki) compliant

Strategic Positioning

Tuotetieto Hiililaskuri is: - ✅ Ideal for architects/designers needing building permit compliance - ✅ Finnish-market focused with local regulatory alignment - ✅ Accessible pricing for SME construction firms - ✅ Integrated with Rakennustieto's existing product databases

DWS IQ 6 is: - ✅ Enterprise-grade multi-regulation platform - ✅ Pan-European compliance (not just Finnish) - ✅ AI-powered automation vs. manual data entry - ✅ Full supply chain carbon tracking (CBAM, Scope 3) - ✅ Beyond buildings: manufacturing, transport, energy sectors
