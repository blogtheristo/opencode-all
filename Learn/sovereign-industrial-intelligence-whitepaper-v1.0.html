European heavy industry sits at a regulatory inflection point. By August 2026, the EU AI Act (Articles 12 and 50), CSRD reasonable-assurance requirements, NIS2 critical-entity obligations, and the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) create a convergent compliance surface that no generic cloud AI platform can serve — because the data those regulations govern cannot legally or strategically leave the enterprise network boundary.

This whitepaper introduces Sovereign Industrial Intelligence — a new category of AI infrastructure purpose-built for European heavy industry. Unlike cloud-hosted AI that exports operational DNA to foreign-jurisdiction servers, Sovereign Industrial Intelligence trains and executes entirely on local NVIDIA hardware inside the customer's network perimeter. The resulting model weights become a permanent, unreplicable corporate asset.

We demonstrate the architecture through a reference deployment at Europe's most demanding industrial facility: the Outokumpu Tornio Stainless Steel Mill in Finnish Lapland — the world's largest integrated stainless steel plant.

3. The Architecture: Operational DNA + Regulatory DNA

Sovereign Industrial Intelligence is built on a dual-helix architecture. Every industrial enterprise generates two irreducible streams of intelligence:

Operational DNA

The unique thermodynamic profiles, acoustic signatures, shift patterns, equipment behavior, and process workflows that define how a specific site operates. This is the knowledge that makes a 30-year plant engineer irreplaceable — and it lives nowhere except inside the facility.

Regulatory DNA

The compliance obligations, reporting cadences, audit trail requirements, and disclosure formats that the regulatory environment imposes on the operation. CSRD timelines, CBAM calculation methodologies, EU AI Act logging schemas, NIS2 incident response protocols.

When you train a model that internalizes both helices simultaneously — on-site, air-gapped — compliance becomes a native byproduct of operational awareness, not a separate cost center.

3.1 The 7-Layer Ontology

Each industry-specific model is built on a structured ontology that captures both Operational and Regulatory DNA across seven layers:

Layer Name Purpose Helix

Layers 1–5 feed the sovereign fine-tuning cascade. Layers 6–7 feed the enterprise sales and investor outreach engines. The ontology is built per industry — 25 industries covering European heavy industry and regulated manufacturing — and then customized per-site during deployment.

3.2 The Sovereign Training Cascade

Training happens in five stages, each narrowing the model from general to site-specific:

  1. Base model delivery — Open-weight foundation (Llama 3.1 8B/70B or equivalent), delivered to customer hardware
  2. Industry fine-tune — QLoRA on industry-specific ontology (~2,000 synthetic training examples, ~€40–60 compute cost via API-generated curriculum)
  3. Location fine-tune — Site layout, local regulations, climate zone, regional supply chain
  4. Factory fine-tune — Plant-specific ontology, machine specs, process parameters, team knowledge
  5. Continuous improvement — Field feedback from operations → next training cycle

Result: A per-customer, per-site unique model that no competitor can copy, rent, or reverse-engineer — because they don't have access to the training data that shaped it.
