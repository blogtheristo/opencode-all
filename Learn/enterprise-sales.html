Climate tech specific requirements

CSRD compliance creates urgent buyer demand through mandatory deadlines: FY 2025 reports due 2026 for companies with 250+ employees, €50M turnover, or €25M assets. Despite the EU Omnibus Package potentially limiting scope to 1,000+ employee companies, uncertainty is driving accelerated procurement as enterprises prepare for worst-case scenarios.

EPBD creates additional urgency with public buildings required to meet zero-emission standards by 2027—three years ahead of private sector requirements. This regulatory timeline favors early movers in building energy management.

NIS2 compliance (mandatory October 2024) affects software vendors serving essential and important entities. DWS IQ 6’s edge architecture provides inherent advantages for NIS2’s supply chain security requirements by minimizing cloud dependencies and enabling EU data sovereignty.

Nordic market specifics

Cultural adaptation matters: Finnish buyers appreciate direct communication with processing time for decisions. Swedish deals require more consensus-building across 3-6 stakeholders. Norwegian buyers are pragmatic but require strong due diligence documentation.

Payment terms: 31-60 days standard; B2B invoicing strongly preferred over credit cards. Annual contracts with multi-year discounts (10-20%) are normative for enterprise deals.

Government procurement portals for B2B2G:

  • Finland: HILMA (hankintailmoitukset.fi) - Kela runs 100+ competitive processes annually
  • Norway: DOFFIN (doffin.no) - 3,300+ active buyers
  • Sweden: Multiple portals (Opic, Mercell, KommersAnnons)

Common mistakes to avoid

Documentation pitfalls that kill enterprise deals:

  1. Feature-focused decks: Enterprise buyers care about outcomes (energy cost reduction, compliance achievement), not technical specifications. Lead with regulatory urgency and business impact.
  2. Missing CFO ammunition: Without ROI calculators and financial justification, champions cannot sell internally. 79% of enterprise purchases require CFO approval.
  3. Delayed security documentation: SOC 2 and ISO 27001 take 3-6 months. Start immediately—enterprise deals stall without security compliance evidence.
  4. Generic case studies: Nordic buyers prioritize local references. Kela validation is gold for Finnish B2B2G; document it thoroughly with quantified metrics.
  5. Overlooking NIS2: As of October 2024, NIS2 compliance is mandatory for vendors serving essential and important entities. Missing documentation disqualifies you silently.
  6. Consensus underestimation: Swedish deals require 3-6 stakeholder alignment. Create persona-specific materials (CFO one-pager, technical architect specs, sustainability officer compliance matrices).
  7. Summer timing: Avoid July-August for Nordic enterprise engagement. Budget planning happens Q4 for next-year procurement—align documentation launches accordingly.
