The insight

Two things worth keeping:

  • A hard-gate reflex is the wrong default for an unproven signal. The truth standard’s existing pattern (§3A) is “register or it drifts,” which could easily be over-read as “register and enforce.” The HIC’s correction here — recommendations first, FAIL enforcement only after an actual misstatement is observed — is the right calibration for a signal that is externally true (Pramaana Labs and RuleArena are real) but has zero DWS-internal usage history yet. Gating a claim nobody has made yet produces false-positive friction with no corresponding drift prevented.
  • Egress-blocked ≠ unverifiable. The direct site fetch failed twice across both turns (same domain, same block), but WebSearch reached independent third-party coverage (TechCrunch, SiliconANGLE, ACL Anthology, dblp, Google Scholar) that verified the substance without ever touching the blocked domain. Worth remembering as a fallback path the next time a specific domain is proxy-blocked but the underlying facts are publicly reported elsewhere.
