Recitals

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (hereinafter "Agreement") is entered into by and between the Parties identified above.

A. The Parties are entering into discussions regarding potential business cooperation and partnership opportunities related to enterprise AI platform capabilities, industrial automation, and digital services (the "Purpose");

B. In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose to the other Party certain Confidential Information and Trade Secrets as defined herein;

C. The Parties wish to establish the terms and conditions under which such Confidential Information will be disclosed, received, and protected in accordance with Directive (EU) 2016/943 on the protection of undisclosed know-how and business information (trade secrets) against their unlawful acquisition, use and disclosure (the "Trade Secrets Directive");

D. This Agreement is intended to protect the legitimate business interests of both Parties while facilitating their commercial discussions.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

Article 1: Definitions

1.1 "Confidential Information"

Any and all information, whether in written, oral, electronic, visual, or any other form, disclosed by either Party to the other, including but not limited to:

  1. Technical Information: software, source code, algorithms, APIs, system architecture, databases, technical specifications, designs, prototypes, and technical data;
  2. Business Information: business plans, strategies, forecasts, financial information, pricing, customer lists, marketing plans, and competitive analyses;
  3. Commercial Information: contracts, proposals, negotiations, partnerships, and investment activities;
  4. Personnel Information: names, expertise, compensation, and organizational structure;
  5. Intellectual Property: patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, and know-how;
  6. Client Information: information relating to clients, their projects and requirements;
  7. Any other information marked as "Confidential," "Proprietary," or "Trade Secret," or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the circumstances.

1.2 "Trade Secret"

Information that: (a) is not generally known or readily accessible; (b) has commercial value because it is secret; and (c) has been subject to reasonable steps to keep it secret — per Article 2 of the Trade Secrets Directive.

1.3 – 1.6 Other Definitions

1.3 "Disclosing Party" — the Party disclosing Confidential Information, including its affiliates and authorized representatives.

1.4 "Receiving Party" — the Party receiving Confidential Information, including its employees, officers, agents, and consultants who need to know for the Purpose.

1.5 "Purpose" — the evaluation and discussion of potential business cooperation and partnership opportunities between the Parties in the field of enterprise AI, industrial automation, and digital services.

1.6 "Authorized Representatives" — persons with a legitimate need to access Confidential Information for the Purpose, who are informed of its confidential nature and are bound by obligations no less restrictive than this Agreement.

Article 2: Obligations of the Receiving Party

2.1 Confidentiality Obligation

The Receiving Party agrees to: (a) keep all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose to any third party without prior written consent; (c) use solely for the Purpose; (d) protect with at least a reasonable degree of care; (e) limit access to Authorized Representatives only.

2.2 Prohibited Actions

The Receiving Party shall NOT: (a) copy or reproduce except as necessary for the Purpose; (b) reverse engineer any Confidential Information; (c) use to develop competing products or services; (d) remove proprietary notices; (e) acquire through any unlawful means per Article 4 of the Trade Secrets Directive.

2.3 Security Measures

The Receiving Party shall implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures; comply with specific security requirements communicated by the Disclosing Party; promptly notify of any actual or suspected unauthorized access; and cooperate in investigating and mitigating any breach.

2.4 Binding of Personnel

The Receiving Party shall ensure all Authorized Representatives are informed of the confidential nature of the information, are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement, and use Confidential Information only for the Purpose. The Receiving Party shall be liable for any breach by its Authorized Representatives.

Article 3: Exceptions to Confidentiality

3.1 Exceptions

Obligations under Article 2 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate was: (a) in the public domain at the time of disclosure; (b) lawfully in its possession prior to disclosure; (c) independently developed without use of Confidential Information; or (d) lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations.

3.2 Legally Compelled Disclosure

If required by law or court order to disclose, the Receiving Party shall: provide prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party (to the extent legally permitted); cooperate in seeking a protective order; disclose only the minimum required; and seek confidential treatment.

Article 4: Intellectual Property Rights

4.1 Ownership: All Confidential Information remains the sole and exclusive property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing herein grants any rights by license or otherwise to any intellectual property of the Disclosing Party.

4.2 No License: Disclosure under this Agreement does not constitute any grant, option, or license under any intellectual property right, whether by implication, estoppel, or otherwise.

4.3 No Warranty: All Confidential Information is provided "AS IS" without representations or warranties of any kind.

Article 5: Return and Destruction

Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, termination, or completion of the Purpose, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and copies thereof, and provide written certification of destruction. One archival copy may be retained solely to determine ongoing obligations.

Article 6: Term and Duration

6.1 Term: This Agreement is effective on the date of the last signature and continues for five (5) years, unless earlier terminated.

6.2 Survival: Confidentiality obligations survive for five (5) years from date of disclosure for general Confidential Information; indefinitely for Trade Secrets.

6.3 Termination: Either Party may terminate with thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Termination does not affect accrued rights or obligations.

Article 7: Remedies

7.1 Acknowledgment of Harm: Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure would cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate, entitling the Disclosing Party to seek equitable relief.

7.2 Injunctive Relief: The Disclosing Party may seek immediate injunctive relief without proving actual damages or posting bond.

7.3 Damages: The Receiving Party shall be liable for direct, indirect, and consequential damages, including lost profits and reasonable attorneys' fees.

7.4 Contractual Penalty: In the event of a material breach, the breaching Party shall pay EUR 100,000 (one hundred thousand euros) per breach. This does not limit the right to claim additional damages.

Article 8: Data Protection (GDPR)

To the extent Confidential Information includes personal data under GDPR (Regulation EU 2016/679), the Receiving Party shall: process only per the Disclosing Party's instructions; implement appropriate security measures; not transfer to third countries without consent; assist with data subject rights; and notify without undue delay of any personal data breach.

Article 9: General Provisions

9.1 Entire Agreement: This Agreement supersedes all prior negotiations and agreements relating to the subject matter hereof.

9.2 Amendments: Only effective if in writing and signed by both Parties.

9.3 Severability: Invalid provisions shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to become valid while preserving the Parties' intent.

9.4 Waiver: No failure or delay in exercising any right constitutes a waiver. Any waiver must be in writing.

9.5 Assignment: Neither Party may assign without prior written consent. Unauthorized assignments are void.

9.6 No Partnership: Nothing herein creates a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship.

9.7 Notices: In writing by personal delivery, registered mail, courier, or email with confirmation.

9.8 Counterparts: May be executed in counterparts; electronic signatures are valid and binding.

Article 10: Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

10.1 Governing Law: Laws of Finland, without regard to conflict of laws principles. Compliant with Directive (EU) 2016/943.

10.2 Amicable Resolution: The Parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through good faith negotiations.

10.3 Arbitration: Disputes not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be settled by arbitration under the Rules of the Arbitration Institute of the Finland Chamber of Commerce — one arbitrator; seat: Helsinki; language: English; proceedings confidential.

10.4 Interim Relief: Either Party may seek interim or injunctive relief from any court of competent jurisdiction.
