The Opportunity

Outokumpu's stainless steel carries a verified, TÜV-validated carbon footprint up to 75% lower than the industry average. Since 1 January 2026 that difference has a price — the CBAM carbon certificate. A fabricator importing Outokumpu steel instead of non-EU steel can eliminate or drastically reduce their CBAM liability.

But most downstream customers do not have the tools to calculate, document, and submit that advantage in a compliant CBAM annual declaration. The gap is not in Outokumpu's product. It is in the tooling layer between your ISO 14067 certificate and your customer's compliance filing.

Why This Strengthens Outokumpu's Commercial Position

  • PCF certificate becomes a sales tool. Today it is a compliance document. With DWS IQ 6, a fabricator's procurement team generates a live cost comparison — Outokumpu steel vs. non-EU alternative — showing the CBAM-adjusted price. The premium pays for itself.
  • Supports the EVOLVE customer collaboration pillar with a concrete, measurable deliverable — not a brochure claim.
