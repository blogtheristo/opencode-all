Executive Summary

Positioning (the one sentence): DWS IQ 7 is the Cortex Orchestrator + AI Actions layer for EU industrial carbon — the only platform that turns an LLM answer into a signed, sovereign regulatory filing.

The industry is converging on one stack: Data → Unified Platform → Semantic/Vector → Frontier LLM → Orchestrator → AI Actions. Snowflake Cortex, Databricks Mosaic, and Palantir AIP commoditize layers 1–4 across 2026–2027. DWS owns layer 5: the carbon balance sheet, the iXBRL signature, and the EU AI Act Article 12 lineage attached to every entry. Old BI ended at a dashboard; AI-native Cortex ends at an answer; DWS ends at a filed CSRD report, a settled CBAM declaration, and a posted journal entry in the customer’s ERP — on hardware in the customer’s building, with no data leaving the EU. Cortex generates text; DWS posts journal entries.

2.6 Carbon Balance Sheet — Debit-Credit Compliance Ledger (PRs 093A–093I)

Cortex-shift framing: This subsection is DWS’s “AI Actions” layer (see Executive Summary). A horizontal Cortex/Mosaic/AIP deployment can generate a carbon dashboard from a Snowflake notebook by Q3 2026; what it cannot do is post a signed, source-traced journal entry that reconciles ETS + CBAM + CSRD into one auditable balance. The ledger is the line item neither competitor can replicate without rebuilding their agent layer. It must ship in DWS7 Phase 2 — 2028 (DWS 8) is too late.

Why now (not DWS 8): The debit-credit ledger is the Series A story. Investors fund infrastructure, not features. A persistent carbon balance sheet that unifies ETS + CBAM + CSRD into one auditable ledger is the moat — and it must be demonstrable before fundraising.

Foundation: The in-memory costBreakdownToLedger() transform already ships in DWS 6 (src/compliance/debit-credit-ledger.ts). Phase 2.6 persists it, adds dual-unit accounting, and connects it to the compliance modules above.

The pitch: “From ‘How do we guess our footprint?’ to ‘How do we reconcile our carbon balance sheet?’”

Design Constraints

  1. Zero dependencies — No npm packages. Pure Node.js + TypeScript.
  2. JSON-native — Every command outputs valid JSON when piped. Human-friendly when TTY.
  3. Pipeable — dws discover compliance | dws run --first --stdin
  4. Backward compatible — New flags are additive. Existing commands never break.
  5. Offline-capable — dws help, dws config, dws scenario work without backend.
  6. EU data residency — All data flows through EU endpoints by default.
