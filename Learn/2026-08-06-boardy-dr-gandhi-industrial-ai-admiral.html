The Story — What happened

Dr. Anuj Gandhi shared a career spanning Indian Railways, defence electronics, heavy industry, BHEL, SKF, and venture building. His account described eleven years inside BHEL, four years at SKF, and two and a half years as a founder. The stated operating experience includes long-cycle procurement, public-sector decision-making, account depth, pricing discipline, and building from zero: a ₹1,500 Cr addressable opportunity, contribution to a ₹250 Cr AC EMU win, close to ₹2,500 Cr of business exposure, railway-segment growth from approximately ₹190 Cr to ₹350 Cr with EBITDA doubling, 66% Vande Bharat share, a MachFox order book grown to ₹120 Cr, and ₹2.1 Cr of first-year Neuromotion Systems revenue within a 106-week defence procurement cycle.

Two openings were sent:

  1. Acknowledgement of the unusual combination of institutional-scale operating

experience and founder-level execution, with an invitation to explore an India-Europe bridge around industrial AI, defence-adjacent systems, procurement, and market entry.

  1. A tentative introduction to a possible India-Europe Industrial AI Admiral role

in the DWS IQ network — a Captain operating through difficult Indian waters — explicitly not presented as a formal offer before understanding Dr. Gandhi's current priorities, capital appetite, and willingness to engage at that level.

The Insight — Why this matters

The conversation surfaced a credible partner hypothesis: DWS IQ's European industrial and sovereign-AI work may need an India-side operator who understands public procurement and long-cycle industrial access from the inside, rather than a generic market-entry adviser.

Dr. Gandhi's background appears relevant because it combines two forms of credibility that are rarely held together: institutional patience in large, procurement-heavy markets and entrepreneurial conviction under early-stage constraints. The Admiral framing should remain a hypothesis until his present focus, capacity, and appetite for a strategic role are understood.

Next steps

  1. Wait for Dr. Gandhi's response to the two openings.
  2. If he responds positively, ask about his current operating focus, available

capacity, capital appetite, and preferred India-Europe contribution.

  1. Only then share the Admiral role structure and discuss a concrete collaboration

path.

  1. Do not describe the role as an offer, appointment, or committed commercial
