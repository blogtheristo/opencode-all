The Story — What happened

The DWS IQ catalog was assessed against external enterprise-AI evidence and the internal MGD/CRC doctrine. The resulting strategy defines doctorate-level intelligence as reproducible, evidence-grounded, audit-surviving inquiry and post-doctorate-level intelligence as governed discovery and validation of novel industrial knowledge.

The source artifact is Strategy_Loop.md.

The Insight — Why this matters

DWS IQ should sell a visible chain from question to evidence to governed decision to measured outcome to retained knowledge. Fleet size, frontier-model comparison claims, hardware specifications, and consulting breadth are not evidence of intelligence.

The catalog should be organized into one Intelligence Core, Intelligence Functions, Industry Research Pods, and Deployment/Adoption enablers. Commodity IT, generic mentoring, staff augmentation, mobility, and unbounded implementation services do not belong in the flagship intelligence line.
