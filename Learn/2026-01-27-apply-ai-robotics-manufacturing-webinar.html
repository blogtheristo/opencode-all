Event Overview

This webinar analyzes ways to strengthen the development and deployment of AI-driven solutions in: - Industrial automation - Human-robot collaboration - Digital twins

The session explores how industry and other stakeholders can contribute to shaping priorities for Europe's robotics and manufacturing ecosystems. It starts with robotics as a cross-sector technology and then zooms in on how robotics and AI-driven solutions can support advanced manufacturing.

Agenda Topics

1. Robotics Vision & Cross-Sector Impact

Latest advancements in robotics and the Commission's vision for the technology's impact across sectors: - Healthcare - Agriculture - Services - Manufacturing

2. Software AI + Physical AI for Manufacturing

How manufacturing can benefit from both: - Software AI: Process optimization, predictive analytics, quality control - Physical AI: Robotics, automation, embodied intelligence - Outcomes: Improved productivity, flexibility, customization of production systems

3. Pilots to Scalable Deployment

Moving from pilots to scalable deployment of AI-driven solutions: - Robotics-enabled applications - Qualification challenges - Integration challenges

4. EC Support & Private Sector Engagement

  • Support from EC in development and deployment
  • Opportunities for private-sector engagement in defining Europe's robotics agenda
