Case Study Logistics Uber Bolt Taksi Helsinki Business Case: The Intelligent Fleet Pilot

Published on December 07, 2025

The Challenge: Fragmented Urban Logistics

In the high-pressure environment of Nordic urban logistics, companies face a trilemma: Efficiency, Sustainability, and Workforce Management.

Legacy systems handle these in silos: * Transport Management Systems (TMS) handle the cargo and routing. * Mobility Platforms (Uber, Bolt) handle the drivers. * Carbon Reporting is often a manual, retroactive spreadsheet exercise.

The missing link? A unified "Brain" that orchestrates these systems in real-time to meet EU Fit for 55 mandates while preserving margins.

The Solution: DWS IQ Integration

We didn't reinvent the wheel. We gave it a brain.

Our Lifetime Fleet Pilot demonstrates the power of the DWS IQ Architecture by orchestrating the dispatch operators that route work to Lifetime Fleet vehicles:

  1. The Logistics Partner (Taksi Helsinki): We leverage our partnership with Taksi Helsinki to access high-volume Kela transport flows. We utilize the underlying Atos infrastructure for robust, ISO-certified data handling, ensuring the "Ground Truth" of where goods and passengers are.

  2. The Mobility Partners (Uber & Bolt): As official partners of Uber and Bolt, we leverage their ecosystems for flexible, on-demand driver allocation. This solves the "driver shortage" bottleneck dynamically.

  3. The Agentic Brain (DWS IQ): Our AI Agents sit on top of these partner APIs. They don't just "report" on the past; they act on the future:

    • Predicting delivery bottlenecks before they happen.
    • Optimizing driver shifts based on carbon quotas, not just time.
    • Harmonizing data between Taksi Helsinki flows and Uber/Bolt availability.

Business Impact & Pilot Results

This is not a theoretical model. It is a live, revenue-generating pilot.

  • Financial Validation: Approximately €7,000/month of services and transport revenue (licensed transport operation + consulting) — not software revenue. It puts the company at cash break-even with a founder salary of €0.
  • Efficiency: Automated orchestration reduced manual dispatch time by 40%.
  • Sustainability: Real-time, transaction-level CO2 calculations ready for CSRD and Fit for 55 compliance.
  • Security: Leveraging ISO 27001 certified data structures ensures enterprise-grade reliability.

The Strategic Advantage

This pilot proves the scalability of the OneLifetime Ecosystem.

By orchestrating on top of the dispatch operators that route work to our own vehicles — Taksi Helsinki, Uber, Bolt and Kela, acting in the dispatch-service role defined by Traficom — we show that DWS IQ can upgrade existing legacy implementations into fully autonomous, carbon-aware operations without ripping and replacing core infrastructure. These operators are commercial counterparties of our licensed transport operation; they are not customers of DWS IQ or any Lifetime software product. We turn "Data Management" into "Intelligent Action."
