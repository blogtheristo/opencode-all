  <h3 style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong><em>What the Best Leaders Do Differently</em></strong></h3><h1 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Outperformers Aren't Just Better, They're Different. (Updated).</h1><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">McKinsey identifies a small group of outperformers—roughly 6% of organizations—that are pulling ahead. They’re not only winning because of optimized models. </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">They’re winning because of better <em>thinking</em>, <em>agent acting, agent orchestration</em> and overall <em>better operational systems</em>.</p><h1 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Workflows Rebuilt, Not Just Automated</h1><ol data-rte-list="default"><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">They <em>rebuild</em> workflow with new actions. </p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">They not just <em>automate</em> a workflow. </p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">They use AI to <em>learn and communicate</em> with old processes.</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">They <em>redesign</em> how work gets done from the ground up with a incremental process.</p></li></ol><h1 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Growth Loops act as continuous improvement engine</h1><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">They <em>think</em> in growth loops, not tasks. The goal isn’t just efficiency—it’s compounding <em>learning</em> and better decisions.</p><h1 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">AI Funded as Core Strategy, Not Side Projects - build ”Act”</h1><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">They fund AI like strategy, not side projects. No endless pilots. They commit, iterate, and operationalize.</p><h1 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Leaders Who Lead by Example ”utilize”</h1><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Their leaders are hands-on. When executives personally ”utilize” AI routines in their own work, they’re 3× more likely to scale it successfully.</p>
