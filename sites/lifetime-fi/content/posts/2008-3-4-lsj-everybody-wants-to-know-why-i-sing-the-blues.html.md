<p><strong>Ubiquitous</strong>. [adj]</p>

<li>(seemingly) present<br />everywhere simultaneously.</li>

<p><em>Oxford English Dictionary</em></p>
<p>The very essence of ubiquitous web 3.0 is the flow. Famous Finnish Pianist once said &#8221; istun vain pianon &auml;&auml;reen ja annan musiikin virrata l&auml;vitseni&#8221; (Olli Mustonen, lainaus muistikuvan perusteella, ei sanasta sanaan).</p>
<p>i.e. &#8220;I just sit down and let the music flow through me&#8221;. Artist uses his/her <em>intelligence </em>and <em>skills</em> converting the flow to a musical presentation he wants to give. Artist&#8217;s whole identity is in that moment. We, listeners, get our own subjective flow from the presentation that combines to our senses, everything we know, intelligence &amp; processing, and other people present at that moment. We feel the music.</p>
<p>What a joy of life !&nbsp;</p>
