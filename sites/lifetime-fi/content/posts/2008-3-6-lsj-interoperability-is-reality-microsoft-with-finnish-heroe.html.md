  <div src="v5"><p>(Lifetime Scope Journal is brought to you from Lifetime Oy Ltd) This morning I ran to Finlandia House to see all new Finnish Heroes Happen Seminar from Microsoft. The launch of MS Windows 2008 Server, MS Visual Studio 2008, and SQL Server 2008 gathered over 1.300 IT people to Finlandia House. As I have stayed out of the IT launches and seminars for over three years mainly because of the studies in Lappeenranta it was nice to meet and see dozens of IT friends and colleagues after this time.</p><p>Microsoft and tens of partner companies had made the&nbsp; necessary arrangements and the day was remarkble well organized. The show started 8am and continued 'till 16pm afterwhich the Party event took over. I missed party this time, but nonetheless show was impressive allright.<br /></p><p>In the Lobby I heard one sad news that one of my colleagues from University days was not participating this time. According a friend he died last month. This news somewhat made day more memorable and yes, a little bit sad as well.&nbsp; I worked with Juhani 1991-1995, and I remember him as loyal friendly person who always dedicated himself to work at hand. Rest in peace ... ...</p><p>Back to the show.</p><p>Microsoft Windows 2008 Server with SQL Server 2008 seems to product platform worth further studying for outsourced data center platform. Call me if interested about this kind of projects.<br /> </p><p>[continued]</p><p>The most news for me is the change in the openess of the software engineering platform MS Visual Studio 2008.
