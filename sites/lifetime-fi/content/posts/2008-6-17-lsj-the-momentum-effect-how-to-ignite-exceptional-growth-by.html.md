  <div src="v5"><p>Exceptional growth seems to be a must have for some people here in Finland. There is Dream of next major global company to pop-up somewhere from the Finnish woods. </p>

<p>For many, this is not a day dream but it is convenient way of charging consulting fees from various Government aided and funded organisations and the so called new company pyramid helping hands. Some of the organizations are themselves public companies and they possess and use considerable power over the <span class="caps">SME </span>network. Some include clever people who have filled their function as chiefs of various helping iniatives. Without mentioning the EU level. To me, all hands-in-their-pockets this is <span class="caps">OK.</span></p>


<p>If thousands of people get their living of thinking ways how to organize and lead the innovative human efforts to find scarce public finance. Sometimes it is enough to give a statement that there seems to be problems in Innovations System to get paid ;-).</p>



<p>To Entrepreneur or Artist herself / himself, to participate to ongoing battle of helping hands this is mostly waste of time. </p>

<p>Working months to add one line to Governmental program want-to-do-some-day section is far away from getting any funding to Lifetime Studios next productions business plan. </p>

<p>My advice here is to put funding focus totally to innovator people (de facto), not the companies (de jure).</p>

<p>Nor does the connections found from the grey area consultants give much of advice how to proceed. As always best knowledge is learned through hard work, not by committee meetings.</p>


<p>As an Entrepreneur Summary: It is OK to keep Innovations Framework as rewarding as possible to really harvest the best innovations and turn them in to global business success stories. </p>

<p>However, this is a task that should be done by helping organizations. </p>


<p>For the Entrepreneur to-do list is more like the following:</p>


<p>1. Create the Pitch that holds promise of how to Ignite Exceptional Growth. </p>

<p>Finding the Momemtum is essential.</p>

<p>2.When funding offers start to pour in to your company: </p>

<p>Check out the Terms sheet wether some if any of the raised money really enter your pockets in the Exit from your Innovationas. </p>

<p>Keep <span class="caps">IPR</span>s  and trademarks tightly to yourself.Protect your Intellectual Capital as well.</p>



<p>3.Concentrate to learn and become Best-of-Class in your Industry.</p>


<p>Best Pitch is when you can operate with your own funding.</p>



<p>I am the Boss of this company. </p>
