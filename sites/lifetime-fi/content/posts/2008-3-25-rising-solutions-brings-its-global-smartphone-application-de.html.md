  <div src="v5"><h1>Rising Solutions Inc. Joins the Symbian Platinum Partner Program</h1><p><strong>San Jose, CA - March 4, 2008</strong> - Rising Solutions Inc., a specialized mobile software solutions and development services company, today announced that it has joined the Symbian Platinum Partner Program to support the growing market for Symbian smartphones, offering its expertise, services and applications to the Symbian ecosystem. Symbian develops and licenses Symbian OS&trade;, the market leading open operating system for mobile phones. Symbian OS is licensed by the world's leading mobile phone manufacturers and to date, over 188 million Symbian smartphones have shipped worldwide to over 250 major network operators.</p><p>Rising Solutions designs, develops and delivers innovative smartphone applications and solutions as well as implements customer specific software development projects world-wide. Through its highly skilled software development teams, Rising Solutions enables mobile handset manufacturers, carriers, enterprises and ISVs to achieve faster time to market for their products and services and to gain a competitive advantage in their target markets.</p></div>
