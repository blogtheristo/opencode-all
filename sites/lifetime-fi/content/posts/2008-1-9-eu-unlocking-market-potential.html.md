  <div src="v5"><p>The European Commission is proposing to unlock market potential for innovative goods and services by lifting obstacles hindering innovation in a first batch of six important markets: eHealth, protective textiles, sustainable construction, recycling, bio-based products and renewable energies. These markets with high economic and societal value represent an annual turnover of more than &euro; 120 billion and 1.9 million jobs in the EU. With this initiative they may increase to over &euro; 300 billion and over 3 million jobs in the EU in 2020. </p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>The &ldquo;Lead Markets Initiative for Europe&rsquo; (LMI) proposed by the Commission will foster the emergence of these markets by notably improving legislation, encouraging public procurement and developing interoperable standards (see Memo/08/5). European enterprises would profit from fair and better chances of entering new fast growing world-wide markets with a competitive advantage as lead producers. LMI would also rapidly bring visible advantage for Europe&rsquo;s consumers in key domains for their welfare.</p><div class='follow-up'><div class='follow-up-caption'><strong>Update</strong> on This conference addresses the role of clusters for innovation and competitiveness in particular by discussing best practice examples of cluster initiatives implemented in different countries. The recently published European Cluster Memorandum and the DG Enterprise and Industry report on Innovation Clusters in Europe have already provided an important input to this discussion. This event is a major milestone for the preparation of the forthcoming Commission Communication on Clusters that will address the question of how to foster stronger clusters in Europe and to better promote cluster cooperation and innovation excellence.For more details please consult the PRO INNO Europe website, where the programme can be found as well as where speeches and presentations will be made available after the conference.</p><p><a href="http://ec.europa.eu/enterprise/newsroom/cf/itemlongdetail.cfm?item_id=1240" target="_blank" class="offsite-link-inline">http://ec.europa.eu/enterprise/newsroom/cf/itemlongdetail.cfm?item_id=1240 &nbsp;</a></p></div></div></div></div>
