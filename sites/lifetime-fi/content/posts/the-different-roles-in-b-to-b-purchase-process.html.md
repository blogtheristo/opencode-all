<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content>
  <div src="v5"><p>Organizational buyers are affected by environmental and individual factors.</p><p>Organizational buying usually involves group decision-making (decision-making unit DMU) and sometimes referred to as the buying centre. In a such group individuals have different roles in the purchase process.</p><p>The roles can be categorized as follows:</p><p>1. Initiator: the person who first suggests making a purchase.</p><p>2. Influencers / evaluators who influence the buying decision.<br /></p><p>3. Gatekeepers who regulate the flow information.<br /></p><p>4. Decider who has the power to negotiate the purchase.<br /></p><p>5.Purchaser is the negotiator for the purchase.<br /></p><p>6. Users and groups of users of the products in question.</p></div>
