<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content>
  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">According Gartner, Cloud management platform (CMP) solutions are advisable to support the management of multi cloud deployments and avoid under management. </p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">By 2021, 75% of organizations without Cloud Management tooling to manage their multicloud environments will spend more than they would have had they stayed on-premises, and will also lack needed policy and governance capabilities, increasing the likelihood of security and/or compliance exposures.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Gartner has identified seven capabilities critical to the success of the use cases commonly involved in this endeavor (see Note 1):</p>
<ul data-rte-list="default">
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Provisioning and orchestration</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Inventory and classification</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Service requests</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Monitoring and analytics</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Manage costs and optimize resources</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Cloud migration, disaster recovery (DR) and backup</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Identity, security and compliance</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The common use cases are:</p>
<ul data-rte-list="default">
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Agile applications</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Reliable applications</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Lift-and-shift data center</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Strategic DevOps transformation</p>
</li>
</ul>
