# From Static Products to a Dynamic Sales Machine

In the construction industry, static e-commerce sites often struggle to convert impressions into sales. At OneLifetime, we observed a critical disconnect: while our development moved fast on GitHub, our product pages on Squarespace remained static and manually updated.

## The Problem

- **1,200+ Impressions, 0 Conversions**: High visibility on Google Merchant Server but no follow-through.
- **Feedback Loop Missing**: Market signals (clicks, search terms) weren't informing product development.
- **Manual Updates**: Product pages lagged behind actual feature sets.

## The Solution: Agent-Driven Development

We are deploying two new DWS Team Agents to bridge this gap:

### 1. Market Signal Agent 📈
Monitors real-time market data from Google Merchant Center, Search Console, and Analytics to identify what potential customers are actually looking for.

### 2. Product Page Generator Agent 📝
Automatically generates and updates product pages based on:
- Real-time development status from GitHub.
- Market demand signals.
- Pricing optimization data.

## Why Dynamic Pages?

By moving from static Squarespace pages to a dynamic system (like this one, built with Python and static generation), we can:
- Test pricing models dynamically.
- Update features instantly as they are committed to code.
- Tailor messaging to specific search intents.
