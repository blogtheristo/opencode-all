  <div src="v5"><p>I got the chance to participate <a href="http://www.itella.com" target="_blank" class="offsite-link-inline">Itella</a>, a direct marketing corporation based in northern Europe,'s&nbsp;<font size="2" face="Trebuchet MS, Verdana, Arial"><font size="2" face="Trebuchet MS, Verdana, Arial"> Itella  &quot;Feedback Marketing&quot;</font></font> meeting in April 2007 where <a href="http://www.weigend.com/" target="_blank" class="offsite-link-inline">Andreas S. Weigend </a>told the audience about his career with Amazon.com and his experiences&nbsp; of customer behaviour and data processing. I here make some bits and pieces that from my notes and experiences of e-commerce 1.0.</p><p>After experiment the traditional E-Commerce 1.0 scenario with<span class="full-image-float-left"><img alt="pieniomena2008.jpg" src="http://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/54c48881e4b0d7a7fa7eddf4/1425316464794-EKBOQH2QV11AA6UUPDGI/pieniomena2008.jpg" /></span> <a class="offsite-link-inline" target="_blank" href="http://pieniomena.com">pieniomena.com</a> shopping centre also some remarks come from my own experience of driving consumer behaviour.</p><p>The interaction between web 2.0 and e-commerce sites was the main reason to drop current solution of pieniomena(tm).com. Why should I pay 29 &euro; for a site that has very limited functionality due the reason I had a &quot;starter package&quot; shopping centre? Secondly I have the burden finding and driving the traffic so that conversion rate is acceptable.</p><p><br>Thirdly I need to keep my customer registries etc. according the law (which costs), bank sell buttons are priced fixed, Adwords needs to be addressed with monthly payments and campaigns, print media used, stock of goods SCM organised and financed, etc., etc. The fixed costs of traditional e-commerce soon raise tremendously. In the end, as the E-commerce 1.0 salesperson said to me directly, many of these poor e-commerce entrepreneurs run out-of-business and money. What a way to make your living knowing the truth altheway</p><br> Ofcourse poor entrepreneurs are paying overheads for the more profitable customers, which everyone wishes to become. And there are indeed few good ones: <a href="http://store.mtv3.fi/epages/MTV3.storefront/?ObjectPath=/Shops/MTV3" target="_blank" class="offsite-link-inline">MTV3store.fi </a>for example. Owned by national broadcast company they have real advertisement advantage to pump up the ads daily in the TV network. This advantage <em>has </em>to create a successful e-commerce 1.0<br>

<p> So, what the heck. I sent closing notice. But only for e-commerce 1.0<br>pieniomena(tm).com brand is something Lifetime Oy Ltd. will continue to use and add value.<br>
And I don't think open source - (OSCommerce.com or similar) solution would be that much more cheaper. Fixed costs stack is coming so many sources.<br> But of course for some of my existing products, mainly books, in stock I need to have sales channel. <br /></p><p>Instead web 3.0 or semantic web world needs makes most important to make interactive, context aware corporate blogging pages. See also Anton Naumenko's great text about access control(previous entry).</p><p> The decision to drop current e-commerce solution was still a toughie. For computer engineer such as I, being the in charge for the e-commerce site feels great. It is great to log-on as DBA and move setting. Also when setting the pro package, priced around 60 &euro; / mo the functionality of the shop becomes an advantage. But before getting the traffic to that level poor entrepreneur will not get.</p><p>I hope that this makes you think before putting the money in the e-commerce 1.0. The duck does not surf there. If you have a star product, again think again. Fortunately my backyard is not filled of truck loads of stuff ordered from china for e-commerce success.<br /></p><p>Instead, Web 3.0, as Mr. Weigend names this in is his discussion,&nbsp; means to me, that the supply chain and business process I want to orchestrate is not fully under my command.

This also means that I have be professional player in all major web 2.0 services that bring value to my web 3.0 supply chain. <br /></p><p>When the supply chain tree leafs touch my 'consumer' I need to find his and her likings so that this person decides to step toward this lifetime Scope and start reading. When this happens, the blog entry would need to include something that makes person to shop - and make revenue to my entity / supply chain. Perhaps you want now order the floating duck AND get some incentive from doing so (As customer should needs to rewarded with a bonus, not only the other participants).<br /></p><p>So stepping out from traditional e-commerce means a huge step. And my need for solid consumer data grows even more. </p><p>What the consumer is doing today? Google, Amazon, MSN are in the know, so what can A poor boy do, but to play in the <a class="offsite-link-inline" target="_blank" href="http://blogtheristo.blogspot.com">Rock and Roll Radio Road Show Band</a>?</p><p>At least I dropped 29 $ / mo e-commerce costs. At least for time being. I closed a costs door.<br /></p><p><br /></p><p>Do leave feedback , please.</p><p>Thank You for being a lifetime friend;-)</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><br><br>
