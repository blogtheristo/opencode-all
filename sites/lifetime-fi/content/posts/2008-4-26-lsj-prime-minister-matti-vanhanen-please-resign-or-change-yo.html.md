  <div src="v5"><p>Prime Minister of Finland is responsible of the image we Finns have abroad. His maneuvers with the ex-girlfriend&nbsp;and their&nbsp;SMS Sex messages communications&nbsp;media show&nbsp;has reached yet another stage when the messages were published in Swedish Internet pages.</p><p>The amount of&nbsp;bad-will levels-off&nbsp;such highs that&nbsp;here in LSJ I wonder why he is still in the job?</p><p align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">I have known Mr. Vanhanen from his public image since 2002 or so. When he started his image was &quot;perfect&quot;: Married, no alcohol, Against EU and Nuclear Power: He&nbsp;had&nbsp;supporters and he supported&nbsp;people and ideas from left to right - social democrats to kokoomus. His character were stiff, but slowly his media appeal turned Finns on. He became celebrity of Finland with intensive media sponsoring and good-fellow network. He divorced and the hell skies opened.</p><p align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">The first Government Mr. Matti Vanhanen led did what it promised starting off&nbsp;from a difficult situation of previous Prime Minister J&auml;&auml;tteenm&auml;ki. This can be seen as one of his legacy from the first Government.</p><p align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">Finns are loyal. Even when the sex scandal were out as published book 2007, the public opinion seemed to favour of him as he announced &quot;<em>What are the limits of Privacy of Prime Minister. Can't I have any personal life?&quot; </em></p><p align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">The Emperor's new clothers are not the privacy Mr. Vanhanen is seeking. The best way to have privacy is&nbsp; talk the talk honestly. And then walk the same walk. In politics, lying is really master art when it comes to personal relationship. People want to know who you are dating. As simple as that. No exceptions in the PM level, I hear you saying. If he decides to go back to Editor of Keh&auml;sanomat I bet his dating will not be in interest of international audiences. But, as today, the Finland's PM's ladies are front page news. <br /> </p><p align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">&nbsp;</p><p align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">Many in Finland&nbsp; think that supporting oneself personal privacy and personal communications is essential to&nbsp;western way-of-life and thinking. In general, Mr. Vanhanen&nbsp;conclusions as&nbsp;such are&nbsp;good and recommendable.&nbsp;But, this is not the point.</p><p>The Point is Mr. Matti Vanhanen, that you have lied for years to Finnish audience about yourself. Your&nbsp;image in the Media mismatch the reality. And reality usually wins.</p><p><em>&quot;You told you were high class, but that was just another lie</em>&quot; like king of Rock and Roll announce in a lyrics that might semantically be linked to the case. And lying is not far away from betrayal. As a consumer I feel that the 'PM product'&nbsp;I hoped and believed 2002-2007&nbsp;to get is not the one I really have got 2002-2008. I am honestly disappointed about his character back those years.</p><p>And here is the real question. Is there any ethical minutes&nbsp;in the doings of Prime Minister Vanhanen in 2002-2007? Trusting third parties say he has been always trustworthy. Does his own behaviour shadow his trustworthiness?<br /> </p><p>Does this SMS sex scandal show to the public, that the man behind the mask is somewhat different? </p><p>Do we care? Most Finns do (as this is common procedures).</p><p>So here comes my personal conclusion: <em>Mr. Matti Vanhanen please resign since you have broken the trust I had with you. Show that you talk the talk, and walk the walk. Thank&nbsp; You. and goodbye from my TV. </em></p><p><em>OR </em></p><p><em>make clear case of changing the overall apperance in the front of the public eye. Emperor's new clothes do not suit you.<br /></em></p><p>The Game plan for PM and other Parliament Members of Finland for 2008 and the years of the second term still to go:<br /></p><p>1. Be honest to those voted you,</p><p>2. Stop fooling around with your Communications. Especially the SMS messaging is doing much of the damage.<br /></p><p>3. Don't buy Emperor's new clothes. People know you too well.</p><p>LSJ will follow the case, stay tuned.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>Thank you for being a lifetime friend.</p><p>blogtheristo</p></div>
