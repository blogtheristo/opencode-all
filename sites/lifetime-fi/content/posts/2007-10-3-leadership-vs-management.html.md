  <div src="v5"><p>Leadership is different from management, but not for the reasons most people think. Leadership isn't mystical and mysterious. It has nothing to do with having charisma or other exotic personality traits. It is not the province of a chosen new. Nor is leadership necessarily better management or a replacement for it.</p><p>Rather, leadership and management are two distinctive and complementary systems of action. Each has its own function and characteristic activities. Both are necessary for success in today's business environment. Management is about coping with complexity. Leadership, by contrast, is about coping with change.</p><p>John P. Kotter, <a href="/lfgs/">Harvard Business Review on Leadership.</a>,(pp.37-60.)</p><p>&nbsp;</p></div>
