  <div src="v5"><p>(LSJ) This summer was special to me. I was a driver of minivan serving elderly people in Munkkivuori, Haaga, Kaskisaari, and some other districts of Helsinki. To me, being old has been something rather far away of a thingy, and since my parents are not yet that old timers yet, especially the lifestyle of elderly people were not that familiar.</p>
<p>To my suprise, the best of the people at their eighties and nineties were in good shape, warm, and always full of fun and &#8216;tweeting&#8217; jokes. I really enjoyed many trips with them!</p>
<p>The services Community Centers, like Riistakallio, Kannelkoti, Haagan Palvelutalo, kept elderly people in a community that lived their own world peacefully. Weekly, a Bus made as Mobile Shopping Mall visited and served those who could not climb to my minibus and head to the local Mall. I  drove 50 to 100 grandmothers and grandfathers to shop and back daily.</p>
<p>I think this is my Ultimate Mobile Office experience so far.</p>
