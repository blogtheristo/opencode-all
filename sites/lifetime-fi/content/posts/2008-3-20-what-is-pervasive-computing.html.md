  <div src="v5"><p>Pervasive computing is the trend towards increasingly ubiquitous (another name for the movement is <em>ubiquitous computing</em>), connected computing devices in the environment, a trend being brought about by a convergence of advanced electronic - and particularly, <a href="http://searchmobilecomputing.techtarget.com/sDefinition/0,,sid40_gci213380,00.html" class="inline">wireless</a> - technologies and the Internet. <br /></p><p>&quot;The pervasive computing vision calls for computation, sensing and networking to be ubiquitous, whether with users or embedded into everyday living environments. A result of ubiquitous networking and computational ability is to form digital boundaries around entities. As such, communities, whether geographically transcending or based at a place, formed with a priori agreements or ad hoc via simply a collocation of interacting devices members, transient or long-living, can form a digital matrix or space in which its participants interact, provide services or utilize services.&quot;</p><p>&quot;the role of past and present as well as future contextual information (where a contextual information describes the dynamic aspects of people, places, devices, platforms, networks, etc.) to develop self-managing devices, middleware, database, applications and networks as well as to enable dynamic cooperation and collaboration between devices to carry out a distributed, autonomous task.&quot; <br /></p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&quot;Submissions topics:</p>   	<ul><li>New technologies and devices for pervasive computing</li><li>New applications of pervasive computing technologies</li><li>New interfaces and modes of interactions between people and pervasive computing devices, applications or environments</li><li>New tools, infrastructures, architectures and techniques for designing, implementing &amp; deploying pervasive computing applications</li><li>Evaluations and evaluation methods, for assessing the impact of pervasive computing devices, applications or environments</li><li>Privacy, security, trust &amp; social issues and implications of pervasive computing&quot;</li></ul></div>
