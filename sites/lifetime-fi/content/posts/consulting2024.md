  <h1 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">AI Engineering and solutions </h1><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">AI Engineering and Solutions at Lifetime Group is built on ethical AI principles, and it follow the EU Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act).</p><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">The EU Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act) is a landmark regulation that sets out a comprehensive framework for the development, deployment, and use of artificial intelligence (AI) in the European Union. It is the first-ever comprehensive legal framework on AI worldwide and aims to foster trustworthy AI in Europe and beyond.</p><h2 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Key Objectives of the AI Act</h2><h3 style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;">Protect fundamental rights and safety</h3><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">The AI Act prohibits the use of AI systems that are inherently discriminatory, unfair, or unsafe. It also requires AI systems to be designed and developed in a way that respects fundamental rights such as privacy, data protection, and non-discrimination.</p><h3 style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;">Promote innovation and competitiveness </h3><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">The AI Act aims to create a legal environment that encourages innovation and investment in AI while ensuring that AI systems are used responsibly. It does this by providing clear rules and guidance for developers and users of AI systems.</p><h3 style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;">Ensure transparency and accountability </h3><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">The AI Act requires AI systems to be transparent and accountable. This means that users should be able to understand how AI systems work and should be able to hold those who develop and deploy AI systems accountable for their actions.</p><h3 style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;">Risk-Based Approach</h3><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">The AI Act takes a risk-based approach to regulating AI, which means that the level of regulation that an AI system is subject to depends on the level of risk it poses. AI systems are classified into four risk categories:</p><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">1.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Unacceptable risk: AI systems that pose an unacceptable risk, such as those that could cause serious harm or death, are prohibited.</p><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">2.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; High risk: AI systems that pose a high risk, such as those that are used in critical sectors such as healthcare, transportation, and finance, are subject to strict requirements.</p><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">3.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Limited risk: AI systems that pose a limited risk, such as those that are used for entertainment or chatbots, are subject to less stringent requirements.</p><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">4.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Minimal risk: AI systems that pose a minimal risk, such as those that are used for simple tasks such as spam filtering, are not subject to any specific requirements.</p><h3 style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;">Enforcement</h3><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">The AI Act will be enforced by national authorities in each EU member state. The European Commission will also have a role in overseeing the implementation of the Act and ensuring that it is applied consistently across the EU.</p><h3 style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;">Impact of the AI Act</h3><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">The AI Act is expected to have a significant impact on the development and use of AI in the EU. It is likely to lead to several changes, including:</p><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">1.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Increased investment in AI research and development as the companies and organizations will need to invest in developing AI systems that meet the requirements of the AI Act.</p><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">2.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Greater adoption of AI technologies: The AI Act is expected to lead to the wider adoption of AI technologies as businesses become more confident that they can comply with the requirements of the Act.</p><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">3.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Increased focus on ethics and governance: The AI Act is likely to lead to a greater focus on ethics and governance in the development and use of AI.</p><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">Overall, the EU AI Act is a landmark piece of legislation that is likely to have a profound impact on the future of AI in Europe and beyond. Resources:</p><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">EU AI Act website: <a href="https://www.europarl.europa.eu/topics/en/article/20230601STO93804/eu-ai-act-first-regulation-on-artificial-intelligence" target="_blank">https://www.europarl.europa.eu/topics/en/article/20230601STO93804/eu-ai-act-first-regulation-on-artificial-intelligence</a> </p><p style="text-align:justify;white-space: normal !important;white-space:pre-wrap;" class="">European Parliament briefing on the AI Act: <a href="https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20240308IPR19015/artificial-intelligence-act-meps-adopt-landmark-law" target="_blank">https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20240308IPR19015/artificial-intelligence-act-meps-adopt-landmark-law</a> </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">AI Now Institute report on the AI Act: <a href="https://ainowinstitute.org" target="_blank">https://ainowinstitute.org</a></p>
