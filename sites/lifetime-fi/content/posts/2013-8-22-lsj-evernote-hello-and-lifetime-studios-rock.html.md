<h2>Evernote Hello and Lifetime Studios Rock !</h2>
<p>Remembering people is hard. Evernote Hello makes it easy by creating a rich, browsable history of individuals, encounters and shared experiences .</p>
<p>Remember people &nbsp; When you meet someone, you can enter their information manually, scan their business card, or instantly connect to a group of people using the Hello Connect feature</p>
<p>Make meetings memorable &nbsp; Hello automatically brings in related information about the people you meet from your calendar, Evernote account, online networks, and more .</p>
<p>Build a rich history &nbsp; You now have a full history of meetings and important information about the people you meet .</p>
