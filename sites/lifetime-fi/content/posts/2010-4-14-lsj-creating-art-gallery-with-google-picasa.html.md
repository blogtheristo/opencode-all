  <div src="v5"><h3>Creating Art Gallery with Google Picasa</h3>
<p>Publishing great entries of (LSJ) is combination of great writing, photos, code segments, and some inspiration (sometimes perspiration). Squarespace.com offers some nice Gallery options for publishing photos. But, when the photos are not yours, it is more realistic to create net folder in Picasa, and give full rights to the Photographer himself over his intellectual property. This is the first try of Picasa Web albums using the slide embedded object.</p>
<p>To be honest, this Picasa Gallery is not yet at the level of Professional&nbsp; Art Gallery, but the good of this solution is that the folder can be shared with different level of rights to all parties involved (let&#8217;s think about presenting a group of new talents and their work). I will post the updated versions later on.</p>
<p>Stay in the picture!</p>
