	Dr Pepper has revealed that it will give everyone in America free drinks if Guns N' Roses release a new album in 2008. We Europeans are waiting similar offer ;-)</p><p><br><br>The
soft drinks company claimed every person in the country will be entitled
to a free beverage <b><i>when</i></b> the rockers release their long-awaited <em>Chinese Democracy</em> LP by the end of the year.<br><br>In
a press release the firm told frontman Axl Rose that they understand
why it has taken so long to produce the record and said former band
members Slash and Buckethead would be exempt from the free drink deal.<br><br>The
company said: "Tired of a world in which Americans idolise wannabe
singers and musicals about high schoolers pass as rock ‘n roll music,
Dr Pepper is encouraging (ok, begging) Axl Rose to finally release his
17-years-in-the-making belabored masterpiece, <b><em>Chinese Democracy</em>,</b> in 2008.<br><br>"In
an unprecedented show of solidarity with Axl, everyone in America,
except estranged GNR guitarists Slash and Buckethead, will receive a
free can of Dr Pepper if the album ships some time - anytime! - in
2008.</p><p>Just add Europe for this offer. Old continent is thirsty as well..&nbsp;</p></div>
