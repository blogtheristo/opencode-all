<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Lifetime Oy Ltd. Company Profile</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Lifetime Oy Ltd. is a small-sized consultant company specialized in digital productions and Project Management, located in Helsinki, the Capital of Finland, Europe. The number of personnel is currently two full-time and a network of freelance consultants and partners.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Lifetime Oy Ltd. was established in 1989 as a result of five students creating in a start-up. The Original business idea was import trade of laptops, media products and Telecom products. We also created a Dbase IV financial application for SME.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">In 1992 other partners moved to other duties while current owner of the company stayed in while shifting business focus into software production. At the time multimedia applications were sold f.e. University of Helsinki.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Lifetime Oy Ltd. export 95 % of it’s productions, it’s main export markets being in EU, especially in the Scandinavia, UK, and Germany. In spite of tough competition, the company has done very well with the newest technologies , like 2.0 applications.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The plan is to grow to 200+ consultant multi productions company in coming years. </p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Own innovative products are also under careful planning. Lifetime Oy’s corporate blogging flagship is Lifetime Scope Journal (LSJ), which has readers all over the world.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">As to the company’s performance this year, currently in pre-seed phase of few business projects, expected net sales will be ca. 120 thousand euros for 2008-2009. The company is privately owned.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The foundation of the company’s vigorous growth is in its specialization, state of the art technology, expertise of Productions Management, focus in customer related projects, and own innovative products and services.</p>
