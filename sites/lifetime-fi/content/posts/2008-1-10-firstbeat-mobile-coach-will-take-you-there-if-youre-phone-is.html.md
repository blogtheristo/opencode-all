  <div src="v5"><p>Let me start by saying the all new Firstbeat Mobile Coach training software is a great innovation I have no question about it. The&nbsp; software is used by McLaren F1 team and the Finnish National skiing Team for example. I downloaded the software and will get back to the usage experiences later on. ASICS software has helped me so far, but something more integrated is needed.<br />  </p> <p><u>My concerns are outside of this software itself.</u></p> <p>I bought a new phone to our kid this Christmas, which include Bluetooth connectivity to share music and stuff between children. This is fine, but I'm somewhat worried as a parent about the consequences of bad Bluetooth implementation. When reading F-Secure blog you learn about the bluetooth hijackers that were there waiting to connect to your mobile. The all new protocol of Bluetooth is obviously safe. Or is it? What happens when it breaks, like every software products sometimes does?</p> <p>What kind of studies is there to settle my self that nothing happens with an open bluetooth phone? There is hundreds of kids running with these new toys to school this spring. <strong>I adviced my kid to shutdown the Bluetooth UNLESS he is sitting with his friend to send the music.</strong> And shutdown the Bluetooth again immediately. Paranoid ? Could be , but to be honest 20 years in software engineer has opened my eyes. You could add F-Secure Mobile Internet to the phone? It's 40 euros per year per kid. </p> <p>Who updates the software? Papa. Is it turned on or off? Does the other kid have security? And they are only 10! Sorry but we are late from basketball training AND then .. <br /> </p> <p>As a father Im responsible about the Bluetooth stack(Thanks Microsoft for fixing TCP-IP today..) of my kids. And it hurts in my tooth already. There is no easy cure. The Symbian intelligent platform is evolving very rapidly. When I do need to update my children phone OS. Once in a month patch tuesday? What happens earlier Monday? I don't like mondays..<br /> </p> <p> If something happens, well you're hurt anyway. Proving that the responsible company is that or this does not help you personally.</p> <p>Lost your business plan and data with open Bluetooth or broken application? You can prove it . Can you stand the court expenses? What year the settlement with M X will be approved?&nbsp; Using paper and pencil and independent consultant is working solution for some companies. </p> <p>The similar issue is with Mobile Coach training software. Using it's high tech features as an unexperienced trainer with a phone that has been attacked? Well, it obvious who will be hurt. Run 100km's in a day. hmmmm. I can do that! Really a thin client solution.<br /> </p> <p>Secondly the usage of mobile phone in the gym: I don't want to walk with my phone from one training machine to the another, running , or cycling class with my phone. Then 50 people forget their phones, there is sweat and water bottles and thieves. What a mess ! It seems already that the etiqette of no phones in the gym is violated and this makes it even worse.<br />  </p> <p>The only real interface I accept is somekind of transmitter like is in Suunto and FRWD training meters. The best idea is embed this trasmitter(s) to the intelligent clothes like NIke and Adidas is already doing.&nbsp;<br />  </p> <p>These embedded transmitters wearable computers, are the interface for me. Mobile phone is not. What if I swim or take Triathlon challenge? Do I take my Amazon Kindle with me? I bet iPhone has solved this.<br /> </p> <p>As conclusion (Free advice from Lifetime Consulting Services - considering a new project hire us!)<br />  </p> <p>1. I would like to buy this Firstbeat Mobile Coach innovation software. And I propably end up doing so after two weeks of thesting.<br /> </p> <p>2. I like to have good wearable trasmitters. The technology is ready out in the stores, but the learning curve is still there.</p> <p>I will get to this later on.<br /> </p> <p>3. I like to have a real personal trainer to do the program like it is today to make sure I'm still doing right things.</p> <p>4. I called my dentist already. He welcomed me and is sure happy of returning customer.<br /> </p> <p>&nbsp;</p> <p><br /> </p> <p>&nbsp;</p> <p>&nbsp;</p> /journal/2007/12/19/lifetime-oy-ltds-business-blog-media-cards-released.html  <!--
