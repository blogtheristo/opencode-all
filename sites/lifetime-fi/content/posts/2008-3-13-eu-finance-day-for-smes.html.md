  <div src="v5"><div class="content-element">                                         <div class="headline">                                                                                          <h3>FINANCIAL INSTRUMENTS FOR SMEs</h3><h3>&nbsp;</h3>                                                                                      </div>                                         <div align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">The European Commission has a set of measures to help finance innovative and growing small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). This includes making finance systems more SME friendly, facilitating finance of start-ups, innovation and growth as well as measures to generate more risk capital investments.<br /></div><p class="bodytext"><br /></p><div align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">Through the EU financial instruments of the <a href="http://ec.europa.eu/cip/eip_en.htm" title="read on: Competitiveness and Innovation Framework Programme" target="_blank" class="external-link-new-window">Competitiveness and Innovation Framework Programme</a> (CIP) a sizeable sum of money has been made available to help SMEs across the EU to access loans and equity to grow and develop their business. These EU financial instruments are operated by the <a href="http://www.eif.org/" title="read on: European Investment Fund" target="_blank" class="external-link-new-window">European Investment Fund </a>(EIF) in cooperation with national, regional and local financial institutions, which comprise the interface with the customer groups.</div><div align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">&nbsp;</div> <p align="justify" style="text-align: justify;" class="bodytext">There are also other EU programmes with measures specially designed for SMEs: Seventh Framework Programme for research and technological development, Jeremie programme and Eurostars project <a href="http://www.sme-finance-day.eu/index.php?id=8#c34" target="_top" class="internal-link">(more)</a></p> class="headline">                                             <h3>What</h3>                                                                                      </div>                                         <div align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">EU Finance Day for SMEs is a series of events in the Member States that the European Commission is organising <em>to inform about the EU financial instruments for SMEs</em> together with the national financial intermediaries that implement these instruments locally. The aim is <em>to raise awareness about different sources of finance</em> and provide <em>a forum for sharing good practices</em> in helping innovative SMEs get easier access to finance. <br /></div><p class="bodytext"><br />For these events, the European Commission will also invite speakers from:</p><ul><li><em>European Investment Fund (EIF),</em></li><li><em>Enterprise Europe Network,</em></li><li><em>European Private Equity and Venture Capital Association (EVCA),</em></li><li><em>European Business Angels Network (EBAN),</em></li><li><em>European Association of Mutual Guarantee Societies (AECM),</em></li><li><em>national and regional financial intermediaries,</em></li><li><em>organisations representing SMEs.</em></li></ul>                                     </div>                                                                          <div class="content-element">                                         <div class="headline">                                             <h3>When</h3>                                                                                      </div>                                         <p class="bodytext">One-day events will be organised in all Member State capitals in 2008-2009. <em>A list of dates will be provided regularly on this site</em>.<br /><br />The <em>launching event will take place in Ljubljana on Friday, 28 March 2008 under the auspices of the Slovenian Presidency</em> &ndash; programme and <a href="http://www.sme-finance-day.eu/index.php?id=13" title="read on: more information in English and Slovenian" target="_top" class="internal-link">more information in English and Slovenian</a>.</p>                                     </div>                                                                          <div class="content-element">                                         <div class="headline">                                             <h3>Who</h3>                                                                                      </div>                                         <p align="justify" style="text-align: justify;" class="bodytext"><em>Invitations to the events will be sent to</em> organisations representing SMEs, financial institutions and financial advisors, policy makers in national governments or regional authorities, chambers of commerce, local venture capital and business angels associations, business organisations, entrepreneurs, incubators, spin-offs, organisations and networks promoting innovation and other similar organisations.&nbsp;&nbsp; </p>                                     </div>                                                                                                                   <div class="headline">                                             <h3>Why</h3>                                                                                      </div>                                         <p align="justify" style="text-align: justify;" class="bodytext">During their start-up phase, SMEs often face a number of problems directly linked to the lack of resources: lack of human resources, lack of experience, lack of expertise in certain fields, lack of contacts, and of course, lack of financial capacity. Business defaults can have many causes but there is a societal interest in reducing hindrances for start-ups and small enterprises with growth potential. <em>One of the most important issues is to improve the access to suitable finance</em>.</p> <p class="bodytext">The Commission has developed a number of actions aimed at addressing these problems, and more details can be found on <a href="http://ec.europa.eu/enterprise/sme/index_en.htm" title="read on: European Portal for SMEs" target="_blank" class="external-link-new-window">European Portal for SMEs</a> (available in all EU languages).</p> <div align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">Helping SMEs to develop their innovative potential and raise awareness about Commission policies and programmes is the aim of the <a href="http://www.enterprise-europe-network.ec.europa.eu/index_en.htm" title="read on: Enterprise Europe Network" target="_blank" class="external-link-new-window">Enterprise Europe Network</a>. This new European Commission initiative offers entrepreneurs a one stop shop where they can seek advice and benefit from a wide range of easily accessible business support services.</div></div>
