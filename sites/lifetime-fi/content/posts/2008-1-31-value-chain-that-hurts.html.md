  <div src="v5"><p>When someone grasp a profit or benefits from someone's other's life with creating a negative value chain, it is basically and fundamentally an immoral action.<br /></p><p>When the negative consequences break legal aspects. it is illicit.</p><p><strong>Some examples of negative value chain cases and argumentation:</strong></p><p>1. When a company lays you off or violate your company's agreement because they like your idea and are not willing to fairly pay you, in a moment of greed, that is negative value chain to you (or your company). It is not ok to say &quot;we did it for more noble purposes, and that's why we did not compensate the original idea owner&quot;.<br /></p><p>2. When a public servant i.e. minister decides to drop a pension pay rate when feeling&nbsp; for a that strategically agile day &quot;what is right&quot; that is negative value chain. It does not matter that the pension is legally accepted by a doctor statements. <br /></p><p>3. When a different politically oriented group decide make a market research, and ask &quot;have you had ever a feeling about the issue&quot;, and when 60 % of the answerers say they have <strong><em>felt so</em></strong>, it is a negative value chain to publicly execute someone because of the <strong><em>felt opinion</em></strong>, if nothing illicit has not been happened.</p><p>4. When a network operator,who your company is paying, has access to your documents, and finds your business plan from the server, a creates new version of your site and business plan, that is a negative value chain. It is not ok by announcing &quot; IPR's are too expensive to fight in the court&quot;.<br /> </p><p>5. It is negative value chain when you want to create a good name, but this is manually replaced with bad search results. It is not OK to do!<br /></p><p>6. It does not help you that this site around your idea is build with big money. It doesn't make things justified to say &quot;I stole some, but I forgive me&quot;.</p><p>7. It is illicit to steal immaterial property of other individual. It will remain that way even if you end up a millinonaire. It is immoral if you just copy the ideas to your linkedin.com CV?<br /> </p><p>8. If your boss cannot stop you taking a position in the company, but he decide not to support in the work in any way just waiting you to collapse or resign, that is a negative value chain. It is not corrected by saying &quot;We are not going to continue the contract. BUT I think you can leave the office few weeks earlier than the original agreement, company will pay&quot;.</p><p>To be honest, this list could go on for looooooooooooooong...<br /></p><p>Internet makes it easier to create negative value chains. Do you accept this in your workplace?</p><p>How much did you earn of negative value chain activities today. When will you pay back? After life?<br /></p></div>
