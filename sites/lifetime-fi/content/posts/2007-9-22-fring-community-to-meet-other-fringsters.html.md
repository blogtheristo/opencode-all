<a href="http://www.fring.com" target="_blank" class="offsite-link-inline">fring™ is a free, downloadable client-based mVoIP (mobile VoIP) </a>application for mass market handsets which delivers the benefits of internet communication combined with mobile telephony to provide feature-rich mobile communication with the usability, cost savings and choice previously only available through multiple PC applications. Enhanced with real-time presence, fring enables users to make free mVoIP calls and live chat with multiple IM providers including Skype, MSN Messenger, ICQ, Google Talk, SIP and Twitter from one, integrated, contact list. fringsters save on free mVoIP calls and chats when using free Wi-Fi internet access or fixed internet data plans instead of costly mobile airtime (GSM/CDMA) minutes.
