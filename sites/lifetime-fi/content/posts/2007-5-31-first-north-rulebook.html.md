  <div src="v5"><p><span class="sizeLess20">First North is an alternative marketplace for small growth companies, providing a world of opportunities on the Nordic and global financial markets. It gives companies greater visibility and ease of access to Northern Europe's largest pool of capital, combining the benefits of being on-market with simplicity. First North is a part of OMX Nordic Exchange. That means that the companies admitted to trading at First North are given the same possibilities as large companies, but the regulations are lighter.</span></p><p>&nbsp;<br /><span class="sizeLess20">And when the company is ready for the main list First Northn Entry opens possibilities.<br /></span></p><p><em><span class="sizeLess20">Tiesitk&ouml; ett&auml; Lifetime Oy:st&auml; saatte yrityksenne hallitukseen osaamista mm. teknologiayritt&auml;jyyden, tuotantotalouden, tietotekniikan alueilta. K&auml;yt&ouml;ss&auml;mme on useita p&auml;tevi&auml; hallitusty&ouml;skentelyn ammattilaisia jotka voivat auttaa yrityst&auml;nne. Yrityksen hallitus on merkitt&auml;vin yrityksen strategian rakentaja. Vahvan hallituksen rakentaminen edist&auml;&auml; yrityksen my&ouml;nteist&auml; kuvaa mm. sijoittajien n&auml;k&ouml;kulmasta.</span></em></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />When Company is entering the Scandinavian First North Market Place the requirements are made for the shares.&nbsp;</span></p><p>2.2.1 Requirements for shares (adapted from the <a class="offsite-link-inline" target="_blank" href="http://omxgroup.com/digitalAssets/26671_First_North_Nordic_Rulebook_April_2007.pdf">First North Rule Book 2007 </a>)<br /></p><p><br /><span class="sizeLess20">In order for shares to be admitted to trading on First North, conditions for sufficient supply and demand must exist. The Exchange will consider this requirement to be satisfied in the following non-exhaustive list of circumstances:<br />&bull; if there is a sufficient number of shareholders, and<br />&bull; if at least 10 percent of the share class to be admitted are held by the general public. &rdquo;General public&rdquo; means persons who directly or indirectly own less than 10 per cent of the share capital or voting capital, or<br />&bull; if the Company retains the services of a liquidity provider.</span></p><p>4.3 Website<br /><span class="sizeLess20">The Company must have its own website on which all published information from the Company to the market shall, unless special cause exists, be readily available for at least two years.</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />Annual reports, prospectuses, and other information provided for distribution to, or kept available to, shareholders shall be readily available on the website, unless special cause exists.</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />The website shall also include the Company&rsquo;s articles of association as in force from time to time and details of the current Board of Directors and senior management and also the name of the Certified Adviser.</span></p><p>4.5 Annual report and accounting principles<br /><span class="sizeLess20">The annual report shall be prepared in accordance with applicable laws or other regulations and in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the Company&rsquo;s home state. If the accounting principles in the Company&rsquo;s home country cannot be regarded as generally accepted, the Exchange may demand supplementary accounting information.</span></p><p><br />4.6 Report of annual earnings figures and half-yearly reports</p><p><br /><span class="sizeLess20">After the Company's Board of Directors has approved the annual accounts, the Company shall immediately publish a report of annual earnings figures containing the most important information from the forthcoming annual report.</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />The Company must publish a <strong><em>half-yearly report.</em></strong></span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br /></span></p><div align="justify" style="text-align: justify;"><span class="sizeLess20">Reports of annual earnings figures and half-yearly reports shall be published as soon as possible, however not later than within three months from the expiry of the reporting period for reports of annual earnings figures and within two months for half-yearly reports. Such reports shall include a statement whether or not the Company&rsquo;s auditor has conducted a review.</span></div><div align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">&nbsp;</div><div align="justify" style="text-align: justify;"><span class="sizeLess20">If a Company decides to publish quarterly reports, then the requirements set out in paragraph three for half-yearly reports shall apply. Quarterly reports shall in such circumstances also include the information set out in sub-paragraphs a) to i) below. For the avoidance of doubt, companies may publish financial information to the market on a quarterly basis in another format than as quarterly reports. In such circumstances, the requirements in the Rules for quarterly reports will not apply.</span></div><div align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">&nbsp;</div><p><span class="sizeLess20">Reports of annual earnings figures and half-yearly reports (except for sub-paragraphs j-l) shall always include:</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />a) a summarized income statement for the financial year and the most recent half-yearly period, including comparative figures for the same period during the previous financial year;</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />b) the balance sheet in summary as of the close of the current reporting period, including comparative figures from the close of the most recent financial year;</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />c) a cash flow statement in summary for the financial year and half-yearly period, including comparative figures for the same period during the previous financial year;</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />d) a summary report showing changes in equity during the financial year and half-yearly period, including comparative figures for the same period during the previous financial year;</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />e) the net earnings per share for the financial year and the half-yearly period including comparative figures for the same period during the previous financial year. Information shall be provided before and after the dilution effects of outstanding convertible debentures, corporate warrants, and suchlike where such dilution significantly reduces earnings per share;</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />f) information regarding the number of outstanding shares at the close of the reporting period and the average number of outstanding shares for the financial year and half-yearly period, including comparative figures for the same period during the previous financial year. The information shall be provided both before and after the exercise of outstanding convertible debentures, corporate warrants, and suchlike where such does not give rise to a substantial increase in the number of common shares;</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />g) explanations of the earnings trend and financial position during the most recent half-yearly period including, inter alia, the effect of significant extraordinary events;</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />h) where information relating to the future is provided, the corresponding information provided in the previous report as well as any changes published since the previous report should also be stated;</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />i) information regarding the date of publication of the next report of unaudited annual earnings figures or half-yearly report;</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />j) proposed allocation of profits;</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />k) information in respect of the planned date of the annual general meeting of the shareholders;</span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20"><br />l) information as to where and when the annual report and financial statements will be made available to the public.</span></p><p>4.7 Forecasts and forecast adjustments</p><p><br /><span class="sizeLess20">Where a company that has published a forecast regarding financial results or turnover finds that the conditions have changed in such a way that the result is believed to significantly deviate from the forecast, the Company shall immediately publish such information.</span></p><p><br /><span class="sizeLess20">If a company intends to release a forecast adjustment or other unexpected significant changes in the financial results and it is assumed that the information will have a significant effect on the share price, the Exchange and the Certified Adviser must be contacted as soon as practically possible before the information is published.</span></p><p><br />4.8 General meetings<br /><span class="sizeLess20">Notices to attend general meetings shall be published in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter in conjunction with the issuance of such notice.<br />The Company shall, in a press release/announcement from the general meeting, immediately publish information that is of significance for the market.</span><br /></p><p>4.9 Changes in the Company&rsquo;s management, advisers etc.</p><p><br /><span class="sizeLess20">All changes in the composition of the Board of Directors, any significant management changes and the resignation or dismissal of auditors, replacement of the Company&rsquo;s Certified Adviser and information on any concluded or cancelled agreement with a liquidity provider shall be published.</span><br /><br />4.10 Incentive programs<br /><span class="sizeLess20">A decision by the Company to introduce a share-based incentive program must be disclosed. The information about this must sufficiently detailed to facilitate an assessment of the program&rsquo;s impact on the Company&rsquo;s earnings and financial position.</span></p><p><br />4.11 Offering of new shares<br /></p><div align="justify" style="text-align: justify;"><span class="sizeLess20">Where the Board of Directors or the general meeting of shareholders of the Company has adopted a resolution in respect of the issuance of new shares or financial instruments with a right to subscribe for newly issued shares or where the Board of Directors decides to propose such a resolution to the general meeting of shareholders, the Company shall immediately publish the resolution, the reasons for the issue, the principal terms and conditions for the issue, as well as the party/parties to whom the issue is directed.</span></div><p><br />4.12 Information to the Exchange or the Certified Adviser<br /></p><p><span class="sizeLess20">The Company shall inform the Certified Adviser about the Company and its business and also provide all information to enable the Certified Adviser to fulfil the Company&rsquo;s responsibilities as set forth in the Rules.<br /></span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20">The Company shall notify the Certified Adviser as soon as possible in respect of new issues, name changes, splits and other similar corporate actions.12 The Certified Adviser is responsible for notifying the Exchange, which undertakes to disseminate the information to the market.<br /></span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20">Where criticism is communicated by the auditors to the Board of Directors or the Company&rsquo;s President in accordance with applicable law in the Company&rsquo;s home state, the Company shall immediately convey such criticism to the Certified Adviser.<br /></span></p><p><span class="sizeLess20">The Company shall notify the Exchange and the Certified Adviser immediately of circumstances that might necessitate a trading halt.</span></p><p><br />4.13 Disclosure of insider transactions<br /><span class="sizeLess20">The Company shall require persons discharging managerial responsibilities and persons closely associated with a person discharging managerial responsibilities within an issuer of financial instruments to notify the Company of transactions in the Company&rsquo;s shares and share related financial instruments. The Company shall publish information about such transactions on its website.</span><br /><br /><br /></p></div>
