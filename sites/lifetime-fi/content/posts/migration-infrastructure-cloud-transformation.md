<h3 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Cloud Migration</h3>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>1. Develop a Cloud Strategy</strong></p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Cloud Strategy should be done early and in a way that prioritizes the right business objectives and optimizes technology journey. Cost Calculators and TCO are useful for analysis when building Cloud Strategy.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>2. Identify the right applications that are migrated</strong></p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A full analysis of architecture, complexity and implementation is easier to do before the migration rather than after. Should Monolith be broken to microservices?</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>3. Choose the hybrid multi-cloud</strong></p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A key aspect of optimization will involve selecting solutions and cloud platform that can help guide the cloud migration process during the transition and beyond.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Key questions include</p>
<ul data-rte-list="default">
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">What tools including security are needed per solution? </p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Can chosen architecture support public, private and multicloud environments?</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">How to scale? </p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">How can it help businesses deal with complex interdependencies?</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">How to avoid lock-in and inflexible architectures and redundant and out-of-date technologies.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>4. Plan early how to maintain data integrity and operational continuity</strong></p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Managing risk is critical, and sensitive data can be exposed during a cloud migration. Post-migration validation of business processes is crucial to ensure that automated controls are producing the same outcomes without disrupting normal operations.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>5. Adopt an end-to-end approach</strong></p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Service providers should have a robust and proven methodology to address every aspect of the migration process. This should include the framework to manage complex transactions on a consistent basis and on a global scale. Make sure to spell all of this out in the service-level agreement (SLA) with agreed-upon milestones for progress and results.</p>

<h3 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Transformation</h3>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">In the past few years, going cloud native has been a big advantage for many companies. But it’s a tough technique to get right, especially for enterprises with critical legacy systems. This practical hands-on guide examines effective architecture, design, and cultural patterns to help you transform your organization into a cloud native enterprise—whether you’re moving from older architectures or creating new systems from scratch.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">By following Wealth Grid, a fictional company, you’ll understand the challenges, dilemmas, and considerations that accompany a move to the cloud. Technical managers and architects will learn best practices for taking on a successful company-wide transformation.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Cloud migration consultants Pini Reznik, Jamie Dobson, and Michelle Gienow draw patterns from the growing community of expert practitioners and enterprises that have successfully built cloud native systems. You’ll learn what works and what doesn’t when adopting cloud native—including how this transition affects not just your technology but also your organizational structure and processes.</p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">You’ll learn:</p>
<ul data-rte-list="default">
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">What cloud native means and why enterprises are so interested in it</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Common barriers and pitfalls that have affected other companies (and how to avoid them)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Context-specific patterns for a successful cloud native transformation</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">How to implement a safe, evolutionary cloud native approach</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">How companies addressed root causes and misunderstandings that hindered their progress</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Case studies from real-world companies that have succeeded with cloud native transformations.</p>
</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Integration</h3>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Delivering excellent customer experiences is no longer a matter of differentiation — it is a matter of survival. To meet customer expectations today, an organization must facilitate seamless cross-app data sharing by integrating the many different applications the customer interacts with throughout their journey. A successful integration strategy will allow a company to generate multidimensional customer insights, anticipate customer needs, and minimize churn.</p>
