<p><a href="http://cordis.europa.eu/fp7/home_en.html">The Seventh Framework Programme for research and technological development (FP7)</a> is the European Union&acute;s chief instrument for funding research over the period 2007 to 2013. CORDIS is the official portal for participating in FP7 and following related developments in European science and technology.</p>
<p><strong>Network of National Contact Points (NCPs)</strong></p>
<p>The network of <a title="National Contact Points (NCPs)" href="http://cordis.europa.eu/fp7/ncp_en.html">National Contact Points</a> is the main structure to provide guidance, practical information and assistance on all aspects of participation in FP7.</p>
<p><strong>What is the NCP network?</strong></p>
<p align="justify">NCPs are national structures established and financed by governments of the 27 EU member states and the states associated to the framework programme. NCPs give personalised support on the spot and in proposers&#8217; own languages.</p>
<p align="justify"><br /> The NCP systems in the different countries show a wide variety of architectures, from highly centralised to decentralised networks, and a number of very different actors, from ministries to universities, research centres and special agencies to private consulting companies. This reflects the different national traditions, working methodologies, research landscapes and funding schemes.</p>
