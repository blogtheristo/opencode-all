After almost two years of secretive development, we launched <a target="_blank" href="http://splice.xignite.com/" onclick="return top.js.OpenExtLink(window,event,this)">Splice</a>, the first on-demand solution allowing developers to customize and share web services. <center><a target="_blank" href="http://splice.xignite.com/" onclick="return top.js.OpenExtLink(window,event,this)"><img src="http://splice.xignite.com/Images/t1logo.png" alt="Splice: the web service mashup platform" /></a></center> The idea for Splice initially came from our clients. While they all enjoyed the ease of integration and intuitive designs of our web services, some requested that we customize them to better fit their requirements. Common requests included optimizing for performance (lower <span class="caps">XML </span>verbosity), reducing the numbers of calls needed to collect multiple data sets, or customizing outputs. Rather than doing this on a one-off basis, we decided to empower you to do it yourself. Indeed, with Splice, you can: <ul><li> Combine many services into a single call. </li><li> Integrate private and 3rd party web services. </li><li> Format a service to exactly fit your application. </li><li> Re-use <em>Splices</em> to power any application. </li><li> Share your services with other developers. </li></ul>Our objective is to foster a community of developers facing like challenges, who are willing to build and share a large library of re-usable <strong>composite web services</strong> built on top of our services and others. Instead of spending time writing custom code that will be a pain to maintain, use Splice to deploy your integration on our servers and share it with other developers. Splice effectively provides <strong>"Integration As a Service"</strong>.<br /> <br /> The best news is that Splice is not only <strong>free to <a target="_blank" href="http://splice.xignite.com/MyAccount/Register.aspx" onclick="return top.js.OpenExtLink(window,event,this)">trial</a></strong>, it is also <strong>free to use </strong>for existing clients under Standard support or above. You can create and operate one free splice per service under subscription.</span></td></tr></tbody></table>
