<h3 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">AIoT and Edge Computing</h3>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Edge computing is a distributed computing framework that brings enterprise applications closer to data sources, such as Internet of Things (IoT) devices or local edge servers. This proximity to data at its source can deliver real business benefits: faster insights, improved response times and better bandwidth availability. (Source: <a href="https://www.ibm.com/cloud/what-is-edge-computing" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IBM</a>)</p>
