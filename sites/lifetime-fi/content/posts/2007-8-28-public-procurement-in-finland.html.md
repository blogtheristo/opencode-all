  <div src="v5"><p>Adapted from http://www.kunnat.net/k_etusivu.asp?path=1;161;122591</p><p align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">About 15 % of GDP (22,5 billion euros) in Finland is spent by the government, municipalities and congregations on the procurement of goods, services and public works. Finnish public procurement is subject to national procurement legislation which derives from the European Community directives on public procurement. Under these rules public sector procurement must follow transparent open procedures ensuring fair and non-discriminatory conditions of competition for suppliers. </p><p align="justify" style="text-align: justify;">The regulation of public procurement aims at a more efficient use of public funds in order to ensure value for money on public procurement financed out of general taxation. A further aim of the regulation on public procurement is to enhance the competitiveness of national and European enterprises. When procuring goods, services or works the contracting authority must take advantage of existing market conditions and improve the functioning of markets.</p> <p><strong>Public procurement</strong> is closely regulated. </p><p>The <em>procurement process</em> usually follows the following pattern: </p> <ol><li>Planning of the procurement  </li><li>Publication of a prior information notice  </li><li>Setting up a specification and other substantive requirements  </li><li>Publication of a contract notice  </li><li>(Exclusion of candidates)  </li><li>Sending the invitations to submit a tender  </li><li>Receiving tenders  </li><li>Opening of tenders  </li><li>Verification of the suitability of tenders sending companies  </li><li>Evaluation of the contents of tenders  </li><li>Choosing the winning tender&nbsp;  </li><li>The decision on public procurement  </li><li>Notification of the decision and the instructions for appeal  </li><li>Concluding the contract  </li><li>Publication of a notice of the results of the award procedure</li></ol>The winning organisation will <br /><ol><li><em>Finding suitable subcontractors <br /></em></li><li><em>Evaluating suitable subcontractors</em></li><li><em>Verification of the suitability of subcontractors</em></li><li><em>Project Management Activities:Nominating Project Steering Group</em></li><li><em>Nominating Project Manager</em></li><li><em>Running the project..<br /></em></li></ol><p>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;  &nbsp;&nbsp;  <br /></p><p>&nbsp;</p></div>
