This makes the text extraction faster and less computationally expensive, allowing it to handle huge volumes of documents efficiently.</em></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><em>It is not a feature for creating new image files or embedding hidden data within images. The output is the extracted text or data, not a modified image. That's a very clear and helpful distinction.</em></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Github Repository </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-OCR">https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-OCR</a> </p><h3 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">DeepSeek-OCR: A New Approach to AI-Powered Text Recognition</h3><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">(Gemini 2.5 Pro)</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The team at DeepSeek AI has open-sourced DeepSeek-OCR, a new model that rethinks how AI understands and extracts text from images. Now available on GitHub, this tool isn't just another OCR—it's an exploration of how large language models (LLMs) can efficiently process visual information.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The core innovation is a concept called "Contexts Optical Compression." This isn't about creating smaller image files. Instead, it’s an internal efficiency trick for the AI. The model can take a full, complex page—complete with text, layouts, and even handwritten notes—and "compress" its understanding of it into as few as 100 internal "vision tokens."</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">This incredible efficiency allows it to process a massive volume of documents at high speed (over 200,000 pages per day on a single GPU) while maintaining high accuracy.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Key Features:</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"> Prompt-Based &amp; Versatile: You can instruct the model with simple prompts to perform different tasks. Instead of just dumping raw text, you can ask it to:</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">   Convert the document to markdown.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">   Parse the figure.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">   Describe this image in detail.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"> High Performance: Built for speed and scale, it's ideal for heavy-duty document processing pipelines.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"> Flexible Implementation: The repository provides code for inference using both Transformers and the high-speed vLLM library.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"> Open-Source: The model is available under an MIT license, with links to the paper and model weights on Hugging Face, inviting developers to build upon it.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">In short, DeepSeek-OCR treats document understanding as an LLM-centric task, using its novel compression technique to deliver remarkable speed and flexibility. It's a powerful tool for anyone looking to extract structured data from documents, parse figures, or simply perform high-speed OCR.</p><p class="" data-rte-preserve-empty="true" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Kiitos täydentävästä tiedosta!
