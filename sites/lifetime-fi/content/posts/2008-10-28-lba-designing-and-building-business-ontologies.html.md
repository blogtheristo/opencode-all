  <div src="v5"><p><strong>Wilshire Conferences and Semantic Arts will hold their next 4-day seminar on &#8220;Designing and Building Business Ontologies&#8221; in San Diego on January 12-15, 2009.</strong></p>
<p>Dave McComb and Simon Robe of Semantic Arts will be the instructors. This is an intensive, hands-on, interactive course which has been taught numerous times over the past two years. Students have attended from a wide variety of public and private organizations, including the US Air Force, IBM, Oracle, Microsoft, Chevron, Boeing, Deloitte, Lockheed Martin, Wells Fargo and ExxonMobil.</p>
<p>We get a tremendous response to this seminar which covers the material in a unique, and uniquely effective, manner. Students who have attended other courses in the field routinely tell us that, after attending ours, they finally &#8220;get it.&#8221; Places are limited to keep the learning more effective and attendance at the most recent sessions has sold out weeks in advance.</p>
<p><strong>After this four-day course, you will understand:</strong></p>
<ul type="disc">
<li>What an ontology is and what it can be used for.</li>
<li>How representing information in an ontology goes beyond a conceptual model or a simple taxonomy.</li>
<li>URIs, RDF triples, OWL, TBox and ABox.</li>
<li>SPARQL, cardinality, namespaces, Qnames, bridging and federated ontologies.</li>
<li>The difference between frame-based/declarative classes and description logic-based/derivable classes.</li>
<li>The difference between open world and closed world models.</li>
<li>The basic principles for designing ontologies for corporate applications.</li>
</ul>
