Competition, the leading annual competition for mobile applications and business plans. The goal of this effort is to bring together the most innovative thinking on applications, services and business models. Business managers, entrepreneurs, developers and creative professionals can benefit from this engagement and will have a chance to get products and services into the hands of millions of Nokia device users, operators, integrators, and enterprises. Act now to be part of this exciting innovation. <br /><br />There is two tracks in Mobile Rules! Competition:<br /><br />Track 1: Mobile Applications categories:<br />Multiplayer/Connected Games, Multimedia, Enterprise Infotainment<br /><br />We are looking for new applications that use the unique enablers of the Nokia Platforms such as maps, navigation, motion detection, video capture and playback, and content sharing. Applications will be judged on uniqueness, design, ease of use, and the overall consumer experience. Don't let the categories restrict your thinking. Innovative applications and business plans that combine technologies and operate across these categories will also be considered. </p><p>Track 2: Mobile Business Plans:<br />Internet Innovation<br />Mobile Sharing<br />Very Human Technology<br />Green Businesses</p><p><br />The main focus of the business plan competition is communication and sharing. Plans are judged on their uniqueness, value to consumers, market potential, customer adoption, revenue opportunity and investor value.<br /></p><p>The competition offers a unique chance for mobile innovators to work with key players in the industry. It also provides <em>networking opportunities</em>, <em>market visibility</em>, and <em>significant rewards</em>.
