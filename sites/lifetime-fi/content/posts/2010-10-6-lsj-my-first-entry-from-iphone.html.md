<p><strong>The first edit session</strong></p>
<p><br />I am finally writing this Lifetime Scope Journal entry with My iPhone. The Api for Squarespace Ipone App works quite easily and intuitively. Words seem to auto translate to Finnish, and I don t know how to change the dictionary in IPhone. Odd words pop-up and if I am not very cautious Finglish is put inside the text.</p>

<p><em>My opinion: Stars 4 / 5 Good: This App really works with IPhone, and feels immediately useful.</em></p>

<p>My first lesson was that the save button is really needed when writing with IPhone. Since editing is made off-line, all changes needs to be saved before trying to add pictures to the Story.I learned this after loosing to the data universe this second paragraph. (online/offline editing)</p>

<p><strong>Q: How I begin to use Squarespace editor in IPhone ?</strong></p>
<p>A: Just go to Apple AppStore in Itunes, connect your IPhone, download the Squarespace app, enter your login information for any Squarespace account, and you are all set for your first Mobile blog entry and admin session.</p>
<p><strong>Q: Do I still need a workstation for editing?</strong></p>
<p>A: Yes, since most of the functionality for modifying the Post are not implemented. Offline editing is great for writing the idea of an entry. Taking pictures and video will make this tool even more useful.</p>
<p><strong>Q: Any plans to use this tool?</strong></p>
<p>A: It would be great to write on-spot story about Marathon Race Vantaa Marathon, Shoot the Running to a video plus zillion photos, upload the story to my workstation to finish.</p>
