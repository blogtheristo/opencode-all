  <div src="v5"><span class="sizeGreater40"> i2010 - A European Information Society for growth and employment</span><br /> 		 		<p>&nbsp;</p><p><strong>i2010 is the EU policy framework for the information society and 		media</strong>. It promotes the positive contribution that 		information and communication technologies (ICT) can make to the 		economy, society and personal quality of life. </p> 		<p><span class="sizeGreater20"> 		 The 		i2010 strategy has three aims:</span><br /> 		 </p> 		<ul><li> 			<p> 			 to create a 			<strong> 			<a class="offsite-link-inline" target="_blank" href="http://ec.europa.eu/information_society/eeurope/i2010/single_infor_space/index_en.htm">Single European Information Space</a></strong> , 		which promotes an open and competitive internal market for information 		society and media services,<br /> 			 </p></li><li> 			<p> 			 to strengthen 			<strong> 			<a class="offsite-link-inline" target="_blank" href="http://ec.europa.eu/information_society/eeurope/i2010/invest_innov/index_en.htm">innovation and investment in ICT research</a></strong> ,<br /> 			 </p></li><li> 			<p> 			 to support 			<strong> 			<a class="offsite-link-inline" target="_blank" href="http://ec.europa.eu/information_society/eeurope/i2010/inclusion/index_en.htm">inclusion, better 					public services and quality of life</a></strong> 		through the use of ICT. </p></li></ul> 		<p> 		 To achieve those aims there are various 		actions 		such as 		regulation, funding for research and pilot projects, promotion 		activities and partnerships with stakeholders. 		<br /> 		<br /> 		The strategy and actions, presented in the <strong> 		<a class="offsite-link-inline" target="_blank" href="http://ec.europa.eu/information_society/eeurope/i2010/key_documents/index_en.htm#i2010_Communication">i2010 Communication</a></strong> 		of 2005, are reviewed and updated through <strong> 		<a class="offsite-link-inline" target="_blank" href="http://ec.europa.eu/information_society/eeurope/i2010/key_documents/index_en.htm"> 		i2010 Annual Reports</a></strong>. The annual report also analyses 				developments in the ICT sector and assesses 				the Member States' progress in implementing their ICT 		objectives. </p> 		<p>Moreover, 		i2010 is currently undergoing a <strong> <a class="offsite-link-inline" target="_blank" href="http://ec.europa.eu/information_society/eeurope/i2010/mid_term_review_2008/index_en.htm">mid-term 		review</a></strong> to make sure that 		it remains up to date with the rapidly changing ICT environment. The 		updated strategy will be presented in spring 2008.</p></div>
