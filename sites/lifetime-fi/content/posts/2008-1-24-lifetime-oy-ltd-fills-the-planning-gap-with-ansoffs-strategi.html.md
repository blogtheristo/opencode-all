  <div src="v5"><p>An interesting aspect of strategic management is the development of specific strategies for achieving company objectives. Strategies must respond to the environment of the company and provide specific guidelines for decision-making in operational management. Lifetime Oy Ltd.'s strategy can be viewed as example case.<br /></p><p>Ansoff's 'planning gaps or 4P gaps' is classic theory of aiming the company objectives with set of strategies. Organisations seeking to grow by gaining a larger market share in their current industry or market follow a penetration strategy.</p><p>Lifetime Oy Ltd. specializes in Consulting including digital brand building, building digital assets with Web services and business blogs, produces services of digital productions, and advisor services including IT consulting, professional project management and IT specialist team member services. Lifetime Oy Ltd. markets are among growth companies that need to market their innovations to the global consumers.<br /></p><p>To close product gap company must increase market share on current markets with current products</p><p> OR </p><p>increase product share by increasing frequency of use, increase quantity used, new models and applications.</p><p>Secondly company has overcome price gap. This can be reached through new products and innovations.<br /></p><p>Distribution gap can be solved with diversification strategy, where new products are introduced to new market according the strategy.<br /></p><p>Promotion gap can be filled through carefully evaluating the market potential in the whole including all segments. In the B2C market it is often achieved by determine the maximum potential individual usage and then extrapolating this by the maximum number of potential consumers.</p><p><em>&quot;The Customers of Lifetime Oy Ltd's consulting Customers read business blogs and consumer digital productions on daily basis. They actively participate in various internet based activities. They modern, mobile world citizens whose income is usually above the national average level. Their accept new solutions and services as evitable evolution. They love to revolutionaze markets, products, market shares, marketing advantages, and therefore are powerful consumers. And they know it.&quot;&nbsp;</em></p><p>To really find needed strategic solutions to solve all 4P gaps company Sales can be met by good strategy work, and and setting the sales objectives in the marketing plan realistic and achievable.<br /></p></div>
