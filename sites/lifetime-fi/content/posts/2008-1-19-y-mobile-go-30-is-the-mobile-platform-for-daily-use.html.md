  <div src="v5"><p>Hello Mobile Lifestyle people!Are you constantly on the run? </p> <p>There is one tool that fits your lifestyle. Y! Mobile Go. &nbsp;</p> <p>I downloaded Y! Go 2.0 first time to my Nokia E61 device in December 2007 and I do like it more and more. Look and feel is clear and stress less. Using application&nbsp; became my favourite which I would even add word addictive.<br /> </p> <p>Now the Y! Go 3.0 (Beta) is also available. This time I'm using N series phone to gather experience of the new mobile platform.</p>   <p><strong>Improvements I missed in Y! go 2.0 (Revisited):</strong></p> <p><br />  1. In Mail: &nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp; I would like to see the list of mails and delete some of mails without opening them.</p> <p>Good thing is to see yahoo mail effortlessly. Could there be support for gmail as well?&nbsp;</p> <p>2. RSS and Atom feed support for Business blogs&nbsp;</p> <p>This seems to be OK. Using One Search is working fine..</p> <p> 3. In Web:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp; support html x.x for browsing more. I want <a href="http://www.kolumbus.fi/lifetimeoy" target="_blank" class="offsite-link-inline">Lifetime Oy Retro page </a>to look and feel the way it is!</p> <p>I got to the web browser of the mobile phone but going back is not easy!</p> <p>4. Secure pop mail support for other email accounts, like local IMAP mail boxers and hotmail more</p> <p>I'm not sure if this feature is there?&nbsp;</p> <p>5. Connection to Fring and Y! IM would be important?</p> <p>&nbsp;Also 3G to 4G and to 7G speeds later on will improve service creatly. Adding that Myspace support will certainly add more and more usage for Y! Go Mobile.<br /> </p>   <p>Overall weight: This stuff is cool. And it is getting serious. <br />  </p>   <p><strong>Y! Go 2.0 Mobile: &nbsp;&nbsp; Lifetime Scope Rank: 4 stars out of 5.</strong></p> <p><strong>Y! Go 3.0 Mobile:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; </strong><strong>Beta Effort Rank: 4 stars out of 5.
