  <p style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Hello Lifetime Friends, exciting news !</p>
<p style="white-space:pre-wrap;">5G network is opening this spring in Finland. Our partner Telia Company has released new 3,5 gHz network in Oulu couple of weeks ago. In Finland, we are using the same bandwidth as many other countries.</p>
<p style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The 5G development opens up new markets and a global ecosystem on devices and services that are 5G standard based. We saw many new openings of 5G devices and smart phones in Barcelona Mobile Congress.</p>
<p style="white-space:pre-wrap;">IoT based on 5G solutions create business capabilities that makes it easy eliminating redundant work phases and optimize running costs, they transform and automate daily operations while improving competitive advantage.</p>
<p style="white-space:pre-wrap;"> 5G based AR / VR is creating completely new business concepts and service models.&nbsp;</p>
<blockquote><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;">“Lifetime Group and its Partners are available for 5G / IoT ecosystem device and application development. We have over 40 different digital roles and more than 20 Cloud Ready Teams built to launch and support all kind of 5G/IoT projects” </p>
</blockquote>
<p style="text-align:right;white-space:pre-wrap;">Risto Anton, Director, Lifetime Group.</p>
