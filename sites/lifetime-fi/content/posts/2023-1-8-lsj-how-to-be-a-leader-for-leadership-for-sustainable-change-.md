<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content>
  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Being a leader for sustainable change involves taking a proactive approach to bringing about positive, long-term improvements in your community or organization. Some key strategies for being an effective leader in this context include:</p>
</div>




















  
  






  
      <img src="https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/54c48881e4b0d7a7fa7eddf4/1673170632139-KASIC7P36SJWMUT7GGLZ/unsplash-image-Ws4wd-vJ9M0.jpg?format=original" alt=""/>
  



<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content>
  <ol data-rte-list="default"><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Clearly define your vision for change: Identify the specific areas where you want to bring about sustainable improvements and communicate this vision to others in a clear and compelling way.</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Engage and mobilize others: Sustainable change requires the support and efforts of a wide range of stakeholders. Engage with others who share your vision and work to mobilize them to take action.</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Foster collaboration: Sustainable change often requires the efforts of multiple organizations, agencies, or groups. Work to build partnerships and collaborative relationships that can help you achieve your goals.</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Build capacity: Help others develop the skills, knowledge, and resources they need to contribute to sustainable change. This may involve training, coaching, or providing support in other ways.</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Lead by example: As a leader, your actions can set the tone for others. Lead by example by embodying the values and behaviors that are consistent with your vision for sustainable change.</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Be persistent: Change can be difficult, and there may be obstacles or setbacks along the way. Remain persistent and committed to your goals, and keep working to move forward even in the face of challenges.</p></li></ol>
</div>
