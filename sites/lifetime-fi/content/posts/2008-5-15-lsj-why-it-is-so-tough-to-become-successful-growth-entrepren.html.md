  <div src="v5"><p>I started Lifetime Oy 1989. I have failed twice for entering markets with my business plan, namely 1992 and 1997. I have got some loans 1996 to start-up, and walked through the new positioning of Entrepreneur in the Finnish Society. </p><p style="text-align: justify;" align="justify">In the 80&#8217;s Entrepreneurs were considered as 1) Crooks and Criminals 2) Crazy Risk takers who risked everything for their business.If things went bad hanging yourself to the tree after loosing everything was the de facto honourable way of closing business! The System administration similar to Soviet Union was the way to do things in the 80s.<br></p><p style="text-align: justify;" align="justify">In the 90&#8217;s new funding&nbsp; instruments emerged and Government of Finland introduced &#8216;women loans&#8217; - money loans guaranteed by State for small business emerged to lighten start-up burden. These loans were something like 10-50 t€ and the real meaning were to help Hair Saloons&nbsp; and other work oriented Entrepreneurs to succeed the start. No one discussed nor offered any Business Angel money. Venture Capital was not available to SMEs. The loan was ofcourse quite too small for anything else than the barber chair with a room. But nonethless it was change of mindset!</p><p style="text-align: justify;" align="justify">&nbsp;</p><p>2005 I started to study Entrepreneurship in the Lappeenranta University of Tech. The Innovations and VCs become familiar and I raised high hopes to finding such a business plan that would be backed up by VCs. Writing Business plans and opening up this site were the first things I learned again.&nbsp; Google Adsense were hot in the news for making money. I hope your site has brought you fortunes. Mine has too ;-)</p><h2>What I have learned in four years? Why it is so hard to become successful Growth Entrepreneur?&nbsp;</h2><h2>&nbsp;</h2><p><span class="entry-content">My definition of Growth Entrepreneurship goes like this: </span></p><p><span class="entry-content">Growth Entrepreneurship consists of 2 simultaneous processes: </span></p><p><span class="entry-content">1. Learning as an Entrepreneur and Man.&nbsp;</span></p><p><span class="entry-content">2. and Developing business ideas in to success stories and profitable Innovations.</span></p><p>At the same time you have to win daily your struggle of staying alive and finding money to support your family activities. </p><p>* The ability to execute Entrepreneur lifestyle IS already sign of commitment.&nbsp;</p><p>* The ability to run business daily routines IS already sign solid business skills.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>Web 2.0 world is fascinating. New startups come to publicity daily. What strikes me is that all of the are published from Silicon Valley Area / USA. No European software services is happening, not at least in the same quantities.</p><p style="text-align: justify;" align="justify">The discussion of Financial issues in Finland is still Government Aid Oriented. The Entrepreneur as person is not really considered that important. They sitill miss next Nokia.&nbsp; Invent <i>some pocket calculator</i>, invent <i>device</i>. Then youre doing real innnovations, I hear them thinking. This is also changing but was the mantra for a decade.<br></p><p style="text-align: justify;" align="justify"> Well, Charlie Chaplin, who is my favourite of running his studios quite successfully with countless productions also ran successful business as well. He was backed up VCs that acknowledged his talent to setup the Chaplin Studios. No Goverment Systems there (1920s).<br></p><p style="text-align: justify;" align="justify">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: justify;" align="justify">Why we, 2008, ( in the EU, Finland, or Globally) are not concentrating on finding and helping new talents that really are innovators or have some other important role in the Innovation value chain? The challenges, f.e. sustainable future, are too important to waste time on secondary pyramidio investment &#8216;systems&#8217;. Only one that miss this game is the third party charging of the &#8216;planning&#8217;. </p><p style="text-align: justify;" align="justify">Writing NIY support forms (30pp) to get something financial help out is now in my agenda. Help! I contacted three business consultants here in Espoo, but none of the understood much of the Forms business. Who does?<br> </p><p style="text-align: justify;" align="justify">Put people in charge and you find the taking the responsibility.</p><p style="text-align: justify;" align="justify">&#8220;Take in coach I&#8217;m ready to play..today&#8221;&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: justify;" align="justify">John Fogerty (Rock on John already 40 years and still going strong!!!)&nbsp;</p><p>[continued]&nbsp;</p><p><br>&nbsp;</p></div>
