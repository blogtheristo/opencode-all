We need to get vaccinated when it is available.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><br><br></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">3. We should make sure in our daily operations that loneliness&nbsp;</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">is one of the biggest real life problems that we want to solve with our own behavior.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><br><br></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">5.
