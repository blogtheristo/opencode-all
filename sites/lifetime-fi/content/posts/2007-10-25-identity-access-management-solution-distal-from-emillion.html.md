Distal can now be used as a SAML proxy<br><br>

Emillion announces that Distal, its identity and access management solution, now can be used as a SAML proxy.<br><br>

The Security Assertion Markup language (SAML) is an XML standard that makes it possible to offer single sign-on between organisations. Although a standard, the adoption on the market has been very slow, probably because the standard requires user organisations to invest in new software to be able to benefit from the standard.<br><br>

Distal allows service providers to start building on SAML, without ruling out the vast majority of customers and partners not yet supporting it. The only requirements on a user organisation to use Distal federation is a basic intranet or portal and 15 minutes by the intranet webmaster. Those requirements are met by close to 100% of large and medium-sized organisation.(More information see www.emillion.fi).<br><br>
