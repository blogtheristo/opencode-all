Forum Nokia calling All Innovators to the Mobile Rules! Competition


Forum Nokia 18th of July Newsletter with co-operation of Lifetime Brands Program(tm)

"Nokia is pleased to launch the Mobile Rules! Competition, the leading annual competition for mobile applications and business plans. The goal of this effort is to bring together the most innovative thinking on applications, services and business models. Business managers, entrepreneurs, developers and creative professionals can benefit from this engagement and will have a chance to get products and services into the hands of millions of Nokia device users, operators, integrators, and enterprises. Act now to be part of this exciting innovation.

There is two tracks in Mobile Rules! Competition:

Track 1: Mobile Applications categories:
Multiplayer/Connected Games, Multimedia, Enterprise Infotainment

We are looking for new applications that use the unique enablers of the Nokia Platforms such as maps, navigation, motion detection, video capture and playback, and content sharing. Applications will be judged on uniqueness, design, ease of use, and the overall consumer experience. Don't let the categories restrict your thinking. Innovative applications and business plans that combine technologies and operate across these categories will also be considered.

Track 2: Mobile Business Plans:
Internet Innovation
Mobile Sharing
Very Human Technology
Green Businesses


The main focus of the business plan competition is communication and sharing. Plans are judged on their uniqueness, value to consumers, market potential, customer adoption, revenue opportunity and investor value.

The competition offers a unique chance for mobile innovators to work with key players in the industry. It also provides networking opportunities, market visibility, and significant rewards. For more information, please visit http://www.uptilt.com/ct.html?rtr=on&s=7z7,td2l,1dm0,8sof,iymh,f3e2,c0s5 or e-mail Forum Nokia at opencworkshop@nokia.com.
 
The submission deadline for mobile applications is January 25, 2008 and the deadline for business plans is November 16, 2007.

Update on 2008-01-07 10:49 by Editor in Chief, Lifetime Studios

Hello Developers, Technologists and Entrepreneurs,
All streams of Nokia Mobile Rules! competition are closing on 25th January
Mobile Applications and Technology Innovations streams of the Mobile Rules! competition are closing in 18 days and entrants are encouraged to submit now on the competition site: www.mobilerules.org and www.mobilerules.org/technology (for technology Innovation).

Total of 39 business plans were selected to the second round from over 150 plans on 14th December. Competition was tough and many good plans were eliminated. All those best 39 are listed at www.mobilerules.org. Second round candidates will still have opportunity to improve their plans by 25th January. Instructions for updating are sent separately.

The winners of all streams will be announced at a ceremony in San Jose on Wednesday 19th March 2008. The place is magnificent City Hall http://www.sanjoseca.gov/cityManager/gallery/gallery_rotunda.asp

Thanks for attending and good luck for all!

Best regards,
