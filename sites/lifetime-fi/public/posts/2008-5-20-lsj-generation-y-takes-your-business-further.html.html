Generation Y is Here

 

Those of us who belong to Generation Y are born in the late 1970’s and early 1980’s.

Generation Y is described in Media as generation with great semantic knowledge, and especially increased team play.

They are passionate for great digital success stories. According Media, among Yers there are no leadership ambitions.

Generation Y is not a fad. It is not a media ploy either. It means  the real players for the time being. Running Mercedes already at their twenties they are the best equipped generation ever. Ride on, dear Y. You have Director’s chair waiting here in Lifetime Studios. Contact today, and we can build things together.

 

In Universitities generation Y is innovation oriented. And thinking world’s problems, they might just be saving the World as well. At least I am putting my hope for these people. Really.

 

In their recent book, Reynol Junco and Jeanna Mastrodicasa (2007) [21] found that in a survey of 7,705 college students in the US:

  • 97% own a computer
  • 94% own a cell phone
  • 76% use Instant Messaging.
  • 15% of IM users are logged on 24 hours a day/7 days a week
  • 34% use websites as their primary source of news
  • 28% author a blog and 44% read blogs
  • 49% download music using peer-to-peer file sharing
  • 75% of college students have a Facebook account [22]
  • 60% own some type of portable music and/or video device such as an iPod. (read more from Wikipedia/references).
