Eight golden rules of Negotiating Skills are


1. Analyze negotiation partner before negotiations begin
What is basic thinking on issue
- what claims does other side have?
- what are their objectives?
What motives does person have.
- personal motives towards negotiation?
- use of body language
- use ofverbal language

2. Negotiation strategy and tactics
What are Your negotiation Goals ?
What are Your negotiation Strategy to achieve those Goals?
What are Your negotiation Tactics to reaching those Goals?

3.What arguments do you have?
First argument shown in the table should be most important.
Show arguments in descending importance order.
Save second most important argument last one.

4. Act and take lead in negotiation table instead of reacting on problem.
- If negotiation partner becomes aggressive offer a deal.
- Make a no / go decision.
- Reach a deal with terms that are acceptable to You.

5. Show your negotiation power to reach a deal.

6. Break through resistance -> create a deal.

7. Make agreement to last at least a year instead of one-timer.

8. Create trusting relationship with the customer.
