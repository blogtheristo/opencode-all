Lifetime Happiness - Because Sustainability belongs to everyone

Published on December 07, 2025

Welcome dear Lifetime Friend to the New Age of Lifetime Happiness.

The Lifetime Group Environmental Sustainability Clause in full can be read from https://lifetime.fi/environment

The Environmental Sustainability Clause includes the following statements:

1 § Purpose and Scope: eco-friendly practices and reduce harmful impacts on the environment—and related activities.

2 § Regulatory Compliance statement includes legal policies and regulations followed in the Lifetime Group.

3 § Resource Management statement: Guidelines on how we manage resources efficiently, promoting recycling, and use of materials.

4 § Waste Management statement: Guidelines how minimizing waste, proper disposal methods, and encouraging recycling and upcycling is done in Lifetime Group companies.

5 § Carbon Emissions statement:

6 § Discharges Reporting: Guidelines how it is done.

7 § Sustainable Supply Chain Management statement: The suppliers and partners are to adhere to our environmental standards, ensuring that sustainability is maintained throughout the supply chain (Scope 3).

8 § Training & Education and building sustainability policies and regulations awareness with training and community sessions for our employees and partners.

9 § Monitoring & Reporting mandates regular monitoring of environmental performance.

10 § Continuous Improvement statement: Guidelines how we deliver continous improvements in our environmental sustainability act.
