Is Mobile TV good for me?

Published on March 31, 2008

"Mobile TV is television service delivered to subscribers via mobile telecommunications networks, such as the mobile phone carriers. South Korea is at the forefront of this developing sector and BT in the United Kingdom was the first company outside Korea to implement Mobile TV. Wikipedia.org

Have you already tried Mobile TV? According studies, watching movies from your phone or PDA is a personal experience. Unlike you watch TV with your family, mobile TV is usually watched alone. Secondly, the interactive services available to mobile TV makes it unique channel for productions houses. It is very likable, that success stories in the Cable TVs will have new followers better suited for the new platform. Friends will be friends also in mobile, but combining games and interactive TV is truly a new world.

This night I will be in Mobile Second Life and watch episode of Friends there

When SMS messaging were first time added to the phones, the idea was to give consumer an error reporting feature that the phone user would use in communicating to the Mobile Network Operator (MNO). The Consumer reinvented the SMS service and took the service for daily communications.

Similar success story were the music ring tones. When Nokia delivered Säkkijärven polkka to the phone back in 1994 no one expected to see a new industry of musical ring tones to build up. But it did.

The user experience with mobile TV will have it blue oceans for similar developments. New markets, new products.

Lifetime Studios is thinking Mobile TV seriously as a strategic goal for the coming years. Extending the business blog concept to mobile TV is already in the go with some serious blog houses (see f.e. Scobleizer TV).

Lifetime Studios intention is become European Channel Productions house in Mobile TV as well.
