Mobilize and Share: MOSH by Nokia Corporation adds Social Networking

Published on March 19, 2008

Dear Lifetime Scope Journal Friend,

Information Value chains.

Try out MOSH. The content you upload has the potential to reach millions of users in hundreds of countries around the globe.

Now there is a new way to make it even easier to find, share, and distribute your content: social bookmarking. This allows you to share your content on sites like del.icio.us, Facebook, Digg, Furl, and StumbleUpon. Just visit any content item and click on the bookmark logos in the upper right-hand corner.

Also try these tips for making sure people find and download your content:

Tagging: The more tags you add to your uploads the easier they are to find. The most popular tag searches are around device type or keywords such as "theme" or "game."

Adding a Preview Image: View and download rates are much higher for applications and games that have preview images and descriptions. Make sure to add these to make your content more attractive. In MOSH, go to "My Uploads" and select "Manage Previews" after choosing an item to edit.
