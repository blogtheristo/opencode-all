Partnership Business Model

Published on August 17, 2007

Your company finds a solution and you like the solution.
You like to run the solution and recommend it.
The solution vendor reaches his sales targets. They go work Partnerships.

The vendor offers you a incentive program.


You have reached a solution AND you are making money with the incentives.
Your sales, dear entrepreneur, peak as well. 

 

New partners sets in and it becomes a winning team community among the partners.

Black hats and black belts people are dedicating into. You hire black hats.

There is Disipline inside the community.

There is Respect to see the power of incentive Supply Chain.

VERSUS

You reach a solution

And they send you the bill.

They succeed first. Best people can be beaten when there is no rules.

The corporation has lost its window of opportunity.


What is your decision dear billionäire?
