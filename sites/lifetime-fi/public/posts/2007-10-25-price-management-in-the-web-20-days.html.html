Price Management in the web 2.0 days

Published on October 25, 2007

Remember at 1990-2000+ how we bought a dozens of software manuals with 1,000+ pages that we looking great in the shelf when girlfriend were visiting ;-) In two-three years those manuals began look wrong numbers in cover and were silently carried out from the office.

Today - blogging, chats, and communities reach millions of people that know exactly what they want to find, learn, and to know today. Any small information is really available, at least with a little fee.

This is an important input signal for creating electronic books as well. Today, I personally prefer guides that are targeting niche-market of my mindset needed a specific information. This means that those  more than 900 pages manual pages are over for me. I rather enjoy reading 10-100 pages deeply covering books that deliver exactly the information I was hoping  - and more !

I want to find easy information that is deeply covering the need of the business issue at hand.

I'm willing to pay for that information but I would like to see some Return of Investment as well from it.
