Digital Marketing is more than thinking of choice of digital channels

Published on January 19, 2008

I had a chance to have a quick read of Finnish Economica series book about Digital marketing written by (Merisavo, M, Vesanen, J. Raulas, M. Virtanen, V., digitaalinen markkinointi, 2006 Talentum Oyj).

Writers handle topic professionally and thoroughly , though I bet they would write much better version of the book if released 2007. Things are moving so fast. The book covers a short chapter about web 2.0. The main focus is in characteristics and usage of different digital channels.

The idea from the book that was presented that digital marketing is like pushing messages though diffrerently positioned and performing digital channels, like email, newsletters, blogs, etc. This type of Marketing Management, according my read and experiences,  etc. is highly undervalued in Europe compared to US.

The book presents solid and real customer and business cases worth digging into.

The idea I was getting from the book is that digital marketing management is about effectively using these push channels. Equally important for Customer Relations Management is the that loyal customers living their digital lives have communities that reflect their lifestyles and consumer preferences.

The push of a marketing message is needed. After that, once that precious customer is found, you dear entrepreneur want to keep her for life.
