(FP) Flow is the Pay as Lifetime Studios Production Strategy

Published on July 31, 2008

So what this one-liner Flow is the Pay really mean here at the Lifetime Studios?



<p> Let me shed some light for the catchy rhyme. The Pay part came when my friend Eric  called me and said “Risto, you have to find your pay”. One started to wonder the phrase, “so how does Eric make his pay?”. The answer is easy, as he is full-time professional in the music industry, his pay comes in the purest form from the flow if the music, i.e. when he plays guitar to his audiences. The flow of music is then distributed Online in live performances, or through digital productions end products, like Blue-ray discs, or iTunes shopping experiences to delight us music lovers.</p>

So Flow is the Pay, catchy phrase for Lifetime Studios, and decent name for this Business Blog about Digital Productions people, landscape, technologies, and fun!
