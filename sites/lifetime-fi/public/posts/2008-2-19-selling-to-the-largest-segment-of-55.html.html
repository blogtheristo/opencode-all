Selling to the largest segment of 55

Published on February 19, 2008

After being among those who did not support President Martti Ahtisaari for the second term and personally using the slot and quickly peeking in to the President Candidacy of Social Democratic Party back 1999, the Finnish President Tarja Halonen now says in the Apu Magazine Cover that she is considering NATO membership. WoW.

After 9 years ofsaying no to NATO this is news for those who supported the Lipponen Ahtisaari lead all this time. President have always said no to this issue in Finland.

Is it that now that Russians are giving their feedback from their own business advantage perspective with high Customs of wood products your policy is getting you nowhere?

Your Moscow card is not working. Your EU policy is not working. Your Finnish policy is not working. I guess it is time to travel somewhere far enough.

Many in Finland have told you over the years that you are creating havoc in the international position of taking Finland out of the Core of EU and not participating in NATO like Mr. Ahtisaari suggested already in 1997. 

So Thank You Tarja for your leadership years. And have a nice holiday somewhere you are travelling. We can chat on about your considerations until 2013.

Your well prepared digital pocket is however now closed. The emperor's new clothes are sure  (not) that pretty.

Russia is among the five most influential nations in near future. Finland is not.

What strategy it has been to starve ourselves 10 years outside the core of the EU? Really Tarja?

Get yourself Internet (finally), and yes, apply NATO membership.
