Consentrating to products 1: Real Estate companies, Great Portland Estates

Published on June 29, 2007

Great Portland Estates is a central London property investment and development company owning over £1.5 billion of real estate. "We aim is to deliver superior returns to shareholders through active asset management, the application of our development skills to create value and  maximising of equity returns through efficient structuring and flexible financing" (Annual Report 2007). Great Portland Estates business mix include Office - £1,195.6m (22%) and Retail - £340.0m (78 %). The company operates in three main locations West End - £1,259.0m (82%), City - £169.1m (11 %), and Southwark - £107.5m (7 %).

The Company has a clear and straightforward strategy." We have made excellent progress against our specific objectives".

1) Develop,

Strategic priorities include: deliver near-term programme on time and budget. Bring forward existing medium-term projects. Add to medium and long-term pipeline.

2) Recycle Capital,

Strategic priorities include: buy properties with low relative rents, with angles to exploit, and to grow medium-term development programme. Sell properties with historically high capital values. With limited further angles. Where capturing rental growth will be difficult.

3) Asset Manage

Strategic priorities include: execute individual property strategies. Create value through asset repositioning.

Great Portland Estates performance was excellent. The success of the group in meeting its strategic objectives is judged by its performance against independent benchmarks using four key performance indicators; Total Shareholder Return (TSR); NAV per share growth; Portfolio Total Property Return (TPR) and Return on Capital Employed (ROCE).

Chairman Mr. Richard Peskin statement:" It has been an active year in every aspect of our business. With the investment market remaining strong, sensible rationalisation has continued at excellent prices, and at the same time we found some attractive purchases."
