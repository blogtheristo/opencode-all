Does Advertisements add anything to your budget?

Published on August 22, 2007

After two months of testing Google Adsense advertising (the two boxes with textual ads) below.first conclusions can be made about the results.

First impression about Adsense was that it brings added value to the Lifetime Scope Journal (LSJ). However, when carefully reading advertisements over time it seems, that not very many of you dear readers care about the adds. Google Analytics show, that average reader spends almost 20 minutes with Lifetime Scope Journal. He or she reads five or more entries, I get an impression that you dear reader must like our work since half of the you come back every now and then.

 
However it seems, that Lifetime Scope Journal (LSJ) does not mix very well with the Adsense links. Fairly I can say, that Google has setup the payment schemas so that every Adsense Exit means fair pay. I think that Google do great with semantically finding those 'grey'  associations for our production. The question is that the content seems to be more interesting to readers that getting to next best consultancy company around here. I do thank you for this, my lifetime friend!

Second ads we deliver comes from a ads network that pay only if something is shopped from the landing pages. These ads have resulted some cleared deals. I think great link is Cyberphoto.fi - one of the Sweden number one camera shops oneline - voted by Swedish consumers. I can honestly say it is close ( or steps ahead) what I had in mind with pieniomena(tm).com shopping centre..
