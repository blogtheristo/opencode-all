Easy driving from Death Valley to Cypress Point Club  Pebble Beach near Monterey, California to play Golf, or to meet friends in Silicon Valley (refers to Santa Clara Valley).


 

Now that the spring is here, my first Spring Poem goes like this: 

In the Flow is the Pay 

I'm a BornGlobal Driving Context Aware,

From Death Valley to the Santa Clara Valley. 

There is no Semantic Web Clouds in the sky,

Only Static Storyboards  in my GPS.

Near Cypress Point,

I'm calling in for the Night,

Stopped here my mobile wagon,

Cos' every wagon has to stop someday, 

In the Flow is the Pay, 

In the Flow I find my Pay. 

(Copyright Risto Päärni, 2008)

The missing Criss-Cross words for Finnish BornGlobal Growth-Oriented Start-Up

Entering global markets has three major challenges:

1. International Marketing skills and Networking

2. Pre-seed funding to learn and to walk.

3. Business Plan covering Growth Strategy.   

Finnish BornGlobal initiative from Tekes and Techno polis Oyj has approved Start-Ups to Equity Pre-Seed or seed phase funding to take next step and challenge learning curve of international markets.

According Kauppalehti (11th of March), among BornGlobal funded companies are Bio Technology Company Plexpress Oy (CEO Juhani Lahdenperä), Fogscreens for Hollywood productions creating Fogscreen Oy (CEO Mika Koivula), and Voyantic (CEO Juho Partanen).
