Question 1: What does Google mean?

The name "Google" is a play on the word "googol," which was invented by Milton Sirotta, nephew of American mathematician Edward Kasner. A googol refers to the number represented by a 1 followed by 100 zeros (A googol is a very large number).

There isn't a googol amount of anything in the universe -- not stars, not dust particles, not atoms. Google's use of the term reflects company's mission to organize the world's immense (and seemingly infinite) amount of information and make it universally accessible and useful.
