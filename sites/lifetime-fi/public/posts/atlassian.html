Product Data Sheet

Atlassian ecosystem. The highly successful Tempo products family have the following products: 

  <img src="https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/54c48881e4b0d7a7fa7eddf4/1567664002224-09UX45W003N92ZZNKKPF/Budgets.png?format=original" alt=""/>

Tempo Budgets - Control project costs, revenues, and resources- Budget, track, and forecast finances and capacity.

Complete, real-time visualization of project and portfolio health with project cost and revenue overviews.

Tempo Budgets Highlights:

  • Complete, real-time visualization of project and portfolio health with project cost and revenue overviews.

  • Earned Value Management (EVM) or traditional reporting.

  • Budget baseline labelling and versioning.

  • Planned versus actual financials and forecasts.

  • Manage staff allocation, capacity and workload.

  • Manage wages, cost rates and overtime pay.

  • Enterprise features (data visibility control, Jira Data Center compatible, API).

  • Integration with Atlassian JIRA, Confluence and Tempo Timesheets and Tempo Planner.

  • Available in English, French and German.

  • Needs Tempo Server.

  <img src="https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/54c48881e4b0d7a7fa7eddf4/1567664078929-QEJ6X4Z5PCHP20XNBE1I/Planner.png?format=original" alt=""/>

Tempo Planner - Resource Planning made easy. With Tempo planner it is possible to find available resources for your project and maximize capacity. Manage your team and plan work in one place at Lifetime Digital Workspace.

Tempo Planner Highlights:

  • Resource Management made easy.

  • Access an overview of all team members, based on users and roles, to find available resources.

  • Report on detailed plans for you and your teams.

  • Save or share your reports.

  • Plan and approve work or request time from other teams.

  • Drag and drop issues or backlog items for easy team planning.

  • Visualize team capacity in real-time with capacity reports.

  • Estimate future resource needs in an instant.

  • Account for vacations, holidays, flexible employee work hours, and distributed teams with easy calendar planning and plan approvals.

    We here at Lifetime Group can help on how to Integrate Tempo Planner with Tempo Timesheets or other business applications like billing.

Tempo Timesheets - Time Tracking made easy. Get better insight into your business with Tempo time tracking. With reliable reports on productivity, billed hours & investments.




Make time tracking a seamless part of your workday. Customize your Tempo home, My Work, for easy time tracking and connect your Google Calendar to automatically fill in your timesheet.

Tempo Timesheets Highlights:


Log work in multiple ways

  • Download the excellent mobile app the Tempo Cloud mobile app for free, available for iOS and Android to log and track time.

  • Connect Google Calendar for your full schedule.

  • Get powerful reporting and see where time is spent, and prepare timesheets for billing

  • Quickly review, approve, or reject timesheets.

  • Plan your time in My Work or you can use Tempo Planner

  • Custom Jira dashboards with Tempo gadgets in real-time

  • Manage time spent across your business, CAPEX, OPEX, billable and internal time

  • Manage teams by role, workload and more

  • Integrate with Tempo Planner and plan all your resources in one place

  • Connect with QuickBooks & start automatically syncing data.

  • Use Automation Scripting (Advanced).

Tempo Timesheets integrates with your Atlassian product. This remote service can:

  • View email addresses of users.

  • Act on a user's behalf, even when the user is offline.

  • Administer the host application.

  • Administer Confluence spaces.

  • Administer Jira projects.

  • Delete data from the host application.

  • Write data to the host application.

  • Read data from the host application.

Automation for Jira - the simple way to automate & extend Jira. All power, no scripts





Automation for JIRA Highlights:

  • Customize and automate Jira your way - no need for custom scripts.

  • Automates manual, repetitive tasks so you can focus on what's important.

  • 3rd Party Integrations including SMS, Slack, Email, Webhooks, Microsoft Teams and Billing, PayRoll.

  • the product has customer support from a team with over 40 years of Jira experience.

Popular use cases

  • Sync parent and sub-tasks

  • Auto-assign issues

  • Create recurring tasks

  • Track SLAs

  • Add sub-tasks on create

  • Auto-close old support tickets

GitHub

Connect your code with your work in Jira Software. A separate Jira subscription is required. With two of your most important tools connected, you'll spend less time managing projects and more time working on them.

GitHub to Jira Highlights

Easily connect one or more GitHub Organizations to your Jira site and select specific repositories to bring your work together. Use the Smart Commits syntax to sync related commits, branches, and pull requests to a project in Jira.

Automatic context in your Jira issues

Get updates and links about what’s happening with your repositories in Jira issues for:

  • Pull Requests

  • Commits

  • Branches

Take actions on Jira issues without leaving your development workflow

Use specific commands in your commit message to perform these actions:

  • Close an issue

  • Add a comment

  • Update time tracking information

  • Transition workflow state

Show Jira information in GitHub

  • Get references to Jira in GitHub issues and pull requests.
