Lifetime Oy Ltd's Innovation Management Process use portfolios

Published on January 19, 2008

First Edition: Why a good business idea is not worth anything, nor it is an innovation?

 

When I was sitting in lectures of Innovation Management in Lappeenranta Univ. of Technology one sentence catches me " a good idea is not worth anything. Innovation Management means that you have to commercialize that idea before you can call it innovation".

Indeed, reading stories from Business Angels decision making of which ideas are funded - which not, gives similar results.

According business angels, a good idea is often invented many times, sometimes simultaneously,  in all over the global innovation playgrounds.

Example situation: Innovation in The Game Industry. The winner has to have

1. Suitable infrastructure for innovation management

2. Right contacts with 0-latency for setup phase. Own and hire Super-connected People with ability code.

3. The credibility to launch the innovation project in the industry.

4. Minimum of 50k Euros for run two people for one year. Govermental Verso Program from TEKES can help another 50k Euros. But don't wait them to do so. Finding next 5m€ in six months to setup from the demo to planning phase.

5. Production phase funding

6. Market Entry funding

-free to use, pay to customize.

The example of casual games have many innovations that has followed similar path.

The difference of successful innovation and a good idea is the successful planning layering,Actual Project Management, especially resourcing:  the people, process and its owner. To play big from the scratch. And doing it right. Even more important is to secure your innovation with Immaterial rights policy and solutions like patents and trademarks. 

Jussi Nurmio, CEO of taivas Corporation and real estate service Igglo, discusses about the theme in  digital Taloussanomat.  He concludes " Every innovation has to have one owner who believes about the innovation . Secondly there has to be a partners that believe to the innovator and is capability to execute the business plan of the innovation."

Being a business angel has to start from creating an innovation portfolio where most of the innovations may turn out to be failures, but the successful ones will bring enough profits to continue in business with a good profit.

Like Drucker said "There is only two activities in a company: Marketing and innovation".

What is wrong in Finnish innovation world is that the business angels are rather investors with a tight bottom line control instead of  a innovation portfolio owners. The governmental money seems to be more interested to keeping seminars for each others and creating small town project managers with political links than global success stories. According WWW.TEKES.FI  the whole innovation funding scheme will be changed 2008. I hope they read this story too.

The innovation build-up process would need the professional entering the SME in the idea phase, not after 'project ready to launch' phase.  Running down for layers of filtering is costly and requires real professionalism.

That is because also you, dear entrepreneur, need to have  4 layers of Innovation Management. 

The schema in Lifetime Oy Ltd. include these layers.

1. Ideas portfolio

  • The fuzzy Front-End of Innovations
  • Time-window
  • IPR

2. Innovation Portfolio

  • Business Angel and investor perspective
  • Techical can-do
  • Preventing errors

3. Business plan Portfolio

  • 4P Marketing Mix
  • Marketing Management
  • Sales Management
  • Promotion

4. Operational Project Management Portfolio

  • Critical project path
  • Ensuring business success
  • Running the last mile to customer loyalty

With these four portfolios and with help of Consulting Advisor service from Lifetime Oy Ltd. you dear Entrepreneur, SME business owner, will become more successful and the business more profitable.

No man can be put to 100% responsible for one idea execution, is the question. I will failure 95%, like the business angel do as well!  But using four stages portfolio model I at least recognize that one idea will filtered at all of these portfolios before conversion to profits is here.
