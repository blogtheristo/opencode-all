Executive Summary

This plan outlines the listing of Lifetime World Token (LWT) on Crypto exchange to expand the token's liquidity and visibility. LWT is a new cryptocurrency launched by a sustainable energy startup that provides hydrogen-based home systems. The token is issued to customers based on their expected hydrogen fuel production/usage and can be used to purchase fuel at discounted rates. Listing on a major exchange will enable token trading, price discovery, and potentially generating additional revenue for the company.

Token Overview

- Token Name: Lifetime World Token (LWT)

- Token Type: ERC-20 (Ethereum blockchain)

- Total Token Supply: 100 million LWT

- Token Distribution:

- 50% allocated to customers based on system purchases

- 30% reserved for future employees/partners

- 20% retained by company

- Use Cases: Purchasing hydrogen fuel, holding as investment asset

Listing Objectives

- Provide liquidity and price discovery for LWT token

- Expand LWT's visibility and accessibility to crypto investors

- Enable customers to easily trade LWT on a trusted platform

- Potentially generate revenue from Binance listing fees/token sales

Requirements for the LWT Listing

- Complete legal/compliance review of LWT's status

- Ensure sufficient token distribution/liquidity

- Provide detailed token documentation and disclosures

- Pay listing fees (approx. $1 million for premium listing)

Marketing Strategy

- Announce listing via company channels & media outreach

- Targeted digital marketing campaigns on crypto/blockchain channels

- Influencer marketing on popular crypto YouTube/Twitter accounts

- Crypto community outreach (Reddit, Telegram, etc.)

Financial Projections

- Marketing Budget: $500,000

- Potential Revenue from Token Sale: $10-20 million (10-20% of retained tokens sold at $1 per token after liquidity buildup)
