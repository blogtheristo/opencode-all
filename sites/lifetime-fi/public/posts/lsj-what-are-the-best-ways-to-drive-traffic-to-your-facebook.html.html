(LSJ) What are the best ways to drive traffic to your Facebook Business Page and Website? (Mahalo Answers)

Published on October 09, 2010

I joined Mahalo Answers the other day. Thoughts?
For Facebook Business Page the best tools to drive traffic to page are

1. Launch FacebookAds campaign combined with Google Adwords campaign (Use Search Engine Optimization to find keywords that rock).

2. Launch email campaign to your current customers and target segment addresses. Repeat the email biweekly with fresh content.

3. Use Social Media f.e Mahalo, XING, LinkedIn Company Profile, Twitter, to promote the pages.

4. Don’t forget those locally important social media sites that are important to your target audience.

5. Use blogging to promote your ideas. Present your ideas. Comment other blogs.

6. Use Personal Branding to integrate

7. Create multiple Facebook Pages to drive traffic inside FB.

8. Deliver ‘Good Name’

9. Be patient.

10. For any further questions you can contact me. Thank you for reading!
