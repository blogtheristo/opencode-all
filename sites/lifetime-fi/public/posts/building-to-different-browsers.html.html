Building to different browsers

Published on December 20, 2007

Building digital assets has to meet the digital 4P criteria.

1. Product building reliable digital brand will take years. Working years without income can become frustrating. The level of knowledge needed to become the next TechCrunch raises all the time. So write to learn, and learn to write. Don't sit and wait next wave of internet boom. Learning is much more fun when you make it your daily routine.

2. Promotion needs careful planning. Stepping right up to the Adwords may turn out wasting money. I found this myself in April 2007. If the product is not yet ready, why spend large sums of money advertising it.  However, I have found Adwords account really helpful in process of learning Business blog promoting. Other advertisement channels should be put in use as well most important search engines and their ad services.

3. Place in digital world is a combination of channel, service build of the blog,  and the user interface experience. If thinking direct internet experience, many users are fond of Internet Explorer 8, Mozilla Firefox 3, Opera 10.

4. Price is not set for this business blog for years to come. However, if you write business blog that is successful you may monetize it by writing book about the subject. If you write about technology, companies want beta testers and trusted testing companies. This will give possibilities for income.
