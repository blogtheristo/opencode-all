(LSJ) Lifetime Oy Ltd. 's Vision for 2011

Published on March 19, 2008

The Mission of Lifetime Oy Ltd. is clear. We want to be Preferred Partner in Digital Productions Eco System. 

Our Customer promise is Professional Project Management  Partner for the Digital Productions.

Born global Only with Attitude.

Lifetime Oy Ltd.'s strategy process and business plan are tied from the very beginning to Organic Growth.

Our business Offering is B-to-B Productions based.

The scope of the offering is set to international productions.

 

Growth is at any stage of a company's lifecycle is a challenge and requires

1. creativity finding the right networks and people as partners, business angels, lifetime friends.

2. and tenacity of carrying out the tasks validated through business strategy.

Lifetime Oy Ltd. aim daily operations to win these hurdles.
