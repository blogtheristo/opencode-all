Car Industry is the leading industry in AIoT solutions. Cars has become distributed computing / edge computing power platforms where the amount of data produced per vehicle is growing rapidly while the need of managing ever more complex car ‘data warehouse’ with raising car owner demand for their vehicle.

The data collected by single car needs to be managed with Intelligent Automation (IA input) while the management platform of the car has to be in control monitoring incoming data and changes in the platform CI/CD.

  • The business development needs to predict trends, learn insight from the data collected is increasing.

  • The update intervals and the car management features toward driverless car are increasing.

  • Building monolith solutions even on cloud computing does not ensure that every car platform operates as promised (network errors, climate condition, etc.) when circumtances change.

  • Car platform needs local support teams that can be scaling up when new issue emerges.

  • Lifetime Digital workspace 2.0 Platform offers local Cloud Ready Teams Go build support, Intelligent Automation, RPA for specific car model and its platform processes that will VW want to buy from us.
