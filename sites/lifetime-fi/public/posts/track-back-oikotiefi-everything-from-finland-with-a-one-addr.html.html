Published on December 19, 2007

Oikotie.fi service deliver "everything with a one address" in vacancies in Finland

 

Oikotie.fi assembles all classified advertisements under one web address. The service includes new and used cars, homes to buy and rent, business premises, job vacancies, courses and exams, as well as travel destinations and travel agencies. The Oikotie.fi web service accepts advertisements from both private people and business companies. And it sells!

For those who seek employees or new job oikotie.fi offers effective job hunting in Finnish markets

 

Oikotie.fi offers a job seeker several different alternatives for searching for and following job vacancies. Using different search criteria, job seekers can screen the jobs best suited to them. Service's free advertising guard keeps an eye on possible vacancies for the job seeker. As soon as a suitable vacancy advertisement is in Oikotie.fi, a message goes to the job seeker by email or as a GSM message. Possible vacancies can also be surveyed by choosing a preferred employer and only retrieving their advertisements. Hints for career planning and job applications are found in the Oikotie.fi Urakeskus career centre.

For those who seek an employee oikotie.fi offers a job vacancy advertisement in Finland's leading recruitment service.

Contact email is oikotie.myynti@sanoma.fi .

The price for an advertisement is € 890 + VAT 22%.

When submitting a job vacancy advertisement for publication in the Helsingin Sanomat daily, you can at the same time announce that you also want the advertisement in the Oikotie.fi service. The newspaper advertisement material will then be converted into a web advertisement as well. The price for a newspaper advertisement is quoted by the newspaper; the share of the web advertisement is € 510+ VAT 22%.

The web advertisement can be seen in the Oikotie.fi service until the end of the application period (4 weeks max). The web advertisement is identical in content with the newspaper advertisement, but does not include logos, pictures or textual highlights.
