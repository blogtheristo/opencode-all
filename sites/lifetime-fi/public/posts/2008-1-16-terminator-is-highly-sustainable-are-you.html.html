The waste management in Espoo is a huge issue. We Finns are keen to create waste. In Central Europe they burn most of the waste, but here until late developments piling waste to enormous areas has been de facto. Sortering does not make things much better albeit the old grannies feel important with bio waste. Ofcourse there is obvious advantages as well(sorry).

But the main problem is how to make waste management a closed system so that
1. everything is burned in controlled way
2. With as little as possible leaks to the atmosphere or ground.

Actually I live in EU not in NA, but does it matter ?
