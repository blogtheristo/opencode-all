(FP) Royal Shakespeare Company Top 10

Published on June 03, 2025

Below is an 06/2025 updated list of the biggest TV stars associated with the Royal Shakespeare Company (RSC), including their notable TV roles, significant RSC stage roles, and now their major movie roles. The actors are selected based on their prominence in television and confirmed RSC performances, with major movie roles drawn from their most iconic or critically acclaimed films. The list maintains the original order and context, integrating movie roles to provide a comprehensive view of their careers across stage, TV, and film.

Notable TV Stars with RSC, TV Roles, RSC Roles, and Major Movie Roles

1. David Tennant

   - **Notable TV Roles**:

     - The Tenth Doctor in *Doctor Who* (2005–2010, 2013, 2023)

     - Alec Hardy in *Broadchurch* (2013–2017)

     - Crowley in *Good Omens* (2019–present)

   - **RSC Roles**:

     - Hamlet in *Hamlet* (2008)

     - Richard II in *Richard II* (2013)

     - Touchstone in *As You Like It* (1996)

     - Romeo in *Romeo and Juliet* (2000)

   - **Major Movie Roles**:

     - Barty Crouch Jr. in *Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire* (2005)

     - John Knox in *Mary Queen of Scots* (2018)

     - Lord Asriel in *The Golden Compass* (2007)

   - **Details**: Tennant’s *Hamlet* was a critical triumph, adapted for BBC, while his *Harry Potter* role as a Death Eater showcased his versatility. His film roles, though fewer than TV, complement his RSC and TV prominence.

2. **Patrick Stewart**

   - **Notable TV Roles**:

     - Captain Jean-Luc Picard in *Star Trek: The Next Generation* (1987–1994) and *Star Trek: Picard* (2020–2023)

     - Charles Xavier in *X-Men* TV adaptations and related media

   - **RSC Roles**:

     - Mark Antony in *Antony and Cleopatra* (1978, Olivier Award)

     - Othello in *Othello* (1997)

     - Claudius in *Hamlet* (2008)

     - Prospero in *The Tempest* (1995, 2006)

   - **Major Movie Roles**:

     - Charles Xavier in *X-Men* series (2000–2017, including *X-Men*, *X2*, *Days of Future Past*)

     - Captain Jean-Luc Picard in *Star Trek: First Contact* (1996) and other *Star Trek* films

     - Gurney Halleck in *Dune* (1984)

   - **Details**: Stewart’s 27-year RSC tenure underpins his iconic *Star Trek* and *X-Men* film roles, blending Shakespearean gravitas with sci-fi stardom.

3. **Judi Dench**

   - **Notable TV Roles**:

     - Jean Pargetter in *As Time Goes By* (1992–2005)

     - Miss Matty Jenkyns in *Cranford* (2007–2009)

   - **RSC Roles**:

     - Lady Macbeth in *Macbeth* (1976)

     - Adriana in *The Comedy of Errors* (1962)

     - Titania in *A Midsummer Night’s Dream* (1962)

     - Roles in *All’s Well That Ends Well* (2003), *The Winter’s Tale* (2015)

   - **Major Movie Roles**:

     - M in *James Bond* series (1995–2015, including *GoldenEye*, *Skyfall*)

     - Queen Victoria in *Victoria & Abdul* (2017)

     - Queen Elizabeth I in *Shakespeare in Love* (1998, Oscar for Best Supporting Actress)

     - Philomena Lee in *Philomena* (2013)

   - **Details**: Dench’s RSC legacy pairs with her commanding film presence, particularly as M in eight *Bond* films and her Oscar-winning role in *Shakespeare in Love*, reflecting her Shakespearean roots.

4. **Ian McKellen**

   - **Notable TV Roles**:

     - Walter in *Vicious* (2013–2016)

     - Guest roles in *Coronation Street* (2005)

   - **RSC Roles**:

     - King Lear in *King Lear* (2007)

     - Macbeth in *Macbeth* (1976)

     - Romeo in *Romeo and Juliet* (1976)

     - Coriolanus in *Coriolanus* (1984)

   - **Major Movie Roles**:

     - Gandalf in *The Lord of the Rings* trilogy (2001–2003) and *The Hobbit* trilogy (2012–2014)

     - Magneto in *X-Men* series (2000–2014)

     - Richard III in *Richard III* (1995, also producer)

   - **Details**: McKellen’s *King Lear* was a landmark, while his *Gandalf* and *Magneto* roles made him a global film icon, with *Richard III* directly tying to his RSC Shakespeare expertise.

5. **Kenneth Branagh**

   - **Notable TV Roles**:

     - Kurt Wallander in *Wallander* (2008–2015)

     - Roles in *Fortunes of War* (1987)

   - **RSC Roles**:

     - Henry V in *Henry V* (1984)

     - Romeo in *Romeo and Juliet* (1983)

   - **Major Movie Roles**:

     - Henry V in *Henry V* (1989, also director)

     - Benedick in *Much Ado About Nothing* (1993, also director)

     - Hercule Poirot in *Murder on the Orient Express* (2017), *Death on the Nile* (2022), *A Haunting in Venice* (2023, also director)

   - **Details**: Branagh’s RSC roles launched his career, leading to his acclaimed Shakespeare film adaptations. His *Poirot* films and *Wallander* TV role highlight his versatility as actor and director.

6. **Helen Mirren**

   - **Notable TV Roles**:

     - Jane Tennison in *Prime Suspect* (1991–2006)

     - Elizabeth II in *Elizabeth I* (2005)

   - **RSC Roles**:

     - Rosalind in *As You Like It* (1968, adapted for BBC in 1978)

     - Cleopatra in *Antony and Cleopatra* (1982)

     - Cressida in *Troilus and Cressida* (1968)

   - **Major Movie Roles**:

     - Queen Elizabeth II in *The Queen* (2006, Oscar for Best Actress)

     - Cesárea in *The Mosquito Coast* (1986)

     - Alma Reville in *Hitchcock* (2012)

     - Maria Altmann in *Woman in Gold* (2015)

   - **Details**: Mirren’s RSC training informed her commanding *Prime Suspect* and *The Queen* performances, with her Shakespearean roles like Cleopatra showcasing her stage prowess.

7. **Sam Heughan**

   - **Notable TV Roles**:

     - Jamie Fraser in *Outlander* (2014–present)

   - **RSC Roles**:

     - Macbeth in *Macbeth* (announced for 2025)

   - **Major Movie Roles**:

     - Alexander in *The Spy Who Dumped Me* (2018)

     - Tom Buckingham in *SAS: Red Notice* (2021)

     - Jimmy Dalton in *Bloodshot* (2020)

   - **Details**: Heughan’s *Outlander* fame precedes his RSC debut, with his action-oriented film roles showing a different facet from his upcoming Shakespearean *Macbeth*.

8. **Ncuti Gatwa**

   - **Notable TV Roles**:

     - The Fifteenth Doctor in *Doctor Who* (2023–present)

     - Eric Effiong in *Sex Education* (2019–2023)

   - **RSC Roles**:

     - Valentine in *The Two Gentlemen of Verona* (2025)

   - **Major Movie Roles**:

     - Ken in *Barbie* (2023)

     - The Artist in *The Last Letter from Your Lover* (2021)

   - **Details**: Gatwa’s breakout TV roles and his vibrant *Barbie* performance align with his RSC debut, marking him as a rising star across media.

9. **Christopher Eccleston**

   - **Notable TV Roles**:

     - The Ninth Doctor in *Doctor Who* (2005)

     - Maurice Scott in *The Leftovers* (2014–2017)

   - **RSC Roles**:

     - Macbeth in *Macbeth* (2018)

   - **Major Movie Roles**:

     - Malekith in *Thor: The Dark World* (2013)

     - Raymond Calitri in *Gone in 60 Seconds* (2000)

     - Major Henry West in *28 Days Later* (2002)

   - **Details**: Eccleston’s intense *Doctor Who* and *Macbeth* roles pair with gritty film performances, showcasing his range from RSC stage to blockbuster films.

10. **Anthony Hopkins**

    - **Notable TV Roles**:

      - Dr. Robert Ford in *Westworld* (2016–2018)

      - Roles in TV movies like *The Bunker* (1981)

    - **RSC Roles**:

      - Coriolanus in *Coriolanus* (1977)

      - King Lear in *King Lear* (1986)

    - **Major Movie Roles**:

      - Dr. Hannibal Lecter in *The Silence of the Lambs* (1991, Oscar for Best Actor)

      - Odin in *Thor* series (2011–2017)

      - Richard Nixon in *Nixon* (1995)

      - Anthony in *The Father* (2020, Oscar for Best Actor)

    - **Details**: Hopkins’ RSC roles in the 1970s–1980s complement his legendary film career, with *Hannibal Lecter* and *The Father* defining his cinematic legacy.

### Notes

- **Selection Criteria**: The list retains the original actors, chosen for their TV prominence and RSC involvement, with movie roles selected for critical acclaim, commercial success, or cultural impact. Films like *Harry Potter*, *Lord of the Rings*, and *James Bond* reflect blockbuster appeal, while roles like Dench’s in *Shakespeare in Love* tie to their Shakespearean roots.

- **RSC Context**: The RSC’s training and performances often shape actors’ film and TV work, with many (e.g., Branagh, McKellen) adapting Shakespeare for the screen.

- **Limitations**: “Major” movie roles are subjective; this list prioritizes iconic or award-winning performances. Some actors (e.g., Heughan, Gatwa) have fewer film roles due to their TV focus but are included for their RSC and TV significance. Sources include IMDb, Backstage, London Theatre, and X posts for recent RSC announcements.

- Verification No discrepancies were found in cross-referencing RSC roles, TV, and film credits across sources like IMDb and theatre archives.
