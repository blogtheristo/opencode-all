(LSJ) Turning Customer Relations into Profitable Customer Accounts with CRM

Published on March 30, 2010

The Goal is to segment and maintain more Profitable Accounts

Developing and leading Customer Relations that turn into profitable Customer Accounts is the most important long-term goal for an Entrepreneur.

Managing in daily operations Customer Relations is done with various methods and tools. The most influencial are

  1. Phone Contacting
  2. Email Contacting
  3. Ads marketing
  4. Writing blog entries
  5. Commenting entries
  6. Tweeting
  7. Updating fan pages
  8. Updating Status Lines
  9. Web page Comments
  10. Adding and commenting Pictures
  11. Participating Discussions
  12. Chat Online
  13. Participating Seminars
  14. Pitch Contents
  15. Online Conferencing B-to-B
  16. Virtual Teams
  17. Writing fan page entries

 

The Role of Social Media in Customer Relations

Social Media in my opinion belongs to both methods and tools of my Customer Relations Management Arsenal. It is very useful of finding new prospects, partners, competitors, and success stories in any industry given.

Focus on Customer loyalty, and retention

The build of Customer Relations take years. The old saying, that keeping existing customers is always cheaper than finding new ones may be true. For finding new customers, web 2.0 based services and Social Media are tools that best serve your interests.

Segmenting the Customer Base

For a Entrepreneur, better profiling and targeting of current customers is vitally important to reduce expenses and raised overall profitability. Price Management, Increased market share are common objectives for Company Executives to address the need for a new Customer Relations Management Project Launch.

Do our customers have easy access to people that can answer their questions ?

Screening and the Evaluation of Project for CRM Automation

To successfully implement a CRM Automation / System in your company you can start by answering the following questions:


1. Is separate Customer Relations Management System (or SaaS) necessary for your business?

2. How beneficial is Customer Relations Management System for a company?  What are the main weaknesses we need to address?

3. What is the strategy and the best tools to combine CRM and Social Media?

4. How Company needs to develop its internal processes to meet the challenge of CRM system to fully benefit of the new way of operations management?

The Evaluation Project

The ignite of CRM project is best lead by experienced company Project Manager assisted with external consultant. The screening of different solutions is usually in the Gantt-Diagram / Task List of the Pre-Project Team.
