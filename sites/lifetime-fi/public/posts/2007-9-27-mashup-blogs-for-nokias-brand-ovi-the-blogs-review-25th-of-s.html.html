ABI Insight: Nokia  Ramps Up Presence in the Mobile Entertainment ...

Nokia recently held a “Go Play” event that ended with the announcement of its new mobile entertainment portal, called Ovi, which potentially diversifies the company from a mobile phone manufacturer into an internet service provider; in line with other larger players like eBay, iTunes, Yahoo!, and Amazon. The distinctive feature of Nokia’s internet service is bringing the content directly to handsets rather than downloading through a PC and “side-loading” to handsets. The latter is the model most online content stores, such as iTunes, SoundBuzz, and Amazon Unbox, rely on. Navigation applications (Nokia Maps), a music download service (Nokia Music Store), and a games download service (N-Gage games) would also be available.
