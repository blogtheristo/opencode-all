The ERP Approach

Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) is great thing for many business issues in Industrial Management perspective.

But where are your business processes? Does your IT manager wish, that they are running in the closed environment, in a cost centre, far away for the customers and partners?

Does results of the ERP project show a lot of costs without strategy to address digital native customer needs nor their wishes and market evolving microtrends?

What is good for from (B-to-B) perspective is not always what your customer needs.

The Customer Oriented Approach

For customers the only visible thing is your website and perhaps a product in shopping mall.
