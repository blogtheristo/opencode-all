Addictions, Net Addictions, drinking, eating, and more

Published on February 19, 2008

Today on 1000 words focus on addictions, net addictions, drinking, eating habits, taking care of your life. This entry also include my personal trainer advice for you who wish to change your ways 2008.
 
My IT Meter showed 2001 105,5 KG, one bottle of vodka per week, one box of beer per week, wine, pizza fridays, daily hamburger meals, no exercise, no family, a lot of stress, bad attitude, bad work results. I felt so bad that I was not able to run 3 kilometers. I was certain that I'm not going to run in my life. ever.

 

I was certain to live rest of my days alone (aged 35 those days).

Captain Closeau bring in the FACTS, please ; (The teaching mode)

Majority of us Finns enjoy drinking Alcohol. Total consumption in 2007 were around 10,5 litres Per Capita.

Finns drink daily beer, Koskenkorva Vodka, and wines.
We like to be among the Tech consumers.
We use daily MS Windows, Symbian, and the internet.

Actually, many of us are addicted to Internet. No harm, but test yourself for addiction levels from Päihdelinkki here
http://www.paihdelinkki.fi/cgi-bin/testaa/testi3go.pl#org (finnish)
Or maybe not.

1000 words continue: 100% of Finnish people under 40 years use internet daily. The digital natives generations that have grown with internet 80% use internet daily (teenagers that is).

The amount of violent crimes is growing.
The amount of virtual gaming is growing.

Finns get too little vitamin D because of the long period without the sunshine.
Majority of Finns are getting overweight and do not exercise daily.
Majority of Finns are going to retire in few years.

Finns consume and the world is warming up.
The Global warming is up 7.1 per cent this month.This year there have not been much of snow in the Southern Finland.

The Finnish inflation is up to 7.1 this month. Consumers demand more. Finnish milk dairy products are out of stock because finns are buying all the milk powder. The EU production level that adjusted to lower Finnish producer down 30 seem today a mistake. All Dairy products are sold in the global markets.

Finns have 7.1 per cent more money to spend. They have bought nearly 70 per cent of the east side of the Finnish country side for summer housing and EU rest area. At the same time, they are 7.1 politically more angry for Europe this year. The Army show their muscles in the border weekly, and every day we read about nationalism feelings hurt in the global media.

Power is addictive too.Being angry consumes energy.

Personally due my business situation I have redused my spending of money 5.2 per cent 2008. As a result, this has reduded my family carbon cloud for something like 6.6 per cent.

I feel depressed for being in poverty.

The good side is that I am really concetrating to healthy way of living.

1. Start daily Gym It becomes a daily habit. [2002,daily]
