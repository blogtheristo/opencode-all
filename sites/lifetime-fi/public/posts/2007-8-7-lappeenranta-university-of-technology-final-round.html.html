Lappeenranta University of Technology final round

Published on August 07, 2007

I heard today that all my courses are finally (hopefully they are all in) in and accepted.

I got an early promise to get papers out from school 30th of AUGUST, 2007.

;-)

It has been a pleasure cruise of 30 months studying, running newspapers at night, building Lifetime Oy Ltd. at days. I have had a 10 days of holiday last weeks over the period. Broke my Mercedes to a tree, and divorced to live entrepreneur to the fullest. Car will be fixed, new people will be found. New customers will appear.perkele.

Somehow I think I'm still in wrong country. There is so much bullshit of pre-seed money and help which seems to so far from my operations. Convincing someone to lend me money with interest rate of 70 percent PA. Get out of here! Why 15 percent is not good enough my dear angel?(yeah - the saviour).

Secondly I get a email weekly from EU convincing EU is helping SME's with everything.

I see great conferenses, great pdf shows. I don't see one page apply form to setup a innovation into project. If it is somewhere I don't find it. Where is it? 50%-100% project funding including hire of teams for hottest web 2.0 applications Europe and the world would love to have.' Your fired!

And then there finnish technology evangelists,like tekes, who hope that I (entrepreneurs) will invent next Nokia.  I need to write 100 pages of business plan to apply 40k €. Thanks for the snacks. What can one poor boy do with 40k? Not form a rock and roll band. Nothing. Getting ready for the Angel deal, which never should be accepted. This looks good in the goverment summary page.

In the end Nokia become software? 

Even when Nokia is exactly executing similar toward software oriented strategy that I am, what the heck I'm not doing right. Where is the money.
