Background 

 

Apple's iPhone has been the biggest mobile industry entry in 2007 so far.Nokia's new multimedia computers like N81 and Ovi are setting new standards for mobile industry. Third major paradigm shift will be set by search engine giant Google Corporation by its Gphone initiative. New mobile operators, f.e. Blyk, are bringing free SMS's and voice services to communities and carefully selected segments- like teenagers.
 
The proposed Deal for Google from Lifetime Scope Editor

1. Re-organize the Supply chain of digital lifestyle globally:(i.e. content provider, mobile operator, phone maker, network operator, advertiser, co-branding, community, customers,markets, etc.),
 
2. lower customers phone bills with google Adwords advertising, low-cost phones, and Google applications including Gpay.
 
3. Take charge to Mobile Network Services as well. Co-Branding and licensing.
 
4. Drive customers and internet traffic where you want to with the new platform and applications
 
5. Invest heavily to best Content Services, since they commit in the end company's long-term revenue.

Google Platform

 

According sources, Google is preparing a new mobile platform, a rival to the Nokia preferred Symbian OS, Microsoft's Windows Mobile, mobile Linux, Palm, and other mobile operating systems.

(Adapted from BusinessWeek.com 09/06/07):" "The new operating system, which may be named gPhone, was developed in part with know-how Google acquired with a startup named Android in 2005. The platform is designed to enable lower-priced "smartphones" featuring more robust Web browsing and multimedia applications. Most importantly for Google, it will work hand in glove with the company's mobile search engine and other Google applications that are already popular on personal computers. Gphone will allow Google to bring new applications to the wireless market faster. A number of handset makers are prototyping of lower-cost phones based on the Google platform. These handsets, expected to sell for about $100, are being shopped around to carriers worldwide, including those in the U.S. On top of the lower price tag.Google also hopes to attract customers with lower monthly cell bills"(End of quote).

(Adapted from 08/24/07 http://www.rediff.com)Talks are believed to be taking place with Bharti Airtel [Get Quote] and Vodafone Essar, respectively India's first and third largest mobile telephony operators, and state-owned Bharat Sanchar Nigam. Sources close to the development said a simultaneous launch across the US and Europe is expected, and announcements would be sent to media firms in India and other parts of the world. US regulatory approval, which is expected soon, is the only hurdle that Google is waiting to cross, they added". (End of Quote). Google plans to invest $8-10 billion for its Gphone initiative.

Customers will have to agree to receive ads on their mobile phones.In Blyk business model currently running Great Britain, 6 advertisements is sent daily to customer phones. To get Blyk, a friend has to recommend new member to the community.(Compare to BLYK.COM).(taloussanomat 25.9.07)
 

Patents 

Google has applied for a patent for a mobile payment system [5] to complement its 'plans to launch' [6] the Google phone, or GPhone. Known as 'GPay' [7], it covers a system that would let the user send a text message to Google giving the details of a payment to a specified recipient. GPay then debits the user's bank account, crediting the money to the payee(wikipedia.org).
 

My humble prediction is, that Google will attempt to introduce a new blue ocean business model, not only for the wireless-services industry, but to the digital lifestyle consumers end-to-end. You walk the digital walk, Google will send you the goodies along the way.

In 2006, Google Corp. generated $10.6 billion in revenue from online search advertising with a 62.7% market share. In mobile market today, Microsoft and Yahoo are currently the market share leaders.

It is expected that Mobile Windows platform will ship more than 20 million devices in 2007.

Yahoo's new Mobile search engine, oneSearch, has been a success story. Opera browser is using OneSearch.

While 15% of U.S. mobile users have browsed Web pages on their mobile phones, only 3% have used a mobile search engine.

Google's new blue ocean business model will appeal to the carriers as they struggle to boost revenues in 'bit-pipe-markets' where the price competition is fierce and predicted revenues will start to fall 2010.(Re-organize)

Blyk's Business Model:give subscribers discounts for agreeing to receive and follow ads on their phones. Google will succeed in bringing a lower-priced phone to the market, the gPhone could have some broader impact than the iPhone, which still costs $400.

With free Web-surfing and search capabilities, business model fill a void in developing markets where many people can't afford, or the to Enter market Niches and communities - like teenagers.

The main goal for Google and its competitors is to provide mobile platform that smoothly integrate all their applications and digital storages to have full control over customer digital lifestyle.


Launching a new operating system Google's advantage is the limited number of applications that will be available for that platform.Google will have full control over the user experience.Google will ensure the success with lower phones bills.

Symbian and Windows Mobile's advantage is rich application ecosystem.There are thousands of third-party  applications to choose from and more being written by software developers every day. This has meant expensive devices and applications for the end users. It is interesting to see wether Gphone really changes the Mobile SCM for the benefit of our digital lifestyles.
