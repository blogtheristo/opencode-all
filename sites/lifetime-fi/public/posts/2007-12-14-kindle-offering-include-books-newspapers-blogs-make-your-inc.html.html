Kindle offering include books, newspapers, blogs. Make your income from business blogging

Published on December 14, 2007

The Amazon Kindle is unique device. With size of smaller than laptop but larger than most mobile devices it offers good reading platform.

With wireless communication books are easy to download to the device. It also delivers hundreds of newspapers daily. This is important for anyone who tries to stay in touch local news.

For me the best thing about Amazon.com Kindle is that offers a channel for serious bloggers to make some income. Presenting a calculus of business blogs based on to subscription model feels much better reasoning in the business plan than just announcing income is found with affiliate marketing.

Really well done, Amazon !

I hope that someone in the Nokia / Symbian platform could do the same. Thank You Nokia!
