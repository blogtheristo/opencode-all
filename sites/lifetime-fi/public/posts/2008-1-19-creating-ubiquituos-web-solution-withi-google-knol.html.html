Creating Ubiquituos Web solution withI Google Knol

Published on January 19, 2008

Google Knol is invitation based site. Its information is based on specialist or gu ru entries. Knol prefer in-depth material to make Automated ads more valuable. It will add intelligence to the internet with concept called ubiquitous Web.

it is there to see wether it's importance will overall make Knol a competitor to Wikipedia.org.

Google Corporation's new Beta Program Knol offers by invitation fresh start-up new Wikipedia.org type semantic web information tool.

"Earlier this week, we started inviting a selected group of people to try a new, free tool that we are calling "knol", which stands for a unit of knowledge. Our goal is to encourage people who know a particular subject to write an authoritative article about it. The tool is still in development and this is just the first phase of testing" announces the Official Google Blog.

This process will close to my opinion one chapter of blogging. If a blog only references other information sources it disadvantage of such blog is that information is without doubt subjective. On the other hand, if your authority is high enough this subjective approach is not a bad thing. But for some bloggers that write only to do Adsense marketing this will be end of chapter?

The Business Blogging Momentum is 2008


The Business Blogging project includes company strategy tied information and products and services tied information. This kind of 'informative' information is always taking sides. With tools like Knol it may become possible to add the big picture more easily to the company business blog. It creates trust and helps reader to place available information in to the right context.

Objective semantic web is a dream that open source gurus will dream on. I fact, how objective is the information found in the current wikipedia.org entries? Who is authoring the objective writings panel?

My estimation is that also other major players in the industry will create their own objective semantic web as well. Keeping people at their reach as much as possible gives best performance to advertising and sales efforts.

With business blogging initiative any company can create global voice that can influence their own industry and local many-to-many business network as well. To support this and make the spear heads of information linking company blog to Knol and Wikipedia.org will enlarge the reach of the business blog, but also gain more authority to the business blog.

Goodbye email and spam, welcome Knol and it's fresh ideas. New worlds are welcome and needed to increase credibility of business blogging as well.
