Grand Opening: Flagship Stadium 15th of October in Helsinki

Published on November 09, 2007

My first visit to Stadium shops happened in 1989 in http://www.Stockholm.se/. My friend and I had normal weekend off boat travel with http://www.vikingline.fi/ to Swedish capital. The first Stadium Store Stockholm were opened in 1987.

A brief Stadium Retail Chain History

The foundation on which Stadium is built was laid back in 1974, when Ulf Eklöf took over the Spiralen Sport store in Norrköping. At the time it was an ordinary sports store, covering an area of 180 m2. Spiralen Sport quickly developed into a sports department store, focusing on low prices and large volumes. Norrköping is also home to the parent company Stadium AB and the Service Office.

The first Stadium store opened in 1987 on Sergelgatan in Stockholm. Stadium was created as an arena of experiences. The characteristic running tracks, the product display solution and the store entrance formed the basis of a new concept that revolutionised the entire sports industry.

The retail store chain opened it's new shop in stadium.Stockholm.se.

Other business activities
In addition to the Stadium stores, the Group also comprises Red Devil, Edins, Sports Outlet, Sportlagret and Stadium Promotion AB, a company making profile and campaign products for companies and organisations.

It was Stadium Promotion that designed the clothes for the Swedish Olympic Team, both in Nagano, Sydney, Salt Lake City, Athens and Torino.

Stadium supports an active life for young people.
