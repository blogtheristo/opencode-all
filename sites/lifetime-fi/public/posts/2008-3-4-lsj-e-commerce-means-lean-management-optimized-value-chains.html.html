(LSJ) E-Commerce means Lean Management optimized value chains

Published on March 04, 2008

The European, US, even Global level E-Commerce Business Networks deliver Lean Management's Best Practices Optimized in Action. The focus is in Customer experience.

The costs per Unit (CPU) is optimized with large Buy-Ins and effectively organized Stock and Logistics. The Product Catalog of The Winning Offering is supported by fat-free and virtually created organization structure. The success story is decorated with contracting partners of  International Logistics Supply Chains and On-line Logistical Services.

The Customer Oriented Approach is dominant in the offering with community services including  strong branding with member discounts and Principles of pricing where the daily winning prices are adjusted to the best pitched Google's level.

The Value Chains are optimized with Industrial Engineering Solutions and Supply Chain Management.

Today the Status Quo  of Retail Industry is changing. The commercial power players of The Digital Pocket Corners and Siberian Outposts of Europe (including Finland) find out statistically that European Consumers are rushing to new shopping experiences outside the Locally managed e-Commerce De facto Catering. 

The main reason, other than Entrepreneur's Risk Avoidance Rule of Thumb, why I postponed the Grand Opening of my Virtual Shopping Centre pieniomena(tm) is the fact that Finnish Retail sector has traditionally been rather heavily concentrated. In 2008, The Finnish Retail Industry is divided into two major Camps (Kesko, S-Ryhmä). The two camps have not been very E-Commerce friendly. Selling and buying daily grocery has been a shopping Centre favoured both the Finnish Consumers and the players.

However the experience shops from 2006 have given me the feel that in the consulting I can give advice or two for those that consider opening a electronic shopping centre.

Opening e-Commerce with small Equity

Nearly all Finnish led E-Shops have suffered the higher buy in price which now is rightleously under the gun by the European Consumers. Why pay for the E-middleman if the same Logistics Company brings in the same Branded Goodies wether products are ordered from Helsinki, Münich or Tallinn?

How to Defend e-Commerce Status Quo in mature Markets?

How to defend current e-Commerce Status Quo? The simple answer is

  1. Empower local friendly service.
  2. Create availability of the entertainment value chain in to lifestyle shopping experience. (Ask Lifetime Consulting to help).
  3. Touch of products daily practices (availability).
  4. Daily Revenue Management with prices variying daily
  5. Fair bargain price segments.
  6. OffShore Shops

The Challengers of European Shopping Experience: Hot eshops for Europeans 2008
