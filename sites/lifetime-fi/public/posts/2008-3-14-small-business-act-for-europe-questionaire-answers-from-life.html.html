Small Business Act for Europe Questionaire answers from Lifetime Oy Ltd.

Published on March 14, 2008

SMEs three business processes needs to tackle but it not easy to find support from government or EU level. SME preseed Business Processes are


1. Survival of daily living in global competition environment. Liquidity. Costs are in Euros, competition makes sales income+profits  in emerging markets currency (Chindia).


2. Organic Growth profitable operations needs substantial learning and networking  and success in sales to local pre-seed phase fund organisations both local and EU level.  For Entrepreneur this is almost too complex to achieve.


3.Growth Entrepreneuship would mean hiring 100 to 200 people in a year to create decent software product to meet time-to-market window. Seed phase funding is not easy if not impossible before organic growth phase is met.
