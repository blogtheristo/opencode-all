Blogging will replace static web pages

Published on May 29, 2007

As I have been writing blogs for two years now, I stopped for a minute to think what the internet was before blogs came along. It is obvious, that for many of us blogging is a way expressing digital native basic needs and deliver the daily bread.

Still, when lifetime.fi home pages where lifted into a blog format I had some doubts of how people will find the stuff. Still, when I'm making new entries into Lifetime Journal I have a feeling that I should keep the static frontpage still alive and delivering the basic company information.

However, if You look Google trends for word blog You notice that blogging is still growing trend. This the opposite of many other technology trends and sites, that seem to have slowly  steady growth , or no growth at all.

So what are people writing in those millions of blogs. One answer can be seen when looking about the Tags.
