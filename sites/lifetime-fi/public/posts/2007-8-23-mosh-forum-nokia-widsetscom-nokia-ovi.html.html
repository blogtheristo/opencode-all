WidSets brings you information normally accessed via the Internet, directly into your mobile phone. Using mini-applications called widgets, it sends you updates made to your favorite websites. The system uses RSS feeds to push information from these websites directly into your mobile phone the minute theyre updated.

Many of our customers are already familiar with RSS, but those of you that arent can check out the What is an RSS feed? section under FAQ.

The best thing about WidSets is its simplicity. You simply install WidSets on your phone, select the widgets that interest you (or create your own in the Studio), and start using it. As soon as one of your chosen websites is updated (e.g. a news service, blog or weather forecast site), the widget for that website sends you an alert. Wherever you are, you are always kept up-to-date.

What is special about WidSets?

WidSets can be personalized. A feature unique to WidSets; facilitating user-generated applications through easy-to-use templates, so that any user - not just developers - can add a favorite website to WidSets.

One of the great things about WidSets is its ability to push content to your mobile phone as soon as it becomes available. You no longer have to use a web browser to find information. Instead, WidSets notifies you when new information is available.

WidSets vs. widgets?

By now youre probably wondering what the different is between WidSets and widgets. WidSets is the service you access via www.widsets.com and which you use on your mobile phone. Widgets, on the other hand, are the little boxes - or mini-applications – inside WidSets that fetch information for you from the internet.

What are WidSets main features?

  • Use widgets on your mobile phone
    Use WidSets to access your favorite RSS feeds, blogs, news or information service – texts and pictures. Or just play games and have fun.
  • Browse the WidSets Library
    Discover the vast collection of widgets available in the WidSets Library.
  • Personalize their appearance
    Create, personalize and tweak your widgets to suit your style and mood.
  • Create widgets and publish them in the Library
    Design your own widgets.
  • Audio alerts when a website has been updated
    A great way of ensuring you dont miss any new information on your favorite website.
  • Bookmark links straight from your mobile phone
    Use the Bookmark feature to create a link to the pages you come across via a widget. Use them later to find the website that the information came from.
  • Help develop WidSets
    Participate in the Forum and suggest improvements to the service.
  • Share interesting stuff with your friends
    Whenever you find something interesting, or a widget you particularly like – tell your friends about it.

How much does WidSets cost?

WidSets is absolutely FREE. And we definitely want to keep it that way.

But… your service provider may charge you for data transferred to your phone, i.e. for visiting websites or using data transfer services such as browsing the Internet. So, bear in mind that whenever WidSets conveniently puts information directly at your fingertips, you may be paying for it.

These costs depend on the contract you have with your operator. Contact your operator for data transfer costs. This is particularly important, as roaming (using another operators network, e.g. when traveling abroad) data costs can be pretty high, so its definitely worth looking into.

WidSets has a useful feature called Traffic Monitor which helps you monitor the total amount of data WidSets is using, and how much data each individual widgets is using. You can also set a limit for the amount of data transferred by your widgets and youll receive a notification when the limit has been reached.

What do I need to start using WidSets?

Very little. Youll need access to the Internet and a mobile phone that supports Java MIDP 2.0. If you are not familiar with Java MIDP 2.0, refer to the user manual for your phone, or the manufacturers website.

You will need to ensure that your mobile phone can connect to the Internet - you usually need a data subscription for your phone for this. If your phone has ever received an MMS (picture message) or a game, it is probably already set up to connect to the Internet and ready for WidSets. If your phone does not yet have Internet access, contact your network operator for assistance. Coverage may also vary depending on your operator and phone model.

If this all sounds very technical, dont worry, just give it a try. Sign up for FREE to WidSets, try downloading the WidSets client onto your mobile phone and follow the installation instructions. If it works, your phone supports WidSets!

Which cell phones support WidSets?

The best way to find out if your mobile phone supports WidSets is to try it out. Its free, so why not? Youll need a phone that supports Java MIDP 2.0 (most mobile phones sold in the last couple of years do) and that is able to connect to the Internet. Unfortunately WidSets is not yet compatible with Nextel or T-Mobile USA.

Were doing our best to make WidSets compatible with as many different phone models as possible. But with the constant flow of new phone models and new widgets, we are never going be able to test every widget on every phone model and every operators network. We are therefore really grateful for all feedback on how WidSets is working/not working on your particular phone model. Give your feedback in the Forum and well do our best to help you. Or, check to see if your question has already been answered.

A list of compatible phone models can be found at: http://www.widsets.com/devices.

  • Then click on the Download the selected widgets to your mobile button.
  • Fill in your e-mail address, username and password and click OK.
  • Enter your phone number. (Dont worry, we hate spam too.) A text message will then be sent to this phone number, containing a link to the WidSets download page.
  • Select the link in the text message and your mobile phones web browser will open the WidSets download page.
  • Install WidSets - install the WidSets client on your phone from the folder you had downloaded it to.
  • Start using WidSets.

Alternatively, you can:

  • Download WidSets by entering get.widsets.com in your mobile phones web browser, and then download the WidSets client directly onto your mobile phone.
Or
  • Download the WidSets installation files to your PC from xxxxxxxxxx and transfer them to your mobile phone via Bluetooth, USB or similar. Then open the JAD file on your mobile.

What is a WidSets client?

 

The WidSets client is a mini-application. It is a window in your phone which is used to control widgets and to view the information contained in them. The WidSets client is installed on your phone and is later used to receive information when you are on the move.

How do I download the WidSets client onto mobile phone?

We will send a text message with a download link for the WidSets client to the mobile phone number you provided when you signed up to the service.

This could take a while, as this relies on your operator. We have done our best to use the best possible text message suppliers, but if for some reason you do not receive the link straight away, please be patient and wait for your operator to deliver it to your mobile phone.

If, however, it still seems as though nothing is happening, go to the ´Settings´ page on www.widsets.com and click the ´Reload´ button. This will send a new text message containing the download link. If that doesn´t help, use your phone´s web browser to download WidSets from get.widsets.com.

WidSets comes in two modes: Lite, for older phones and Full, for phones with a higher processing power and more memory.

If you are experiencing problems using the Full mode version, try the Lite mode to see if it helps. And vice versa, if the Lite mode is performing well, try using the Full mode.

Using WidSets

How does WidSets work?

WidSets is a mobile service keeping you up-to-date with the latest news available on the Internet, using widgets. By selecting various widgets from the widget Library, or by creating your own widget in the Studio, you can keep an eye on your favorite websites. Widgets send you information from RSS feeds, image galleries and your favorite web services. Why not set an audio alert on your widgets, to alert you whenever information on these websites has been updated? In addition to news and information, you can also play games and interact with your web communities, with your mobile phone.

What are the do's and dont's of using WidSets?

In order to make WidSets a pleasant experience for everyone, we have established a few do's and don'ts. Basically don't offend anyone - with abusive material or content that may be unsuitable for a public site.

Please read our Terms of Service. They'll give you a pretty good idea of what's allowed and what will get you banned. We have tried our very best to make them understandable to people other than law students.

What are widgets?

Widgets help you easily access information as soon as it's available on the Internet. They take the hassle out of trying to keep up-to-date with the enormous flood of information we are faced with every day.

Widgets use RSS feeds, something many of you have been using on the Internet for quite some time. But for those of you who aren't yet familiar with RSS, they 'push' information to your phone in the form of short descriptive links. You can then either read the brief description or download the entire content. Rather than having to first find a computer and then surf from site to site, you can have the information delivered straight to your mobile phone.

New widgets are constantly being developed, providing unlimited possibilities - not only do they deliver news updates to your phone, alert you when someone has posted a comment on your blog, inform you about a traffic jam, or deliver the latest gossip as soon as it's available, they also allow you to play games and interact with your web communities.

What is an RSS feed?

Websites that are constantly updating their information, such as news pages, use a service that generates a special 'feed' containing just the headlines. To receive this RSS feed, you need a 'client'. WidSets is a client. You tell WidSets which RSS feeds you are interesting in - we call them 'widgets'- and WidSets tells you when they have been updated.

It may sound a bit complicated, but it really is very simple. Instead of wading through all those newsletters or visiting websites, you just open your WidSets client and scan the headlines from your chosen widgets. If something catches your attention, click on the link and the full story will be displayed.

How can I change the RSS feed in a widget?

Configuring an RSS feed address is fairly straightforward. Websites with an RSS feed usually have a small 'RSS' or 'XML' logo on their home page. This is the feed's web address or URL, which you have to copy into your widget.

Copy the address into your widget by right-clicking on the logo and selecting 'Copy Shortcut'. Open the widget you want to modify and right-click to paste the URL into the RSS Feed URL box.

There is another, simpler, way of doing this. If you are using an Internet browser with an RSS reader extension (for example Firefox with the Sage RSS reader extension), you can simply drag and drop the RSS feed from the RSS reader into the widget's URL field.

What can I do in the Options-menu in the WidSet client?

The options menu enables you to manage your WidSets client, share widgets with friends, change the settings of individual widgets and send the content of a widget to your email address or Bookmarks folder.

Here are the key options on the WidSets client:

  • Send widget - if you think someone might be interested in WidSets or a particular widget, enter the person's phone number and send a widget directly to their phone. If they have never used WidSets, they'll also receive the download link for the WidSets client.
  • Connection - switch between the Http and Socket connections if you're having trouble connecting to the Internet with your WidSets client. The default setting is a Socket connection, but if your service provider doesn't support this form of connection, you may have to switch to Http.
  • Remove – this is a handy way of cleaning up WidSets and removing widgets that you never use. Removing idle widgets enhances the performance of your WidSets client.
  • Reload - this option refreshes the content of the selected widget, giving you the most up-to-date information available.
  • Settings - use alerts to notify you when a particular widget has been updated, or change the settings for switching between feeds in widgets with multiple feeds. Some widgets have content that is specified by tags, for instance tags for photo galleries or news topics. You can also specify whether you want to see images or the green plus sign as notification about updated content.
  • Send - use the 'send' option when you find something interesting. You can send the content straight to your Bookmarks folder or to an email address.
  • Help – here you will find simple instructions on how to use the WidSets client.

What are widget alerts and how can I use them?

Widget alerts can be set for each individual widget. When the alert is set to 'on', it informs you when the content of that widget has been updated.

To set an alert, go to 'Options' -> 'Widget' -> 'Settings' and select an alert from the seven alternatives. Widgets change visually when there is new content available.

There are a total of six audio alerts and one vibrating alert. The 'vibra' alert only works if your phone supports vibrating alerts.

You can adjust the volume of the audio alerts, on a scale of 1-5. If for some reason your alert function doesn't work, make sure the vibrating alert and warning tones are enabled on your profile.

What is the green plus sign shown on widgets all about?

If you see a green plus sign on one of your widgets, it means that there is new content available for that particular widget.

You don't even have to open your widgets to see if they have been updated!

How do I search for widgets in the library?

Our widget Library is constantly expanding. There is more and more information available every day and it is our job to make it as easy as possible for you to discover what is available. You can access the Library from your phone or from www.widsets.com.

Here's how you browse the Library:

  • Search - type in search words to narrow your search to specific topics
  • Categories – short-cuts to specific categories such as news, weather, etc.
  • Browse by tags - view the tag cloud of available widgets
  • Latest widgets - newest additions to the Library
  • Most popular - find out which widgets are most popular amongst WidSets users
  • Top rated - list of top-rated widgets in the Library
  • Slick Picks - check out the hottest downloads available on WidSets

For a full listing of available widgets, leave the search field blank.

How do I transfer widgets to my WidSets mobile client?

Once you are logged in (on www.widsets.com) and your WidSets client is connected (on your phone), select the widgets that are of interest. Then click on 'Synchronize' to transfer them to your phone. There is also a 'tooltip' function on this page (open by clicking on the ? in the top right-hand corner of the window) which will help you if you get stuck.

What can I do in the Studio section?

You will find several templates in the Studio for creating your own widgets. Change the way your widget looks, name it and give it an RSS feed address.

It's fast and easy. The Skin Editor is a great feature for personalizing your widgets. Just follow the simple step-by-step instructions or check out our Forum posting under 'Tricks and Tips' to learn exactly how to bring your widget to life by changing its style, icon, background color, etc.

If you have already created a widget for your website or blog, why not learn how to promote your widget? All you need to do is select a short script for your website's source code. Find out more under 'Promote Widgets'.

If you have some web development skills, you can also create a widget for your own web service in developer.widsets.com.

What is the Bookmark function used for?

Whenever you stumble across something interesting in a widget and you want to check it out again later, or just keep it for future reference, you can store it by clicking on the Bookmark.

Content which you have bookmarked from your mobile phone is stored under 'Bookmarks' on www.widsets.com – you must be logged in to access it. We love this feature - let us know what you think.

What exactly is MOSH?

What exactly is MOSH?

MOSH is a content sharing site where community members upload, distribute and manage content to be viewed and enjoyed on mobile devices. With MOSH, anything from applications like mobile games, to videos, blogs, songs or photos are now accessible and distributable on your mobile device.

How does it work?

There are three key elements to MOSH:

  1. 1. A website
  2. 2. A mobile website
  3. 3. An application for mobile devices (available for download on Nokia devices only)

The website is your main source for accessing the wide range of content available through MOSH. It is here where you can create your profile, upload content, manage your collections and specify which selects to send to your mobile device as mobile feeds.

The mobile website is where users with both Nokia and non-Nokia devices can access mobile feeds and view the MOSH service.

The application allows users quick access to MOSH from their Nokia device.
