## Executive Summary

TechSeed Nordics is now the Nordic GTM and lifecycle-support partner for Siemens Building X, Desigo CC, and Navigator. This partnership puts you in front of a massive pain point: Nordic real-estate CFOs, sustainability officers, and operations teams spend €500K–€2M per year per portfolio on manual compliance work — CSRD disclosure, EPBD energy-performance certificates, EU Taxonomy reporting, EN 15978 lifecycle assessment.

**DWS IQ is the compliance automation layer that turns that spend into a revenue stream for you.**

We do not compete with your Siemens systems-integration business. We complement it. Building X generates telemetry; we operationalize the compliance layer that makes that telemetry auditable and submission-ready. You remain the integrator and customer relationship owner. We provide the compliance-automation IP.

**Revenue opportunity:** €15K–€25K annual recurring per portfolio customer × 6–12 customers per year = €90K–€300K annual revenue with 60%+ reseller margin.

**Pilot target:** 3–5 Nordic real-estate portfolios in FY2025 CSRD Wave 2 reporting cycle. **Measurable outcome:** signed, submission-ready CSRD disclosure, EPBD certificate, and Taxonomy alignment — delivered in 90 seconds per portfolio, zero manual work.

## The Problem

Nordic real-estate is under regulatory convergence pressure with hard deadlines:

| Regulation                        | Deadline                          | Current State                                   | Cost/Portfolio/Year |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| **CSRD Wave 2**                   | FY2025 reporting (due April 2026) | Manual mapping from Building X data; 4–6 months | €120K–€180K         |
| **EPBD 2024 Recast**              | Effective Jan 2025                | No standard tooling; spreadsheet-based          | €80K–€120K          |
| **EU Taxonomy Art. 7.1–7.7**      | 2026 annual disclosure            | New metrics; no automation                      | €60K–€100K          |
| **EN 15978 Lifecycle Assessment** | 2026–2027 (Fit for 55)            | Embodied carbon + material tracking             | €80K–€150K          |

**Total annual spend per portfolio:** €340K–€550K, managed by fragmented teams (finance, legal, operations, sustainability).

**Your opportunity as integrator:** Today, your Siemens deployments end at the operational dashboard. Your customer still needs to hire consultants for the compliance layer. **If you own the compliance layer, you own the relationship value.**

---

## The Solution: DWS IQ as Your Reseller Channel

DWS IQ provides three core components, all of which you resell under your brand:

### Aegis — Regulation-to-Control Mapping

Building X sensor data → CSRD E1 disclosure  
Desigo HVAC + controls → EPBD energy-performance certificate  
Facility logs → EU Taxonomy alignment  
Material asset register → EN 15978 carbon footprint

You integrate once; we provide the compliance schema binding. Updates (e.g., new Taxonomy metrics, CSRD scope changes) flow through our platform — you don't rework client implementations.

### Sovereign Infra — Jurisdiction-Specific Automation

- **EU standard stack:** CSRD + EPBD 2024 + Taxonomy + EN 15978 (all built-in, zero config)
- **Data residency:** All processing on EU-sovereign compute (Scaleway, Aiven, UpCloud, DGX Cloud Frankfurt) — zero US routing, GDPR-native
- **Future-ready:** As new regulations land (2026–2027 Fit for 55 acceleration, ASEAN ESG rules, US energy reporting), we update the platform; you inherit the updates

### Connect — Audit-Ready Writeback

- **Native integration:** Building X API → DWS IQ → submission-ready output
- **KYA-governed control:** Approved changes (HVAC setpoints, maintenance work orders, renovation triggers) flow back into Desigo CC with full audit trail
- **Signed output:** Every metric tied to sensor source, calculation rule, regulatory basis, and approver — ready for Big 4 auditor review with zero rework

## Why This Works for TechSeed

1. **No competition with Siemens** — You remain the systems integrator and customer relationship owner. We are the IP layer you resell.

2. **Higher deal value** — Today, you sell Building X + integration. With DWS IQ, you sell Building X + integration + compliance-automation = 25–40% higher deal value per customer.

3. **Longer customer retention** — Compliance is mandatory and ongoing. Once your customer is on DWS IQ, switching costs are high — they lose audit continuity and have to rework their regulatory baseline.

4. **Regulatory updates without rework** — When CSRD scope expands or Taxonomy gets new metrics, we update the platform. You don't have to re-engineer client implementations.

5. **Channel credibility** — Offering Big 4-audit-ready compliance automation makes you a more credible strategic partner to Siemens customers. You move from "systems integrator" to "compliance and operations transformation partner."

6. **Siemens co-selling** — Once you land 2–3 pilot customers, Siemens SI Building Nordics will reference you in their sales playbook. Frida's "action from data" becomes a three-party story: Siemens (hardware/software) → TechSeed (integration) → DWS IQ (compliance automation).
