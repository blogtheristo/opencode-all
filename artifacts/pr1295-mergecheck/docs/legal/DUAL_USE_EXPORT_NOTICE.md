## Purpose

This notice addresses export control obligations under EU Dual-Use Regulation (EU 2021/821) as they apply to DWS IQ Aegis and related products that may serve defence, government, or dual-use applications.

## Applicable Regulation

- **EU Dual-Use Regulation** — Regulation (EU) 2021/821 setting up a Union regime for the control of exports, brokering, technical assistance, transit, and transfer of dual-use items
- **Finnish Export Control** — Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs, Export Control Unit
- **Wassenaar Arrangement** — Categories 4 (Computers) and 5 (Telecommunications and Information Security)

## Product Classification

### DWS IQ 6 (SaaS)
- **Classification:** Not controlled — general-purpose enterprise compliance software
- **Export control:** No restrictions
- **Rationale:** Does not contain controlled technology; standard SaaS delivery

### DWS IQ Aegis (Sovereign Platform)
- **Classification:** Potentially controlled — single-tenant sovereign AI platform deployed for government/defence customers
- **Export control:** Pre-screening required for defence customers
- **Relevant categories:**
  - Category 4A003: Computers designed for aggregation of computing power
  - Category 5A002: Information security systems using cryptography
  - Category 5D002: Software for information security
- **Rationale:** Sovereign deployment, cryptographic capabilities, potential military end-use

### KYA Framework (Software)
- **Classification:** Not controlled — governance and compliance framework
- **Export control:** No restrictions
- **Rationale:** Standards documentation, no controlled technology

## Export Screening Requirements

Before engaging with any defence or government customer for Aegis deployment:

1. **End-use verification** — Confirm the intended use is not for weapons of mass destruction, military applications prohibited under EU sanctions, or delivery to embargoed destinations
2. **End-user screening** — Screen against EU sanctions lists (Consolidated Financial Sanctions List), UN sanctions, and Finnish national sanctions
3. **Catch-all clause** — Under Art. 4 of Regulation 2021/821, even non-listed items require authorization if the exporter is informed that items are/may be intended for WMD, military end-use in embargoed countries, or as parts for military items exported from EU without authorization
4. **Intra-EU transfer** — No export license required for transfers within EU/EEA
5. **Documentation** — Maintain records of all export screening for 5 years

## Sanctions Compliance

Lifetime Oy complies with:
- EU sanctions (Council Regulations under CFSP)
- Finnish national sanctions (Ministry for Foreign Affairs)
- UN Security Council sanctions

Lifetime Oy does not provide services to:
- Sanctioned countries, entities, or individuals
- End-users on EU, Finnish, or UN sanctions lists
