## 1. Purpose

Single indexed registry of every standard, regulation, and protocol that **DWS IQ**, **Firehorse**, **Lifetime Fleet**, and **Sovereign Database** products rely on, claim conformance with, or target for certification.

**Why:** Standards coverage is a sales asset (enterprise RFP gate), a legal defence (EU regulatory audit), and an engineering discipline (process maturity). Without a single source of truth, agents and humans re-research the same standards for every customer.

**Out of scope:** Country-specific laws outside EU jurisdiction (e.g. US HIPAA, Chinese GB standards), matured internal conventions that are not externally recognised.

---

### 3.6 Sovereignty Tier Map (DWS positioning)

**Purpose.** DWS will not use one word — "sovereign" — to mean three different things. This tier map is the canonical definition used by the `/sovereignty-assess` skill, by sales proposals, and by any partner co-marketing that touches the word "sovereign."

**Rule.** Match the tier to the workload first, the vendor second. Never market a vendor above the tier their corporate structure can support.

| Tier | Name                            | Definition                                                                                      | CLOUD Act reach                                   | Claimable examples (reference architecture)                                                                        | Typical workloads                                                                  |
| ---- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | **Full EU Sovereign**           | EU-owned legal entity, EU-controlled board, no US parent anywhere in the chain                  | Outside reach at the legal-entity level           | Aiven (Helsinki), Scaleway (Paris), OVHcloud (Roubaix), IONOS (Karlsruhe), SUSE (Nuremberg)                        | Defence, critical infrastructure, regulated public sector, certain NIS2 essentials |
| 2    | **EU Operational Sovereignty**  | EU data residency + EU personnel + EU escalation. Parent company outside the EU (typically US)  | Parent still reachable; operations kept in-region | **Red Hat Confirmed Sovereign Support** (IBM parent, EU-citizen engineers, in-region storage, isolated escalation) | Most CSRD, GDPR, EU AI Act enterprise workloads                                    |
| 3    | **EU Region, non-EU Ownership** | Data lands in a European region of a non-EU vendor; no further operational or legal sovereignty | Standard CLOUD Act / non-EU discovery order reach | AWS / Azure / GCP EU regions without a sovereign cloud overlay                                                     | Non-sensitive workloads, dev/test, public content                                  |

**Red Hat partner note.** Red Hat Confirmed Sovereign Support (announced 6 November 2025, generally available early 2026) is classified here as a **strong Tier 2**. Red Hat's own language is careful — "confirmed," not "absolute" — and DWS co-marketing will match that care. When a customer workload requires Tier 1, DWS pairs the Red Hat platform layer with an EU-sovereign data layer (Aiven / Scaleway) and the DWS KYA identity spine; the stack then satisfies Tier 1 data and Tier 2 platform with honest labelling at each layer.

**Enforcement.** `/sovereignty-assess` scores against this table. A vendor marked Tier 2 must never be sold into a Tier 1 RFP. Full rationale and partner framing: `onelifetime.world/posts/redhat-sovereign-move-three-tiers.html`.

**See also (added v3.61, 2026-05-28):** the universal three-pillar sovereignty model (Data / Technology / Operational) × three-jurisdiction emphasis profile (EU / US / Singapore) is canonical in `docs/PHD_FIREHORSE_EN.md` §14.23 and registered in `DWS_TRUTH_STANDARD.md` Row 16. The pillar model and the vendor-tier model in this section operate at different layers: pillars describe _what kind of sovereignty_ (data, technology, operational); vendor tiers describe _how sovereign the vendor is_ (T1/T2/T3). A complete procurement coordinate is therefore (Pillar, Tier, Jurisdiction). Field-Note explainer at `onelifetime.world/posts/sovereignty-field-note-2-three-pillars.html`.

## 5. Lookup Procedure for Agents

When an agent (or human) needs to determine which standards apply to a given product, integration, or RFP:

1. Identify the **product/integration/feature** in Section 4 (integration map) or 3 (registry).
2. Resolve every applicable row; note standards marked ✅ Active (claimable today) vs. 🎯 Target (roadmap only).

## 8. Sales Standards

Standards governing how DWS IQ 6 / Lifetime Oy sales and investor outreach is structured and executed.

### 8.1 Pitch Standard

**Document:** `PITCH_STANDARD.md`
**Version:** 1.1 (Active)
**Scope:** All outbound sales and investor pitches originating from Lifetime Oy / DWS IQ 6.

**Rule:** The word "pitch" always means a four-part deliverable, produced in order:

| #   | Part                   | Purpose                                                 |
| --- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | DD (Due Diligence)     | Research on target company or investor firm             |
| 2   | Person Investigation   | Research on the specific named contact                  |
| 3   | Letter Wedge Formation | Outbound message ≤250 words, four-beat structure        |
| 4   | Appendix by Case       | Tailored evidence — regulatory, traction, or deal terms |

**Pitch types:**

| Type     | Target                      | Storage path                                             |
| -------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Client   | Enterprise buyer            | `sales-outreach/PITCH_<COMPANY>_<CONTACT>_<YYYYMMDD>.md` |
| Investor | VC / PE / angel / strategic | `investor-outreach/PITCH_<FIRM>_<PERSON>_<YYYYMMDD>.md`  |

A document missing any part is not a pitch. Full quality gate and part specifications: see `PITCH_STANDARD.md`.
