3. Digital Goods & Software

Per EU Consumer Rights Directive, the 14-day right of withdrawal applies to digital goods until the moment the download or streaming process has begun with your express consent and acknowledgement that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal.

However, Lifetime Oy extends this period. You may request a full refund for any SaaS subscription within 14 days of the initial purchase date, provided no Consulting/Expert hours have been consumed.

4. Expert Services & Consulting

Services that have already been performed (e.g., hours spent on workshops, coding, or advisory) are non-refundable. Retainers may be cancelled with 30 days' notice.

5. How to Request a Return or Refund

To initiate a return or refund request:

  1. Email: support@lifetime.fi
  2. Subject: Return Request - [Order #]
  3. Timeline: We process all verified return/refund requests within 5 business days. Refunds will be returned to your original payment method (PayPal/Revolut/Bank Transfer).

  • Physical Merchandise (mugs, hats): €0.00 (Free). No cost to return.
