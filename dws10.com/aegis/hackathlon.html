The Demo

Live: dws cbam-scan --company

A Nordic construction company. A single CLI call. The agent chain runs end-to-end — ERP connector, parallel agent fan-out, KYA-governed reasoning, signed audit trail. No dashboard, no SaaS login, no slideware.

Under the Hood

Four moves. No magic.

Step 1
ERP Pull
Visma connector authenticates with customer credentials and pulls invoice lines, material codes, and supplier geography — straight into the agent bus.
Step 2
18-Agent Fan-out
VulcanAgent, GaiaAgent, EmissionsAnalyst, LogisticsAgent, ComplianceAgent, MaterialOracle, and 12 more — all run in parallel. Same codebase as DWS IQ 6.
Step 3
KYA + Firecracker
Every agent runs in its own Firecracker MicroVM. KYA Engine logs every decision with reasoning lineage — EU AI Act Article 12 native, court-defensible.
Step 4
Signed Report
Structured CBAM tariff exposure report. SHA-256 + Ed25519 signed audit trail. Regulatory submission-ready — no human assembly, no dashboard export.
