Business Case: Generative Low-Carbon Design

Minimizing Embodied Carbon at Design Phase

Challenge: Decisions made in the early design phase determine 80% of a building's lifecycle carbon impact. Architects often lack real-time data on the carbon footprint of material choices.

Solution: DWS IQ's DesignPartner Agents integrate with BIM software (like Revit). They analyze structural requirements and suggest low-carbon material alternatives (e.g., mass timber vs. steel) and structural optimizations that use less material without compromising safety.

Impact:

  • Up to 40% reduction in embodied carbon
  • Automated lifecycle assessment (LCA) reporting
  • Compliance with green building standards (LEED, BREEAM)
