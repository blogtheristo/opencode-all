Deployment Architecture

Aegis is engineered local-first for deep and reliable industrial operations. In manufacturing control rooms, dual-use facilities, and air-gapped plants, compliance execution cannot rely on external connectivity. Local operation is an architectural requirement for critical environments, while cloud hosting remains a valid deployment choice for broader enterprise workloads.

  • Local / Edge: Engineered for manufacturing control rooms and other critical or dual-use sites. Core agent execution, evidence capture, identity checks, audit logs, and human kill authority stay strictly on-site, so core operational outcomes do not depend on network availability.
  • EU-Sovereign Cloud: Designed for non-critical enterprise workloads, development environments, cross-site reporting, and organizations that accept network dependency.
  • Hybrid: Delivers local execution paired with optional outbound synchronization, backup, or compliance reporting to an EU-sovereign cloud.

While local-first execution is required for control-room deployments, Aegis does not force a universal offline model onto non-critical corporate functions. Commercial pricing scales with operational coverage, hardware footprint, SLA, and support guarantees — not by charging a premium tax for local deployment. To eliminate upfront friction, the €45K Aegis Pilot proves the complete local-first operational path directly on existing customer infrastructure or a compact edge appliance before committing to large-scale DGX silicon procurement.

Supply continuity. The models we adopt ship as open weights, so the inference layer is portable by design rather than tied to one vendor's stack. A non-NVIDIA fallback path (AMD, Intel accelerators) is carried in our supply-risk plan, and hardware compatibility is validated per engagement rather than assumed.
