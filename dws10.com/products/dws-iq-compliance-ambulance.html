Why a third room

The Control Room (one node) and Situation Room (two bridged nodes) live in your building on mains power. The Compliance Ambulance takes the same DWS IQ capability mobile — onto a site vehicle or a health-care transport. The difference is energy: a vehicle can't feed a desktop-class node, so the silicon changes.

What's inside

  • NVIDIA Jetson Thor (T5000) — 2070 FP4 TFLOPS · 128 GB LPDDR5x · configurable 40–130 W · 3.5× energy efficiency vs AGX Orin. Energy-optimized for vehicle power.
