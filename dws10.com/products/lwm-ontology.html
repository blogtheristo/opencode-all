What's Inside

7 Semantic Layers Per Industry

  1. Domain Concepts — Core vocabulary of the industry (e.g., electric arc furnace, stainless steel grade, melt shop)
  2. Regulatory Terms — ETS allowance, CBAM import obligation, CSRD disclosure point, EU AI Act Article 12 log event
  3. Operational Vocabulary — Process language as practitioners use it (shift patterns, batch IDs, sensor naming conventions)
  4. Process Taxonomy — Hierarchical classification of industrial processes aligned to NACE Rev. 2
  5. Compliance Requirements — Per-regulation obligation mapping with deadlines, thresholds, and reporting formats
  6. Commercial Intelligence — Buyer roles (25-role enterprise taxonomy), buying journey stages, sales wedge mapping
  7. Investor Positioning — Per-industry TAM, regulatory tailwinds, and growth signals
