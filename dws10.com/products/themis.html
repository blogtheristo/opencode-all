Named after the Greek goddess of divine law — Themis is DWS's proprietary implementation of the open KYA standard, productised as the Aegis Control Plane. Identity attribution, Article 12 logging pipeline, Annex III systems registry, and runtime policy gating, bundled into one sovereign control plane that ships with every Aegis deployment and is available standalone for Sovereign Infra (Tier 2) customers.

KYA = the protocol (open standard). Themis = DWS's implementation. Aegis = the product.

Why Themis Now

  • EU AI Act enforcement: 2 August 2026. High-risk AI systems must have automatic, tamper-evident record-keeping with six-month minimum retention (Art. 26(6)), Annex III classification, Art. 12 logging, and Art. 72 post-market monitoring. Themis ships this as one product.
  • Sovereign by design. At Tier 1, no US-incorporated vendor anywhere in the Themis data path. Zero CLOUD Act exposure. The only EU-built alternative to Credo AI, OneTrust AI Governance, and IBM Watsonx Governance for defence-grade customers.
  • KYA-compliant. Implements the open KYA standard v1.7 — identity attribution (Pillar 1), capability gating (Pillar 2), forensic observability (Pillar 3), Memory Audit Trail (KYA-S §10), and Systems Inventory & Policy Gate (KYA-S §11). No proprietary protocol lock-in.
  • Defence-grade audit. Hash-chained append-only ledger with per-event SHA-256 chain on Aiven PostgreSQL (Finnish-owned, EU region). Court-defensible tamper evidence that exceeds the Article 26(6) minimum.
