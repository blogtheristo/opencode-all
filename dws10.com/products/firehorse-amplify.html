Typical Usage Scenario

Critical flow meter stops working during holiday period

A construction portfolio tracks district heating consumption across 15 buildings. One meter fails on Friday evening before a long weekend. Under manual processes, this might go unnoticed for days—causing missing data in the next CSRD report.

With Firehorse Amplify:

  • The workflow expects hourly data from all 15 meters
  • When meter #7 stops sending data, the system immediately flags the anomaly
  • Alert goes to facility manager + backup contact via email and WhatsApp/webhook
  • Fallback estimate is calculated from historical pattern and weather data
  • Report generation continues with flagged estimate, maintaining deadline compliance
  • Audit log records the failure, alert, fallback calculation and final resolution

Result: No missing data, no delayed report, no manual heroics during the weekend. Full audit trail for the next compliance review.
