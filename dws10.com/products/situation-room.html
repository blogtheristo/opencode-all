VPK example — Drone defense dual-use scenario

The scenario: 2026, eastern Finland border municipality. A vapaapalokunta runs a sovereign-by-default situation room out of the kalustohalli. Their dual-use mandate covers fire/rescue plus civil defence including unknown UAS (unmanned aerial system) sightings — drones that may be hobby, may be hostile, may be both. CER & NIS2 require auditable detection-to-decision logs.

Here's how Situation Room runs it without leaving the building:

  • Detection (vision): YOLOv8-x on Node A processes 4 simultaneous camera feeds (tornin katto, kalustohalli, kuntakeskus, kylätie). Bounding box + class + confidence streamed to the situation board in real time.
  • Detection (acoustic): BirdNET-derived acoustic model on Node A's secondary lane classifies the rotor signature — quadcopter? fixed-wing? Confirms or rejects the visual detection within 200 ms.
  • Reasoning: Qwen 2.5 72B (sharded across both nodes) cross-references detection + flight corridor SOP + KRITIS / CER rules + local NOTAM database. Generates a recommendation: "Likely commercial quadcopter, flight corridor B7, not in restricted zone. Log + observe. If sustained > 5 min in restricted zone, escalate."
  • Comms: Whisper transcribes the duty officer's radio call to a partner brigade; TTS broadcasts an automated mutual-aid SitRep over Virve in FI & SV.
  • Independent check: Llama 3.1 70B on Node B runs the same scenario independently. If both models agree, confidence is high. If they disagree, the operator sees both — and decides.
  • Dual-use audit log: every detection, every recommendation, every operator decision lands in the Article 12 log — replicated across both nodes. CER / NIS2 inspector arrives, you hand them a sealed export.
  • Sovereignty: nothing transits external networks. No detection footage, no Virve transcript, no recommendation prompt ever crosses your firewall. CLOUD Act doesn't reach the kalustohalli.
