Most compliance failures start with broken data pipelines: someone forgets to export last month's consumption, a critical meter stops sending alerts, or a validation step gets skipped during summer holidays.

Firehorse Automation removes these failure points. It connects your existing systems—ERP, SAP, IoT sensors, approval queues—and runs the full cycle automatically: collect → validate → alert → report. Every run is logged, every error triggers an alert, and every output is audit‑ready by design.

Zero‑Touch Compliance Methodology

  1. Map failure points. Workshop with your team to identify the top 5 manual bottlenecks (e.g. monthly energy export, quarterly carbon calculations, EPBD renovation tracking).
  2. Design 3 core workflows. Event‑driven automation that runs on schedule or on trigger (e.g. new invoice → extract energy cost → update dashboard).
  3. Deploy in your environment. Runs on EU‑based serverless infrastructure or inside your existing cloud, depending on security requirements.
  4. Validate for 4 weeks. Monitor real performance, adjust thresholds and schedules, handle edge cases.

Typical Usage Scenario

Critical flow meter stops working during holiday period

A construction portfolio tracks district heating consumption across 15 buildings. One meter fails on Friday evening before a long weekend. Under manual processes, this might go unnoticed for days—causing missing data in the next CSRD report.

With Firehorse Automation:

  • The workflow expects hourly data from all 15 meters
  • When meter #7 stops sending data, the system immediately flags the anomaly
  • Alert goes to facility manager + backup contact via email and Slack
  • Fallback estimate is calculated from historical pattern and weather data
  • Report generation continues with flagged estimate, maintaining deadline compliance
  • Audit log records the failure, alert, fallback calculation and final resolution

Result: No missing data, no delayed report, no manual heroics during the weekend. Full audit trail for the next compliance review.
