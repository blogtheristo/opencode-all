Zero-Touch Compliance Methodology

"Eliminate the copy-paste." Firehorse Automation targets the "swivel chair" integration where humans move data between systems: * Orchestration: Connects DWS IQ, ERP, and IoT. * Error Handling: Intelligently manages API failures or bad data. * Auditability: Every step of the automation is logged.

Usage Scenario: The Broken Sensor

A critical flow meter fails on the factory floor.

Firehorse Automation handles the incident: 1. Detect: The Ingestion Workflow notices the data stream has stopped. 2. Alert: It creates a maintenance ticket in your ERP (e.g., SAP PM). 3. Mitigate: It flags the data gap in DWS IQ and applies the approved "estimation method" for compliance reporting (flagging it as estimated). 4. Report: It adds an incident note to the Monthly Report. 5. Outcome: Compliance continuity is maintained, and maintenance is dispatched instantly.
