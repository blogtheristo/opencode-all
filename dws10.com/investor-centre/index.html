DWS IQ Aegis
Governed sovereign AI for regulated European industry

DWS IQ Aegis is a single-tenant AI platform for regulated and dual-use industrial workloads. It is designed for organisations that need controlled AI execution, traceable evidence and deployment choices that match operational and data requirements.

Aegis supports three deployment modes:

  • Local / Edge: customer-controlled environment
  • Hybrid: local or private infrastructure combined with selected services
  • EU-Sovereign Cloud: EU-hosted deployment with defined technical and contractual boundaries

The deployment mode is selected separately from the product tier.

The problem

AI systems are moving from generating text to taking bounded operational actions. In regulated environments, the control questions are direct:

  • Who authorises an agent to act?
  • What mandate and information did it use?
  • What evidence supports the result?
  • Can the action be limited, reviewed, revoked and reconstructed?
  • Can the system run in an environment appropriate for the workload?

Aegis is built around those control and evidence requirements.

What Aegis provides

  • Bounded permissions and execution scope
  • Human approval and review points where required
  • Evidence provenance for inputs, decisions and outputs
  • Traceable logs and machine-readable records
  • Version binding for policies, models and workflows
  • Revocation and fail-closed control patterns
  • Single-tenant deployment options
  • Local, hybrid and EU-hosted operating models

Aegis is designed to produce auditable evidence and controlled outputs. It does not itself create legal certification or guarantee regulatory compliance. Final legal, regulatory and operational acceptance remains deployment-specific and belongs to the customer and its appointed experts.

Initial commercial wedge

The first commercial wedge is governed industrial evidence generation.

A bounded workflow can generate installation-level emissions evidence from approved source data and return a structured, citable output for procurement, ERP or CBAM-related workflows. The initial use case is deliberately narrow: prove the evidence flow, measure operational value and establish whether the workflow should move into repeatable paid use.

Product versions

Aegis Standard

For commercial regulated-industry workloads requiring controlled AI execution, evidence handling and a defined deployment boundary.

Aegis Defence

For defence, dual-use and critical-infrastructure contexts requiring a more restrictive operating model and deployment assessment.

Aegis Sovereign

For customers requiring a customised architecture, infrastructure arrangement, governance model and implementation scope.

Final configuration, infrastructure, integrations, security controls and acceptance criteria are defined per deployment.

Commercial model

Aegis is sold as a product deployment with onboarding and recurring platform fees. Supporting engineering and outcome-based services are scoped separately where required. Current canonical pricing is maintained on the DWS IQ price list. Investor Centre does not duplicate prices that may change.

Evidence status

Current evidence includes the KYA-S1 governance specification and deterministic local test work covering defined permission-band behaviour. This is evidence for the tested scope, not a claim of complete KYA conformance, independent certification or production-wide validation.

Market and buyers

Aegis is intended for European organisations operating in regulated or strategically sensitive environments: industrial manufacturing, defence and dual-use, critical infrastructure, energy and utilities, supply-chain and procurement operations, and organisations handling regulated operational evidence.

The initial buyer is responsible for operational risk, compliance evidence, industrial data, procurement or controlled AI deployment.

Company and founder

Lifetime Oy is a Finnish technology company building DWS IQ, a product family for sovereign and governed AI in regulated European industry.

Founder and CEO Risto Anton Päärni has more than 35 years of experience across software engineering, ICT infrastructure, industrial project management and demanding system deliveries, including Borealis and the Finnish Tax Administration.

The company is focused on converting Aegis capability into customer conversations, paid industrial pilots and repeatable commercial deployments.

The investment

Lifetime Oy is raising €650,000 through a SAFE at a €3.8M valuation cap from EU/EEA-domiciled investors.

The round is intended to fund product hardening, industrial pilot execution, customer evidence, sales execution, reseller-channel preparation, Nordic expansion and required infrastructure and operating capacity.

These are the targets the round is intended to fund, not achieved outcomes. Current, tested status is described under “Evidence status” above; nothing in this section should be read as a completed milestone.
