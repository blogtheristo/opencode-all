The Captain Roles

Lifetime Oy is building a focused investor group around DWS IQ, a sovereign AI product portfolio for regulated European industry. The structure combines one Admiral who anchors the round with a small number of Captains who bring industrial capital, domain authority and access to the markets where governed AI must be bought, deployed and accepted.

1. The Customer-Access Captain

We are looking for EU/EEA industrial investors who can bring more than capital: access to named buyers, plants, procurement processes and decision-makers in sectors where governed AI must earn trust before deployment — subject to verification of those relationships during due diligence.

2. The Domain Captain

Each Captain would represent a real industrial domain, such as chemicals, defence manufacturing, construction, metals, water treatment or critical infrastructure. The role is to add sector judgement and commercial reach around a defined customer motion.

3. The Evidence Captain

DWS IQ is built around turning regulated industrial work into traceable evidence, controlled decisions and accepted operational outputs. We want Captains who understand what evidence a serious industrial buyer can actually approve and buy.

4. The Deployment Captain

Aegis is designed for local/edge, hybrid and EU-hosted deployment. We are looking for Captains who understand long-cycle industrial deployment, infrastructure, security review, and the difference between a convincing demo and an accepted production configuration.

5. The Distribution Captain

The Captain role is for an investor who can help convert industry-specific pain points into a repeatable sales funnel: customer meeting, dedicated evidence demo, paid pilot, and Aegis production proposal.

The Captain role is not a decorative title for a passive angel. It is intended for investors who understand how industrial software gets bought, deployed, operated and accepted.
