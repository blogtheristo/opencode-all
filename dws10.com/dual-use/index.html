Consider a representative disruption: a structural failure constrains output at a regional lime-processing facility. Lime is a flux agent in steel production — when domestic lime supply tightens, steelmakers import, and non-EU steel triggers CBAM carbon tariffs. Lime is also a cement feedstock, a chemical precursor (calcium carbide), and a critical raw material under the EU CRM Act, which grants strategic projects the same security priority as critical infrastructure.

The Six Regulations You Need to Know

Plain Language. No Legal Jargon.

If you work in procurement, supply chain management, or compliance at a European manufacturer — this is what is landing on your desk. These are not future regulations. They are live now or go live in 2025-2027.

2025–2026 milestones: the first wave of CSRD reports (covering FY2024) was filed in 2025; the EU AI Act is phasing in across 2024–2026 (Article 12 logging obligations on the high-risk path); CBAM remained in its transitional phase through 2025 ahead of the definitive regime; dual-use export control runs under Regulation (EU) 2021/821; and the European Commission adopted its EDIP work programme in 2026.
