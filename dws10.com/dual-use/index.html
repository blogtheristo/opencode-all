🔍 What Are Dual Use Industries?

Dual-use industries produce goods and technologies that have both civilian and military applications. These sectors face unique sustainability reporting challenges due to security considerations while still being subject to EU environmental regulations.

🛩️ Aerospace & Aviation

Aircraft manufacturing, satellite systems, UAV/drone technology, space launch services.

🔒 Defense Manufacturing

Military vehicles, naval vessels, defense electronics, ammunition and weapons systems.

🛰️ Space Technology

Satellite manufacturing, ground stations, launch infrastructure, space debris management.

⚡ Critical Infrastructure

Energy grids, telecommunications, water systems, transportation networks.

⚖️ EU Regulatory Framework

EU Taxonomy & Defense

The EU Taxonomy Regulation initially excluded defense, but the 2024 European Defense Industrial Strategy (EDIS) recognizes the need for sustainable defense production.

✅ Applicable Regulations

  • CSRD: Large defense contractors must report sustainability metrics
  • EU Taxonomy: Environmental objectives apply to manufacturing processes
  • Fit for 55: Energy efficiency and emissions targets for facilities
  • CBAM: Carbon border adjustment for imported materials (steel, aluminum)

🔐 Security Considerations

  • Classified information exemptions in CSRD reporting
  • Supply chain disclosure limitations for sensitive components
  • On-premise deployment options for air-gapped environments
  • NATO/EU security clearance compatible data handling
