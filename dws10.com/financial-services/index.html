🇪🇺 EU Sustainable Finance Framework

The EU has created the world's most comprehensive sustainable finance regulatory framework, affecting all financial institutions operating in Europe.

📊 SFDR

Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation. Article 6, 8, and 9 fund classifications. Principal Adverse Impact (PAI) reporting.

🏷️ EU Taxonomy

Classification system for environmentally sustainable activities. Green Asset Ratio (GAR) for banks. Taxonomy-aligned investments.

📋 CSRD for Finance

Banks, insurers, and asset managers must report on financed emissions, climate risks, and transition plans.

🌡️ Climate Stress Tests

ECB/EBA climate risk stress testing. Physical and transition risk assessment. TCFD-aligned disclosures.

📅 Key Deadlines

  • 2024: CSRD reporting for large financial institutions
  • 2025: Full SFDR PAI reporting, expanded Taxonomy criteria
  • 2026: CSRD for listed SMEs, full MiCA implementation

₿ Crypto & Digital Assets

The EU's Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) regulation includes sustainability requirements for crypto-asset service providers.

⚡ Energy Consumption Disclosure

MiCA requires disclosure of energy consumption and environmental impact for consensus mechanisms (PoW, PoS).

🌱 Carbon Footprint Tracking

Track carbon intensity per transaction. Compare PoW vs PoS networks. Offset integration for carbon-neutral crypto.

📊 ESG Scoring for Tokens

Environmental scoring for crypto assets. Help investors choose sustainable digital assets.

🔗 On-Chain Carbon Credits

Integration with tokenized carbon credits. Voluntary carbon market (VCM) tracking. Retirement verification.
