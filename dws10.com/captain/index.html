The Five Questions

How Captain Actually Works

Every Captain we've signed has asked the same five questions in the same order. Here are the honest answers — before the deck, before the call.

Q1 · The product

How does the platform work?

Plain-language outcome in, audit-ready answer out. 18+ specialist EU-sovereign agents do the regulatory math. You ask “is this project CSRD-compliant?” — you get a scored result with the rule it was measured against, the gap, and what to do next. Every run is logged.

Q2 · The business

How much does it make per month?

Today: low five figures monthly, deal-by-deal. Trajectory: €900k ARR by end of 2027. Pricing isn't speculative — €4,999 per deployment, €6,500 per bundle. Every customer saves more in a quarter than they pay us in a year. That's why the curve works.

Q3 · The ticket

How much money to get in?

€50k SAFE for a Captain seat (own one industry). €250k SAFE for a Fleet Admiral chair (own the cluster of 6 Captains beneath you). Both convert at the next priced round; cap and discount set in final docs.

Q4 · The return

What do I get back?

Three streams, they stack: SAFE equity upside at conversion, 5% carry on every deal closed in your industry for the life of the customer, and strategic rights — intro rights, industry vote on roadmap, co-branded launch, path to Fleet Admiral → CEO Council.

Q5 · The path

How do I start this week?

Tonight: one-pager + SAFE terms by email. Within 48h: 30-min call with the CEO — camera on, ID confirmed, no deck. By Friday: wire to Lifetime Oy (Finland), your name on the seat. First quarterly briefing in three weeks.

Q6 · The seat

Why is scarcity real?

30 industries, 30 Captains. One per seat. When Steel is taken, no second Steel Captain exists. The scarcity is structural, not marketing. Construction is filling. Maritime is in conversation. Entertainment & Cultural Industries is still open.

Standard step on our side: camera on, ID confirmed before SAFE docs land. We protect you, we protect the cap table, and we keep the impersonation playbook out of the seat board. KYC first, paper second.
