Lifetime Oy Opens Platinum Industry Captain Seats for Europe's Leading Fit for 55 Platform

HELSINKI — Lifetime Oy today announced the opening of Platinum Industry Captain seats for DWS IQ 6, the industrial climate intelligence platform covering ETS, CBAM, CSRD, EPBD, and 12 more EU mandates across 16 regulated industries.

The company is seeking operators who know their vertical inside out — not passive investors, but founding crew members who will shape how the platform serves their industry. Each Platinum Captain owns one of 16 EU industry verticals on the platform, with status, visibility, and direct influence over the product roadmap.

"This is not a generic angel round. Every Captain brings domain knowledge — not just capital. A Captain of Construction understands building sites. A Captain of Maritime knows what happens mid-ocean. A Captain of Chemicals has the Rolodex that opens procurement doors. We are matching investors to industries so every seat accelerates the platform."
— Risto Paarni, Founder & CEO, Lifetime Oy

As a Platinum Industry Captain, you:

  • Own your vertical on the platform — named, acknowledged, permanent
  • Get founding login access to the World Model + Situation Room Reports
  • Shape how DWS IQ 6 serves your industry
  • Receive quarterly briefings and priority routing for design partner introductions
  • Annual 1:1 strategy session with the CEO on your industry's roadmap
  • First right of refusal on follow-on rounds for your vertical
  • Lock in at EUR 3.8M valuation cap with 20% discount — best terms before the second tranche

The first tranche raises EUR 500,000 through 8 Platinum Industry Captain seats at EUR 50,000 SAFE each, with Deputy Captain seats available at EUR 25,000 for rising stars who want to learn the ropes and build toward Platinum. The remaining 8 Platinum seats open in a second tranche at a higher valuation.

Construction is filling now. Seven more first-tranche verticals are open.
