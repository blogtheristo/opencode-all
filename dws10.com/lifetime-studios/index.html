Lifetime Studios

At Lifetime Studios, our team carefully hand-crafts and validates every AI agent candidate for the Lifetime Agent Foundry. Every accepted agent becomes a High Quality Workflow (HQW) and a tracked project inside the DWS IQ product family—managed end-to-end across its full lifetime with Lifetime Scope Signals, and led by Human-in-the-Loop.

The Best of our Agents and Artefacts are accepted into the Lifetime Agent Foundry. They can be open-sourced, licensed, or delivered as part of an enterprise customer rollout—ensuring high enterprise security and quality standards are met:

  • SOC 2 Type II readiness: SOC 2 CI/CD V1 implemented — auditable change control, build/release gates, and automated evidence capture across security, availability, and confidentiality controls.
  • ISO 27001 alignment: ISMS-aligned policies, risk management, and continuous improvement.
  • GDPR + EU data residency: privacy-by-design, data minimization, and EU-hosting options where applicable.
  • NIS2 readiness: implemented governance + risk management controls, centralized logging/monitoring, and an incident response process with runbooks and escalation for reportable events (incl. supplier/supply-chain considerations).
  • EU AI Act compliance by design: documentation, transparency, and risk controls for AI features as applicable.
  • Identity & access security: least-privilege RBAC implemented; strong authentication enforced; and audit logs captured for access and privileged changes.
  • Lifetime Scope Signals: milestone-based agent gating (READY/COMPLETE/BLOCKED) to coordinate work, reduce risk, and keep end-to-end traceability.
  • Monitoring & incident response: centralized logs, alerting, runbooks, and post-incident review.
  • Vulnerability management: SAST + dependency scanning, remediation SLAs, and patch discipline.
  • Secure SDLC: threat modeling, code review, change control, and release discipline.
  • Data protection: encryption in transit and at rest; key management practices.
  • Backup & recovery: backup strategy with recovery testing (RPO/RTO targets defined per environment).
  • Vendor & supply-chain risk: dependency governance, secrets hygiene, and third-party risk review for critical providers.
  • Enterprise delivery: documentation, rollout playbooks, and handover support for customer security teams.
