What is CSRD?

The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) is the EU law requiring companies to report on environmental, social, and governance (ESG) impacts using the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS). It replaces the previous NFRD with far more detailed requirements.

Expanded Scope

From ~11,700 (NFRD) to ~50,000 companies. If you have 250+ employees or are EU-listed, you're likely in scope.

82 ESRS Disclosures

Detailed reporting across Environment (E1-E5), Social (S1-S4), and Governance (G1) standards. Double materiality assessment required.

Mandatory Audit

Sustainability reports must be independently audited — limited assurance initially, moving to reasonable assurance by 2028.

Digital Tagging (XBRL)

Reports must be digitally tagged in XHTML/iXBRL format for machine-readable access by regulators and investors.
