Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)

1) What is Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)?

The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) is a new EU law that requires companies to report on their environmental, social, and governance (ESG) impacts. It aims to make sustainability reporting more consistent and comparable, ensuring that investors and other stakeholders have access to reliable information about companies' sustainability performance.

Key aspects of CSRD include:

  • Expanded Scope: It significantly broadens the number of companies required to report compared to the previous Non-Financial Reporting Directive (NFRD).
  • Detailed Reporting: Companies must report according to European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS), which are much more detailed than previous requirements.
  • Audited Information: Sustainability information must be audited, similar to financial reporting, to ensure its accuracy and reliability.
  • Digital Tagging: Reports must be digitally tagged, making them easier to access and analyze.
