Why Optimus Needs a Compliance Bridge

Tesla sells EVs in Europe under hardware type approval. Optimus is different — it is a physical AI system. That lands it under the EU AI Act, Machinery Regulation 2023/1230, GDPR, and NIS2 simultaneously. Without a sovereign compliance wrapper, Optimus waits in the hallway for legal clearance.

Regulation The Friction DWS IQ Resolution
EU AI Act — Annex III High Risk Optimus in industrial settings = high-risk AI. Requires notified body conformity assessment. DWS IQ provides Article 12 logging, reasoning lineage, human override — the full conformity layer.
CLOUD Act Tesla/xAI are US entities. US authorities can demand any data they store, including EU facility data. Grok inference runs 100% locally on GN-100 NVIDIA cluster. xAI never sees customer data.
Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 Optimus as "machinery" needs CE marking — 12–18 months through EU notified body. DWS IQ provides technical file, EU-language documentation, and 27-country compliance support.
GDPR Article 22 Robot cameras + sensors = biometric/operational data processing by a US entity. Human Captain always has final override. DWS IQ enforces this architecturally with audit trail.
NIS2 Directive Energy, transport, manufacturing = critical infrastructure. US-cloud AI is legally unacceptable. Air-gapped GN-100 cluster. Zero US cloud dependency for any operational function.
EU AI Act Article 12 Every Optimus AI decision must be automatically logged and auditable. DWS sovereignty audit logging layer captures all AI events on-prem. Logs never leave the private fabric.
