The Circular-Economy Regulatory Frame

Waste & recycling operators sit at the intersection of the EU Waste Framework Directive, the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) for biomass-derived streams, and CSRD ESRS E5, whose first-wave disclosures on resource use and circular economy began in 2025 (covering FY2024 large-undertaking reports). DWS IQ 6 maps tonnage, diversion rates, and material-recovery telemetry to these obligations as auditable, deterministic outputs.
