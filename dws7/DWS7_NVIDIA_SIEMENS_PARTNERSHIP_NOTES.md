# DWS7 - NVIDIA & Siemens Partnership Notes
## CES 2026 Industrial AI Operating System Announcement

**Date:** CES 2026
**Event:** Siemens Keynote
**Speakers:** Jensen Huang (NVIDIA CEO) & Roland Busch (Siemens President/CEO)
**Topic:** Expanded Partnership for Industrial AI Operating System
**Reference:** Siemens keynote video (YouTube, ~9-13 minute mark)

---

## Executive Summary

At CES 2026, NVIDIA and Siemens announced an expanded partnership to build an "Industrial AI Operating System." This collaboration integrates NVIDIA's accelerated computing, AI platforms (Omniverse, CUDA-X), and models with Siemens' industrial software, digital twins, automation, and domain expertise—redefining manufacturing, design, production, and supply chains through physical/industrial AI.

The partnership positions AI as the new "operating system" for industry, comparable in impact to the introduction of electricity. It aims to enable Industry 5.0-level integration of people, machines, and intelligence.

## Key Concepts & Terminology

**Industrial AI Operating System:** Comprehensive AI infrastructure that acts as the foundational layer for all industrial operations, analogous to how operating systems underpin computing.

**Physical AI:** AI systems that interact with and influence the physical world, not just digital information.

**Active Intelligence:** Digital twins that don't just simulate but actively predict, adapt, and influence physical systems.

**Industry 5.0:** Next evolution of industrial production integrating human creativity, AI intelligence, and robotic precision.

**Industrial Metaverse:** Converged digital-physical environment where industrial operations are simulated, monitored, and optimized.
