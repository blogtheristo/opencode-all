Sovereign Partners (Tier 2)

DWS IQ runs a 3-tier model on purpose: Aegis (Tier 1), Sovereign (Tier 2), and Connect (Tier 3). This is how we serve defence-grade, regulated-enterprise, and global ecosystem needs without forcing one security posture for every customer.

Tier 1: Aegis Partners Tier 2: Sovereign Partners Tier 3: Connect Partners

Why we have a 3-tier model

  • Risk segmentation: each tier has clear data, infra, and compliance boundaries.
  • Procurement fit: buyers can start in Connect, scale to Sovereign, then move to Aegis when needed.
  • Faster sales cycles: one partner model supports startups, enterprises, and defence customers.
  • No dead ends: capability and assurance increase tier by tier without rebuilding integrations.

Why Shufti should support all three tiers

  • One integration, three revenue lanes: Connect volume, Sovereign enterprise contracts, Aegis high-assurance programs.
  • Step-up assurance journey: KYC/KYB and identity controls can be tiered by customer risk profile.
  • Compliance continuity: a single identity trust layer helps audits and onboarding across all tiers.
  • Lower partner ops cost: same provider, same control plane, fewer exceptions in delivery.
