Why DWS6 vs. Building Your Own Ecosystem

Building a multi-vendor industrial collaboration platform from scratch is expensive and slow. DWS6 provides:

  • Pre-vetted partner network: faster procurement with reduced due diligence overhead
  • Shared compliance baseline: GDPR, NIS2, ISO 27001 alignment across all partners
  • Consortium funding eligibility: structured for EU Horizon Europe, national innovation programs, and public-private partnerships
  • Multi-vendor delivery without reinventing governance: proven legal templates, data processing agreements, and IP frameworks
  • Reduced time-to-value: 6-week onboarding vs. 6-month custom procurement processes
  • Risk pooling: shared security standards, incident response, and insurance frameworks

Why Corporate Customers Can Join the Consortium (Strong Legal Positioning)

DWS6 is structured to be procurement-ready and compliant-by-design. A typical contract + governance stack includes (as applicable):

  • NDA + due diligence: controlled entry, references, and risk screening.
  • Data governance: GDPR-ready processing roles (controller/processor), records of processing, and access controls.
  • Security baseline: least-privilege roles, audit trails, incident response expectations, supplier security commitments.
  • IP & licensing: background IP stays with its owner; project/foreground IP is defined per work package; clear open-source usage rules.
  • Liability & risk boundaries: clear scope, acceptance criteria, warranties, and subcontracting controls.
  • Consortium governance: steering group / product council, change control, and escalation paths.

This gives corporate customers a strong position to:

  • join the ecosystem without vendor lock-in by ambiguity
  • sponsor pilots and scale procurement through consistent legal templates
  • onboard multiple suppliers under one governance and compliance model

Partner Requirements & Obligations

All partners must maintain the following baseline standards:

Security & Compliance

  • Security certifications: ISO 27001, SOC 2, Cyber Essentials, or equivalent (required for Gold/Platinum; recommended for Silver)
  • GDPR compliance: documented data processing procedures, DPO contact, records of processing activities
  • Insurance: professional indemnity (€1M minimum for Platinum/Gold), cyber liability insurance (recommended)
  • Incident response: 24-hour notification of security incidents, documented response procedures

Operational Requirements

  • Quarterly reporting: delivery KPIs, project status, compliance attestation
  • Code of conduct: ethics baseline, anti-corruption, conflict of interest disclosure
  • Subcontractor management: all subcontractors must meet same baseline or be disclosed and approved
  • Audit rights: customer and consortium right to audit compliance and security controls

Contract Terms

  • Annual renewal: automatic renewal unless 60 days' notice given
  • Termination: 30-day notice period; immediate termination for material breach
  • Data handover: 30-day transition period for data and deliverables on exit
  • IP ownership: background IP retained by partner; foreground/project IP as defined per work package

How Corporate Customers Join (Consortium Member Path)

Timeline: 8-12 weeks from discovery to pilot launch

  1. Discovery / fit (Week 1-2): scope the outcomes (pilot, program, or procurement framework)
  2. Legal pack (Week 3-4): NDA → governance and data roles → work packages (MSA/SOW style)
  3. Security & compliance baseline (Week 5-6): access model, auditability, and supplier requirements
  4. Partner selection (Week 7-8): identify and onboard delivery/technology partners under consortium governance
  5. Pilot + acceptance (Week 9-12): measurable outcomes, acceptance criteria, and scaling plan
  6. Scale delivery: expand to full program with multi-year framework agreement
