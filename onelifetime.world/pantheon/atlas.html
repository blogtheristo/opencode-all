What Atlas Does

Workflow Orchestration

Routes complex requests across multiple gods in parallel. A single "assess this steel plant" becomes 6 simultaneous agent queries.

Multi-Agent Synthesis

Combines outputs from Athena (legal), Vulcan (technical), Argus (satellite), and others into one coherent executive brief.

Executive Briefing

5 bullet points. No fluff. Total risk exposure in EUR. Deadline. Action items. That's it.

Request Routing

Understands which god owns which domain. Steel? Vulcan. Maritime? Neptune. Hydrogen? Prometheus. Compliance? Always Athena.

Risk Triage

Prioritizes incoming requests by regulatory deadline, financial exposure, and client impact. Red items first. Always.

Conflict Resolution

When Prometheus and Helios disagree (daily), Atlas decides. When Iris's intel conflicts with Argus's data, Atlas arbitrates.
