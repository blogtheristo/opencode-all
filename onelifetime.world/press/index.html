March 26, 2026 · Helsinki, Finland

Lifetime Oy launches DWS IQ Aegis for regulated and dual-use operations

HELSINKI — Lifetime Oy announced DWS IQ Aegis, an EU-sovereign AI platform for defence organisations, dual-use manufacturers, and other regulated operators. The release combines AI governance, compliance logging, sovereignty monitoring, and SAR-enabled operational intelligence in one deployment model.

"European defence and regulated operators need AI systems they can deploy under their own governance rules. Aegis was built to give them auditability, sovereignty controls, and operational visibility in one platform."

— Risto Anton Paarni, Founder & CEO, Lifetime Oy

DWS IQ Aegis brings together six specialised AI agents under the KYA (Know Your Agent) framework, covering 25 EU-regulated industries. The platform includes SAR-enabled monitoring, sovereignty controls, and Article 12 logging for compliance-sensitive workflows.

Aegis is available for deployment on European sovereign cloud and selected enterprise private cloud environments. It is positioned for organisations operating in the Finnish defence and deep-tech ecosystem and for other regulated operators requiring auditable AI deployment.
