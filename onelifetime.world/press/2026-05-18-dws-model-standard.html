Lifetime Oy publishes DWS Model Standard v1.0 — a binding rule for which AI models DWS runs, where, and under which constraints

Published in English, Finnish, Swedish, German and French. Issued by Lifetime Oy (FI07724079, Espoo, Finland). Contact: risto@onelifetime.world.

EN — English #en

HELSINKI, 18 May 2026 — Lifetime Oy today published DWS Model Standard v1.0, a binding internal standard that turns LLM selection at DWS into a (Jurisdiction × Room × Sensitivity) lookup. The standard approves nine models across five tiers — Frontier API, Sovereign Reasoning, In-House Brain, Edge Multimodal and Partner Laptop — and codifies three rules:

  • Sensitivity dominates jurisdiction.
  • NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Nano Omni is the universal vision pick.
  • The in-house Lifetime World Model V1 replaces commercial reasoning models for the Control Room after V1 GA on 12 June 2026.
EU-sovereign defence workloads must declare no-cloud-fallback by design. The standard is internal-only and does not commit DWS to a public vendor list. Boring is the goal.
