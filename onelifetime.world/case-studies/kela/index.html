Kela — a dispatch operator for Lifetime Fleet, not a software customer

Kela, Finland's Social Insurance Institution, reaches Lifetime Fleet vehicles as a dispatch operator for reimbursed passenger transport. It is a commercial counterparty of Lifetime Oy's licensed transport business — not a customer of DWS IQ, DWS IQ Aegis, or any Lifetime software product.

Correction, 29 July 2026. An earlier version of this page presented Kela as a DWS IQ software pilot with CSRD reporting outcomes. That framing was wrong and has been retired. There is no DWS IQ pilot with Kela. The relationship is a transport-dispatch relationship, described accurately below. Canonical source: docs/TRACTION_CANONICAL.md.

What the relationship actually is

Lifetime Oy operates a licensed passenger-transport business (Lifetime Fleet). Ride orders reach its vehicles through dispatch operators — Kela, Uber, Bolt and Taksi Helsinki — acting in the dispatch-service (välityspalvelu) role defined by Finnish transport regulator Traficom.

Dispatch operators charge commission on completed jobs and also buy transport capacity. Money flows from the dispatch operator to Lifetime Oy for transport performed, not from Lifetime Oy's software to a paying software customer. Kela does not license, subscribe to, or pilot DWS IQ.

Scope of the transport relationship

  • Role: Dispatch operator (Traficom-defined dispatch service).
  • Service: Kela-reimbursed passenger transport, including accessible and special-needs journeys.
  • Operator: Lifetime Fleet — Lifetime Oy's licensed passenger-transport business.
  • Geography: Helsinki metropolitan area, Finland.
  • Duration: The founder has driven Kela-reimbursed transports for roughly 17 years.
  • Languages: Finnish, Swedish, English.

Where the revenue sits

Lifetime Oy's transport operation and a small consulting practice together run at approximately €7,000/month. That puts the company at cash break-even with a founder salary of €0, and it is what funds product development. It is not platform or software revenue and must not be presented as such.

The software pipeline is separate and is stated separately: two enterprise pilots were contracted in Q1/2026 at €29,950 each (€59,900 booked). Neither is Kela.

What's verifiable today

  • The relationship. Kela is a dispatch operator for Lifetime Fleet's licensed passenger-transport business. It is not a DWS IQ customer, pilot, or reference.
  • The platform. DWS IQ 6 is in production and accessible at dws10.com, with skilled-agent architecture, EU-sovereign hosting, and CSRD-aligned output structure. DWS IQ Aegis is in preorder, with general availability in September 2026.
  • The sovereignty posture. EU data residency, no US transfer, no shared model training — auditable in sovereign-partners.
Why the transport operation matters anyway. Running a regulated transport business is where the platform's fleet, emissions and compliance telemetry comes from — real vehicles, real journeys, real reporting obligations. That is a genuine advantage. Presenting the dispatch operators behind it as software customers was not, and that claim has been retired.
