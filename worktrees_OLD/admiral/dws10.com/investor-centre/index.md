# DWS IQ: Sovereign AI for Regulated European Industry

**Lifetime Oy is opening a focused EU/EEA investor structure around DWS IQ. One Admiral anchors the round. Eight Captains bring industrial capital, domain authority and access to customers. The model is designed around a simple principle: the people financing industrial AI should understand how industrial AI gets bought, deployed and accepted.**

## The Admiral

The Admiral anchors the round and provides:
- Anchor capital and institutional confidence
- Strategic judgement on financing, governance and growth
- Experience with enterprise software, industrial markets or regulated technology
- Help shaping the company's capital and commercial command structure
- A serious view of the transition from product capability to repeatable industrial revenue

The Admiral is not promised a board appointment, return or liquidity. Governance and information rights remain subject to final investment documents.

## The Captains

The Captains are industry-rooted investors. Each Captain should bring capital together with a credible connection to a defined industrial motion, such as:
- Access to industrial buyers, plants and decision-makers (subject to verification of relationships)
- Domain knowledge in chemicals, defence, metals, construction, water, energy or critical infrastructure
- Understanding of long-cycle procurement and deployment
- Regulatory, security, compliance or operational judgement
- Distribution, partnership or customer-introduction capability
- Practical insight into what evidence an industrial buyer can approve and purchase

The Captain role is not a decorative title for a passive angel. It is intended for investors who understand how industrial software gets bought, deployed, operated and accepted.

## Round Architecture

Target: €650,000 SAFE
Valuation cap: €3.8M
Investor scope: EU/EEA-domiciled investors
Structure: 1 Admiral and 8 Captain seats
Indicative Admiral allocation: €250,000
Indicative Captain allocation: 8 × €50,000 = €400,000

This fills the €650,000 target exactly. A ninth Captain seat may be held as a strategic reserve or used only for oversubscription if the round size and documents are updated. It is not a ninth full €50,000 allocation in the current structure.

## Investor Access

Initial Level 1 access presents the Admiral and Captain investor one-pagers, the DWS IQ and Firehorse product families, the commercial thesis, the round architecture and the proposed investor roles.

Further due-diligence materials are released through the authorised DWS IQ investor environment after access review. The process may include mutual due diligence, the investor's portfolio presentation, investment thesis, check size, EU/EEA status and value-add profile, together with NDA and DPA processes where applicable.

Noux may serve as the guided front door and navigation layer. The authorised DWS IQ environment remains the source for access control and protected company materials.

## What We Are Looking For

We are looking for one Admiral and a focused group of Captains who can help Lifetime build an industrial company, not merely complete a financing transaction.

The right investor asks: Which industrial problem is solved first? What evidence does the buyer need before approving a pilot? Which deployment and procurement barriers must be removed? What customer access or domain judgement can I contribute?

The objective is straightforward: build a company that customers can understand, test, buy, deploy and expand.
