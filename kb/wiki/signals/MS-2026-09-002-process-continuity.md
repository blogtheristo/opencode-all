# Applied learning (world model 1.2.0)

**Old assumption.** AI competence is a sustainable sales argument.

**New learning.** Durable competitive advantage is process continuity, governance and retained
organizational learning. The model is a swappable component; the world model is not. What
changes with the model is _how an insight is found_, not _what the insight means_
(Construction → Waste Reduction → Competitiveness is a business relation, not a model relation).

**Evidence.** HIC observation 24.09.2026 (Boardy shortcuts in a long-horizon whole, "result
only and out"); prior occurrence a824bc0 (advisor switch when the whole grew); Boardy's own
corroboration 24.09.2026 06:11 ("the vendor changes, the world model doesn't"; "the governance
layer is the product").

**How agents must use this.**

- Content (lifetime.fi, Journal, dws10.com KYA/Coaching): sell continuity, evidence, traceability,
  knowledge retention, governance. Never a model name as the advantage.
- Lead and tender scoring: a buyer's knowledge-continuity pain is a qualifying dimension.
- Workflows: every retry re-enters its gate; no "result only" path.
- **Excluded contexts:** Investor Centre and Legal Hub do not read this learning (HIC decision).

**Open refuting test.** Model-independence pressure test: same input, different model
(Claude → DGX Spark Ollama) through Chapter 14 CAPTURE→INSIGHT must yield the same registry row
and Application Record. Until run, the claim is not shown to a buyer as proven.
