# Applied learning (world model 1.4.0)

**Old assumption.** The customer needs more information. Agentic Commerce = AI chat.

**New learning.** The customer needs less uncertainty. Agentic Commerce = need recognition and
uncertainty removal regardless of interface. The active ontology is a reasoning layer; chat is at
most one of its interfaces, and people dislike chat unless it is excellent. Default interface is a
choice path that tells the visitor what each answer rules out ("I recognise five possible causes,
two are likely; answer this one question and we rule out three").

**How agents must use this.** Measure decision confidence gained, not content consumed. Blog
series explain; they are not the fix for a buying obstacle. Never open with "tell me about your
problem"; open with "which best describes your situation?".
