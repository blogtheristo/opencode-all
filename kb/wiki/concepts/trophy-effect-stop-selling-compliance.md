# The Trophy Effect

**Version:** 1.0 · **Status:** Active (internal) · **Scope:** DWS first-touch GTM
**Full treatment:** `docs/plans/2026-09-21-signed-work-gtm-pivot.md` · field note `docs/field-notes/2026-09-21-trophy-effect-stop-selling-compliance.md`

## The mechanic

A funded task force must declare victory. Copying a six-to-eight-month-ahead evangelist fills the announcement. Payday continues. **AI × compliance** becomes a _status good_ (the trophy the budget already bought), not a _workflow good_ (the factory that ships the artifact that unfreezes money).

You cannot sell a status good to the holder of the trophy. You sell the workflow good to whoever still has a tender, shipment, invoice, or grant frozen.

## What this means for DWS

- First-touch never leads with the word _compliance_.
- Track A (evangelist) stays public and will be copied. Do not invoice it.
- Track B (engineer) is DWS IQ emitting signed, KYA-gated, Article-12-logged artifacts. That is the SKU.
- Public-sector Door #1 after a trophy is the programme owner who must show replicability (VTV 10/2026 gap), not the task force that just announced.

## Fence

The HIC anecdote (named agency, euro figure, "they copy my work") stays internal. Public copy uses the mechanic, not the accusation.
