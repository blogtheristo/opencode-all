## 📱 The Product

### What We Built
A complete enterprise-grade mobile application for Nordic construction management with:

- **6 Full Screens** - Home, Diary, Inspection, Carbon Report, Dashboard, Settings
- **Nordic Design** - Swedish Blue, Norwegian Blue, SAP-style interface
- **70% Automation** - Auto-fill from edge nodes, AI defect detection
- **Fit for 55 Compliance** - Real-time EN 15978 carbon tracking
