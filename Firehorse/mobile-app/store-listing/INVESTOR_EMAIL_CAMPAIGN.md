## 📝 Email Best Practices

### Subject Lines
✅ **Good**:
- "Firehorse Mobile is LIVE on Google Play"
- "500+ downloads in 3 days"
- "[Named prospect] wants to pilot" (only once genuinely true)
- "Round closing Friday"

❌ **Bad**:
- "Investment opportunity"
- "Check out our app"
- "Meeting request"
- "Following up"

### Body Copy
✅ **Do**:
- Start with the news (product launch)
- Show traction (numbers!)
- Make it scannable (bullets, emojis)
- Include clear CTA (calendar link)
- Add P.S. with urgency

❌ **Don't**:
- Long paragraphs
- Jargon or buzzwords
- Multiple asks
- Attachments (use links)
- Generic templates

### Timing
✅ **Best times**:
- Monday 9-10 AM (fresh week)
- Tuesday 10-11 AM (settled in)
- Thursday 2-3 PM (before weekend)

❌ **Avoid**:
- Friday afternoon (weekend mode)
- Monday afternoon (catching up)
- Lunch hours (12-1 PM)
