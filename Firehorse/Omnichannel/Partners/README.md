# Partnership Development & Collaboration

## Overview

This directory manages partnership outreach, collaboration opportunities, and strategic alliance campaigns.

## Partnership Types

### 1. Technology Partners
- **Focus**: Integration partners, API partnerships
- **Goal**: Expand product capabilities
- **Examples**: SaaS tools, platforms, infrastructure

### 2. Channel Partners
- **Focus**: Resellers, affiliates, referral partners
- **Goal**: Expand market reach
- **Examples**: Agencies, consultants, distributors

### 3. Strategic Alliances
- **Focus**: Co-marketing, co-selling
- **Goal**: Mutual growth opportunities
- **Examples**: Complementary products, industry leaders

### 4. Content Partners
- **Focus**: Guest posts, webinars, podcasts
- **Goal**: Brand awareness and thought leadership
- **Examples**: Media, influencers, industry publications

## Partnership Stages

1. **Identification**: Research potential partners
2. **Outreach**: Initial contact and introduction
3. **Qualification**: Assess fit and opportunity
4. **Negotiation**: Terms and agreement
5. **Onboarding**: Integration and setup
6. **Activation**: Launch partnership
7. **Management**: Ongoing relationship

## Outreach Strategy

### Initial Contact Template

```
Subject: Partnership opportunity - [Your Company] × [Their Company]

Hi [Name],

I've been following [Their Company]'s work in [specific area] and see strong synergy with what we're building at [Your Company].

[Brief value proposition for partnership]

Would you be open to exploring a potential partnership? I'd love to share some ideas on how we could collaborate.

Best,
[Your Name]
```
