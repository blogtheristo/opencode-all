## The Opportunity

Outokumpu's stainless steel carries a verified, TÜV-validated carbon footprint up to 75% lower
than the industry average. Since 1 January 2026 that difference has a price — the CBAM carbon
certificate. A fabricator importing Outokumpu steel instead of non-EU steel can eliminate or
drastically reduce their CBAM liability. But most downstream customers do not have the tools to
calculate, document, and submit that advantage in a compliant CBAM annual declaration.

The gap is not in Outokumpu's product. It is in the tooling layer between your ISO 14067
certificate and your customer's compliance filing.

## The Partnership Model

**Zero cost to Outokumpu. No exclusivity required.**

### What Outokumpu commits to (Phase 1 — Pilot)

- Nominate 2–3 fabricator accounts willing to participate in a 60-day pilot
- Provide PCF certificate data for the grades those accounts purchase
- Introduce DWS to the nominated account contacts

### What Lifetime Oy commits to (Phase 1 — Pilot)

- Onboard nominated fabricator accounts at no charge
- Deliver a complete, submission-ready CBAM annual declaration for each account using
  Outokumpu PCF data as the verified source
- Demonstrate the cost differential vs. equivalent non-EU steel import in CFO-readable format
- Report pilot results to Outokumpu within 60 days

## Why This Strengthens Outokumpu's Commercial Position

1. **PCF certificate becomes a sales tool.** Today it is a compliance document. With DWS IQ 6,
   a fabricator's procurement team can generate a live cost comparison — Outokumpu steel vs.
   non-EU alternative — that shows the CBAM-adjusted price. The premium pays for itself.

2. **Supports the EVOLVE customer collaboration pillar.** The CBAM tooling layer is a concrete,
   measurable form of customer collaboration — not a brochure claim.

3. **Amplifies your CBAM advocacy.** You have argued publicly for stricter enforcement and broader
   scope. Every fabricator who can quantify the CBAM advantage reinforces that argument with
   real purchase data.

4. **Positions Outokumpu ahead of scope expansion.** If CBAM expands to downstream steel-intensive
   goods by 2027 as you have lobbied for, the fabricator network already using DWS IQ 6 will be
   ahead of compliance — and they will have purchased from Outokumpu to get there.
