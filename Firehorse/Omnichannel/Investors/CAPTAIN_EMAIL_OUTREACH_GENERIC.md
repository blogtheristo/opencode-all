## Follow-Up Sequence

### Day 3 — Nudge (if no reply)

**Subject:** `Re: [original subject]`

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Floating this back up — I know inboxes are brutal.
>
> Happy to do a quick 15-minute call instead of a full demo if that works better for your schedule.
>
> — Risto

**Rule:** One follow-up only. If no reply after Day 3, stop. Do not send a third email. Move to next contact.

### Day 7 — LinkedIn DM (cross-channel, if not already connected)

Switch to LinkedIn DM using `CAPTAIN_LINKEDIN_DM_GENERIC.md` templates. Reference the email:

> Hi [First Name] — I sent you an email earlier this week about [REGULATION] automation. Thought LinkedIn might be easier. Happy to share a quick demo link if you're curious. — Risto

## Personalization Checklist

Before sending ANY email, verify:

| # | Check | Where to Find |
|---|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Their exact role and industry | LinkedIn headline + About |
| 2 | Company sustainability stance | Annual report, ESG page |
| 3 | Something specific they said/did | Recent LinkedIn post, conference, press |
| 4 | Correct regulation for their industry | Use the keyword map above |
| 5 | Company size (determines CSRD urgency) | >250 employees = CSRD mandatory 2025 |

**Rule:** If you can't find a specific personalization hook, don't send. Generic emails get <3% reply rates. Personalized emails get 10–20%.
