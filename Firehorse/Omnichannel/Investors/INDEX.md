```
1. Find Investor (Dealroom, Crunchbase, LinkedIn)
   ↓
2. Qualify (Use DWS6_INVESTOR_PROFILES.md checklist)
   ↓
3. Score Priority (1-10 scale)
   ↓
4. Research Portfolio (Find 2-3 relevant companies)
   ↓
5. Find Contact Info (LinkedIn, website, Crunchbase)
   ↓
6. Add to Supabase (Complete profile)
   ↓
7. Personalize Email (Use angles from guides)
   ↓
8. Send via Automation (Daily campaign)
   ↓
9. Track Engagement (Opens, clicks, replies)
   ↓
10. Follow Up (7-day, 14-day sequences)
```

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Quality over Quantity**: 50 perfect-fit contacts > 200 mediocre ones
2. **Personalize Everything**: Reference specific portfolio companies
3. **Prioritize Ruthlessly**: Focus on Priority 8-10 first
4. **Research Thoroughly**: 15 min per investor = better response rates
5. **Track Everything**: Use Supabase to log all interactions
6. **Follow Up Systematically**: 7-day and 14-day sequences
7. **Learn & Iterate**: Optimize based on response data
