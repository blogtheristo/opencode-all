## Personalization Guide

The teaser works only if it's personalized. Before sending, check:

| Check | Where to look |
|-------|--------------|
| Their role in construction | LinkedIn headline, About section |
| Their company's sustainability stance | Annual report, ESG page, LinkedIn posts |
| A specific thing they said/did | Recent LinkedIn post, conference talk, press mention |
| The right regulation to reference | CSRD for large firms, EPBD for building energy, EN 15978 for carbon LCA |

**Examples of personalization openers:**

- "I saw NCC's Climate 2030 roadmap — impressive targets. How are you tracking EN 15978 across projects?"
- "Your post about concrete waste reduction caught my eye. We're automating exactly that with SiteSense Agent."
- "PORR's EcoVadis Gold rating stands out. Curious how much of that reporting is still manual."
- "Skanska's net-zero 2045 target is ambitious. Is EPBD compliance automated yet or still consultant-driven?"
- "I read about STRABAG's 400+ digital initiatives. Does that include Fit for 55 automation?"
