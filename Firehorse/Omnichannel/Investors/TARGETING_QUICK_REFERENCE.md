## ✅ Must-Have Criteria (Disqualify if Missing)

- ✅ Invests at **seed/seed-extension** stage (not pre-seed only, not Series A+)
- ✅ Check size: **€15k–€50k** (angels) or **€100k–€500k** (funds)
- ✅ Geographic focus includes **EU** (Nordics/DACH/Benelux/UK)
- ✅ Comfortable with an early software base of **€59,900 in contracted Q1/2026 pilots** (not requiring €50k+ MRR)

---

## 🎯 Strong Fit Indicators (Prioritize)

- 🎯 Portfolio includes: construction tech, infra SaaS, climate reporting
- 🎯 Thesis mentions: ESG, CSRD, regulatory compliance, "mandatory spend"
- 🎯 Understanding of industrial/B2B sales cycles
- 🎯 Experience with EU regulations and compliance markets
- 🎯 Network in construction, infrastructure, or ESG consulting

---

## ❌ Red Flags (Avoid)

- ❌ Consumer/B2C focused
- ❌ Only invests in US/Asia markets
- ❌ Requires €50k+ MRR for seed stage
- ❌ No portfolio overlap with climate/construction/ESG
- ❌ Pre-seed only (too early) or Series A+ only (too late)
