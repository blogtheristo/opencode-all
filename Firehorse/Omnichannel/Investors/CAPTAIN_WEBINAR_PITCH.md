## Structure (60 min total)

### Section 1 — The Cost of Non-Compliance (10 min)

**Presenter:** Risto Anton Päärni (or co-host from advisory firm)

**Content:**
- CBAM Phase 2 timeline: reporting → financial obligations in 2026
- CSRD double materiality: what the first wave of reports revealed
- Real penalty numbers from DWS IQ 6 calculator data:
  - ETS non-compliance: EUR 100/tonne penalty + surrender obligation (currently ~EUR 65/tonne = 54% premium)
  - CBAM: full import duty on unregulated scope 1 emissions (cement, steel, aluminium, fertilizer, hydrogen, electricity)
  - CSRD: national penalties varying EUR 10k-10M depending on member state + investor litigation risk
- Case study: what a mid-size steel importer faces in 2026 without automation

**Key slide:** "The Compliance Cost Curve" — shows manual reporting cost scaling non-linearly with regulation count (one regulation = manageable, 5+ = breaking point)

**Transition:** "So what are the companies that are actually ahead doing differently?"

### Section 2 — How Leading Firms Automate Compliance Intelligence (15 min)

**Presenter:** Risto Anton Päärni

**Content — LIVE DEMO of DWS IQ 6:**
1. **Situation Room** — show a real industry dashboard (anonymized pilot data)
   - ETS position tracker: allowances vs. actual emissions
   - CBAM exposure by product/supplier origin
   - CSRD double materiality matrix
2. **CLI + /compliance-check** — run a live compliance check from terminal
   ```
   dws agents
   dws run compliance --input '{"reg":"CSRD","industry":"steel"}'
   ```
   Show the JSON output, explain what each field means
3. **Morning Briefing** — show the 06:00 CEO Cockpit email (real example, anonymized)
   - "This is what arrives at 6 AM every day. No login required. No dashboard to check."
4. **Cost comparison:** EUR 15-50/month infrastructure vs. EUR 150-500k/year consultant + manual reporting

**Key message:** "The platform runs on less than your team's monthly Slack bill. The intelligence it produces replaces a 3-person compliance team's quarterly output."

**Transition:** "Now, who builds this for your specific industry?"

### Section 3 — The Captain Model (10 min)

**Presenter:** Risto Anton Päärni

**Content:**
- Not a generic angel round — a seat at the table
- 8 Platinum seats, one per industry vertical
- What a Captain gets:
  - Founding login to the World Model (your industry's compliance brain)
  - Quarterly Captain briefings with live data review
  - 1:1 strategy sessions with the CEO
  - Direct input on product roadmap for your vertical
  - Pro-rata rights in subsequent rounds
- SAFE terms: EUR 50k, EUR 3.8M cap, 18% discount, MFN
- Why the structure works: "You're not buying equity in a pitch deck. You're buying a seat in a live platform with EUR 7k+ MRR and 100% retention."

**Key slide:** The seat map (8 industries, which are filled, which are open)

**Tone:** This is the pitch section, but keep it conversational, not salesy. "I'm not asking you to invest today. I'm asking you to consider whether your industry deserves a Captain."

### Section 4 — Q&A (15 min)

**Moderated by:** Co-host or Risto

**Prepared seed questions (if Q&A is slow):**
1. "How does DWS IQ 6 handle industry-specific regulations that vary by member state?"
2. "What's the integration path with existing ERP systems like SAP?"
3. "How do you ensure data sovereignty under GDPR for EU clients?"
4. "What happens after the Captain programme fills — what's the next raise?"

**Rule:** Never dodge a question. If you don't know, say "I'll follow up with the exact data." Credibility > polish.

## Co-Hosting Strategy

**The key is co-hosting. A solo webinar gets 20 attendees. A co-hosted one with an association or advisory firm gets 200.**

### Tier 1 — Industry Associations (highest leverage)

| Association | Focus | Audience Size | Approach |
|-------------|-------|--------------|----------|
| CEFIC | European Chemical Industry | 29,000 member companies | "We automated their compliance — let's show how" |
| EUROFER | European Steel Association | Key steel producers | CBAM is existential for them |
| CEMBUREAU | European Cement Association | Cement producers | ETS Phase 4 is their top issue |
| ECSA / ICS | European/International shipping | Maritime operators | FuelEU + ETS for shipping |
| EPBD Alliance | Building energy performance | Real estate / construction | EPBD recast deadlines approaching |
| BusinessEurope | Cross-sector EU industry | 40M+ companies | Broadest reach |
| Finnish-German Chamber of Commerce | DWS home market | SMEs + mid-caps | Local credibility |

**Pitch to association:** "We'll provide the technical content and live demo. You bring the audience and moderate. Your members get actionable compliance intelligence. No cost to you."

### Tier 2 — Advisory Firms (credibility multiplier)

| Firm | Why | Approach |
|------|-----|----------|
| Big 4 (EY, PwC, Deloitte, KPMG) sustainability practice | They advise CSRD clients | "Let's co-host on automation vs. consulting — we complement, not compete" |
| Law firms with ESG/compliance practice | Regulatory expertise | "You explain the law, we show the automation" |
| Boutique sustainability consultancies | Niche industry expertise | "Your domain knowledge + our platform = webinar gold" |

**Pitch to advisory:** "Your clients are drowning in manual reporting. We'll show them the automation layer. You keep the advisory relationship. We get platform awareness."
