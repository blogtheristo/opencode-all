# Customer Acquisition & Engagement

## Overview

This directory contains strategies and workflows for customer acquisition, onboarding, and engagement campaigns.

## Campaign Types

### 1. Customer Acquisition
- **Target**: New potential customers
- **Goal**: Convert leads to trial/demo
- **Channels**: Email, social, content marketing

### 2. Product Launch
- **Target**: Existing customers + prospects
- **Goal**: Announce new features/products
- **Channels**: Email, in-app, blog

### 3. Onboarding
- **Target**: New customers
- **Goal**: Successful product adoption
- **Channels**: Email sequences, in-app guides

### 4. Engagement
- **Target**: Active customers
- **Goal**: Increase usage and retention
- **Channels**: Email, push notifications, webinars

### 5. Re-engagement
- **Target**: Inactive customers
- **Goal**: Win back churned users
- **Channels**: Email, special offers

## Customer Segments

1. **Prospects**: Not yet customers
2. **Trial Users**: Testing the product
3. **New Customers**: First 90 days
4. **Active Users**: Regular engagement
5. **Power Users**: Heavy usage
6. **At-Risk**: Declining engagement
7. **Churned**: Former customers

## Campaign Structure

```
Customers/
├── acquisition/          # New customer campaigns
├── onboarding/          # Welcome and setup sequences
├── engagement/          # Active user campaigns
├── retention/           # Churn prevention
├── reactivation/        # Win-back campaigns
└── templates/           # Email and content templates
```

## Key Metrics

- **Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Cost per new customer
- **Conversion Rate**: Lead → Customer
- **Activation Rate**: Trial → Paid
- **Engagement Score**: Product usage metrics
- **Retention Rate**: Monthly/annual retention
- **Churn Rate**: Customer loss rate
- **Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Revenue per customer
