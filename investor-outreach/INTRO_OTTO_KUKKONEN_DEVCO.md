2. **He thinks in value-at-stake economics.** The McKinsey piece he shared is industry-by-industry value-at-stake — the same register DWS should pitch in (cost of non-compliance, labor saved), not "climate software."
3. **Consolidation thesis rhymes.** DevCo consolidates operational excellence into niche leaders; DWS consolidates fragmented EU compliance into one ledger. Natural opener.
