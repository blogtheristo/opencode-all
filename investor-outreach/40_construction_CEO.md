# European Construction Decision-Makers Contact Database

This research identified **40 key executives** across 5 major European construction companies for B2B outreach targeting ESG, NIS2 compliance, and digital transformation. All contacts hold strategic, compliance, risk, or operational leadership roles with direct relevance to regulatory compliance and sustainability initiatives.

---

## Executive summary by role type

The database includes **8 CEOs/C-suite**, **5 CFOs**, **4 CDOs/CTOs**, **7 ESG/Sustainability leaders**, **5 Compliance officers**, **3 Risk/Legal executives**, and **8 operational leaders**. The strongest NIS2/CSRD targets are ESG leads (Lena Hök at Skanska, Fanny Gruboeck at PORR, Katharina Aspalter at STRABAG) and digital transformation leaders (Jens Hoffmann at STRABAG, Martina Steffen at HOCHTIEF). All five companies are publicly listed and subject to both NIS2 and CSRD requirements.
