# Investor Intro Note — Tesi (Finnish Industry Investment Ltd)

## Status: COLD — Land First, Cascade

Tesi is the cohort's warmest entry: a state-owned, market-driven investor with a real early-stage mandate and a stated Finnish dual-use focus. Not a captain-seat buyer (the Captain tier has zero traction and is being redirected to client captains anyway) — but a credible holder of the round's single **Admiral (Fleet Admiral) seat: €250K base, scalable €250K–€650K, board-observer rights + first right of refusal on all follow-on rounds**, and an ecosystem-introduction partner. The play is to offer Tesi the Admiral seat (a sovereign EU/EEA lead, thesis-perfect for the cap-table sovereignty rule) and let it cascade into the Finnish dual-use network (Lifeline, FiBAN DEFSEC, DEFINE Finland).

**Admiral seat fit (prime target).** Tesi is the recommended target for the round's one Admiral seat: €250K is plausible for its early-stage/strategic mandate, board-observer rights fit a state co-investor, the dual-use remit aligns, and a Finnish/EU-domiciled Admiral satisfies the memo's EU/EEA lead-investor sovereignty requirement. The seat **scales to €650K**, so if Tesi wants more conviction exposure it can solo-anchor the entire round — a cleaner instrument for a state investor than a fragmented multi-seat structure. The ROFR on follow-ons reframes the check as a strategic option on the next ICEYE-pattern company, not a seed bet. Nokia / NGP Capital is the alternate for the same seat (see `INTRO_NOKIA_NGP.md`); it is one seat, so Tesi gets first offer.

## Fit Assessment

1. **Real early-stage mandate** — Unlike the pension funds and Solidium in this cohort, Tesi can and does invest directly at early stage. The €650K SAFE is below its typical direct ticket, but the relationship and a follow-on at the next priced round are the realistic prize.
2. **Explicit Finnish dual-use remit** — Tesi is actively seeking Finnish defence / dual-use companies and co-organised DEFSEC 2025. DWS IQ's EU sovereign compliance infrastructure is squarely in-thesis.
3. **ICEYE pattern owner** — Tesi backed ICEYE across rounds into the €1B/€10B raise. The narrative "you backed the sovereign-intelligence layer; here is the sovereign-compliance layer, three rounds earlier" lands with someone who lived the ICEYE compounding.
4. **EU-sovereign, no CLOUD Act exposure** — Helsinki-domiciled state investor; aligns with our sovereign-ownership posture.
5. **Cascade engine** — One Tesi conversation opens Lifeline, FiBAN DEFSEC, DEFINE Finland, and NATO Innovation Fund adjacencies — where €50K–€150K co-investment into our SAFE is achievable.

## Drawbacks / Mitigations

- **Ticket-floor mismatch** — Tesi's direct tickets are typically well above €650K. *Mitigation:* lead with relationship + watchlist for the next priced round; ask for ecosystem intros now, not a check.
- **State investor process is slow** — IC cycles, co-investor requirements. *Mitigation:* treat as a multi-month relationship; use V1 launch (Arctic 15, Jun 15) and first German sales data (Aug 15) as natural check-in milestones.
- **Co-investor expectation** — Tesi prefers to co-invest, not lead seed. *Mitigation:* line up Lifeline / FiBAN DEFSEC angels first; bring Tesi in as a co-investor signal.
