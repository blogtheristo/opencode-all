# LIFETIME OY · INVESTOR DECK V6 · 2026 Q3

**Classification: CONFIDENTIAL — DRAFT FOR HIC APPROVAL**

---

## 1 · THESIS

Lifetime is building DWS IQ, a governed system of action for regulated European industry. It turns industrial workflows into controlled execution, signed evidence and reviewable outputs.

The company is testing two routes to revenue. Enterprise engagements are the current commercial focus. Nordic industrial SMEs are a second, unvalidated entry scenario.

---

## 2 · THE COMMERCIAL QUESTION

**Which route produces the first repeatable paid customer motion?**

- **Scenario A:** enterprise-led deployment through a narrow, production-relevant workflow.
- **Scenario B:** SME entry through DWS IQ Essentials, followed by expansion to Pro and Enterprise.

Both scenarios use the same evidence architecture, control plane and contract model. Neither is presented as validated until a paid customer produces measurable evidence.

---

## 3 · SCENARIO A · ENTERPRISE WEDGE

**Target:** regulated industrial companies with a defined operational, compliance or evidence problem.

Current commercial conversations include Outokumpu and Kemira. These are enterprise prospects and examples of customer-specific discovery, not booked revenue or validated repeatability.

**Entry motion:**

- one business owner
- one site, process or workflow
- one defined data boundary
- one measurable output
- one paid pilot decision

The product is adapted to the customer's problem. Partner products and infrastructure appear only when they solve a stated customer requirement.

---

## 4 · OUTOKUMPU EXAMPLE

**Potential wedge:** signed, machine-readable emissions evidence for downstream customer and CBAM workflows.

The hypothesis is that Outokumpu-sourced data could be delivered as an evidence packet that procurement, filing and audit systems can consume directly. The initial test is whether this replaces or improves an existing customer evidence package.

**Validation required:**

- customer-owned workflow confirmed
- data source and verification authority confirmed
- measurable time, cost or acceptance benefit
- paid pilot scope and buyer identified

---

## 5 · KEMIRA EXAMPLE

**Potential wedge:** chemical process compliance recording using a customer-specific demo and controlled data upload.

The demonstration should use a defined scenario, such as process compliance recording, with a bounded CSV or approved sample dataset. The output must show the evidence produced, who reviews it and what a paid pilot would prove.

**Validation required:**

- real process and pain point confirmed
- data boundary approved
- output reviewed by the responsible operator or compliance owner
- pilot benefit and acceptance criteria agreed

---

## 6 · SCENARIO B · SME ENTRY HYPOTHESIS

**Target scenario:** Nordic industrial SME with multiple services or sites, a small IT team and a concrete need for safe updates, operational inventory or evidence.

DWS IQ Essentials is planned as the low-friction entry tier. The target customer profile, budget, decision cycle and Q4 timing are hypotheses, not traction.

**Essentials would include:**

- inventory and service visibility
- evidence and audit export
- safe updates, health checks and rollback
- CLI and managed deployment
- GitHub and one operational runtime connector

---

## 7 · SME LADDER HYPOTHESIS

**Essentials → Pro → Enterprise.**

The test is whether a customer first buys operational risk reduction, then asks for governance, analytics or broader deployment capability.

**Validation metrics:**

- more than 50% of beta customers deploy within two weeks
- more than 80% connect both launch connectors
- more than 30% buy Pro within 90 days
- upgrade reason is governance or analytics, not missing core functionality

**These are planned tests, not current performance.**

---

## 8 · WHAT IS PROVEN TODAY

- Lifetime has industrial software and compliance experience.
- DWS IQ is being structured around governed execution and evidence.
- Outokumpu and Kemira provide concrete enterprise discovery contexts.
- The current commercial work is narrowing toward customer-specific workflows and paid pilots.

**Not claimed:** validated SME demand, Essentials revenue, repeatable enterprise conversion, retention, churn or software revenue beyond the canonical register.

---

## 9 · BUSINESS MODEL

**Enterprise:** paid discovery or pilot, followed by production deployment and expansion.

**SME scenario:** Essentials entry, modular Pro expansion and eventual Enterprise path.

**Revenue sources remain separate:**

- software and governance
- deployment and integration
- recurring support

The product architecture is shared. Commercial validation is separate by scenario.

---

## 10 · EXECUTION PLAN

1. Convert one enterprise conversation into a paid, bounded pilot.
2. Produce a measurable customer-owned outcome for Outokumpu or Kemira.
3. Complete the Essentials launch gate before selling it.
4. Run the first SME beta only after deployment, rollback, evidence export, isolation and support controls pass.
5. Compare both motions by time to paid commitment, delivery effort, referenceability and expansion signal.

---
