## Slide 1 — The Thesis

> **Price AI like labor, not like a tool.**

**DWS 365 Skilled Agents** — and its sibling **Firehorse 365 Skilled Agents** — sit in the **human
budget**, not the SaaS budget. 16 Skilled Agents running on the **DWS IQ** platform replace 6–8 EU FTE
at a fraction of fully loaded cost, anchored to human cost rather than inference cost, so gross margin
holds as foundation models commoditize.

**The spine in one line:** labor-anchored pricing · regulation drives demand · MACC + AppExchange drive
distribution.

**Positioning:** software-led, priced like labor, with software margins. The product does the work and
delivers the signed artifact; a thin service layer just gets the customer live.

**The ask:** €650k SAFE (oversubscription to €1.0M) · €3.8M post-money cap · 20% discount · 24-month
maturity.

## Slide 2 — Why the Lens Change Is the Whole Argument

| Dimension   | Tool lens (SaaS, copilot)     | Labor lens (DWS 365 Skilled Agents)                  |
| ----------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Buyer       | IT / line-of-business admin   | Department head with a headcount budget              |
| Unit        | Seat, edition, feature flag   | Task completed, case closed, report delivered        |
| Anchor      | Other software                | Loaded labor cost / consultancy day rate / BPO       |
| Pricing     | €X/user/month                 | €X/case, €X/report, €X/agent-month at SLA            |
| Value       | "X% faster for your team"     | "Replaces N FTEs at Y% of fully loaded cost"         |
| Procurement | Software budget               | Services / operations budget                         |
| Margin      | Compressed by seat saturation | Anchored to human cost — expands as model costs fall |

When the unit of value is **the work itself**, the price ceiling is set by what that work costs today
(human + overhead), not by what comparable software costs. **SaaS multiples ceiling out where labor
multiples start.**

## Slide 3 — The Problem: Mandatory Work, Not Optional Software

Five regulations each mandate **a piece of work** that must be produced, signed, and defended:

| Regulation     | What it forces                                                                                                    | When                                           |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **CSRD**       | Assured sustainability reporting, ~50k EU undertakings in the first wave                                          | 2026/27; second wave 2028                      |
| **CBAM**       | Reporting + financial liability on importers of cement, iron, steel, aluminium, fertiliser, electricity, hydrogen | Transition closes 2026                         |
| **EU AI Act**  | Conformity assessment + post-market monitoring for high-risk systems                                              | In force from Aug 2026                         |
| **NIS2**       | Sovereignty and audit trail for essential / important entities                                                    | Operational across Member States since Q4 2025 |
| **Fit for 55** | Material-emissions cap-and-trade pressure                                                                         | From 2026                                      |

A copilot produces a draft. A regulator does not accept a draft. **The buyer's problem is that the work
has to be done, by someone accountable, on a deadline that does not move.**

## Slide 4 — The Product: 16 Skilled Agents, Themis-Coordinated, KYA-Governed

**Platform:** DWS IQ — Brain (fast inference) · Memory (governed Lifetime World Model) · Body (edge:
7–15W, offline-capable) · Hands (MCP agents that produce the artifact).

- **Themis** is the case coordinator — routes work across the 16 agents, assigns sub-agents, escalates
  to human-on-loop exactly where the regulation requires a human.
- **KYA (Know Your Agent)** is the runtime trust layer — every action bound to a verified human identity
  at trust class TC-0…TC-4, with TC-4 Leash Snap chain termination for anomalous chains.

Together they produce a deliverable that is **audit-trailed, signed by a human, and explainable** —
precisely what CSRD assurance, CBAM declarations, EU AI Act conformity and NIS2 incident reports
require. **A copilot produces a draft; DWS produces the signable.**

6 months. No per-seat cannibalization — we sell the role, not access.

## Slide 9 — The 4P Capital Classes: Why the Re-Rate Holds

DWS is not a SaaS vendor; it is a **System of Action operator**. Four durable, non-purchasable capital
classes accumulate — each with a specific valuation effect.

| Capital class  | What it is                                                                                            | Valuation lever                                                                            |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Prompt**     | Codified intelligence — 30+ skills, 50-agent foundry, eval datasets                                   | Moves the comp from SaaS (~4× forward ACV) to vertical labor-AI (~6×)                      |
| **People**     | Seven months of HIL-calibrated operator judgment, documented                                          | Lowers the execution / key-person discount                                                 |
| **Plant**      | Sovereign mobile two-room stack (Control Room + Situation Room), EU-resident, Article-12 audit-logged | Regulatory moat + ACV durability                                                           |
| **Perception** | Governed world model, closed sense→decide→act loop                                                    | The only class with data-network effects and physical-AI character — **the re-rate lever** |

**In one line:** Prompt and People move the comp set off the SaaS curve onto the labor curve; Plant makes
the revenue durable enough to underwrite that multiple; **Perception justifies the top of the range** — a
competitor can buy edge hardware, but cannot buy a year of governed, lineage-logged observations.

The same four classes are how an acquirer should score any agentic operator. The acute risk in a
shrinking agentic company is **People and Perception walking out the door together** — calibrated
judgment and the world model that judgment built leave as a pair, and neither is on the balance sheet.
DWS scores on all four _because_ it endured the human-in-the-loop phase instead of skipping it.
