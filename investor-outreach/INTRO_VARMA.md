1. **Thesis clears the bar at scale** — Varma's ICEYE participation shows sovereign / dual-use deeptech is allocatable within its mandate once de-risked. DWS IQ is the same family, earlier.
2. **Concrete fund route exists** — Varma is a Lifeline LP. Lifeline is a seed-stage investor on DWS IQ's actual wavelength and a DEFSEC co-organiser. Landing Lifeline gives Varma indirect exposure now and a relationship for later.

- **Institutional process weight** — Long committee cycles. *Mitigation:* lead with auditability + EU sovereignty to shorten institutional comfort; treat as long game.
