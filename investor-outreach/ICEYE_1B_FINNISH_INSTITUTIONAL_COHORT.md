## The Admiral seat — the right lever for this cohort

The €50K Captain seat is below the noise floor for a €60B pension fund — and as of 2026-06-09 the Captain tier has **zero investor interest**, so it should not be put in front of this cohort at all. The Captain tier is being repositioned toward **client captains** (customers who take a named industry position), not investors. (Round economics updated 2026-06-09 — `INVESTOR_MEMO.md` still shows the older numbers and needs syncing; see note at end.)

The right lever is the **Admiral (Fleet Admiral) seat — €250K base, scalable €250K–€650K, 1 seat, full board-observer rights + first right of refusal on all follow-on rounds**. Two properties make it ideal for an institution:

1. **It can anchor the whole round.** An Admiral may take €250K or scale up to the full **€650K** — i.e. a single institution can *solo-anchor the entire round*. For a Tesi or Nokia that is far cleaner than co-sitting in a fragmented multi-seat structure.
2. **It is a strategic option, not a financial position.** Even at €250K the check **options a board-observer foothold and pro-rata into the next ICEYE-pattern company's priced rounds** — the one framing under which an institution can justify a sub-mandate ticket.

The memo also **requires the Admiral (lead) to be EU/EEA-domiciled** — a cap-table sovereignty rule. All five targets are Finnish and pass cleanly; a sovereign Admiral (Tesi or Solidium) is thesis-perfect for the Aegis narrative.

But it is **one seat**, and it does not move everyone:

Keep the Captain tier (zero traction) off the table for this cohort; redirect it to **client captains**.
