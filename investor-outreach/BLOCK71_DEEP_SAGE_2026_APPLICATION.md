## 4. The Problem

EU industrial companies face three simultaneous compliance deadlines with no unified infrastructure:

- **CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)** — fully operational from 2026. Steel, cement, aluminium, fertiliser, hydrogen, and electricity importers must declare embedded emissions per shipment. Default emission factors inflate costs by 20–40% versus actual. Manual process: 8–12 hours per declaration. Failure means customs hold.
- **CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive)** — Wave 1 in 2026, Wave 2 in 2027. Double-materiality assessments, Scope 1/2/3 emissions, taxonomy alignment. Average enterprise cost via consultants: €180,000–€350,000 per year.
- **ETS Phase IV (Emissions Trading System)** — tightening caps through 2030. Real-time surrender position tracking is required to avoid over-surrender or under-compliance penalties.

The same dynamic is now arriving in Asia. **Singapore's Carbon Tax rises from S$25 to S$45/tCO₂e in 2026 and S$50–80 by 2030.** Japan's GX-ETS goes mandatory in 2026. Korea K-ETS Phase 4 begins. China's national ETS is expanding from power generation into steel, cement, and aluminium. No platform connects these obligations to the underlying transactions that generate them — in Europe or in Asia.

---

## 5. The Solution: DWS IQ — the Carbon Balance Sheet

DWS IQ is a multi-agent AI platform that converts every industrial transaction into a compliance journal entry — automatically, in the same second the payment clears.

**How it works:**

1. A shipment, allowance trade, or supplier invoice is paid (SEPA, Qivalis, Singapore FAST, future CBDC — rail-agnostic).
2. DWS IQ reads the transaction and embedded emissions data via MCP connectors to SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, or the customer ERP.
3. Three journal entries are written in the same second:
   - CBAM obligation updated (tCO₂e + EUR liability).
   - CSRD Scope 1/2/3 emission booked (debit/credit ledger).
   - ETS account reconciled (surrender position updated).
4. Audit trail is written to a tamper-evident Article 12 log (7-year retention, EU AI Act compliant).

The platform runs on **NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano** (€500/device, 15W) at the customer's edge — no cloud egress, no CLOUD Act exposure, sub-100ms inference. The same hardware footprint serves a Hamburg steel plant and a Jurong Island refinery.

**Architecture (Firehorse Brain–Memory–Body–Hands):**
- **Brain:** DWS IQ orchestration layer, MCP-native.
- **Memory:** Regulation RAG (CBAM, CSRD, ETS, K-ETS, Singapore CT, GX-ETS).
- **Body:** Connectors to SAP, Oracle, MS Dynamics, Workday, customs data feeds.
- **Hands:** 21 production agents (Firehorse Report, Firehorse Flow, MaterialOracle, SiteSense, Compliance, Schedule Genius, EmissionsDataAnalyst, etc.).

## 6. Innovation & Defensibility

Three validated innovations, each with academic citation in the Metropolia master's thesis (2026):

1. **Carbon debit-credit ledger** — first application of Pacioli's double-entry principles (1494) to CO₂ compliance. No competitor (Sweep, Persefoni, Watershed, Normative, Greenly) has this architecture. They sell reports; we sell the ledger underneath the reports.
2. **6-layer harness engineering** — information architecture that raised AI compliance accuracy from 52.8% to 66.5% without changing the underlying model. Published in master's thesis, Metropolia University, 2026.
3. **Payment-agnostic compliance recording** — compliance obligations recorded at the transaction level, independent of the payment rail. First implementation of a MiCA-aware industrial compliance ledger in Europe; the same architecture extends naturally to MAS-supervised stablecoins and PBOC's e-CNY corridor.

Replicate time: 3–5 years per pillar. The network effect is in the verified emission factors — each new customer's audited emissions improve the CBAM default-factor model for every other customer.
