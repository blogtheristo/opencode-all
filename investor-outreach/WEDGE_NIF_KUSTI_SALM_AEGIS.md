## Why this is the wedge (not just a contact)

Our two NIF-adjacent assets pointed at the same fund from opposite directions — and Kusti Salm is the hinge:

- `CASE_FRANKENBURG_RIGA.md` sells Aegis **into** Frankenburg (sovereign data + agent governance + EU AI Act layer for the Riga/Ādaži FieldFoundry).
- `INTRO_ERIN_HALLOCK_NATO_INNOVATION_FUND.md` pitches Aegis **to** NIF as an investor.

Kusti Salm closes the loop: he is the **buyer** in the first document and a **board director** in the second. That makes the commercial sale and the investment thesis the _same conversation_:

1. **Customer reference = board-level conviction.** Most founders pitch a fund cold and hope a partner champions them. Here, a successful Frankenburg pilot gives a NIF _board member_ direct, first-hand proof — the strongest possible internal advocate.
2. **Procurement-readiness is NIF's stated thesis.** The NIF Dual-Use Forum framed the priority as turning deep tech into _deployable, procurement-ready_ capability. Aegis making a NIF-portfolio-shaped company (Frankenburg) sovereign and EU AI Act-compliant _is_ that thesis executed.
3. **Estonia ties the knot.** Salm is Tallinn-based; Estonia is both a planned Frankenburg FieldFoundry site and the **Lifetime OÜ** leg of the group — a single jurisdiction where customer, fund director, and our own entity overlap.
4. **The warm path already exists.** ~29 mutual connections means the intro to Salm does not have to be cold.
5. **He is a network, not a node.** Salm also chairs the **Milrem Robotics** supervisory board (a second marquee Aegis customer — Europe's leading UGV maker) and is the **former Permanent Secretary of Estonia's Ministry of Defence** and ex-Chairman of **ICDS**. One relationship reaches two dual-use customers, the NIF board, and the top of Estonian defence procurement — i.e. the exact "demand signal + procurement pathway" alignment the Dual-Use Forum said Europe is missing.

## The play (sequence)

1. **Open with Frankenburg, not NIF.** Approach Kusti Salm as Frankenburg's CEO with `CASE_FRANKENBURG_RIGA.md` — the sovereign-manufacturing-layer pitch. Investor framing stays implicit at first.
2. **Run the entry-path step 1** from the case: a sovereignty + EU AI Act gap assessment on the Riga/Ādaži data estate. Small, concrete, credible.
3. **Let the NIF conversation surface naturally.** Once Frankenburg sees value, Salm's NIF board seat makes the investor discussion a warm, informed follow-on — not a separate cold pitch.
4. **Parallel-track Erin Hallock** as the partner-level NIF relationship (`INTRO_ERIN_HALLOCK_NATO_INNOVATION_FUND.md`), now bridged by Salm rather than opened cold.
5. **Use the warm connections** (Keith / Seppo + 27) for the first intro to Salm.

## Risks / honest caveats

- **Governance separation.** A NIF director will (rightly) wall off his Frankenburg buying decision from any NIF investment decision; conflict-of-interest rules apply. _Mitigation:_ treat the commercial and investment tracks as independent — the value is reference credibility and warm access, not a quid pro quo.
- **Roles verified, contact still to confirm.** CEO (Frankenburg), NIF board, Milrem chair, ex-MoD Permanent Secretary all confirmed from his LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/kusti-salm-644159b2). _Mitigation:_ confirm best contact channel and current email before send.
- **Frankenburg must want it first.** The wedge only works if the commercial pilot has real pull. The case study is illustrative, not a signed engagement.
