# DWS Vision

## Three Visions. One Platform.

### Vision I — Climate Intelligence

Make EU climate compliance as automatic as payroll.

Every regulated company — in construction, logistics, manufacturing, energy, healthcare, public sector — deserves a single agent that knows the regulation, reads the ERP, calculates the exposure, and files the report. Not once. Every quarter. Without a consultant in the room.

The regulation is the product roadmap. CSRD, ETS, CBAM, Taxonomy, Fit for 55, EPBD — each directive is a new workflow the agent already knows. Each new rule is not a threat to the business model. It is an expansion of the addressable market.

---

### Vision II — Sovereign Intelligence

Sovereignty is not a feature. It is a segmentation strategy.

We offer three tiers — and each one opens a different buyer segment:

**Full Sovereignty (Aegis)** — Single-tenant, EU-only, zero CLOUD Act exposure.
For regulated enterprises where data cannot leave Finnish or German soil.
This is where defense, critical infrastructure, and government-adjacent customers operate.

**Sovereign Infra** — EU-hosted, multi-tenant, managed infrastructure.
For mid-market companies that need EU residency without a dedicated instance.

**Connect** — Global cloud, managed compliance layer on top.
For international customers in ASEAN and US who need DWS IQ without full EU sovereignty.

Every tier runs KYA — so every agent is auditable, leashable, and traceable regardless of where it runs.
The tier determines the infrastructure. The conscience layer is non-negotiable everywhere.
