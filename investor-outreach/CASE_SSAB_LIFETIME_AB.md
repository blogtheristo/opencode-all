# Case Study (Illustrative Target) — Lifetime AB business written in SSAB case

> **Framing note:** This is an _illustrative target case_. It maps the Lifetime AB business model onto SSAB to show fit and value — it does **not** claim an existing contract, pilot, or engagement with SSAB. Use as a worked example in investor conversations.

## 1. Why SSAB

SSAB is the largest Nordic steel producer, with Swedish operations (Oxelösund, Borlänge) inside the Stockholm/Mälardalen logistics corridor where Lifetime AB launches in autumn 2026 (Kungens Kurva, Huddinge). Steel carries the heaviest regulatory carbon load in the EU: **ETS Phase 4, CBAM, and CSRD** apply simultaneously, and SSAB's fossil-free transition (HYBRIT-style direct-reduction) makes verifiable, auditable carbon data a board-level priority — not a reporting afterthought.

That combination — a major buyer in our launch geography, with acute ETS/CBAM/CSRD exposure and a decarbonisation mandate — is exactly the customer the Lifetime AB business is built to serve.

## 2. What Lifetime AB sells into SSAB

| Lifetime AB pillar                     | SSAB value                                                                                                                                                  | Buyer                            |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **DWS IQ — climate intelligence**      | Measures and verifies CO₂ across inbound/outbound logistics and yard movements; produces court-defensible ETS/CBAM/CSRD audit trails under the KYA Standard | Sustainability / Compliance, CFO |
| **Lifetime Fleet — operator platform** | Zero-emission, optimised last-mile and site logistics around the Stockholm corridor; dynamic routing and remote fleet management                            | Logistics / Plant operations     |
| **Autonomous robotics (RaaS)**         | Robot-assisted yard and last-mile movement; Edge AI (NVIDIA Jetson / Isaac) for object recognition and routing in plant environments                        | Operations / Automation          |
| **Sovereign Data**                     | Fully closed, Nordic-hosted data environment for sensitive supply-chain and emissions data — no US/Asian cloud exposure                                     | CISO, Procurement                |

## 3. The Dual Use angle (for defence / dual-use investors)

SSAB is not only a steel producer — it is a **critical-materials supplier** whose supply chain matters to national resilience and defence procurement (the same thesis Olof Faxander / Gungnir Capital operates on). The capability Lifetime AB builds for SSAB — decentralised fleet management in contested network conditions, Edge AI sensor fusion, and a sovereign data environment — transfers directly to security-of-supply and defence logistics. **A commercial SSAB engagement de-risks and finances the Dual Use moat.**

## 4. Business model & economics

- **DWS IQ:** B2B SaaS subscription, priced to the loaded cost of the compliance/sustainability work it replaces (per-outcome or per-agent-month), 80%+ gross margin.
- **Lifetime Fleet + RaaS:** monthly B2B operator licence + per-route service fees; predictable recurring revenue.
- **Foundation funds the moat:** the cash-flow-positive mobility business carries operating cost; investor capital goes 100% to AI development, NVIDIA integrations, and Dual Use market entry.

## 5. Entry path

1. **Carbon-data pilot:** DWS IQ instruments one logistics lane into/out of a Stockholm-corridor SSAB site; deliver a verified ETS/CBAM/CSRD audit trail.
2. **Zero-emission logistics:** Lifetime Fleet operates that lane with nollutsläpp vehicles; DWS IQ quantifies the saving vs. baseline.
3. **Scale + RaaS:** extend to yard automation (robotics) and additional lanes; expand the sovereign-data footprint.
4. **Dual Use crossover:** package the resilient fleet + sovereign-data capability for security-of-supply / defence logistics (Försvarsmakten, Försörjningsberedskapscentralen / Huoltovarmuuskeskus).

## 6. Related target — Outokumpu

The same playbook applies to **Outokumpu**, the global leader in stainless steel, headquartered in Helsinki with its main works at **Tornio** (the largest integrated stainless mill in Europe). Outokumpu mirrors the SSAB profile from the **Lifetime Oy (Finland)** side of the group:

- **Same regulatory load:** ETS Phase 4, CBAM, and CSRD apply to stainless production; Outokumpu already markets low-carbon stainless (Circle Green), so verifiable, auditable carbon data is a live commercial differentiator — exactly what DWS IQ produces.
- **Same critical-materials / Dual Use thesis:** stainless and the ferrochrome supply chain (Kemi mine) are security-of-supply assets, reinforcing the same defence/resilience crossover argument.
- **Geographic fit:** anchors the Finnish leg (Lifetime Oy, Uusimaa + national Traficom operator licence) the way SSAB anchors the Swedish leg (Lifetime AB, Stockholm corridor + Transportstyrelsen).

Together, **SSAB (SE) + Outokumpu (FI)** give the pitch a two-country, two-producer anchor that matches the Lifetime Group structure — one heavy-industry decarbonisation + sovereign-data case per jurisdiction. Both remain illustrative target cases, not claimed engagements.
