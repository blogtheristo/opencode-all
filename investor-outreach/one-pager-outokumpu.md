# DWS IQ 6: Outokumpu Pilot as Proof of Concept

## Executive Summary

DWS IQ 6 addresses the mandatory EU compliance regulations (CSRD, Fit for 55, EPBD) that create unavoidable purchase decisions for industrial companies. The Outokumpu case demonstrates a concrete €120k per 1,000 tonnes saving for fabricators switching from non-EU defaults to EU-verified steel, establishing measurable business impact.

## 1. Regulatory Need Drives Demand

- **CSRD, Fit for 55, EPBD** create mandatory compliance obligations
- **CBAM** requires verified carbon data for imports
- **EU regulations** force companies to purchase compliance solutions or face penalties
- Markets respond to necessity, not preference

## 2. Outokumpu Case: Financial Impact

**The Core Value Proposition:**
- Fabricators switching from non-EU defaults to Outokumpu steel save up to €120k per 1,000 tonnes
- This represents the direct financial impact that creates buying decisions
- Current EU default factor = 1.852 tCO₂e/t
- Outokumpu verified factor = 1.6 tCO₂e/t (lower than industry average)
- CBAM certificate cost reduction: €120,380 vs. €32,500–€58,500

## 3. Execution Capability: Aegis/Themis

- **Aegis**: Sovereign AI infrastructure (EU-hosted, GDPR/CSRD compliant)
- **Themis**: Regulatory intelligence and compliance verification
- Platform reliably carries regulated work to completion with audit trails  
- DWS IQ 6 proves execution capability for compliance workflows

## 4. Deployment Management: DWS Capacity Class + KYA

- **DWS Capacity Class**: Scalable deployment architecture  
- **KYA (Knowledge, Yields, and Actions)**: Governance framework for regulated deployment
- These tools make deployment manageable, consistent, and audit-ready

## 5. Investment Use: Milestone-Focused

**€2M Investment Allocations:**
- 40%: Engineering - Platform development, MCP agent library, construction vertical agents
- 30%: Sales & GTM - Pilot conversion, healthcare expansion  
- 20%: Infrastructure - Private cloud, GDPR/SOC2 compliance
- 10%: Compliance & Legal - Regulatory tracking, contract protection

## Key Point

This represents one deep workflow that can be repeated across verticals - not just a broad architecture. The Outokumpu pilot demonstrates:
- Financial impact that drives real business decisions  
- Technical capability to execute compliance workflows
- Replicable model that scales to healthcare, construction, manufacturing, etc.

The platform doesn't just provide intelligence - it builds the System of Action operator for regulated European industry.
