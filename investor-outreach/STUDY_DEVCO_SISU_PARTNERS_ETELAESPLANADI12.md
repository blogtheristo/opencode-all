| **Portfolio** | **Vexve** (mission-critical valves for energy-infrastructure / district heating), **Medix Biochemica** (diagnostics raw materials), **Bluefors** (cryogenics for quantum computing) |

### Why DevCo is a partner, not an investor

DevCo buys *control* of mature industrials with eight- and nine-figure revenues. DWS at this stage is invisible to that mandate — there is no instrument by which DevCo's fund invests €50K of growth equity into a pre-seed SAFE. So drop the "investor" framing entirely for DevCo and pursue the **partnership** angle, which is genuinely strong:

1. **Portfolio-as-customer channel.** Vexve, Medix Biochemica and Bluefors are exactly the EU industrials carrying the compliance load DWS automates — **CBAM** (Vexve sells into energy infrastructure and exports across borders), **CSRD** sustainability reporting, **EU AI Act** conformity, embodied-carbon / EN 15978 reporting on the manufacturing side. One warm intro from DevCo's portfolio-operations team can put DWS in front of three serious industrial buyers who sign €25K+ contracts. That is the Messe-grade customer the funnel is built for.
2. **Active-owner thesis rhymes with ours.** DevCo's pitch is "consolidate operational excellence into niche leaders." DWS's pitch is "consolidate fragmented EU regulatory compliance into one ledger." Same consolidation logic, different axis — a natural conversation opener that respects how they see the world.
3. **Network gravity.** Matti Alahuhta's chairmanship signals a deep Finnish industrial board network. A reference customer inside the DevCo family is worth more than the SAFE cheque we'd never get from the fund.
4. **Sovereign / Nordic-built fit.** Nordic-headquartered, no US-cloud dependency — aligns with the kind of operational sovereignty industrial owners increasingly demand.

### Drawbacks / reality check

- **Stage gulf.** Do not pitch the SAFE. Pitch a **pilot inside one portfolio company** (Vexve is the sharpest CBAM/energy-infra fit).
- **Procurement, not romance.** Portfolio CFOs buy on a hard compliance-deadline ROI, not founder vision. Lead with the signed artifact (EN 15978 LCA report, CBAM gap analysis), not the platform story.
- **Long cycles.** PE-portfolio sales cycles are slow and committee-driven. Treat this as a Q3–Q4 2026 cultivation, not a June close.

### Recommended approach (DevCo)

- **Channel:** intro to a DevCo **portfolio-operations / value-creation** lead, or directly to **Vexve** sustainability/compliance owner — *not* the fund's investment partners.
- **Ask:** a 30-minute scoping call on a single CBAM/CSRD pilot at Vexve.
- **Lead with:** the signed compliance artifact + a one-line ROI vs. consultancy day-rate. Suppress the SAFE entirely.
- **Do not send:** the investor one-pager. Send the customer/value-prop material instead.

| **Recent deals** | Silmäsairaala Valo → Silmäasema (May 2026); Finnish Minerals Group → majority of IONCOR (Sep 2025); Software Sauna (2025) |

### Why Sisu is a route, not a direct investor

Sisu **advises** on transactions and **raises** capital for clients — it does not deploy its own fund into a startup SAFE. So "Sisu Partners as investor" is really three distinct, valuable relationships:

1. **Capital-raising adviser / placement route.** Sisu runs equity and debt capital-advisory mandates. They are a conduit to Finnish institutional and family-office money — the kind of follow-on capital DWS will want for the €500K–€1M seed *after* the SAFE, not the SAFE itself.
2. **Nasdaq First North Certified Adviser.** If DWS ever targets a **First North** listing (a realistic Nordic growth-company path), a Certified Adviser is *mandatory*. Sisu is one. Early relationship-building here is a cheap option on a future listing.
3. **Valuation / fairness-opinion credibility.** When the seed or a strategic round needs a defensible valuation, Sisu's name carries weight with Finnish boards and co-investors.
4. **Advior International reach.** Their 14-firm alliance is a cross-border M&A/capital network — relevant when DWS scales into DACH (the Messe thesis) and needs German-side corporate-finance contacts.

### Drawbacks / reality check

- **Too early to engage commercially.** Sisu takes mandates with success fees on deals of meaningful size; a pre-seed at our scale (two contracted enterprise pilots, €59,900 booked) is below their commercial threshold today. Engage as **relationship + future option**, not a paid mandate now.
- **Deal generalist, not a thesis investor.** Their sector list is broad (food/ag to healthcare); don't expect cleantech/vertical-AI conviction. Frame DWS in transaction terms (defensible ARR growth, regulatory-tailwind demand), not vision terms.
- **They work for the client who pays.** A future mandate aligns them with us; a casual intro does not. Calibrate expectations accordingly.

### Recommended approach (Sisu)

- **Channel:** a relationship coffee — "introduce DWS, watch our V1 launch and Messe data, talk again when we run the seed."
- **Ask:** keep us on their radar for the **2026 H2 seed**; flag First North as a medium-term path.
- **Lead with:** traction trajectory (V1 June 15, first German sales data Aug 15, retention), framed as a transaction-grade growth story.
- **Watch for conflation:** "Sisu" appears on several unrelated entities (e.g. Arctic Sisu / Sisu Energy & Environmental in clean-energy press). The brass plate at Eteläesplanadi 12 is **Sisu Partners, corporate finance** — keep the others out of the file.

## 3. The Eteläesplanadi 12 cluster — strategic note

Four plates, one door: **DevCo · Norra Finans · Sisu Partners · Nordic Trustee.** Read together they map the **entire capital lifecycle** of a Nordic growth company — working capital → equity/M&A advisory → ownership → debt-capital-markets administration:

| Plate | What they are | Where they sit in DWS's lifecycle | Relevance now |
|-------|---------------|-----------------------------------|----------------|
| **DevCo Partners** | Active-owner PE, majority stakes in €50–500M niche leaders | **Ownership / scale-up capital** + portfolio-as-customer channel | **High** — partner play via Vexve/Medix/Bluefors compliance needs |
| **Sisu Partners** | Boutique corporate finance (M&A/IPO/capital raising), First North Certified Adviser | **Equity raising / listing** | **Medium** — H2-2026 seed adviser + future First North route |
| **Norra Finans** | Invoice financing / factoring / SME business loans (Swedish-rooted, HQ Haparanda; Helsinki, Stockholm, Luleå, Vaasa) | **Working capital / cash-flow** — unlock receivables | **Low now** — relevant once DWS has invoiced enterprise ARR to factor; bridges cash gaps without dilution |
| **Nordic Trustee** | Leading Nordic **bond trustee & loan-agency** provider (3,000+ assignments, 800+ issuers); part of **Ocorian** since 2021 | **Debt capital markets** — bond/loan administration, security/payment agent | **Future** — only at a bond-issuance scale far beyond pre-seed |

**Takeaway:** one address gives DWS a partner channel (DevCo portfolio), a future equity adviser and listing route (Sisu), a non-dilutive working-capital option once ARR is invoiced (Norra Finans), and the debt-capital-markets layer for much later (Nordic Trustee). None of it fills the current SAFE — but it's the full Finnish private-capital stack in one photo. Map it now; harvest DevCo + Sisu in H2 2026, keep Norra Finans in the back pocket for the first enterprise-invoice cash gap.

### 3.1 Norra Finans — short profile

- **What:** Nordic specialist in **invoice financing / factoring**, invoice purchasing, business loans, and debt collection for SMEs.
- **Footprint:** Swedish-rooted, **HQ Haparanda**; operating since 2015 across Helsinki, Stockholm, Luleå, Vaasa. Group = Norra Finans Sverige AB + Norra Finans Oy (Finland) + Norra Finans Inkasso AB.
- **Relevance to DWS:** **non-dilutive working capital.** Once DWS bills enterprise customers on net-30/60 terms, Norra Finans can advance cash against those receivables — bridging the gap between signing a €25K+ contract and getting paid, without touching the cap table. Not relevant until invoiced enterprise ARR exists; keep as a back-pocket cash-flow tool, not an investor.

### 3.2 Nordic Trustee — short profile

- **What:** The leading Nordic **bond trustee and loan-agency** provider — facility agent, security agent, calculation/payment agent, escrow, plus restructuring & enforcement. 3,000+ active assignments for 800+ issuers across ~30 countries.
- **Structure:** Offices in Copenhagen, Frankfurt, Helsinki, Oslo, Stockholm. Part of the **Ocorian** group since Oct 2021 (global fiduciary/capital-markets services, ~USD 270bn AUA).
- **Relevance to DWS:** **debt capital markets, much later.** Nordic Trustee administers bonds and syndicated loans — infrastructure a company taps when issuing debt at scale, far beyond a pre-seed. No action now; note it as the eventual debt-stack counterparty if DWS ever raises venture/growth debt or a bond. Informational only.
