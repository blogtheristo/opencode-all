Investor Deck v6 · 2026 Q3

Sovereign Intelligence for Regulated European Industry

September 2026

Thesis

Lifetime is building DWS IQ, a governed system of action for regulated European industry. It turns industrial workflows into controlled execution, signed evidence and reviewable outputs.

The company is testing two routes to revenue. Enterprise engagements are the current commercial focus. Nordic industrial SMEs are a second, unvalidated entry scenario.

The Commercial Question

Which route produces the first repeatable paid customer motion?

Scenario A: Enterprise-led deployment

Through a narrow, production-relevant workflow with regulated industrial companies.

Scenario B: SME entry

Through DWS IQ Essentials, followed by expansion to Pro and Enterprise tiers.

Both scenarios use the same evidence architecture, control plane and contract model. Neither is claimed as validated until a paid customer produces measurable evidence.

Scenario A: Enterprise Wedge

Regulated industrial companies with defined operational or compliance problems

Current commercial conversations include Outokumpu and Kemira — enterprise prospects, not booked revenue.

Entry motion:

  • One business owner
  • One site, process or workflow
  • One defined data boundary
  • One measurable output
  • One paid pilot decision

The product is adapted to the customer's problem. Partners and infrastructure appear only when they solve a stated customer requirement.

Outokumpu Example

Signed, machine-readable emissions evidence for downstream customers and CBAM workflows

The hypothesis is that Outokumpu-sourced data could be delivered as an evidence packet that procurement, filing and audit systems can consume directly.

Validation required:

  • Customer-owned workflow confirmed
  • Data source and verification authority confirmed
  • Measurable time, cost or acceptance benefit
  • Paid pilot scope and buyer identified

Kemira Example

Chemical process compliance recording using customer-specific demo and controlled data upload

The demonstration uses a defined scenario with a bounded dataset. The output must show the evidence produced, who reviews it and what a paid pilot would prove.

Validation required:

  • Real process and pain point confirmed
  • Data boundary approved
  • Output reviewed by the responsible operator or compliance owner
  • Pilot benefit and acceptance criteria agreed

Scenario B: SME Entry GTM

Nordic industrial SME with multiple services, small IT team, concrete evidence needs

DWS IQ Essentials is planned as the low-friction entry tier. Target customer profile, budget, decision cycle and Q4 timing are hypotheses, not traction.

Essentials would include:

  • Inventory and service visibility
  • Evidence and audit export
  • Safe updates, health checks and rollback
  • CLI and managed deployment
  • GitHub and one operational runtime connector

SME Ladder GTM

Essentials → Pro → Enterprise

The test is whether a customer first buys operational risk reduction, then asks for governance, analytics or broader deployment capability.

Validation metrics:

  • >50% of beta customers deploy within two weeks
  • >80% connect both launch connectors
  • >30% buy Pro within 90 days
  • Upgrade reason: governance or analytics, not missing core functionality

These are planned tests, not current performance.

What Is Proven Today

  • Lifetime has industrial software and compliance experience
  • DWS IQ is structured around governed execution and evidence
  • Outokumpu and Kemira provide concrete enterprise discovery contexts
  • Current commercial work is narrowing toward customer-specific workflows and paid pilots

Not claimed:

Validated SME demand, Essentials revenue, repeatable enterprise conversion, retention, churn or software revenue beyond the canonical register.

Business Model

Enterprise: Paid discovery or pilot, followed by production deployment and expansion.

SME scenario: Essentials entry, modular Pro expansion and eventual Enterprise path.

Revenue sources remain separate:

  • Software and governance
  • Deployment and integration
  • Recurring support

The product architecture is shared. Commercial validation is separate by scenario.

Execution Plan

  1. Convert one enterprise conversation into a paid, bounded pilot
  2. Produce a measurable customer-owned outcome for Outokumpu or Kemira
  3. Complete the Essentials launch gate before selling it
  4. Run the first SME beta only after deployment, rollback, evidence export, isolation and support controls pass
  5. Compare both motions by time to paid commitment, delivery effort, referenceability and expansion signal
