## Fit Assessment

1. **Thesis owner, proven** — Solidium did not just back sovereign intelligence; it *led* and board-seated it. Nobody in Finland has more conviction in "Finnish-owned sovereign deeptech as a national asset."
2. **EU-sovereign by charter** — Its entire mandate is Finnish/EU sovereign ownership. DWS IQ's sovereign-compliance-infrastructure thesis is mission-aligned, not adjacent.
3. **Regulatory tailwind understood** — As a steward of Finnish industrials carrying ETS / CBAM / CSRD exposure, Solidium feels the compliance-cost problem DWS IQ solves across its own portfolio.
4. **ICEYE precedent legitimises growth-equity exceptions** — The €40M ICEYE convertible proved Solidium *will* step outside listed strategic stakes for a sovereign deeptech of national importance. That is the door — at the right scale.

## Drawbacks / Mitigations

- **No seed mandate — the core blocker** — Solidium does not invest at €650K SAFE stage. The ICEYE entry was at growth scale. *Mitigation:* do not pitch the SAFE. Build a relationship and a credibility record; reactivate at Series A/B.
- **National-importance bar** — Solidium acts where Finnish ownership of a nationally significant company is at stake. *Mitigation:* the honest path is to *become* nationally significant first (EU sovereign-compliance category leadership), then Solidium is a natural cap-table anchor and acquisition-defence partner.
- **Board-seat / governance weight** — Solidium takes board seats and governance influence. *Mitigation:* frame as a strength for a later, larger round — not a friction for now.
