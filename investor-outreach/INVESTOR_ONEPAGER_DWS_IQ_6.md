# DWS IQ 6: Sovereign Compliance Intelligence Platform

## Executive Summary

DWS IQ 6 addresses the mandatory EU compliance regulations (CSRD, Fit for 55, EPBD) that create unavoidable purchase decisions for industrial companies. The platform enables enterprises to manage compliance workflows with sovereign infrastructure, verified intelligence, and audit-ready execution. The Outokumpu case demonstrates a concrete €120k per 1,000 tonnes saving for fabricators switching from non-EU defaults to EU-verified steel, establishing measurable business impact.

## 1. Regulatory Need That Drives Demand

- **CSRD, Fit for 55, EPBD** create mandatory compliance obligations for EU-regulated industries
- **CBAM** requires verified carbon data for imports, forcing companies to use compliance solutions or face penalties
- **EU AI Act** and other regulatory frameworks demand auditable, trustworthy AI systems
- Markets respond to necessity, not preference
- The regulatory momentum is accelerating with new compliance requirements and penalties coming into force

## 2. Outokumpu Case: Proof Point with €120k/1,000 tonnes Financial Impact

**The Core Value Proposition:**
- Fabricators switching from non-EU defaults to Outokumpu steel save up to €120,000 per 1,000 tonnes
- This represents a direct financial impact that creates buying decisions and validates compliance as revenue driver
- Current EU default factor = 1.852 tCO₂e/t
- Outokumpu verified factor = 1.6 tCO₂e/t (lower than industry average)
- CBAM certificate cost reduction: €120,380 vs. €32,500–€58,500

## 3. Execution Capability: Aegis/Themis Governance Architecture

**Platform Architecture:**
- **Aegis**: Sovereign AI infrastructure (EU-hosted, GDPR/CSRD compliant, CLOUD Act-free)
- **Themis**: Compliance verification and audit trail infrastructure with EU AI Act Article 12 logging
- **KYA Standard**: Identity-bound agent governance with zero-trust segmentation using Firecracker MicroVMs
- Execution is managed through DWS IQ platform's agent swarm orchestration with governed auto-research capabilities

## 4. Deployment Management: DWS Capacity Class + KYA

**Governed Infrastructure:**
- **DWS Capacity Class**: Scalable deployment architecture with four tiers (Core, Production, Enterprise, Sovereign/Scale)  
- **KYA (Know Your Agent)**: Governance framework that defines where, with what isolation, and at what scale agents can run
- Secure Model Execution Boundary defines where and how third-party AI artifacts may run using five component controls
- Enables consistent, audit-ready deployment across all 25 regulated industries

**€2M Investment Allocations:**
