2. Problem – Compliance Burden & Data Challenges in Construction
• Construction companies face 40–80 hours of manual Excel work per project for CSRD/Fit for 55 reporting, with penalty risks of €10,000–50,000 annually for incomplete compliance.
• Project and asset data is fragmented across designers, contractors, supervisors, and systems—no single “source of truth” for energy, emissions, or lifecycle costs.
• Reporting is delayed, costly, and error-prone, delivered via Excel, PDFs, and consultants, and does not guide daily decision-making.
• Changes on site (material swaps, delays, design changes) are logged in different systems or not at all—no real-time view of cost, carbon footprint, or contract penalties.
• Fluctuations in raw material prices and availability, subcontractor risks, and delays are not visible to management for forecasting—decisions are made on gut feeling, without scenarios for the next 6–24 months.
• Clients, financiers, and authorities demand transparent, verifiable data—but sites still rely on fragmented systems, manual checklists, and isolated BI reports, lacking unified climate intelligence.
