## Drawbacks / Mitigations

- **€3M program floor — core blocker for this round** — The €300M program will not touch a €650K SAFE. *Mitigation:* do not apply now; use the relationship to get on OP's radar and time the program for the €3M+ Series A.

- **"Established business" criterion** — The program wants proven growth companies. *Mitigation:* the V1 launch (Jun 15) + first German sales data (Aug 15) + recurring-revenue growth are exactly the evidence that qualifies us later; check in at those milestones.
