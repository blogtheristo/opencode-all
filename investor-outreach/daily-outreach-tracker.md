## Weekly Review Questions

**Every Friday, answer these:**

1. **Did I send 25 emails this week?** Yes / No
   - If no: Why not? What will I change next week?

2. **What was my response rate?** \_\_\_% (responses / emails sent)
   - Target: 15-25%
   - If <15%: Try more personalization or better targeting
   - If >25%: You're crushing it!

3. **How many calls did I schedule?** \_\_\_
   - Target: 1-2 per week
   - If 0: Follow up with people who responded but didn't commit to call

4. **What objections came up?**
   - "Too early" → Share profitability timeline
   - "Not my focus area" → Ask for intro to right angel
   - "Valuation too high" → Explain traction justifies €3.8M cap
   - Other: \_\_\_

5. **Who should I follow up with next week?**
   - Angels who responded but haven't scheduled call
   - Angels who said "not now but check back in a month"
   - Angels who intro'd me to other angels

## When You Get Stuck

### "I ran out of warm intros"

→ Start cold outreach using templates in 01-angel-investor-letter.md
→ Target: Green Angel Syndicate, proptech angels, AI/edge computing angels

### "I'm not getting responses"

→ Check response rate: If <10%, improve targeting or add more personalization
→ If 10-20%, that's normal - just send more volume
→ Try shorter emails (3 sentences max)

### "People are responding but not committing to calls"

→ Make it easier: "Are you free Tuesday 2pm or Wednesday 10am?"
→ Send Calendly link: "Book any time that works: [link]"
→ Reduce time ask: "Just 15 minutes to share pilot results"

### "Calls go well but no commits"

→ Ask directly: "Based on this call, are you interested in joining the round?"
→ Create urgency: "We're closing this portion in 2 weeks"
→ Share momentum: "We have €50K committed already"

### "I missed a day/week"

→ Don't beat yourself up
→ Don't try to "catch up" by sending 25 emails in one day
→ Just restart: Send 5 emails today, 5 tomorrow
→ Consistency > perfection
