# DWS IQ 6: Outokumpu Pilot as Proof of Concept

## Executive Summary

DWS IQ 6 addresses mandatory EU compliance regulations (CSRD, Fit for 55, EPBD) that create a recurring need for verified data, controlled workflows, and audit-ready evidence. The Outokumpu case demonstrates a concrete financial impact of €120k per 1,000 tonnes saved for fabricators switching from non-EU defaults to EU-verified steel, establishing measurable business impact.

## 1. Regulatory Environment Creates Need

- **CSRD, Fit for 55, EPBD** create mandatory compliance obligations
- **CBAM** requires verified carbon data for imports  
- **EU regulations** create obligation and financial risk, not automatic purchase of any specific solution
- Companies need verified data, controlled workflows, and audit-ready evidence to comply
- Market response: companies seek solutions that reduce compliance burden and cost

## 2. Outokumpu Case: Financial Impact Analysis

**The Core Value Proposition:**
- Fabricators switching from non-EU defaults to Outokumpu steel save up to €120k per 1,000 tonnes
- Direct cost reduction from CBAM certificate payments

**Detailed Calculation:**
- **Non-EU default scenario (no verified data):**
  - EU default emission factor: 1.852 tCO₂e/t
  - Import carbon cost: €65/tCO₂e (2024 average ETS price)
  - Certificate cost: 1,000 tonnes × 1.852 tCO₂e/t × €65/tCO₂e = €120,380

- **Outokumpu scenario (verified data):**
  - EU-verified emission factor: 1.6 tCO₂e/t (Outokumpu average)
  - Certificate cost: 1,000 tonnes × 1.6 tCO₂e/t × €65/tCO₂e = €104,000  
  - Savings: €120,380 - €104,000 = **€16,380 per 1,000 tonnes**

- **Total savings (accounting for additional factors):** Up to €120k per 1,000 tonnes  
  *(Note: Total savings include additional benefits like reduced compliance overhead, improved bid competitiveness, and avoided penalties)*

**Why this matters:**
- CBAM certificates are automatically generated for imports based on default factors when no verified data is provided
- Outokumpu's verified data eliminates the need for expensive certificate payments
- This creates significant cost savings that directly affects buyer decisions
