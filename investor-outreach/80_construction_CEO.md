---

## Connection to Morning Briefing (DWS IQ / Firehorse)

**Morning briefing** is the daily site-level ritual where site chiefs brief workers on safety, weather, schedule, and incidents before shift start. DWS IQ / Firehorse delivers **AI-powered morning briefings** to site chiefs—aggregating safety alerts, weather, near-miss reports, and daily work plans into a single control-room view.

**Why this matters for executive outreach:**

| Executive Level | Morning Briefing Connection |
|-----------------|----------------------------|
| **Site Chiefs / COOs** | Direct users: receive AI-generated briefings, incident summaries, weather alerts. Value: faster, safer start-of-day decisions. |
| **ESG / Sustainability** | Morning briefing data feeds ESG reporting: safety metrics, near-miss trends, carbon-aware scheduling. CSRD requires traceable operational data. |
| **Compliance / NIS2** | Site operations generate critical infrastructure data. NIS2 requires visibility into OT/IT at essential entities—morning briefing platform = operational visibility layer. |
| **CFO / Digital** | ROI story: reduced incident response time, fewer missed briefings, audit trail for safety compliance. Digital transformation starts at the site. |

**Pitch angle:** *"Your site chiefs get AI-powered morning briefings. Your board gets NIS2/CSRD-ready dashboards from the same platform."*

## BYO Agentic AI Angle

**BYO (Bring Your Own) agentic AI** means customers plug in their preferred AI models and agents—Claude, GPT, Llama, Groq, or on-prem—instead of being locked into a single vendor. DWS IQ / MCP Hub is model-agnostic: the platform orchestrates workflows (morning briefing, compliance dashboards, SiteSense) while the customer chooses which AI powers the reasoning.

**Why this resonates with construction executives:**

| Concern | BYO Agentic AI Response |
|---------|-------------------------|
| **Vendor lock-in** | Use your existing Microsoft/Google/Anthropic contracts. Swap models as strategy evolves. |
| **EU AI Act / sovereignty** | Run EU-hosted or on-prem models. Data stays in your jurisdiction. |
| **Cost control** | Route high-volume tasks to Groq/fast models; complex analysis to Claude. Optimise spend per use case. |
| **Compliance / audit** | Agentic AI = traceable reasoning. Know which model made which recommendation. NIS2/CSRD require explainability. |
| **CDO/CIO mandate** | "We're the orchestration layer. You bring the AI." Fits multi-cloud, multi-model enterprise strategies. |

**Pitch angle:** *"Bring your own agentic AI. We provide the construction workflows—morning briefings, compliance, SiteSense—you choose the brain."*

**Best contacts for BYO angle:** CDOs (Jens Hoffmann, Gilles Godard), CIOs (Susanne Lithander—Finance + IT), Strategy leads (Renaud Digoin Danzin), **Petri Kasari (Sweco)—knowledgeable on multi-agent systems**. These roles own vendor selection and AI roadmap.
