## The calibration warning (read first)

Asking a busy €10B-company CEO to introduce a pre-seed startup to his marquee investors is a **real favor**, not a free action. 1st-degree + 194 mutuals is a strong signal, but it is not the same as rapport. Match the ask to the actual relationship:

- **If rapport is strong** (you've spoken, met, or have real mutual history): you can ask for one or two specific intros directly.
- **If it's a thinner connection**: do NOT open by asking for five investor intros. Open with a low-friction, ego-respecting ask — his *read* on the sovereign-compliance thesis — and let an intro offer emerge. People protect their investor relationships; a generic "intro me to all your VCs" ask can cost the connection.

**Default play: lead with ONE targeted intro (Tesi), not the whole cohort.** Tesi is the one that can actually take our Admiral seat, and a single, specific, easy-to-forward ask is far more likely to convert than a five-name blast. Add Solidium / others only if he's receptive.
