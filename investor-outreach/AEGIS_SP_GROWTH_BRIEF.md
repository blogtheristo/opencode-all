DWS IQ AEGIS — WHY THIS REVENUE IS BANKABLE

WHO WE ARE. Lifetime Oy (Business ID 0772407-9, Espoo) ships DWS IQ Aegis: a
Finnish software platform that automates EU-mandated compliance reporting for
industrial and defence supply-chain companies. Published list pricing, subscription
revenue, Finnish hosting. Availability: August 2026, sold from a live platform
business (5 paying customers, EUR 7K MRR, 100% retention as of March 2026).

1. THE DEMAND IS WRITTEN INTO LAW, NOT FASHION.
   Our customers do not buy Aegis because they want to — they buy it because
   NIS2, the CER Directive, CSRD, CBAM, the EU AI Act, and Germany's KRITIS
   make the reporting mandatory, with fines for non-compliance. Compliance
   spend is non-discretionary: it survives budget cuts and recessions. Every
   new EU defence programme (the Commission's five joint defence projects,
   incl. DECODER with 26 participating countries and Eastern Flank Watch with
   13) adds reporting obligations for every supplier in the chain — each one a
   prospective customer.

2. SOVEREIGNTY IS MARKET ACCESS, NOT IDEOLOGY.
   EU and national defence buyers increasingly cannot place sensitive
   supply-chain data on US-owned platforms (CLOUD Act exposure). The dominant
   alternatives — Palantir, C3.ai — are all US-headquartered. Aegis is 100%
   Finnish-owned, hosted on Finnish infrastructure (UpCloud, Tesi-backed,
   PiTuKri-audited), which means Aegis is eligible for exactly the contracts
   the US stack is disqualified from. Shrinking competitive set, growing
   mandate: that is market access by construction.

3. THE PRICE POINT MAKES PROCUREMENT FEASIBLE.
   Aegis Year 1 costs EUR 72K (EUR 59,990 onboarding + EUR 999/mo), published
   list price. US alternatives start at an estimated EUR 500K-2M/year
   (negotiated, unpublished). At EUR 72K, a mid-tier manufacturer or defence
   subcontractor can buy Aegis inside normal management purchasing authority —
   no board case, no multi-year tender. Short sales cycles, repeatable deals,
   no dependence on a single mega-contract.

4. REVENUE QUALITY.
   - Recurring subscription: EUR 999-4,999/mo per customer on top of onboarding
   - Retention: 100% to date (zero churn); customer value EUR 70K-120K/logo/year
   - 2026 plan: EUR 900K ARR (25 customers x EUR 36K average) — an average deal
     is 0.9% of a Palantir-class contract, sold to a market of thousands of
     EU-regulated companies rather than dozens of governments
   - Pricing is public (dws10.com) and auditable against invoices

5. DEMAND EVIDENCE FROM HOME GROUND.
   The events driving this market are happening to Finnish infrastructure
   first: the EstLink 2 power cable and C-Lion1 telecom cable (both Finnish-
   operated) were cut in winter 2024-25, triggering months of repairs, a NATO
   response mission, and hardened EU reporting obligations. The April 2025
   Iberian blackout (47M people, ~USD 1.8bn output loss) showed the same
   pattern EU-wide. Each such event converts directly into compliance
   obligations — and compliance obligations convert into Aegis subscriptions.

USE OF FUNDS. Loan capital funds revenue generation, not runway: (1) launch of
the certified reseller programme (JM-oikeus tier; Nordic reseller agreement in
negotiation with TechSeed — 60%+ reseller margin model) and (2) incorporation
of Lifetime AB in Stockholm (planned November 2026) to serve Swedish
defence-industrial customers. Both steps are gated on confirmed revenue
milestones — the money creates more money.

TRAJECTORY. European NATO-region defence spending is projected to exceed
EUR 800bn by 2030. Every euro of it flows through supply chains that must
document sovereignty, security, and carbon compliance. Aegis is priced so the
whole chain can afford it.
