## Slide 2: The Problem (Regulation & Pain)

### EU Climate Regulations Are Creating Unavoidable Compliance Burden

**Real-World Pain Points:**

1. **40–80 hours of manual Excel work per project**
   - Construction & logistics companies manually tracking emissions across multiple sites
   - Data scattered across invoices, fuel receipts, subcontractor reports
   - No standardized format, high error rate, audit failures

2. **€10,000–€50,000 in fines & penalties**
   - Projects delayed due to missing CSRD documentation
   - Public tenders rejected for incomplete climate reporting
   - ETS compliance failures leading to direct financial penalties

3. **Lost business opportunities**
   - Cannot bid on public contracts without climate compliance
   - Private sector clients requiring ESG documentation
   - Supply chain partners demanding Scope 3 reporting

### The Regulatory Timeline: Companies Must Buy Something

| Year      | Regulation                                              | Impact                                                     |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2024–2025 | **CSRD** (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive) | Fewer than ~5,000 large EU companies must report directly (post-Omnibus)                           |
| 2025–2026 | **Fit for 55** – Building renovation wave               | All major renovations require energy/climate documentation |
| 2026–2030 | **EPBD** (Energy Performance of Buildings Directive)    | Mandatory building passports across EU                     |
| 2027–2035 | **ETS expansion** (Emissions Trading System)            | Construction, logistics, healthcare included               |

**The mandate is clear: companies must buy climate compliance tools or face penalties.**

### MCP-Native Architecture: Platform vs. Point Solution

**Why DWS IQ 6 is Defensible**

1. **MCP-Native Platform Architecture**
   - Model Context Protocol (MCP) = open standard for AI agent interoperability
   - Customers can plug in their own agents via MCP hub
   - Network effects: the more agents in the ecosystem, the more valuable the platform

2. **Private Cloud, EU-Hosted, GDPR/CSRD Ready**
   - No data leaves customer's jurisdiction
   - Full audit trail for compliance
   - SOC 2 / ISO 27001 ready infrastructure

3. **Vertical-Specific Agent Library**
   - Pre-built compliance agents for each vertical (logistics, construction, healthcare)
   - Regulatory knowledge baked into the Memory layer (RAG)
   - Continuous updates as regulations evolve

4. **Customer Data = Moat**
   - Each deployment improves the model's understanding of that vertical
   - Anonymized, aggregated data improves all customers' predictions
   - First-mover advantage in data accumulation

**Technical Moat**

```
Competitors → Point solutions (static dashboards, manual data entry)

Competitors → Cloud SaaS (data leaves EU, compliance risk)

DWS IQ 6   → 8 verticals via modular agent architecture

Competitors → System of Intelligence (reports what happened)
```

**Core Positioning (verbatim):** We are not a SaaS vendor — we are a _System of Action operator_ that carries regulated work reliably to completion with a full audit trail.
