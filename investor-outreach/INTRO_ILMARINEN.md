## Fit Assessment

1. **Thesis clears the bar at scale** — Ilmarinen's ICEYE participation shows sovereign / dual-use deeptech is allocatable within a pension mandate once de-risked. DWS IQ is the same family, earlier.
2. **EU-sovereign, fiduciary alignment** — A Finnish pension insurer values EU data residency, auditability, and regulatory defensibility — exactly DWS IQ's product attributes.
3. **Direct customer logic** — Ilmarinen's own portfolio companies carry CSRD / ESG-reporting obligations. There is a *buyer* story here independent of the *investor* story — possibly the faster door.
4. **Long-horizon capital** — Pension capital is patient; if DWS IQ becomes category infrastructure, Ilmarinen is a natural late-stage holder.

## Drawbacks / Mitigations

- **No direct seed mandate — core blocker** — Pension fiduciary duty rules out €650K SAFE risk. *Mitigation:* do not pitch the SAFE. Pursue the fund route (be in a Varma/Ilmarinen-backed VC's portfolio) and a growth-round relationship.
- **Heavy diligence / committee process** — Institutional procurement and IC timelines. *Mitigation:* treat as long-game; lead with auditability and EU-sovereign posture, which shorten institutional comfort cycles.
- **Investor vs. buyer ambiguity** — The investment door is years out; the *customer* door (compliance tooling for Ilmarinen's portfolio) may be nearer. *Mitigation:* offer a free compliance assessment on one portfolio company as the relationship opener.

- **Lead with auditability + EU sovereignty + the ICEYE pattern**, not MRR. Pension institutions weight governance and defensibility over growth-rate.
