## KYA-S Certification & D&O Evidence Infrastructure

### KYA Standard — Know Your Agent / Know Your Architecture

DWS IQ 6 is governed by the **KYA Standard** (v1.6), a dual-purpose framework unique in the market:

- **Know Your Agent (KYA-S):** Hardware-attested agent identity, capability gating, and forensic audit trails. Every AI agent deployed through DWS IQ 6 carries a cryptographic identity, operates within explicit capability bounds, and produces court-defensible logs.
- **Know Your Architecture (KYA-O):** Financial optimization and agent self-optimization governance. Agents iteratively improve their own models under safety bounds, delivering **11–30% efficiency gains** benchmarked from real autoresearch runs.

**Why this matters to investors:** KYA is not a feature — it is a _standard_. Customers build compliance workflows on top of it. Switching costs are structural, not contractual. No competitor has an equivalent governance framework for autonomous agent swarms across EU-regulated industries.

### D&O Evidence Infrastructure — EU AI Act Article 99(4)

Agent-governed decisions create personal liability exposure for directors and officers. Under the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689), Article 99(4) imposes fines up to **€15M or 3% of global turnover** for violations of high-risk deployer obligations (Arts. 8–15, 16, 26); Article 99(3) raises that to €35M or 7% for prohibited-practice violations under Art. 5. National corporate governance codes add personal exposure for directors who approved AI deployments without documented controls. DWS IQ 6 addresses this through KYA-S certification:

- **Article 12 logging:** Every agent decision is automatically logged with reasoning lineage (Firehorse forensic telemetry) — the evidence EU authorities look for under Art. 12 automatic-logging obligations.
- **Article 26 deployer evidence:** KYA-S produces the documented governance that D&O insurers require before granting AI-governance coverage. Insurers typically report an **8–12% premium range** for documented AI governance; DWS cannot commit an insurer to a specific discount.
- **Parametric insurance integration:** KYA trust scores can feed insurer risk models — higher trust scores can support lower premiums through direct negotiation with the carrier.
- **Liability containment:** Capability gating ensures agents cannot exceed their authorized scope, reducing "rogue AI" exposure.

**Investor relevance:** D&O insurance is a €2B+ European market. The 8–12% premium range reported by insurers for documented AI governance is a quantifiable, recurring savings driver for enterprise adoption. DWS IQ 6 is the compliance infrastructure — priced in line with SAP GRC / Microsoft Purview (€10k–€50k/year) — that produces the evidence customers need. **The fines, premiums, and legal defence remain with the customer.**

## Revenue Model — Two Tiers

DWS IQ 6 uses a **dual revenue model** optimized for market segment:

### DWS6 (Standard SaaS — Tiers 1–3)

| Component                 | Mechanism                                                         |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **SaaS Subscription**     | Fixed monthly fee: €499–€1,999/mo (Enterprise: custom)            |

**Why SaaS:** SaaS-era enterprise buyers expect predictable monthly costs. Revenue sharing models (gain-share, outcome-based) create friction in procurement — CFOs want a line item, not a variable percentage. SaaS + API is the model they know and trust.

### Enterprise World (Tier 4 — Alpha-Share)

For **Enterprise World** customers at pricing Tier 4 — large-scale deployments where Alpha-Share's value-aligned incentives are understood and welcomed:

| Component                 | Mechanism                                                                                      |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Base SaaS**             | Platform subscription (MRR)                                                                    |
| **Alpha-Share**           | Decline-to-Scale: 12% (first €1M), 8% (€1M–€10M), 5% (above €10M) of verified efficiency gains |
| **Performance Guarantee** | Forward-looking credit on verified production fault within 30 days                             |

**Why Tier 4 only:** Alpha-Share requires sophisticated buyers who understand value-based pricing. SaaS-era customers see revenue sharing as risk, not alignment. Enterprise World customers — large industrials, sovereign deployments — see it as proof that DWS has skin in the game.

### Performance Guarantee (both tiers)

- **Threshold:** Minimum efficiency gain target agreed at onboarding based on baseline audit.
- **Measurement:** Gains verified quarterly via Firehorse forensic telemetry — auditable, tamper-evident data.
- **If underperformance:** Customer receives a credit on the next billing cycle proportional to the shortfall.

**Financial impact:** The dual model maximizes addressable market. SaaS captures the broad mid-market; Alpha-Share captures outsized value from Enterprise World deployments. Projected **8–22x ROI** within 18 months in heavy-industry deployments.

## Agentic Efficiency Law

> _"The best thing of all is when an agent makes other agents better."_
> — Andrej Karpathy

DWS IQ 6 operationalizes a principle we call the **Agentic Efficiency Law**: when governed agent swarms operate under the KYA Standard, they compound efficiency gains across the fleet — each agent's optimization improves the operating context for every other agent in the swarm.

This is not a methodology. It is a **governance wrapper** — the KYA Standard ensures that self-optimization happens within safety bounds, with full audit trails, under human oversight. The compounding effect is a natural consequence of governed autonomy at scale.

**What this means in practice:**

- A single agent optimizing energy consumption in one building improves the baseline model for all buildings in the portfolio.
- Compliance agents that learn from one regulatory filing reduce preparation time for the next filing across all customers.
- The 11–30% efficiency gains measured in autoresearch runs are _per-agent_. Fleet-level gains compound.

**Investor relevance:** The Agentic Efficiency Law creates a flywheel — more agents deployed → more data → better optimization → higher platform revenue (SaaS + API + Enterprise World Alpha-Share) → more customer value → more agents deployed. This is the network effect for industrial AI.
