# Positioning Case — Kertos as the Richard Würl / Redstone Wedge

_Repository source-of-truth for the "one layer down from Kertos" claim used in `INTRO_RICHARD_WURL_REDSTONE.md` and `INVESTOR_ONEPAGER_RICHARD_WURL_REDSTONE.md`. This document is the master; those two lean on it. Repo wins on conflict (Truth Standard §2E)._

## 1. Why this case exists

The Würl outreach opens on **"you backed Kertos → you already know this lane."** That wedge only works if the Kertos comparison is **accurate and flattering to Kertos** — Richard Würl (Principal, Redstone VC) sat in Kertos' seed round and knows the company far better than we do. A wrong or dismissive Kertos claim in a cold letter to its own investor is a credibility own-goal. This case pins the facts and the exact layer distinction so every Kertos sentence we send is defensible.

So this is not a passing portfolio line — it is a two-round conviction position in exactly the regulated-compliance-automation lane DWS operates in. That is the single strongest reason he is worth a cold letter.

## 3. The layer distinction (the actual wedge)

Kertos and DWS IQ are in the **same macro-lane** — EU regulated-compliance automation, AI-native — but at **different layers, for different buyers, on different obligations.** They are **complementary, not competing.**

| | **Kertos** | **DWS IQ (Lifetime Oy)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Layer** | The compliance **program** — the organization's own management system | The industrial **filing** — the outcome an operator owes the regulator |
| **Obligation type** | Management-system / attestation (GDPR, ISO-class infosec, NIS2, DORA, SOC 2, EU AI Act governance) | Outcome filing on **physical goods & emissions** — **CBAM** declaration, **CSRD/ESRS** report, **EU AI Act Article 12** evidence |
| **Who owes it** | Any company handling data / running systems | **Physical industrial operators** — steel mills, cement plants, the 25 EU-regulated industries |
| **Buyer** | DPO / CISO / infosec | Sustainability / compliance lead at an industrial |
| **Sold as** | Automated compliance **program** (platform / seats) | The finished regulated **outcome**, paid on the outcome — not usage (MGD north-star doctrine) |

**One-line version:** _Kertos automates the compliance program a company runs; DWS IQ produces the specific filing a steel mill or cement plant owes the regulator. Same lane, one layer down, at the industrial obligation itself._

**Why the distinction matters to Würl:** it means DWS is **not a Kertos competitor** — no portfolio conflict — while still being a lane he has twice underwritten. The adjacency is the pitch: he can back the industrial-filing layer without touching his Kertos position.

## 4. How to use it in the outreach

- **Lead (already in the letter):** "Redstone's investment in Kertos is why I'm writing — same regulated-compliance lane, one layer down at the industrial obligation itself." Keep it; it is grounded.
- **If he replies "how are you different from Kertos?":** use §3 verbatim — different layer, different buyer, different obligation, **complementary**. Do not disparage Kertos; position as the industrial-filing layer beneath the program.
- **If "too small for a Redstone lead":** the existing reply-handling playbook stands (personal-angel cheque / warm intro to Redstone for the next round / referral in his compliance-fintech network). The Kertos adjacency is what makes the *next-round* Redstone intro credible.
- **Do not claim** Kertos does CBAM/CSRD industrial filings, or that DWS does GDPR/SOC 2 program management — that is the whole point of the distinction, and conflating them breaks the wedge.
