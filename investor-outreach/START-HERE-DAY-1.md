## FAQ

**Q: What if I don't have 5 LinkedIn connections who are investors?**

A: That's fine. Look for:
- Founders who've raised money (they know angels)
- People who work at VCs (they know angels)
- Anyone who's posted about climate tech
- Your old boss/colleagues who might know someone
- Friends/classmates who are doing well financially

Everyone knows someone who invests.

**Q: What if I don't get any responses?**

A: Totally normal. 80% won't respond. That's why you send 5/day, not 5 total.

Send 25 emails this week = expect 3-5 responses = success.

**Q: What if someone says no?**

A: Say "Thanks for considering! If you know anyone else who might be interested, I'd appreciate an intro."

30% of "no" replies turn into intros to other angels.

**Q: What if they ask questions I can't answer?**

A: Say "Great question - let me send you our detailed deck/GitHub/financial model: [link]"

Then schedule a call: "Can we jump on a 30-min call to walk through it?"

**Q: Should I personalize more?**

A: Not yet. Send 25 generic emails first. See what response rate you get.

If <15% respond, then add personalization. But don't let perfect be the enemy of sent.
