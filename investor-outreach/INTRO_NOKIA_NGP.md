## Status: COLD — Strategic Now, NGP Capital at Series A

Nokia appeared as a Finnish strategic investor in ICEYE's €1B round — a connectivity / sensing / sovereign-infrastructure adjacency play, not a financial-VC ticket. For DWS IQ there are two doors: a **strategic partnership / co-sell** angle with Nokia now, and a **venture-financing** relationship with NGP Capital (Nokia-anchored, Series A+) for the next priced round. Offer it only if Tesi passes or stalls; it is one seat. Captain seats are off the table (zero traction; reserved for client captains).

## Fit Assessment

1. **Sovereign-infrastructure adjacency** — Nokia's ICEYE participation signals appetite for sovereign / mission-critical infrastructure. DWS IQ's EU sovereign-compliance layer is adjacent to Nokia's industrial-digitalisation and sovereign-networks narrative.
2. **Espoo neighbour** — Nokia HQ and Lifetime Oy are both in Espoo. Local, EU-sovereign, no CLOUD Act exposure — a clean strategic-fit story.
3. **Enterprise / deeptech is NGP's lane** — NGP Capital invests in enterprise and deeptech at Series A+. DWS IQ's category (EU compliance automation for industrials) is on-thesis once we reach their stage.
4. **Co-sell potential** — Nokia's industrial / private-wireless customers are exactly the regulated manufacturers that need ETS / CBAM / CSRD evidence. A partnership / co-sell motion could precede any investment.

## Drawbacks / Mitigations

- **Stage mismatch — core blocker** — NGP Capital enters at Series A+, not a €650K SAFE. *Mitigation:* don't pitch the SAFE to NGP; build the relationship for the next priced round, and pursue the strategic / co-sell door now.
- **Strategic-investor caveats** — Corporate strategic capital can carry information-rights and roadmap-influence strings. *Mitigation:* keep early engagement at the partnership level; defer equity to a later, cleaner round.
- **Two distinct entities** — "Nokia" (strategic) and "NGP Capital" (independent VC) are different doors with different contacts. *Mitigation:* run them as two tracks; do not conflate them in outreach.

## Strategy

- **Strategic / co-sell first**, investment later. Lead with the industrial-customer overlap (Nokia's private-wireless / industrial base needs compliance evidence) — a partnership conversation, not a raise.
- **NGP Capital** — a parallel, longer-horizon relationship for the Series A; introduce the company now, ask for the round later.
- **Aegis / sovereignty framing ON**, anchored as regulatory compliance infrastructure (legal mandate).
