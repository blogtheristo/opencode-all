# PE Insights — Operating Partner Captain Seat Outreach

## Thesis: Why Operating Partners, Not VCs

PE Operating Partners and Value Creation leads are the right Captain Seat buyers because:
1. They deploy tools ACROSS portfolio companies (multiplier effect)
2. They understand the "orchestrator" role — managing AI agents is their next evolution
3. They have budget authority for portfolio-wide tools (€36K/company is a rounding error in a value-creation plan)
4. They don't need to be convinced that compliance is mandatory — they live it at every portfolio company
