# DWS IQ: Sovereign AI for Regulated European Industry

## The Investor Structure

**Lifetime Oy is opening a focused EU/EEA investor structure around DWS IQ. One Admiral anchors the round. Eight Captains bring industrial capital, domain authority and access to customers.**

### Admiral
- One EU/EEA-based Admiral who anchors the €650K SAFE
- Brings institutional judgement and helps shape the company's next commercial phase
- Provides anchor capital and strategic guidance on financing, governance and growth

### Captains
- Eight EU/EEA-based Captains who bring industrial capital, domain authority and access to customers
- Each Captain brings more than capital: access to named buyers, plants, procurement processes and decision-makers (subject to verification of relationships)
- Represent real industrial domains such as chemicals, defence manufacturing, construction, metals, water treatment or critical infrastructure

## Round Architecture

Target: €650,000 SAFE
Valuation cap: €3.8M
Investor scope: EU/EEA-domiciled investors
Structure: 1 Admiral and 8 Captain seats
Indicative Admiral allocation: €250,000
Indicative Captain allocation: 8 × €50,000 = €400,000

This fills the €650,000 target exactly. A ninth Captain seat may be held as a strategic reserve or used only for oversubscription if the round size and documents are updated. It is not a ninth full €50,000 allocation in the current structure.

## The Value Proposition

DWS IQ is building a sovereign AI platform for regulated European industry. Our approach combines:

- **Sovereign AI infrastructure**: Designed to support GDPR- and CSRD-related workflows; deployment-specific assessment required
- **Industrial AI expertise**: Deep understanding of industrial markets and compliance requirements
- **Product portfolio**: 116-product industrial software portfolio with 13 serious DWS IQ products

## Why This Structure Works

The Admiral-Captain structure is designed around a simple principle: the people financing industrial AI should understand how industrial AI gets bought, deployed and accepted.

- **Admiral**: Provides the anchor capital and institutional confidence
- **Captains**: Bring industry-specific access, domain knowledge and commercial reach
- **Combined**: Is intended to support more repeatable industrial deployment
