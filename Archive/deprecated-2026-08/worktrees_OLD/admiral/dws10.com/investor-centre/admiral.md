# The EU Industrial AI Anchor

**Lifetime is building a sovereign AI platform for regulated European industry. We are looking for one EU/EEA-based Admiral to anchor the €650K SAFE, bring institutional judgement and help shape the company's next commercial phase.**

## The Industrial Command Role

**We are not looking for a passive lead. The Admiral would help us build the capital, governance and commercial command structure around DWS IQ as it moves from product capability into repeatable industrial deployment.**

## The Portfolio-to-Market Role

**Lifetime has a 116-product industrial software portfolio, with 13 serious DWS IQ products forming the core commercial layer. We are looking for an Admiral who can help turn that breadth into disciplined market focus without destroying the long product tail that represents our delivery capability.**

## The Admiral Role

The Admiral anchors the round and provides:
- Anchor capital and institutional confidence
- Strategic judgement on financing, governance and growth
- Experience with enterprise software, industrial markets or regulated technology
- Help shaping the company's strategic direction
- A serious view of the transition from product capability to repeatable industrial revenue

The Admiral is not promised a board appointment, return or liquidity. Governance and information rights remain subject to final investment documents.

Target: €650,000 SAFE

Investor scope: EU/EEA-domiciled investors
