# Request Access to Investor Centre

## How to Request Access

To request access to the DWS IQ investor programme, please send an email to [investor@dws10.com](mailto:investor@dws10.com) with the following information:
- Your name and company
- Investment thesis and check size
- Relevant domain expertise or access to industrial buyers
- Interest in the Admiral or Captain investor roles

## Qualification Process

All requests undergo a manual review process including:
- Verification of EU/EEA status
- Assessment of investment thesis and financial capability
- Evaluation of value-add profile and domain expertise
- Initial screening for alignment with the Admiral-Captain structure

Qualified parties will receive a Level 1 access invitation, which includes:
- Admiral and Captain investor one-pagers
- The DWS IQ and Firehorse product families
- The commercial thesis
- The round architecture
- The proposed investor roles

## Next Steps

Following Level 1 access, interested parties may proceed to:
- Mutual due diligence (Level 2)
- Further discussion of investment terms
- Review of final documentation

## Data Handling

Access to full due-diligence materials requires:
- NDA and, where applicable, DPA or other data-handling documentation, subject to legal review

No investment, appointment, acceptance or access to full due-diligence materials is implied by this page. Final decisions and terms are subject to direct review and applicable documentation.
