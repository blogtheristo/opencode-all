## The Core Thesis: "We Extend Your Data Chain Into Operations"

Rakennustieto owns the first link in Finland's construction carbon data chain. One Click LCA uses their EPDs for design-phase LCA. But after the building is built, the chain stops.

DWS IQ extends it into operations compliance.

```
RAKENNUSTIETO (Standards & Data)         DWS IQ (Operations Compliance)
─────────────────────────────           ──────────────────────────────
RT cards → how to build                 ERP data → how it actually operates
RTS EPDs → material carbon (A1-A3)      Operational carbon (B1-B7)
CO2data.fi → emission factors           Emission factors → CSRD evidence
40,000 professionals                    CFOs / sustainability directors

"The data"                              "The report"
```

**Why this partnership works:**

1. **Data source** — CO2data.fi emission factors are inputs to our CSRD calculations
2. **Chain completion** — Design standards → construction → operations compliance
3. **Finnish ecosystem** — Both Helsinki-based, both serve Finnish construction industry
4. **Foundation mission** — Rakennustietosäätiö RTS promotes construction quality and sustainability
5. **No competition** — They provide data/standards, we provide compliance automation
