## The Core Thesis: "Your Output Is Our Input"

```
ONE CLICK LCA (Design Phase)          DWS IQ (Operations Phase)
─────────────────────────────          ──────────────────────────
Building LCA (A1-A5)          →→→      Operational compliance (B1-B7)
Material carbon at design      →→→      Actual carbon in operations
One-time assessment            →→→      Continuous quarterly reporting
Architect / engineer           →→→      CFO / sustainability director
250,000+ LCA datasets          →→→      ERP data + LCA baselines
20+ BIM integrations           →→→      6 ERP connectors (SAP, IFS, Dynamics)
                               →→→
"What SHOULD this building emit?"      "What DID this company ACTUALLY emit?"
```

**Why this partnership works:**

1. **No competition** — DWS IQ doesn't do LCA. We consume LCA outputs, not produce them.
2. **Churn reduction** — One Click LCA customers finish design-phase LCA and churn. If DWS IQ imports their outputs for ongoing compliance, customers keep their subscription active.
3. **Revenue share** — We send referrals back for design-phase assessments when CSRD customers need baseline carbon data.
4. **Platform extension** — Extends One Click LCA's platform into the operations lifecycle they don't cover, without building it.
5. **Mutual customer value** — Complete the building lifecycle: design → construction → operations → reporting.
