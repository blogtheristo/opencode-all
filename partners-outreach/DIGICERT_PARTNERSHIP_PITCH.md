## Letter as sent (23.06.2026)

**To:** updates@digicert.com
**From:** Risto Anton Päärni, CEO, Lifetime Oy
**Subject:** Partnership exploration — DigiCert PKI + DWS identity & sovereignty layer

---

Dear DigiCert team,

I watched the framing around your 2026 Global PKI Research Report with Adam Strange and Jeremy Rowley, and one line stayed with me: everyone depends on PKI, but no one owns it.

That is exactly the gap we work on.

DigiCert owns the certificate lifecycle — issuance, discovery, automation, the trust infrastructure enterprises run on. We are not a CA, and we are not trying to be. We build the layer above it: a verified-identity spine that binds every action, human or agent, to a named and auditable owner, with EU AI Act Article 12 reasoning lineage behind it.

In other words, you make the certificate. We help make sure it has an accountable owner the auditor can actually read — and that the trust store sits inside the EU data residency tier the customer's regulators expect.

Three reasons I think this is worth a conversation:

- **Complementary, not competitive.** Your platform plus our identity and sovereignty layer closes the ownership gap your own research names — without overlapping product.
- **The EU wedge.** European enterprises buying PKI in 2026 face NIS2, CSRD, and EU AI Act review. We already operate with EU sovereignty tiers and an identity model built for that audit. That is where we can carry a joint story.
- **Post-quantum readiness.** Crypto-agility starts with knowing what you have and who owns it. Combined, we give customers the inventory-to-accountability path before they rotate algorithms.

Would you be open to a 30-minute call to explore a referral or technical-integration partnership? I can bring our identity-spine architecture and a short joint-customer hypothesis to the table.

With love and respect for the work,

Risto Anton Päärni
