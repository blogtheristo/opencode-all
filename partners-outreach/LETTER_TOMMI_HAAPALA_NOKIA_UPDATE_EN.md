# Update: Tommi Haapala (Nokia)

**Subject:** DWS IQ Aegis × Nokia × DEFINE — where we are, and a concrete next step

---

Tommi,

a short update since our April discussions, and a proposal for the next step.

**Where Aegis is now.** We have sharpened Aegis into a clear position: **sovereign industrial intelligence for dual-use manufacturing** — a single-tenant, EU-sovereign AI platform on Finnish infrastructure, zero CLOUD Act exposure, with every agent decision court-defensible (KYA Standard + EU AI Act Article 12). It covers the full regulatory stack (dual-use export control under Reg. 2021/821, plus ETS/CBAM/CSRD/NIS2) from one environment, across 21 EU-regulated industries.

**What's new since April:**

- **Five-language EU go-to-market surface** — the Aegis intro and product pages now render natively in FI, SV, DE, FR and EN, with terminology aligned to the canonical EU sources (Reg. 2021/821). This matters for cross-border defence supply chains where the buyer, the regulator and the prime speak different languages.
- **EDIP / DIANA context** — the Commission's €1.5B EDIP work programme (adopted 30 March 2026) makes sovereign compliance infrastructure a funding precondition; we are positioning Aegis directly against that requirement, and Riihimäki / DEFINE anchors us in the Finnish defence ecosystem.

**Why this is relevant to Nokia.** The Aegis × Nokia × DEFINE angle we discussed — sovereign networks and mission-critical connectivity meeting a sovereign, court-defensible AI/compliance layer — has only strengthened. Aegis is the operational-intelligence layer that rides on top of the kind of sovereign network fabric Nokia provides.

**Proposed next step.** A short working session (30–45 min) to map one concrete joint use case — for example, a dual-use supply-chain visibility pilot combining Nokia's sovereign connectivity with Aegis compliance and sovereignty monitoring — that we could take into the DEFINE ecosystem together. I can share an updated one-pager under our existing NDA ahead of the call.

Would a slot in the next two weeks work on your side?
