## Singapore Market Entry (application section — paste-ready)

### Why Singapore, why now — two demand waves, one entry point

**Wave 1 — agentic commerce needs a trust layer, and Singapore is where
that conversation is happening.** Autonomous AI agents are beginning to
transact — pay, order, commit — across exactly the platforms that run
through Singapore: regional commerce, logistics, and payments. Every
current agent-payment rail (Coinbase x402, Google AP2, Stripe's agent
toolkit) binds the _human_ account and leaves the agent itself without
identity, bounded authority, or an audit trail that carries legal weight.
We already run the missing layer in production: **KYA — Know Your Agent**
— cryptographic agent identity, a revocable spending mandate, and
replayable audit built into an operating agent platform with paying
customers. We are standardizing it as **KYA-2, the open conformance
standard for AI agents that transact**, with a European patent track and an
EIC Accelerator application in motion. Singapore is the natural second home
for this standard: the region's agentic-commerce ecosystem (payments
platforms, super-apps, marketplace operators, and the MAS regulatory
perimeter) will need agent-level trust infrastructure before any other
market — and no regional player owns it yet.

**Wave 2 — compliance data for EU-bound trade, proven demand we already
serve.** From 2026, every steel, cement, aluminium and fertiliser shipment
from ASEAN into the EU needs a CBAM declaration with verified
embedded-emissions data. Singapore is the regional control point — the HQ
for the trading houses, MNCs, and system integrators that route those
exports. DWS IQ already does this work in Europe: deterministic compliance
scoring across ETS, CBAM, CSRD and the EU AI Act, running for 6 paying
customers at €7k MRR with 100% retention since launch. A Singapore foothold
puts that engine adjacent to ASEAN-side CBAM demand — the exporters who
must produce verified data with no EU-side tooling of their own.

The two waves converge on one product truth: **compliance-grade trust
infrastructure for autonomous systems** — agents that transact (Wave 1)
and the regulated data flows they generate (Wave 2). One platform serves
both; Singapore is the only city where both markets concentrate.

### Entry plan — concrete, staged, already precedented

1. **Entity.** If selected, Lifetime incorporates a Singapore entity within
   three months of programme commencement, per programme requirements. This
   is not improvisation: our corporate structure already follows a
   jurisdiction-per-market model — Lifetime Oy (Finland: core R&D and all
   IP), Lifetime AB (Sweden: Nordic expansion) — and our platform
   architecture is explicitly scored against a **three-pillar sovereignty
   model covering EU, US, and Singapore** jurisdictions. The SG entity is
   the market vehicle for APAC; IP and core engineering remain in Finland.
2. **First customers.** Beachhead ICP: (a) one Singapore trading house or
   marketplace operator piloting **agent-initiated transactions under a
   KYA mandate** (procurement or subscription agent, spend-capped,
   auditable); (b) one ASEAN exporter needing **CBAM-verified emissions
   data** for EU-bound shipments. Both pilots run on the existing platform
   — no new product required; deployment is configuration plus edge
   hardware where needed (NVIDIA Jetson line, shipped product).
3. **Channel.** Microsoft is our forcing function: the programme co-runner
   is also our stack alignment — we ship a Microsoft 365 agent consultancy
   offering and built our customer adoption methodology on Microsoft's AI
   Adoption Framework. The BLOCK71/NUS network supplies what we cannot
   build from Helsinki: warm access to the Singapore payments and commerce
   ecosystem now defining agent-driven transactions.
4. **Delivery capacity without relocation risk.** Execution is not gated on
   Nordic headcount: a founder-led core plus **100+ contracted engineers
   across two certified partners** (both under NDA, both actively
   delivering today — one in Kochi, three time zones from Singapore)
   means APAC delivery hours are already in the network.
5. **Milestones (10-week programme horizon).** Weeks 1–3: Singapore ICP
   validation, 30 named accounts across both waves. Weeks 4–6: first pilot
   signed (agentic-commerce mandate pilot preferred). Weeks 7–9: second
   pilot (CBAM data), MAS/IMDA regulatory-perimeter mapping for agent
   transactions. Week 10: Investor Day with two live pilots and the KYA-2
   standard as the demo spine.

### What we ask from the programme

Not product help — the platform is shipped and revenue-generating. We need
what Singapore uniquely offers: ecosystem access to the agentic-commerce
platforms and trading houses concentrated there, mentorship on APAC
enterprise sales motion, and the credibility of the BLOCK71 × Microsoft
umbrella when we walk into rooms where a Finnish SME is an unknown. In
exchange, the cohort gets the one thing none of the payment rails have
built: the trust standard that makes agent-driven commerce auditable —
developed in the EU, proven with paying customers, and brought to the
region where agentic commerce is being decided.
