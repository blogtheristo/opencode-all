## Why This Matters

1. **Directly matches the stated technical gap.** Token-cost optimization and MCP wire-format hardening are exactly what Risto flagged as open — Dayna's GCF work is proof, not a claim.
2. **She disclosed she's job-searching, actively, now.** The Open to Work signal (immediate start, contract accepted, remote) means a vague, unpaid "partner position" ask risks losing her to a role that pays this month — the offer needs a paid, near-term component regardless of how the longer-term role is framed.
3. **CTO-level potential is the stronger offer than a Captain-seat / fundraiser role.** DWS IQ runs on a very small operating team, so someone with genuine ownership capability over the MCP/orchestration and deployment layer has outsized leverage — bigger upside for both sides than treating her primarily as a route to investor or Captain-candidate introductions.
4. **Earn-the-role beats title-upfront.** A 3-month paid engagement with real ownership is a genuine trial for a CTO/technical-partner role — for her and for DWS IQ. Her European network becomes valuable _after_ she understands the company and investor story, additive rather than a precondition.
5. **Relocation interest is real but a separate, longer-horizon track.** Worth keeping alive, but shouldn't gate the near-term ask.
6. **A position beats a project.** Dayna gets contractor offers regularly — another one loses her before the conversation starts. Leading with a founding-level position (Industrial Captain + Technical Partner, CTO door open) is the differentiated offer; a paid trial alone is not.
7. **Capital-in and technical role are not sequential, they're parallel.** The €50K Captain commitment buys her a real position in the industrial ecosystem regardless of the technical outcome; the skills/fit test decides the CTO question independently. Neither should be sold as contingent on the other, and the message must not read as "pay to get the CTO job."
8. **The €50K payback story is the real blocker, not a detail.** Asking someone to commit €50K without a credible way to see it back within ~12 months is a weak ask regardless of how the position is framed. This is unresolved — do not promise a number in the letter that hasn't been modeled.
