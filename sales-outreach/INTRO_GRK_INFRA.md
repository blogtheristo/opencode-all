## The Wedge — "Your best year is the right moment to build the compliance infrastructure for the next one"

GRK Infra builds the physical infrastructure Finland needs. Roads. Bridges. Rail. Port. They are infrastructure — not just contractors.

**The compliance gap:**

CSRD Wave 2 lands for GRK Group in FY2025 (report due 2026–2027). This requires:

- Scope 1: Direct emissions from GRK's construction machinery fleet (diesel, LNG, HVO)
- Scope 2: Purchased electricity for site operations
- Scope 3 (partial): Material supply chain emissions (aggregates, steel, concrete)
- Project-level carbon tracking: regulators and public clients increasingly require project-level breakdowns, not just company totals

Today, this data lives in fuel receipts, machinery logbooks, and subcontractor invoices. Collecting and formatting it for CSRD takes weeks per reporting cycle — manually.

**What DWS IQ 6 does:**

1. Automated Scope 1/2 data collection from GRK's machinery fleet and site energy meters
2. Project-level SPI/CPI dashboard — cost + schedule + carbon in one view
3. CSRD Wave 2 report generation: submission-ready output from real project data
4. Timeline: from first data connection to first draft CSRD report — 30 days, not 6 months

**The Tier A argument:**

> "GRK:n kannattavuus 2025 on paras pitkään aikaan. Se on juuri oikea hetki rakentaa compliance-infrastruktuuri, joka kestää kasvun — ei silloin, kun CSRD-puute on kriisi ja pistää julkiset tilaukset vaaraan."
