## The Wedge — "Every 0.1% margin improvement at €430M scale is €430k"

Kreate is Tier C: stable, disciplined, consistently around sector median. The next margin improvement does not come from winning more contracts — it comes from operational efficiency.

**Three DWS IQ 6 angles for Kreate:**

### Angle 1: CSRD reporting cost reduction (entry wedge)

- Kreate is already producing mandatory CSRD reports as a listed company
- The pain: manual data collection from project sites, subcontractor data requests, sustainability team overhead
- The fix: DWS IQ 6 automates Scope 1/2 collection and CSRD report generation
- Reference: "DWS IQ 6 leikkaa raportoinnin 60 tunnista 8 tuntiin kuukaudessa"
- Cost of pilot: €5,990/month × 5 months = €29,950 vs. estimated €40–80k/year in reporting labor

### Angle 2: AI orchestration for project portfolio (Tier C kärki per construction-profitability-targeting-FI.md)

- Real-time SPI/CPI dashboard across Kreate's active project portfolio
- Early warning on cost overruns before they hit the P&L
- Mobile-first: Kreate's project managers check project health from the site, not the office
- "Joka prosenttiyksikkö marginaalia tulee operaatioista, ei myynnistä."

### Angle 3: Public procurement differentiation

- Finnish public clients (HSL, Väylävirasto, municipalities) increasingly require CSRD-compatible sustainability documentation in tenders
- Kreate's listed-company CSRD capability + DWS IQ 6 reporting speed = tender competitive advantage

**Lead with Angle 1** (CSRD cost) because it is an existing pain with an existing budget (Kreate is already spending money on CSRD compliance). Angle 2 and 3 are for the second meeting.
