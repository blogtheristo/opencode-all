## Part 1 — DD (Due Diligence): Skanska AB

**Industry & Vertical:** Global construction and project development. Revenue ~SEK 175B (2024). Operations in Sweden, Norway, Finland, Poland, UK, and USA. Listed on Nasdaq Stockholm.

**Regulatory Exposure:**
| Regulation | Deadline | Skanska Impact |
|---|---|---|
| CSRD | FY2025 report (2026) | Full group + value chain ESRS E1 reporting |
| EPBD | 2028 | Whole-life carbon mandatory for EU new builds |
| EN 15978 / NS 3720 / BR18 | Current — tender requirement | Multi-jurisdiction LCA outputs for Nordic public works |
| CBAM (supply chain) | 2026 | Steel, cement, aggregates sourced cross-border |
| SBTi 1.5°C commitment | Ongoing | Scope 3 value chain emissions tracking required |

**Size & Financials:** One of the world's largest construction groups. Nordic operations ~40% of group revenue. MSCI ESG rating: A. Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) signatory.

**Current Pain Points:**

- Multi-jurisdiction LCA reporting (SE/NO/DK/FI/PL/UK) currently fragmented — different tools per market
- CSRD Scope 3 supply chain data requires supplier carbon scoring at scale, not yet automated
- Innovation mandate (Hök's role) creates pressure to demonstrate measurable digital sustainability uplift
- Green premium pricing in public tenders dependent on auditable, jurisdiction-specific LCA outputs

**Tech Stack Signals:** Enterprise SAP environment. BIM (Revit/Tekla) on major projects. Skanska has made previous investments in proptech and construction innovation platforms.

**DWS Fit Score: High.** Skanska's multi-jurisdiction footprint and SBTi commitment map directly to DWS IQ's one-pipeline, multi-standard output capability (ESRS E1, EN 15978, NS 3720, BR18). Supplier scoring and BIM/SAP ingestion address both the CSRD Scope 3 gap and green tender requirements.

**Competitive Context:** Skanska has evaluated or uses tools like One Click LCA and Normative for parts of this. DWS IQ differentiates on ERP-native ingestion, unified multi-standard output, and AI orchestration reducing manual touchpoints.
