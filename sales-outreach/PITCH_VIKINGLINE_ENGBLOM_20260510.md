## Part 1 — DD (Due Diligence): Viking Line / Gasum Maritime

**Industry & Vertical:** Baltic Sea passenger ferry and cargo maritime operations (Viking Line). Nordic energy and maritime LNG/bio-LNG supply and bunkering (Gasum). Both operate at the intersection of maritime decarbonisation regulation and fleet transition investment.

**Viking Line Regulatory Exposure:**
| Regulation | Deadline | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| FuelEU Maritime | 2025 (in force) | Greenhouse gas intensity reduction obligations on all Baltic routes |
| EU ETS (maritime) | 2024–2026 phased | Carbon allowance cost on all EU/EEA voyages |
| CSRD | FY2025 report (2026) | Full sustainability reporting including fleet emissions |
| IMO CII rating | Annual | Carbon Intensity Indicator — public rating affects charterer/partner trust |
| EPBD (port facilities) | 2028 | Shore power and terminal energy obligations |

**Viking Line — Company Profile:** Finnish family-owned shipping company. Revenue ~€600M (2024). Fleet of 7 vessels operating Helsinki–Stockholm, Turku–Stockholm, Tallinn routes. Announced bio-LNG bunkering partnerships with Gasum for Baltic routes. Mats Engblom (SVP Finance) leads financial planning and likely oversees compliance cost management for ETS allowances.

**Gasum — Company Profile:** Nordic gas, biogas, and LNG infrastructure and supply company. Operates LNG bunkering for maritime clients across Nordic and Baltic ports. Active in FuelEU Maritime pooling arrangements on behalf of shipping clients.

**Current Pain Points (Viking Line):**

- EU ETS maritime carbon allowance cost is a new and growing P&L line — no integrated forecasting tool
- FuelEU Maritime pooling with Gasum requires traceable, auditable fuel consumption and GHG intensity data per voyage
- CSRD fleet emissions reporting requires Scope 1 voyage data at a granularity most legacy fleet management systems cannot produce
- IMO CII rating is public — a poor rating affects reputation with cargo clients and port authorities

**DWS Fit Score: High.** AI-driven fleet optimisation (Lifetime Fleet maritime module) addresses voyage-level fuel consumption data, ETS allowance forecasting, and FuelEU GHG intensity calculation. Traceable compliance output covers CSRD Scope 1 and IMO CII in a single pipeline.

**Competitive Context:** Maritime compliance tools (DNV Veracity, Wärtsilä Fleet Intelligence) address technical vessel performance. DWS IQ differentiates on the financial/compliance layer — ETS cost forecasting, FuelEU pooling audit trail, and CSRD report generation — which fleet management tools do not produce.
