# Keenon × Lifetime Fleet — Nordic Distribution & Operating Fit

**One line:** Keenon builds the robots; Lifetime Fleet deploys, operates, and
governs them across the Nordics on a pay-as-you-go model — so Keenon gets a
data-backed reference market, not a box-shifting reseller.

---

## Who we are

**Lifetime Oy** (Espoo, Finland) — operator of **Lifetime Fleet**, a Physical-AI
robotics division. We run service-robot fleets in the Nordics on an
NVIDIA ISV stack, governed under our own KYA autonomy standard for EU AI Act and
NIS2 compliance.

|                     |                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Model**           | Deploy + operate (pay-as-you-go), not resale                                                                                                                            |
| **Footprint**       | Nordics, anchored in the Helsinki capital region                                                                                                                        |
| **Operators today** | Live fleet funded by the licensed transport and services operation (~€7,000/month services and transport revenue, not software revenue). No retention rate is asserted. |
| **Stack**           | NVIDIA Connect ISV; on-prem inference; Control Room monitoring                                                                                                          |
| **Compliance**      | EU AI Act Art. 12 logging, NIS2, GDPR — handled our side                                                                                                                |

## What we want from Keenon

Authorized **distributor + operating partner for Finland and the Nordics**,
leading with the **KLEENBOT C40** and extending to delivery and hospitality lines
as each category's demand is confirmed.

## The division of labour

| Keenon brings                                          | Lifetime Fleet brings                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Robots: cleaning (KLEENBOT C40), delivery, hospitality | On-site deployment & commissioning                            |
| Onboard navigation, docking, OTA firmware              | Control Room monitoring & field service                       |
| Spare parts & service network                          | Pay-as-you-go customer contracts                              |
| Product roadmap & training                             | EU AI Act / NIS2 / GDPR governance                            |
|                                                        | Uptime / utilization / cost-per-task reporting back to Keenon |

## Target customers (Nordics)

- **Facility & retail** — small-to-medium spaces fit the C40's design point.
- **Hospitality** — hotels, restaurants: delivery and reception robots.
- **Healthcare & senior care** — corridor delivery; we already operate in
  healthcare-adjacent transport.

## Why this beats a standard reseller for Keenon

1. **Operated, not shelved** — utilization is our job, so robots actually run.
2. **Compliance de-risked** — we carry the EU AI Act / NIS2 burden locally.
3. **Channel proof** — measured outcomes become a referenceable Nordic case.
4. **Existing operation** — robots land into a live fleet, not a cold start.

## The ask

A 20-minute call to scope:

- Territory and any existing Nordic exclusivity.
- Minimum volume / order commitments for distributor status.
- Service, spare-parts, and firmware-update terms.
- Whether Keenon's fleet API/telemetry can feed our Control Room.
