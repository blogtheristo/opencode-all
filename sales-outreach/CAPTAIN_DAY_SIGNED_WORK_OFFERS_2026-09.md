## How to use this file

Three motions, not three named accounts. Each offer is a template with bracketed slots
the HIC fills per target. **Do not send a slot unfilled.** Named-target qualification
(ICP gate, decision-maker, pain signal) follows the `INTRO_*.md` pattern — build that
file per account before sending; this file supplies the copy, not the targeting.

**The fence, applied to every line below:** the first sentence never contains the word
_compliance_ and never names a regulation. What the recipient hears first is the money
that is currently frozen. KYA, Article 12 and the regulation names appear in the body,
as the reason the artifact holds up, or not at all.

**Offer in all three:** Captain Day — One-Day AI Outcome, €1,200 (VAT excl.), on-site or
remote. One day, one shipped artifact. Reduced rate if we do not ship same day. That
last clause is the whole pitch under this fence — it is the thing a task force cannot
put in a press release.

## Fences observed

- No regulation and no "compliance" in any subject line or opening sentence.
- No named public body as an antagonist; no third-party funding figure; the Valtori
  anecdote appears nowhere.
- No "replace your people", no uncapped liability, competitors implicit.
- Speed as "days, not quarters" — no contractual hour-count.
- Price €1,200 and the same-day clause are quoted from `dws10.com/config/products.json`
  (`ai-consultant-day`), not invented.
- Every claim about the recipient is a bracketed slot, so nothing is asserted about a
  company before the HIC verifies it.
