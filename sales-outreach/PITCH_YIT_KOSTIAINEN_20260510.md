## Part 1 — DD (Due Diligence): YIT Oyj

**Industry & Vertical:** Finnish-headquartered construction and urban development company. Operations across Finland, Sweden, Norway, Russia (divested), and Baltic states. Revenue ~€1.8B (2024). Listed on Nasdaq Helsinki.

**Regulatory Exposure:**
| Regulation | Deadline | YIT Impact |
|---|---|---|
| CSRD | FY2025 report (2026) | ESRS E1 mandatory — includes Scope 3 construction emissions |
| EN 15978 / YM (Finnish) | Current | Life cycle carbon required for Finnish public housing tenders |
| EPBD | 2028 | Residential and commercial new builds — whole-life carbon disclosure |
| EU ETS (construction plant/fleet) | 2027 | Site machinery and logistics fleet enter carbon cost scheme |
| CBAM (materials) | 2026 | Steel, aluminium, cement sourced cross-border face border carbon |

**Size & Financials:** Revenue ~€1.8B. ~4,500 employees. Focused on urban development (residential, business premises, infrastructure services). Finnish government and municipal entities are key clients — making green tender compliance critical for pipeline.

**Current Pain Points:**

- Site-level carbon tracking for construction plant and fleet not automated — manual monthly reconciliation
- Finnish public housing tenders increasingly require YM-compliant LCA — currently handled project-by-project without a unified pipeline
- EU ETS expansion to construction logistics in 2027 means fleet carbon costs will appear on P&L — no current forecasting tool
- CSRD Scope 3 supply chain data from subcontractors is unstructured and hard to aggregate

**Tech Stack Signals:** Microsoft Dynamics ERP (confirmed). Fleet management tools in use but carbon integration unclear. BIM workflows on major residential projects.

**DWS Fit Score: High.** Lifetime Fleet + DWS IQ Base addresses both the fleet carbon tracking gap (ETS 2027 preparation) and the YM/EN 15978 LCA compliance pipeline for Finnish public tenders. Dynamics integration is supported.

**Competitive Context:** Finnish construction IT market has local players (Visma, Buildercom) handling project management. DWS IQ differentiates on carbon compliance automation rather than project delivery tools — complementary positioning.
