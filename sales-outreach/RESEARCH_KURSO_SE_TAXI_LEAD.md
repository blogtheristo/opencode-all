# Research — kurso.se ("Taxi driver + entrepreneur, Finland → Stockholm")

**Inbound signal:** `Taxi driver + företagare från Finland till Stockholm. info@kurso.se`

## What kurso.se is

**Kurso** (`kurso.se`, storefront `kurser.kurso.se`) is a **Swedish online e-learning
company** selling self-paced digital courses, focused on the **road-transport /
professional-permit** space (taxi, freight/gods, work-on-roads, construction).

- Contact: `info@kurso.se` · contact page `kurso.se/kontakta-oss` · Facebook presence.
- **Legal entity not yet confirmed.** The site blocks automated fetching (HTTP 403) and the
  org.nr / registered address / owners did not surface in search. **Open item:** confirm via
  Bolagsverket / allabolag.se.

## Flagship product (the "taxi" connection)

**"Yrkeskunnande Taxi"** — online course for the professional-competence exam required to run
a taxi business in Sweden.

- ~99 lessons, ~500 practice questions.
- Covers **law / EU regulation, taxi traffic law, permits** + **business & economics**
  (accounting, VAT, pricing, cost calculation, budgeting).
- ~4 months access; most finish in **1–3 weeks**.
- Prepares for the **Trafikverket** written exam (booked & paid separately).
- Also sells _Yrkeskunnande Gods_ (freight) and broader _yrkestrafiktillstånd_ courses.
- Material is **Swedish-only** — no English/Finnish version advertised (relevant for a
  Finnish customer).

## The "Finland → Stockholm" angle (regulatory reality)

A Finnish person wanting to drive/operate a taxi in Stockholm is exactly Kurso's target
customer. Per **Transportstyrelsen**:

- **Finland is in the EEA**, so a Finnish citizen _can_ drive a Swedish taxi.
- A foreign (Finnish) qualification is **recognized but may be conditioned on a
  "kompensationsåtgärd"** — a Trafikverket test or an adaptation period at a Swedish taxi firm.
- To **run a taxi business** (not just drive) you need a **taxitrafiktillstånd**, and the
  designated _trafikansvarig_ (transport manager) must have **passed the yrkeskunnande exam at
  Trafikverket** — precisely what Kurso's course prepares for.

## Bottom line

`kurso.se` is a **Swedish taxi-permit training provider**. "Taxi driver + entrepreneur from
Finland to Stockholm" reads as the **buyer persona**: a Finnish driver/entrepreneur who needs
the Swedish professional-competence exam (and likely a compensation test) to set up or operate
a taxi in Stockholm. Natural fit is Kurso's _Yrkeskunnande Taxi_ course — caveat: Swedish-only,
and formal recognition runs through Transportstyrelsen / Trafikverket.
