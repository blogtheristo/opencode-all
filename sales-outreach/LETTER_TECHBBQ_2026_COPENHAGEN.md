**Why now, and why the growth potential is real.** More than 50,000 EU companies must produce this compliance data by 2028, and the regulatory clock is not negotiable. We are an NVIDIA Connect ISV Partner and a Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub member, and we hold three InvestEU Portal listings. The wedge is open: incumbents sell cloud-only tooling into sites that have no reliable cloud, while the penalty for non-compliance is customs seizure and audit liability — not a missed report.

TechBBQ puts us in front of the international investors sizing EU climate-compliance infrastructure, the corporates who are themselves the regulated buyers of CBAM/CSRD/ETS tooling, and the ecosystem partners who shorten our route to each new industry. A seat is not a conference badge for us — it is a concentrated week of pipeline: investor conversations for the round, design-partner conversations for two new verticals, and Nordic-to-EU distribution partnerships. We will come prepared with a booked meeting schedule and leave with a measurable follow-up list.

I would be honoured to represent Finnish and Nordic deep tech at TechBBQ 2026, and I am confident the summit will convert directly into investment, partnership, and customer momentum for a company solving a mandatory, time-bound European problem.
