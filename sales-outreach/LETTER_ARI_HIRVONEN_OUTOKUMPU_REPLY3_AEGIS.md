### English (send version)

> Ari,
>
> One number that should be on your desk: the EU goods deficit with China hit EUR 359.8 billion in 2025. Global steel overcapacity is projected at 721 million tonnes by 2027 — nearly five times total EU steel consumption. China is not competing; it is flooding. The EU's trade defence instruments (CBAM full enforcement from 2026, the Foreign Subsidies Regulation, active anti-dumping measures on steel) are the policy response. But those instruments only work if the compliance infrastructure behind them is fast and sovereign.
>
> That is specifically why Aegis, not a generic CBAM tool.
>
> Generic tools (Sphera, Watershed, Carbon Chain) solve the calculation. They run on US-origin cloud. When Outokumpu calculates CBAM tariff exposure on Chinese-origin steel imports, or documents Foreign Subsidies Regulation due-diligence on a procurement decision, that is procurement-sensitive intelligence. It cannot transit AWS or Azure — CLOUD Act reach applies regardless of which EU region those services claim to run in. The corporate ownership chain is US jurisdiction.
>
> Aegis is in preorder — general availability September 2026 — and it runs on UpCloud Helsinki. EU-sovereign, Finnish company, no CLOUD Act surface. The same platform that covers CBAM (VulcanAgent), ETS allocation burn (GaiaAgent), Foreign Subsidies Regulation due-diligence (ComplianceAgent), and NIS2 critical-entity posture (SentinelAgent) — all on one signed, auditable pipeline that your IP function can actually govern. The IP veto path I described in the last message is not a bolt-on; it is built into the same agent stack.
>
> The timing argument is also direct: CBAM full enforcement is 2026. NIS2 critical-entity status for a steel producer of Outokumpu's scale is not optional. And the Chinese overcapacity signal — gallium, germanium, graphite export bans already done — means the supply-chain monitoring requirement is going to get deeper, not lighter.
>
> Palantir charges EUR 920K/year minimum and has 8% international commercial growth because European buyers do not trust its CLOUD Act exposure. We are EUR 72K in Year 1 and sovereign by construction. That is the gap.
>
> Still interested in the pilot? I can have the detailed pilot sheet in front of you within 24 hours.
>
> — Risto
