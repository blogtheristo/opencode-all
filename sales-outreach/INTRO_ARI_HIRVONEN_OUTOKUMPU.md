## The wedge — "Your CBAM disclosures are IP filings in disguise"

Outokumpu's competitive moat in low-carbon stainless (Forta, Core, Ultra, Circle Green) is **partly process IP**: scrap ratio above 90%, ferrochrome electrified with low-CO₂ Finnish grid, EAF route, slag valorization. Every euro of CBAM-driven premium Outokumpu earns vs. imported high-CO₂ stainless is paid for, in part, by that process IP.

CBAM (Q3 2026 first definitive declarations), EPD requests, automotive customer Scope 3 audits, and CSRD Wave-2 disclosures now require Outokumpu sales engineers to publish, per product, per quote:

- Embedded emissions intensity (tCO₂e/t steel)
- Energy mix and primary input shares
- Scrap ratio and recycled content
- Process route attestations

Those are **published technical disclosures**. Today they exit Outokumpu through sustainability and sales-engineering, with the IP / Patent function **not** in the approval loop. Three consequences:

1. **Competitive intelligence leak.** H2 Green Steel, SSAB HYBRIT, ArcelorMittal XCarb, and Aperam can — and do — read these disclosures across customer tenders.
2. **Trade-secret erosion.** Detail levels demanded by automotive OEMs (BMW, Mercedes, Stellantis, Volvo) trend deeper each year. A field that started as "tCO₂e/t" now wants process route, energy provenance, and recycled-content provenance.
3. **AI Act / IP-prosecution exposure.** Any AI tool used to draft these disclosures that touches US cloud is, for Outokumpu's IP function, a discovery and Cloud-Act exposure surface for inventions in prosecution.
