---

Hi Ari,

Following the Outokumpu motion: the Commission's diversification instrument, flagged for Q3 2026, would cap single-country supply at ~30–40% and require at least three sources for critical inputs. For steel that puts manganese ferroalloys and graphite electrodes — both heavily China-dependent — into the same disclosure surface as CBAM. Proving "three sources" and origin to customers and regulators leaks the same competitive detail (source country, supplier mix) that the CBAM wedge already addresses.

The win is one origin-evidence engine that satisfies CBAM, the diversification cap, and CRM Act together — not three filings — with IP review built into each disclosure.

If that fits how you see it, I'd like to map it into the pilot path.

Best,
