## The Wedge — "Your fleet emissions are a rising cost line. DWS IQ 6 is the instrument to manage it."

Posti Group operates Finland's largest delivery fleet. Every diesel kilometer is:

1. **A CSRD Scope 1 reporting obligation** — mandatory from FY2024 (large company rule), submitted to the authority and to the public
2. **An ETS2 future cost** — EU ETS2 from 2027 covers road transport; Posti's fleet is above the threshold; every tonne of CO₂ from the fleet will have a direct financial cost
3. **A fleet electrification decision** — Posti is electrifying but at pace limited by cost and charging infrastructure; understanding the real emissions baseline at route level enables optimal electrification sequencing

**What DWS IQ 6 does:**

1. Real-time Scope 1 tracking at vehicle level — per route, per depot, per service line
2. CSRD fleet emissions report: submission-ready output, not estimated
3. ETS2 cost projection dashboard: price scenarios × fleet composition × electrification timeline
4. Electrification ROI calculator: which routes to electrify first based on actual emissions density

**The financial argument:**

> "Posti Group: ETS2 alkaa 2027. Jokainen dieselkilometri saa hintalapun. DWS IQ 6 on mittaristo, jolla Posti optimoi laivaston sähköistymisen ennen kuin kustannus iskee — ei sen jälkeen."
