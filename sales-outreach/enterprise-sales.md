## Common mistakes to avoid

**Documentation pitfalls that kill enterprise deals:**

1. **Feature-focused decks**: Enterprise buyers care about outcomes (energy cost reduction, compliance achievement), not technical specifications. Lead with regulatory urgency and business impact.
1. **Missing CFO ammunition**: Without ROI calculators and financial justification, champions cannot sell internally. 79% of enterprise purchases require CFO approval. 
1. **Delayed security documentation**: SOC 2 and ISO 27001 take 3-6 months. Start immediately—enterprise deals stall without security compliance evidence.
1. **Generic case studies**: Nordic buyers prioritize local references. Kela validation is gold for Finnish B2B2G; document it thoroughly with quantified metrics.
1. **Overlooking NIS2**: As of October 2024, NIS2 compliance is mandatory for vendors serving essential and important entities.  Missing documentation disqualifies you silently.
1. **Consensus underestimation**: Swedish deals require 3-6 stakeholder alignment. Create persona-specific materials (CFO one-pager, technical architect specs, sustainability officer compliance matrices).
1. **Summer timing**: Avoid July-August for Nordic enterprise engagement. Budget planning happens Q4 for next-year procurement—align documentation launches accordingly.
