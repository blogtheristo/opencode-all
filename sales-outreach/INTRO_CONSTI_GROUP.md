## The Wedge — "Consti is already doing EPBD. We close the data loop."

Consti's business model IS the EPBD implementation:

- EPBD 2024 recast (transposition: May 2026) mandates building owners to upgrade to EPC class D or better by 2030–2033
- Building owners who must comply are Consti's CUSTOMERS — they buy renovation services from Consti to meet the mandate
- The demand spike is real: Consti's order book should be growing as EPBD pressure materialises

**The gap DWS IQ 6 fills:**

Today, when Consti completes a renovation (e.g., facade + HVAC upgrade), the building owner receives:

- A certificate of work completed
- Maybe an EPC certificate if an auditor was engaged
- No real-time energy performance monitoring to prove the expected savings are materialising

DWS IQ 6 closes this loop:

1. **For Consti's own CSRD reporting:** Automate Scope 1/2 reporting from renovation sites — reduce from 60+ manual hours/month to 8
2. **For Consti's customer value proposition:** Offer post-renovation energy monitoring as a differentiator — "we don't just renovate, we prove the results"
3. **Partnership angle (medium-term):** Consti white-labels or refers DWS IQ 6 to renovation clients as part of the project handover

The first pitch is angle #1 (CSRD automation). Angles #2 and #3 are for the second conversation.
