# Email Draft: Jacob Bentzel (TechSeed Nordics)

**Subject:** DWS IQ + Building X: A Revenue Opportunity for TechSeed

---

Jacob,

Thanks for getting back so quickly — I think we have a real synergy here.

TechSeed is now the GTM partner for Siemens Building X across Nordics. That puts you in front of a problem that's worth €500K–€2M per portfolio per year: Nordic real-estate CFOs are drowning in manual compliance work. CSRD disclosure, EPBD energy-performance certificates, EU Taxonomy reporting, EN 15978 lifecycle assessment — all manual, all fragmented, all consuming 4–6 months per portfolio.

**DWS IQ solves that, and we want you to own the revenue.**

We don't compete with your Siemens integration business. Building X generates telemetry. We operationalize the compliance layer that turns that telemetry into audit-ready, submission-ready output. You remain the integrator and customer relationship owner. **We provide the IP.**

**The revenue model is straightforward:**

- **Implementation:** €30K–€50K per customer (one-time, project-based)
- **Annual SaaS:** €15K–€25K per customer (recurring, minimum 2 years)
- **Your margin:** 60% of all SaaS revenue

**Example customer:**

- Implementation: €40K
- Year 1 SaaS: €20K (€12K to you at 60%)
- Year 2+ SaaS: €20K/year (€12K/year to you)
- **3-year deal value:** €100K total; €36K margin to TechSeed

If you land 6 customers in Year 1 (which is realistic given your Siemens channel), that's:

- €240K implementation revenue
- €72K annual SaaS margin (recurring)
- **Year 1 total: €312K**

And that margin scales — Year 2 is 12 customers (€384K), Year 3 is 18 (€456K).

**The pitch to your customers is simple:** Building X is now a compliance asset, not just an operational asset. One command. 90 seconds. CSRD disclosure + EPBD certificate + Taxonomy alignment — all signed, all audit-ready.

**Why this works:**

1. You own the customer relationship and integration — we just provide the compliance IP
2. Higher deal value — you sell integration + compliance automation instead of just integration
3. Longer customer retention — compliance is mandatory and ongoing; switching costs are high
4. Regulatory updates come through our platform — you don't rework client implementations every time CSRD scope changes

I'd love to dig into this with you and Frida in a short call. If the pilot works, distribution becomes the scaling story — Siemens refers customers to you, you deploy Building X + DWS IQ compliance layer as the standard offering.

I've attached an Investment Memorandum that goes deeper into the numbers and partnership structure.

Looking forward to talking next week.

Best,  
Risto
