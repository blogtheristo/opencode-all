# Email Draft: Frida Ahlberg (Siemens SI Building Nordics)

**Subject:** DWS IQ + Building X: The Missing Operational Layer for Regulated Industry

---

Frida,

I think this is the right wedge for Siemens.

DWS IQ is the missing operational layer for regulated industry. We turn compliance from manual overhead into infrastructure that lowers risk, cuts cost, and scales across EU, ASEAN, and US jurisdictions.

Today, regulated companies are still handling critical compliance work through fragmented legal, finance, and operations processes. That creates duplicated effort across subsidiaries, slower audit response, higher enforcement risk, and avoidable margin leakage. The work is mandatory, but the tooling is still too manual.

DWS IQ changes that.

- **Aegis** converts regulation into control, giving teams a practical layer for audit trails, governance, and operational accountability.
- **Sovereign Infra** handles jurisdiction-specific workflow automation, so the same company can operate cleanly across different regulatory environments.
- **Connect** links directly into existing ERP and financial systems, so compliance lives inside the workflow instead of sitting beside it.

The point is not compliance software. It is **less friction across industrial workflows, stronger auditability, and better cross-entity control.** In practice, that means lower operating cost, faster regulatory response, and a system that gets stronger with every deployment.

For Siemens, I would start with one business unit, one pain point, and one measurable pilot outcome. If the pilot works, the rest becomes a distribution story.

If this is of interest, I'd love to set up a short call and map one concrete use case.

I've also attached an Investment Memorandum that goes deeper into the pilot structure and ROI. (It's also for Jacob at TechSeed — I think the three-party motion works well.)

Looking forward to hearing from you.
