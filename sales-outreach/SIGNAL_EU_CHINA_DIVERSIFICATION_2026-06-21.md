## 1. The signal and why it sells now

EU goods trade deficit with China hit **€359.8 bn in 2025** (Eurostat). On **5 June 2026** Trade Commissioner Šefčovič said diversification "requires a dedicated instrument," tied to the trade-defence review due **Q3 2026**. The emerging law would **cap single-country supply at ~30–40%** and require **≥3 sources** for critical inputs, naming **chemicals, industrial machinery, automotive, advanced manufacturing**.

**Correlation level: ULTRA** for the two named-sector hats (Chemicals, Manufacturing), **HIGH** for the rest. This is a regulatory-mention signal of the strongest kind — a _pending binding obligation_ with a dated milestone (Q3 2026), not a soft trend.

**Timing (per `signaalimyynti.md`):** the proposal window is the outreach window. Reach door #1 **now**, ahead of the Q3 proposal — the wedge is "audit your single-country exposure _before_ the cap is law," not after.

---

## 2. The universal wedge

> Every affected company will have to **prove** that no critical input exceeds the single-country cap and that ≥3 qualified sources exist. Today they cannot — they have no tiered country-of-origin map. DWS IQ's **supplier-concentration audit + country-of-origin mapping** produces that evidence, and it **reuses across CRM Act, CBAM, and the diversification law** instead of three separate projects.

The diagonal win (registry §2): the same headline — _"audit-ready supply-chain origin evidence"_ — lights up Procurement (compliance), Sustainability (CRM/CBAM overlap), CFO (supplier-leverage cost), and Sponsor (de-risking mandate). One artifact, four advocates.

## 5. Do / don't

- **Do** lead with the verified China-share figure for that exact input — it is the proof-of-homework the signal method requires.
- **Do** frame DWS evidence as **reusable across CRM Act + CBAM + diversification law** — that is the cost lever for R23 CFO.
- **Don't** assert the 30–40% cap as adopted law — it is pre-proposal. Say "flagged for Q3 2026."
- **Don't** pitch the product before naming the customer's specific input exposure (anti-pattern in `signaalimyynti.md` §7.3).
