## Part 1 — DD (Due Diligence): HUS — Helsinki University Hospital

**Industry & Vertical:** Largest public hospital district in Finland. Operates 21 hospitals and 350+ units across the Helsinki metropolitan area. ~25,000 employees. Annually serves ~700,000 patients. Critical public infrastructure under Finnish public health law and EU directives.

**Regulatory Exposure:**
| Regulation | Deadline | HUS Impact |
|---|---|---|
| CSRD (healthcare sector) | FY2025 report (2026) | Mandatory sustainability reporting for large public entities |
| GDPR + NIS2 | Active | Strict data residency and security requirements for patient data |
| EU Sovereign Cloud framework | Active — procurement gate | EU tender contracts require GDPR-aligned cloud with data residency |
| EU AI Act (Article 10) | 2026 | AI systems used in healthcare classified high-risk — audit trail mandatory |
| EPBD | 2028 | Large hospital building stock faces energy performance mandates |

**Size & Financials:** Public entity — annual budget ~€2.2B. IT budget substantial given breadth of digital transformation agenda. Procurement via public tender processes.

**Current Pain Points:**

- Digital transformation ambition (AI, data platforms) constrained by GDPR and sovereign cloud requirements — US hyperscaler deployments create compliance exposure under EU scrutiny
- CSRD reporting for a large healthcare estate (buildings, fleet, supply chain) currently manual and fragmented
- EU AI Act mandates Article 10 data governance and audit trails for clinical AI tools — current platforms may not be compliant
- EU tender contracts increasingly require GDPR-aligned, data-residency-guaranteed cloud infrastructure

**Tech Stack Signals:** Existing integrations with Finnish national health data systems (Kanta, Apotti). Investment in data platform and AI initiatives. Sensitive to vendor lock-in given public procurement constraints.

**DWS Fit Score: High.** DWS IQ 6 private cloud deployment on Google Cloud Run europe-north1 (Helsinki) provides GDPR-native data residency. AI orchestration with Article 12/EU AI Act audit logging addresses clinical AI governance. CSRD building estate reporting automates the sustainability compliance gap.

**Competitive Context:** Major Finnish IT integrators (TietoEVRY, CGI) handle much of HUS's IT estate. DWS IQ differentiates on sovereign cloud-native AI compliance tooling rather than general IT delivery — a complementary rather than competing position.
