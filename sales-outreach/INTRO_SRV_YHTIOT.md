**The compliance gap:**

- Scope 1: Construction machinery and fleet
- Scope 2: Site electricity and energy
- Scope 3 (partial): Material supply chain
- EPC performance data for commercial developments (increasingly requested by buyers at transaction)

The manual effort: 60+ hours per month collecting, reconciling, and formatting this data for a listed company with active projects.

**What DWS IQ 6 does:**

1. Automated Scope 1/2 data collection from SRV's project sites

4. Single platform — not a compliance consulting project
