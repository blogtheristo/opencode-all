## The Wedge — "YIT has the rarest CSRD problem in construction: they own both ends"

YIT is in a unique position in Finnish construction:

**End 1 — Builder:** When YIT pours concrete, installs HVAC, and runs a 200-person jobsite, they generate Scope 1 emissions (diesel, gas, machinery). This must be reported at project level in CSRD Wave 1.

**End 2 — Developer:** When YIT sells a completed apartment to a buyer or a commercial building to an asset manager, that building's future EPC rating and operational energy performance is increasingly the buyer's condition for purchase. EPBD recast 2024 means the buildings YIT delivers must hit EPC class D or better by 2033 — and buyers are starting to price this in now.

**The gap:**
Today YIT collects energy and emissions data manually, from paper receipts, subcontractor invoices, and site managers' notes. Two separate reporting chains (CSRD own-operations vs. EPC data for sold buildings) mean two separate manual processes.

**What DWS IQ 6 does:**

1. Unified Scope 1/2 data collection across YIT's project portfolio — automatically
2. CSRD Wave 1 report generation from real project data, not spreadsheets
3. EPC-grade performance dashboards for completed developments — proof for buyers
4. Timeline: first draft CSRD-ready report in 30 days, not 6 months

**The restructuring angle:**
YIT divested Baltic, Central European, and Russian operations in 2022–2023 and refocused on Finland. The pressure on cost efficiency and Finnish competitiveness is real and acknowledged. Compliance automation fits this story: same output, less manual overhead.
