# Disclosure-as-IP Exposure — SSAB Zero / Docol fossil-free (one-page brief)

**Prepared for:** Christina Friborg, EVP & Head of Sustainability, SSAB (cc Carl Orrling, EVP CTO)
**By:** Risto Päärni, Lifetime Oy · risto.paarni@lifetime.fi
**Basis:** Public data only — modeled, not measured. Validated only in a pilot.

---

## The one question this brief answers

> *As SSAB takes fossil-free commercial across all product groups in 2026, every
> CBAM / EPD / Scope-3 disclosure attached to an SSAB Zero or Docol quote is a published
> technical document. How do you get the verified fossil-free premium into the quote
> fast — and keep the hydrogen-DRI process detail from leaking to competitors?*

SSAB's premium **is** transparency — so the goal is not "disclose less." It is
**disclose the premium-proving facts in minutes, and gate the crown-jewel process detail
at field level.** CBAM definitive declarations (Q3 2026) push granularity further.

---

## What an SSAB Zero / Docol disclosure can reveal (public-data model)

| Disclosure field | Routine level today | If granularity drifts | What a competitor can infer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Embedded emissions (tCO₂e/t) | Route average (near-zero claim) | Per-heat / per-coil | Hydrogen-DRI batch performance; furnace/route efficiency |
| **Hydrogen / DRI route detail** | Aggregate | Per-shift consumption, reduction profile | **The HYBRIT crown jewel** — fossil-free process economics |
| Electricity mix / PPA | Annual fossil-free claim | Per-quarter / per-site | Renewable sourcing strategy; demand-response economics |
| Iron-ore / pellet feed (e.g. LKAB) | Supplier-aggregate | Per-source / per-grade | Upstream supply strategy; cost sensitivity |
| Ore-based vs scrap route | Route label | Per-heat mix | Process-route IP; ramp behaviour of new plants |
| Verifier scope | Standard EN 15804+A2 | Plant-level metrics | Expands public surface for competitor patent counsel |

**Competitor set already mining this:** Outokumpu, ArcelorMittal XCarb, **Stegra**
(ex-H2 Green Steel, Boden), ThyssenKrupp (tkH2Steel), Salzgitter SALCOS, Aperam. Each
fossil-free EPD is intelligence for the others.

---

## The exposure, in three lines

1. **Premium at risk:** SSAB's world-first fossil-free premium is paid for *by* the
   hydrogen-DRI process IP. Over-disclosure on exactly the SSAB Zero / Docol products
   you're scaling in 2026 erodes the moat that earns the premium.
2. **Speed at risk:** as fossil-free goes commercial across all product groups, the
   disclosure volume to automotive OEMs (Volvo, Mercedes, BMW, Stellantis) spikes —
   the manual cycle (weeks) throttles premium capture vs competitors who can attach a
   verified disclosure inside the customer's window.
3. **Governance gap:** disclosures exit through sustainability + sales engineering under
   deadline pressure; the process-IP owners (CTO/R&D) are not in the publication loop.

---

## Why this is urgent now — SSAB's own stated strategy

SSAB is publicly the **fossil-free leader**, with commercial fossil-free across all
product groups in **2026** and SSAB Zero as the brand spearhead. That strategy *is*
process IP at scale. The faster the premium scales, the more disclosures carry the
crown-jewel detail to OEMs — and to competitors. **Protecting disclosure granularity
protects the premium SSAB has promised the market.**

---

## The fix (what we'd prove in a free, no-PO measurement)

- **Get the verified fossil-free premium into every quote in minutes** (modeled ~99%
  cycle-time cut), with an EU AI Act Article 12 audit log per disclosure.
- **CTO/R&D + Sustainability author field-level allow / redact / aggregate rules** per
  product line, enforced by a cryptographic, human-bound gate **before** publication —
  the hydrogen-DRI recipe stays behind the gate.
- **EU-sovereign** (no US CLOUD Act perimeter on disclosure drafts or process IP).

**Two outcomes measured on one SSAB Zero or Docol line:** (1) disclosure cycle time,
(2) process-IP coverage rate (ad-hoc → ~100%). Numbers are **SSAB's own**, not our claims.
