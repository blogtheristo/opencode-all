## The wedge — "C4IMobility's governance spine, before the first sprint"

C4IMobility is a fusion program. The moment C2 + comms + AI compute leave the lab and enter a moving vehicle, four things become non-optional:

1. **Cryptographic operator identity for every AI-mediated action.** Who authorised the model to fire / hand off / re-route / deny? Today this is a logbook problem. Tomorrow it is an Article 12 + AI Act Annex III + Reg-FD-equivalent for the FDF problem. **KYA (Know Your Agent)** ships this primitive.
2. **Auditable veto chain — TC-4 "Leash Snap."** Autonomous and semi-autonomous defence agents must be terminable mid-mission with a verifiable human-bound cryptographic signature. This is the difference between a "lethal autonomous" system being procurable in EU vs. being a press incident.
3. **Sovereign-NVIDIA compute (Jetson edge in-vehicle / DGX Spark depot / EU cloud).** No US Cloud Act perimeter on tactical or operational data; no foreign model training on defence telemetry. EU AI Act Annex III aligned by construction.
4. **24/7 Lifetime EU support.** When a tender comes in at 18:00 Friday or a vehicle fault report lands at 03:00 Saturday, the support stack is staffed in EU, by EU citizens, against an EU-cleared identity.

DWS IQ delivers all four. The C4IMobility LOI is the calendar peg.
