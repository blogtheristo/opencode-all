# EU Compliance Thresholds Reference (2026)

## CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive)

Reporting obligation applies to:

- Listed companies (any size) in the EU: from fiscal year **2024** (first report 2025)
- Large non-listed companies (2 of 3 criteria): from fiscal year **2025** (first report 2026)
  - > 250 employees OR
  - > €50M revenue OR
  - > €25M balance sheet
- Listed SMEs: from fiscal year **2026** (first report 2027), with opt-out until 2028

## CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)

Applies to **imports** of physical goods in sectors:

- Cement, iron/steel, aluminium, fertilisers, electricity, hydrogen

Does NOT apply to:

- Services, software, financial products
- Domestic EU production

Transitional period: Oct 2023 – Dec 2025 (reporting only)
Full mechanism: from **Jan 2026** (financial adjustment applies)

## EU AI Act

- Prohibited AI: from **2 Feb 2025**
- High-risk AI obligations: from **2 Aug 2026**
- GPAI model obligations: from **2 Aug 2025**
