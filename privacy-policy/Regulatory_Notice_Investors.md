# Regulatory Notice for Investors

**Lifetime Oy** (Business ID: FI07724079) is an Espoo-based technology company building the operating system for intelligent industries. This notice provides context on our regulatory positioning and the investment opportunity.

---

## The Opportunity: Compliance as a Growth Engine

The European Union's "Fit for 55" package and the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) have created a definitive market shift. Industrial companies are no longer just *incentivized* to report on climate impact—they are **mandated** to do so, with significant financial penalties for non-compliance.

Lifetime Oy is positioned at the intersection of this regulatory wave and the AI revolution.

### Why We Are Fundable
1.  **Pilot Revenue:** We are not pre-revenue. We are generating **€7k-14k MRR** during our pilot phase from logistics partners (Uber/Bolt/Kela fleets).
2.  **Regulatory Tailwind:** The market for our **Firehorse Reports** is created by statutory EU deadlines (2025 for Healthcare, 2026 for Manufacturing, 2028 for Construction).
3.  **Technical Moat:** We offer a complete system—Brain (DWS IQ), Memory (World Model), and Hands (Fleet)—not just a dashboard.
4.  **Efficiency:** We leverage extensive startup credits (Google, AWS, Groq) and NVIDIA edge computing to maintain a low burn rate while delivering enterprise-grade performance.

## Financial Governance & Integrity

We adhere to strict financial and operational governance standards to protect shareholder value and ensure long-term viability.

*   **Anti-Money Laundering (AML):** We enforce strict sanctions screening and do not engage with entities on EU/US watchlists.
*   **Data Sovereignty:** Our "Private Cloud" architecture ensures client data remains in the EU, mitigating GDPR compliance risks that often stall enterprise sales.
*   **Intellectual Property:** All core platform IP is wholly owned by Lifetime Oy. Client deliverables are clearly separated from our Background IPR in our Master Service Agreements.
