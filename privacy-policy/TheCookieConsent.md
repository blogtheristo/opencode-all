## Layer 2: The "Manage Preferences" Modal

**Title:** Cookie & Data Preferences

### 1. Strictly Necessary (Always Active)
These are required for the website to function and to ensure the cybersecurity of our services in compliance with the NIS2 Directive. They cannot be switched off.

### 2. Analytics & Performance
*   **Default State:** [ Toggle: Off ]
*   **Description:** Allows us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. Data is aggregated and anonymized.

### 3. AI & Marketing Personalization
*   **Default State:** [ Toggle: Off ]
*   **Transparency Notice (EU AI Act):** Enabling this option allows Lifetime Oy to process your behavioral data to train our internal AI models. This helps us recommend relevant content and services to you. We do not use this data for high-risk profiling or biometric identification.

**Buttons:**
*   [ Save My Preferences ]
