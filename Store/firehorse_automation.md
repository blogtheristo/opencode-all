## Usage Scenario: The Broken Sensor
**A critical flow meter fails on the factory floor.**

**Firehorse Automation handles the incident:**
1.  **Detect:** The Ingestion Workflow notices the data stream has stopped.
2.  **Alert:** It creates a maintenance ticket in your ERP (e.g., SAP PM).
3.  **Mitigate:** It flags the data gap in DWS IQ and applies the approved "estimation method" for compliance reporting (flagging it as estimated).
4.  **Report:** It adds an incident note to the Monthly Report.
5.  **Outcome:** Compliance continuity is maintained, and maintenance is dispatched instantly.
