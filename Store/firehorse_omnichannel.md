# Firehorse™ Omnichannel Next

**Price:** €499.00 / campaign cycle
**Includes:** one 4‑week test cycle, setup workshop, and final recommendations session.

## Description
**The Launch Command Center for Sustainable Products.**

Firehorse Omnichannel is a specialized AI marketing engine designed to launch "Green" and "Regulatory" products in the EU. It doesn't just buy ads; it finds the specific decision-makers (Sustainability Directors, CFOs) who are currently searching for compliance solutions, and delivers your message across LinkedIn, Google, and Email with zero waste.

## Why Firehorse Omnichannel?
**Green Launches Have Zero Margin for Error.**
Most product launches fail because they spend 80% of the budget finding the audience. Firehorse flips this: we use regulatory data (who *needs* to report CSRD?) to define the audience first, then spend your budget only on high-probability targets.

**Outcome:** At the end of a 4‑week cycle, you know exactly which channels and messages deliver qualified leads, validated by data, not guesswork.

## Market Focus Optimization
**"Where should I target my €2,000 budget?"**
Firehorse solves the "Allocation Problem". Instead of guessing, our AI agents analyze real-time signal data to split your budget dynamically.
*   **Budget Allocation:** Automatically shifts funds from low-performing channels (e.g., broad Google Ads) to high-performing ones (e.g., niche LinkedIn groups) every 24 hours.
*   **Penalty Avoidance:** Ensures all marketing claims are checked against the **EU Green Claims Directive** to prevent "Greenwashing" accusations.

## Usage Scenario: The €2,000 Test Launch
You want to launch a new sustainable product with a €2,000 test budget. Firehorse Omnichannel runs the validation loop for you:
1.  **Deployment:** It splits your budget across 2–3 high-potential channels (e.g., €800 LinkedIn, €800 Google, €400 niche/community).
2.  **Analysis:** The Marketing Optimization Controller tracks engagement, cost per click, and lead quality signals.
3.  **Optimization:** Within 48 hours, it detects that LinkedIn is driving ESG leads while Google is underperforming.
4.  **Action:** It recommends shifting budget to the best-performing segments.
5.  **Outcome:** You get a ranked view of channels by cost-per-qualified-lead.

## EU Compliance & Regulation Features
**Built for the Era of the AI Act and DSA.**
Marketing in the EU is no longer the Wild West. Firehorse Omnichannel ensures your campaigns are safe and compliant by design:

*   **EU AI Act Transparency:** Automatically tags AI-generated content and chatbots as "Automated System" to meet transparency obligations.
*   **Green Claims Directive:** The "Greenwashing Guard" agent scans your ad copy against EU taxonomy standards to flag risky claims (e.g., "Eco-friendly" without proof) before you publish.
*   **GDPR & ePrivacy:** Built-in consent management for tracking pixels and data collection. We do not store PII without explicit opt-in.
*   **DSA (Digital Services Act):** Adherence to ad transparency rules for online platforms.
