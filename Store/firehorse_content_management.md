Every carbon credit claim needs proof. Certificates, audit logs, and reports need a secure, organized home to feed into your Annual Report. Missing documentation means failed audits and **penalties**.

**At the end of the deployment, every green claim you make is one click away from its source proof.**

## Evidence Vault Methodology
**"Where is that certificate?"**
Stop digging through email chains. Firehorse Content Management treats compliance documents as critical assets:
*   **Auto-Tagging:** AI reads your documents and tags them by regulation (e.g., #EU-ETS, #Scope3).
*   **Expiration Watch:** Alerts you before a critical green certificate expires.
*   **Linkage:** Connects the document directly to the data point in DWS IQ.

## Usage Scenario: The Unannounced Audit
**An auditor demands proof of renewable energy sourcing for Q3.**

**Firehorse Content Management executes the retrieval:**
1.  **Search:** You type "Energy Certificates Q3" into the vault.
2.  **Retrieve:** The Search Agent finds the 3 PDFs provided by your utility provider.
3.  **Verify:** It shows the metadata linking these files to the Q3 Carbon Report.
4.  **Share:** You generate a secure, temporary link for the auditor.
5.  **Outcome:** The auditor validates the claim in minutes. No panic, no lost files.
