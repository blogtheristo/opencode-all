## Why this product

AI literacy is now a legal requirement, not a nice-to-have. EU AI Act Article 4 obliges
organisations to ensure their people can use AI systems competently. But literacy alone does not
make a team productive — engineers need a _discipline_ for working with an autonomous coding agent:
how to scope it, govern it, review its output, and keep quality high. Claude Code Coaching delivers
both: the literacy and the discipline, on your own codebase, without sending any IP to a US-hosted
service.
