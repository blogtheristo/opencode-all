## Carbon Penalty Avoidance (CBAM)

The EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism requires CBAM certificates at the 50-tonne steel import threshold. KYA agents:

- **Track embedded emissions in real-time** (not quarterly Excel)
- **Calculate rebates automatically** (Rebate Pulse — CBAM Art. 9 carbon price deductions)
- **Generate court-defensible attestations** (TPM-sealed, EU AI Act Art. 12 compliant)

**Example:** Steel import from Turkey — KYA Rebate Pulse deducts Turkish carbon tax (~€5/t), saving €40–60/tCO2e versus manual filing.
