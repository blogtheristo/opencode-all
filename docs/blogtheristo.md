## 2. Professional Summary

35 years in industrial IT. Factory floors in Finland. ERP migrations across Scandinavia. Cloud architecture for manufacturers still running systems from the 1990s. The degree that sits at the intersection of engineering and business — Industrial Management — is exactly where compliance problems live.

In late 2018, drawn not by a business plan but by a question he could not put down, Paarni started building the systems that would become DWS. He spent a year inside the mathematics of machine learning before the rest of the industry understood what transformers would become. When ChatGPT launched in November 2022, the world rushed to understand what he had been quietly exploring for four years. He did not need to learn AI. He needed to deploy it.

Six successive versions of DWS followed — each a complete rebuild, each closer to what industrial compliance actually demanded. The European Commission took notice: three versions (DWS 3, DWS 4, DWS IQ 6) earned listings on the InvestEU Portal (INPO-20254383). Not a single fortunate submission. Three consecutive iterations, validated at the Commission level.

He still writes code daily. He manages the GitHub repository. He reviews architecture decisions. When a demo breaks, he fixes it himself.

## 3. What I Build

### DWS IQ 6 — Climate Intelligence Platform

Multi-agent AI platform automating climate compliance across 16 EU industries under Fit for 55. Four-layer architecture:

- **Brain** — Multi-agent orchestration (Google Cloud Run + Groq LPU)
- **Memory** — Lifetime World Model (15,000+ EU regulatory documents, 16 industry verticals)
- **Body** — Firehorse Suite (reports, automation, content DMS, omnichannel)
- **Hands** — Lifetime Fleet (vehicles, IoT, drones — already operational, EUR 7k+ MRR)

### The EUR 333,000 Edge

Competitors route all AI inference through cloud APIs. Lifetime deploys NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano devices at the edge — construction sites, factory floors, maritime vessels. Annual savings: EUR 333,000 per deployment vs. cloud-only competitors. Hardware ROI payback: under 2 months.

### 16 Industries, One Stack

**Tier 1 (critical):** Power & heat, iron & steel, cement & lime, chemicals, aviation, maritime, road transport, construction.

**Tier 2 (important):** Agriculture, waste management, aluminium, pulp & paper, petroleum refining, food & beverage, glass & ceramics, mining.
