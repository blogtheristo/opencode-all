ENTRY TIER · 3× DGX Spark

On-Prem Process Co-Pilot for Kemira

Frontier reasoning at the chemical plant edge — 100% on-prem

Positioning. This is the entry tier of the DGX Spark edge family. The 4× flagship runs 753B GLM-5.2 with a 1M-token context for the most documentation-heavy assets; this 3× build trades that ceiling for a lower price and a single-site footprint, and upgrades to 4× by adding one node.

Assumptions. Kemira facts are limited to publicly known ones (Finnish chemicals company — water solutions and pulp & paper, HQ Helsinki, proprietary formulations, EU-regulated production). The scenario, KPIs, and costs are illustrative planning figures, not measured Kemira data; Stage 1 replaces them with site-actuals.

Executive Summary

In continuous and batch chemical production, an unplanned line trip or a run of off-spec batches costs thousands to tens of thousands of euros per hour. The answer is scattered across historian/SCADA streams, SOPs, batch records, equipment manuals, SDS, and REACH dossiers — and the most valuable of these, formulations and process recipes, are trade secrets that must never leave the plant network.

The On-Prem Process Co-Pilot is a 3× NVIDIA DGX Spark cluster at a single Kemira site: 384 GB unified memory under ~0.75 kW, a 200–300B-class open MoE with a long context window, turning a deviation into a precise, source-cited disposition in seconds — no data leaving Kemira's network. A ~$18,000 outlay pays for itself by avoiding ~1–2 hours of unplanned stoppage.

The Problem (Process-Chemicals Context)

  • Documentation crisis. Root-cause sits across hundreds of thousands of pages — P&IDs, SOPs, batch records, O&M manuals, SDS/REACH, years of historian trends. No engineer fuses that under shift-time pressure.
  • Trade-secret & sovereignty constraint. Formulations are crown-jewel IP. Sending telemetry and recipe context to a public cloud fails on IP, process-safety (Seveso), and EU data-residency grounds. OT is segmented from IT by design.
  • Latency constraint. Shift engineers need an answer at the panel in seconds, not a remote API round-trip.

The Solution: On-Prem Process Co-Pilot

  • Hardware. Three DGX Spark units via a 100/200 GbE switch (ConnectX-7) — 384 GB unified memory, ~0.75 kW, fed from site power + UPS.
  • Model. A 200–300B-class open MoE at NVFP4. At ~235B / 4-bit, weights ≈ 118 GB, leaving ~260 GB for a long (256K–512K) context plus system tasks.
  • Inference engine. vLLM over NCCL, tensor or pipeline parallelism across the three nodes (see TP=3 caveat).

Operational Workflow

  1. Ingestion. On a deviation, the shift engineer drops the historian window (24–48 h) plus the line's SOPs, recent batch records, and equipment manual into the Co-Pilot.
  2. Contextual analysis. A long context lets the model read procedures, batch history, and telemetry together — exact data, not lossy RAG.
  3. Actionable output. In seconds, a source-cited disposition:
“Conductivity drift on Line 2 polymer feed began 02:40, consistent with the dosing-pump cavitation signature in SOP-114 §6.3. Off-spec risk on the next two batches. Verify suction-side strainer ΔP, re-prime per SOP-114, and hold batch B-2287 for QC retest before release.”

Not 4×: the 753B / 1M flagship needs 512 GB (at 3× the weights leave only ~7.5 GB — no KV-cache room). Not 2×: 256 GB squeezes long-context KV cache and leaves no headroom; 3× buys comfortable context and a clean upgrade path.

Assumptions & Caveats

  1. Open-weight model, not the GLM-5.2 flagship. Confirm exact model, NVFP4 checkpoint, commercial license, and context length before purchase.
  2. TP=3 sharding constraint. Tensor parallelism across 3 GPUs needs attention-head / intermediate dims divisible by 3; otherwise use pipeline parallelism or TP=2 + a third node for overflow. Validate on the bench.
  3. Long context ≠ 1M. This tier targets 256K–512K — one line's SOP/batch/manual bundle plus a telemetry window. Million-token fleet corpora are the 4× flagship's job.
  4. ROI inputs are operator-specific. The 40% figure and "1–2 hours = payback" are illustrative; Stage 1 measures Kemira-actuals.
  5. OT integration is the real work. Read-only historian/SCADA access, OT/IT segmentation, and process-safety sign-off gate the project — not the hardware.

Upgrade Path

Start at 3× for one site. Add a fourth Spark to reach 512 GB and unlock the 753B GLM-5.2 / 1M-token flagship when a site's documentation depth justifies it — same cabinet, same network, no rip-and-replace.
