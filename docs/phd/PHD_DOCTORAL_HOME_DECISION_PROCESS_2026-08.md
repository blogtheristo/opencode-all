## 3. What the topic brief has to test (three questions, not one)

Per HIC direction, the brief at `docs/gtm/TECOSA_TORNGREN_RESEARCH_TOPIC_BRIEF_2026-08.md` exists to test three separate things, and a "yes" to one does not imply "yes" to the others:

1. **Does Törngren want to (co-)supervise this specific topic, through KTH's formal structure?** — a personal, not institutional, question. TECoSA cannot itself agree to supervise anyone — see correction below.
2. **Does the topic fit a KTH doctoral (or licentiate/postdoc) subject** (ITM / Mechatronics, or wherever the relevant school turns out to be)? TECoSA is Törngren's research centre — the environment and industrial-collaboration structure he could offer — not the admitting body. Admission, the subject area, supervisors, the ISP and the degree are **KTH's**, exactly as they were for other KTH PhDs supervised in TECoSA's orbit (e.g. Vishnu Moothedath, supervised by KTH professors James Gross and György Dán, not by "TECoSA").
3. **Is there a real funding and employment model?** — per KTH's own doctoral-studies guidance (HIC-supplied, not independently fetched — `kth.se/en/studies/phd/become-a-phd-student/introduction-to-doctoral-studies-at-kth-1.527044`), a standard KTH PhD is a **four-year full-time degree**, requiring **at least two supervisors (one designated main supervisor)** and a **personal Individual Study Plan (ISP)** (`intra.kth.se/en/forskning/doktorand/forskarstudier/handledning-och-individuell-studieplan-1.679302`). **Correction, ground truth from Törngren's own 22.07.2026 email:** he does not consider the standard 4-year form a good fit here, given the ~3-year EU-project horizon, and proposed **a licentiate degree or an industrial postdoc (2+1 years)** instead, plus a **~250 KSEK/year KTH desk-fee**. This is real, unprompted input from the actual supervisor candidate — more concrete than the generic KTH guidance, and it should anchor step 4 of §2 rather than the generic four-year assumption.

## 4. Why this makes the LUT comparison fair

**Kari Smolander's retirement, by itself, does not decide LUT out.** The disqualifying condition is narrower and needs to be checked explicitly rather than assumed:

- **If LUT can name a continuing main supervisor** — someone who commits to the full four-year horizon, not Smolander personally — the LUT track remains a structurally safe anchor, and everything already built there (the locked six RQs, RQ5/RQ6 grounded in DWS's own production system per `2026-07-25-memo-smolander-question-status.md` §5) carries forward without disruption.
- **Without such a named, continuing supervisor**, LUT is **not** a safe four-year anchor, for exactly the same structural reason a KTH PhD needs a real main supervisor — a doctoral degree cannot be safely anchored to a person who will not be there for its duration.

**This has not been checked yet.** It is an open question for the LUT thread (Smolander correspondence, `docs/lut/2026-07-25-memo-smolander-question-status.md`), not a settled fact. The correct next action on the LUT side is to ask directly whether LUT has, or intends to name, a continuing main supervisor for this line of work — not to treat retirement as an automatic disqualifier, and not to treat "Kari is still active today" as an automatic reassurance either.
