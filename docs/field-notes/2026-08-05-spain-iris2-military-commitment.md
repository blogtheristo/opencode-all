## Summary

Spain's Ministry of Defence has committed **up to ~€2 billion (~$2.3bn)** to build a **sovereign multi-orbit military satellite capability** to be integrated into IRIS², making Spain **the largest national financial contributor to IRIS² to date** — larger than Poland's €656M commitment covered in the previous field note. The commitment was presented at Spain's National Committee for Technological Security and Sovereignty (29 July 2026) as one of 15 new Special Modernization Programmes (SMPs) planned for 2026, and represents Spain's largest space-defence investment to date.

**What it funds:** satellite development, ground infrastructure, control systems, user terminals, and long-term operational capabilities — specifically for **protected military communications**, not the civil/governmental broadband framing that dominated the Poland announcement. **Hispasat** (Spanish satellite operator, part of the SpaceRISE consortium alongside SES and Eutelsat) leads development of key IRIS² ground-infrastructure elements and the constellation's very-low-Earth-orbit (VLEO) segment.

**One-line read:** where Poland's IRIS² commitment was framed as sovereign broadband + resilience infrastructure, Spain's is framed explicitly as **military satellite sovereignty** — a cleaner, more direct match to Aegis Defence than the Warsaw signing was.

| Aegis fit              | Sovereign AI application layer over a resilient civil bearer (paths A–E, prior note) | Same five connection paths apply, but the **defence-tier framing (KYA human-signature ceilings, Article 12 audit for classified/controlled data)** is a direct match rather than an analogy |

The five connection paths already mapped in the Poland note (A: sovereign application layer, B: sovereign backhaul for edge-cloud inference, C: ground-segment NIS2/CER governance, D: national go-to-market beachhead, E: cross-vertical intelligence over a resilient bearer) **all apply unchanged** — Spain's commitment doesn't require new mechanisms, it sharpens which one leads. For a defence-tier pitch specifically, **path C (ground-segment governance) and path A (sovereign application layer) are now the strongest**, since the funded scope explicitly includes control systems and user terminals — exactly the kind of attached AI/decision system Aegis's KYA engine and Article-12 pipeline are built to govern under a defence posture (human-signature ceilings regardless of trust score, per `Legal/KYA_STANDARD_v1.md` §14).

**Programme-scale context, worth noting but not load-bearing for the Aegis thesis:** secondary sources this session describe IRIS² adding 66 early-delivery LEO satellites (launches from 2029) ahead of a full ~264-satellite system around 2032 — a modification to the ~290-satellite/2030 timeline in the July note. **Not reconciled here** — flag as a moving target if IRIS² timeline figures are cited externally; verify against a primary source before using either figure in an external deck.
