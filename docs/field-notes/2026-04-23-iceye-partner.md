## 3. Product — Core Capabilities (as stated)

- SAR satellite constellation operates **day and night, in any weather**.
- Latency: intelligence to customer **within days** (vs. "weeks or months" for optical retrospective mapping, per Andy Read quote).
- Operating model: delivered **two ways** — (a) as intelligence service to customers; (b) as **sovereign system** where governments operate their own constellation.
- Stated customer segments: defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance, emergency management.
- Named product: **Deforestation Solution** (the SKU deployed to JGI in eastern DRC).

## 4. Partnership Announced 22 April 2025 — ICEYE × Jane Goodall Institute (JGI)

### Parties

- **ICEYE** — see §2.
- **Jane Goodall Institute USA (JGI)** — founded 1977; 30 offices globally; Roots & Shoots program active in 75 countries; VP Conservation Science: **Dr. Lilian Pintea**; janegoodall.org, rootsandshoots.org.

### Scope

- Deploy ICEYE's **Deforestation Solution** across conservation corridors in the Congo Basin.
- **Initial focus:** eastern Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC).
- **Expansion:** Gombe National Park, western Tanzania (site of Goodall's 1960 research; JGI Gombe Stream Research Center).

### Threat surface being monitored

- Illegal mining
- Illegal logging
- Settlement expansion
- General forest disturbance
- Bushmeat hunting (context — not directly monitored by SAR)
- Habitat loss and landslide/flash-flood risk around Gombe

### Wildlife at stake

- **70,000–100,000 chimpanzees** estimated in eastern DRC rainforests.
- Congo Basin holds the most diverse ape population in the world.
- Gombe: Goodall's original chimpanzee research population — significant genetic-diversity concern from habitat fragmentation.

### Operating-environment facts

- Eastern DRC: persistent cloud cover renders optical satellites unreliable for weeks–months at a time.
- Dense tropical canopy blocks conventional optical observation of ground activity.
- Active conflict makes sustained ground monitoring dangerous and resource-intensive.
- JGI field teams and local partners provide ground-truth integration with the SAR intelligence.

### Named spokespeople and titles

- **Andy Read** — VP of Government Solutions, ICEYE.
- **Dr. Lilian Pintea** — Vice President of Conservation Science, Jane Goodall Institute.

### Direct quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> "SAR cuts through the Congo Basin's persistent cloud cover and delivers intelligence to the teams that need it most within days rather than weeks or months. The real differentiator is a step change in persistence. Instead of retrospective mapping, we provide continuous, site-level intelligence—giving conservation teams on the ground the timely insight they need to act."
> — Andy Read, VP of Government Solutions, ICEYE

> "For 25 years, JGI has converted satellite data into community-led conservation action. By integrating ICEYE's advanced SAR technology, we can now see through the clouds to provide near-real-time insights for vital ecosystems, further enabling our conservation partners with actionable information of unprecedented precision."
> — Dr. Lilian Pintea, Vice President of Conservation Science, Jane Goodall Institute
