   - TECoSA's headline noun is literally **"Trustworthy … Edge Computing Systems"** — the same two load-bearing words as the DWS **edge control room capital** thesis and the DWS **trustworthy-agent** posture. 3. **TECoSA is a consortium, not a single lab.** ~19 industrial partners means the value is not only Törngren's group but a **pre-assembled Nordic industrial network** around trustworthy edge systems — a potential channel, not just a research contact.

## Positioning — how to talk to him (board guidance, 14.07.2026)

**Lead with trustworthy edge computing, not compliance.** His register is _"safety evidence"_ and _"trustworthiness,"_ not _"regulatory compliance."_ Regulation (EU AI Act, NIS2) is the _outcome_; his frame is the _engineering discipline_ that produces it. Pitching "we help you comply" reads as vendor; pitching "we generate audit-grade safety evidence for agentic edge systems" reads as peer.

**The concrete bridge:** map the DWS **Article-12 logging pipeline** onto his **safety-argumentation / audit-grade-evidence** framework. In his world, an assurance case is only as strong as the evidence chain feeding it; DWS's KYA trust scoring + Article-12-grade agent-decision logging is exactly such an evidence chain for _autonomous/agentic_ systems at the edge. Same idea, his vocabulary.

**Two specific hooks to name in the reply:**

- His **position paper on trustworthy edge computing in industrial applications** — signals we did the homework and we're on his exact turf.
- The **AD-EYE testbed** as an analogue: what AD-EYE does for automated-driving _evaluation_, DWS aims to do for sovereign edge-AI **verification** — a shared "evaluate/verify before you trust" philosophy.
