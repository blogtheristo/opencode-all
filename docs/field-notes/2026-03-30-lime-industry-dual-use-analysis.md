## Context

A fire at a Nordkalk facility prompted analysis of the lime (calcium carbonate) industry as a dual-use sector. Nordkalk's communications director Tiina Roine confirmed the facility. The fire may have originated from a compressor room in a decommissioned building. No spread to other production facilities, no injuries, no hazardous materials stored in the building.

Nordkalk describes itself as Northern Europe's leading limestone company with operations in Finland, Sweden, Poland, and Spain. The company is part of the international Sigmaroc group.

## Dual-Use Classification

The lime industry is a classic dual-use sector. While limestone products are not weapons, they are critical raw materials for industries essential to defense and national security.

### Civilian / Commercial Uses (Primary Business)

- **Environmental protection**: Neutralizing acidity in lakes and forests (liming), flue-gas desulphurisation in power plants
- **Agriculture**: Improving soil pH for better crop yields
- **Construction**: Key ingredient in cement, mortar, and asphalt
- **Water treatment**: Purifying drinking water and treating wastewater
- **Paper and glass**: Essential for large-scale industrial manufacturing

### Strategic and Industrial Dual-Use Applications

- **Steel production**: Lime is used as a flux to remove impurities (phosphorus, sulfur) during steelmaking. Tanks, ships, and artillery require high-quality steel, which requires lime.
- **Chemical industry**: Precursor for manufacturing various chemicals, including those used in explosives or specialized polymers.
- **Defense industrial base**: Disruption in lime supply chains can paralyze a nation's steel production and heavy manufacturing capabilities.
