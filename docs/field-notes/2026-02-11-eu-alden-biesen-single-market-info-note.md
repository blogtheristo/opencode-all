## Summary

Tomorrow (12 February 2026), EU leaders will gather in Alden Biesen, Belgium, for an informal retreat to rethink the EU's approach to competitiveness, autonomy, and prosperity.

The central thesis: **the Single Market is Europe's true superpower.** With 450 million consumers, it is the most powerful card the EU holds — and leaders are converging on a strategy to use it to its fullest potential.

## Key Points

1. **Single Market = 450 Million Consumers**: The EU Single Market is the world's largest integrated consumer base with harmonized regulation. This is not just a trade zone — it is a regulatory superpower that sets global standards (the "Brussels Effect").

2. **Competitiveness Rethink**: EU leaders are moving beyond regulation-as-burden toward regulation-as-competitive-advantage. Companies that are EU-compliant by design gain privileged access to this 450M consumer market.

3. **Autonomy Agenda**: The retreat signals acceleration of EU strategic autonomy — reducing dependency on non-EU technology, energy, and supply chains. This directly strengthens the case for EU-native platforms.

4. **Prosperity Through Standards**: The EU's regulatory framework (Fit for 55, ETS, CBAM, CSRD, AI Act) creates a compliance moat that protects EU-native companies from foreign competitors unwilling to invest in compliance.
