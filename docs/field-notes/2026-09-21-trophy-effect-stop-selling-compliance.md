## The Insight

### The Trophy Effect

A funded task force must declare victory. That is its job. Copying a six-to-eight-month-ahead evangelist fills the announcement. Payday is the reward function. None of that requires a signed artifact that actually moves money.

So **AI × compliance** is now a _status good_ (the trophy the budget already bought), not a _workflow good_ (the factory that ships the artifact that unlocks a tender, a shipment, an invoice, or a grant). You cannot sell a status good to the holder of the trophy. You sell the workflow good to whoever still has money frozen.

June 2026 already moved from fines-avoidance to tender-velocity (`docs/field-notes/2026-06-10-offensive-positioning-dws10.md`; Brand §22 Offensive block). That was a **half-pivot**. It still sells the noun _compliance_. The trophy-holder already owns that noun.

The full pivot: **first-touch never says compliance.** First-touch says signed work that releases money. Compliance remains inside the engine (KYA, Article 12, ontology). It is not the offer.

### What they copy vs what they cannot copy

| Copied on payday                              | Not copied in a standing committee              |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Evangelist advice, vocabulary, slide doctrine | Running Agent Outcome Factory (Pillar 2)        |
| "We have an AI task force" announcements      | KYA-gated, Article-12-logged signed artifacts   |
| The _idea_ of AI-for-compliance               | Governed ontology (noise-floor line, Brand §22) |
| Six-to-eight month lag, loudly                | Plant Capital + Perception Capital (Brand §23)  |

Keep giving the doctrine in public. That is demand-creation, and they will copy it. Sell only the uncopied loop.

### Public-sector door that is actually open

VTV 10/2026 (repo primary): VM's 10 M€ programme and Sitra require _monistettavuus / skaalautuvuus_; the state has **no operating model** to productise or share AI solutions; Valtorin and DVV's AI mandate is unclear even to VM (`docs/PHD_VTV_RAHOITUSOHJELMA_ANALYYSI.md` §3).

The trophy closed the _compliance_ door at Valtori. The _replication factory_ door is the one VTV named and the state still does not have. That buyer is the programme owner who must show productivity and replicability (R12 / R23), not General Counsel (current §4 Door #1 = R05).
