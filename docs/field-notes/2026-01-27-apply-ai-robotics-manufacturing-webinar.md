## Event Overview

This webinar analyzes ways to strengthen the development and deployment of AI-driven solutions in:
- **Industrial automation**
- **Human-robot collaboration**
- **Digital twins**

The session explores how industry and other stakeholders can contribute to shaping priorities for Europe's robotics and manufacturing ecosystems. It starts with robotics as a cross-sector technology and then zooms in on how robotics and AI-driven solutions can support advanced manufacturing.

## Agenda Topics

### 1. Robotics Vision & Cross-Sector Impact
Latest advancements in robotics and the Commission's vision for the technology's impact across sectors:
- Healthcare
- Agriculture
- Services
- Manufacturing

### 2. Software AI + Physical AI for Manufacturing
How manufacturing can benefit from both:
- **Software AI:** Process optimization, predictive analytics, quality control
- **Physical AI:** Robotics, automation, embodied intelligence
- **Outcomes:** Improved productivity, flexibility, customization of production systems

### 3. Pilots to Scalable Deployment
Moving from pilots to scalable deployment of AI-driven solutions:
- Robotics-enabled applications
- Qualification challenges
- Integration challenges

### 4. EC Support & Private Sector Engagement
- Support from EC in development and deployment
- Opportunities for private-sector engagement in defining Europe's robotics agenda

### EU AI Act Compliance (August 2026)

This webinar aligns with DWS6's EU AI Act compliance requirements:

**Article 12: Automatic Logging**
- Robotics systems in manufacturing require comprehensive event logging
- Digital twins need full traceability of AI decisions
- Physical AI actions must be automatically logged

**Reasoning Lineage Documentation**
- Human-robot collaboration decisions require traceable reasoning chains
- Manufacturing AI outputs must include provenance metadata
- Integration with Lifetime World Model (Memory) for compliance
