## The Structural Thesis

1. **Software-native + AI-native + vertically integrated beats 60-year-old incumbents** — proven in the most incumbent-protected market on earth (Anduril vs Lockheed/Raytheon). The playbook is validated, not theoretical.
2. **Win by matching the buyer's doctrine shift, not by winning feature bake-offs.** Anduril didn't out-build the F-35; the Pentagon changed doctrine to "intelligent mass" and Anduril was already shaped right.
3. **Procurement vehicle > deal.** The $20B Army framework (120+ buys → one) is the real prize. Become the framework, not a line item.

## What Transfers to DWS — and What Doesn't

4. **The mechanism does not transfer.** "Intelligent mass" works because drones are physical/attritable. Software agents aren't — marginal cost ~0 — so a "fleet of cheap agents" is NOT a moat. It's a commodity race anyone wins in a weekend.
5. **DWS's doctrine shift is the opposite shape: accountable, sovereign outcomes.** The scarce/hard thing wins — EU AI Act Art. 12 lineage, EU residency, the buyer's verdict — not volume.
6. **The open seat is the EU-sovereign version of the playbook.** Anduril (US-native) structurally can't take it (CLOUD Act, EU AI Act). That gap is the DWS wedge.

## The Drift Trap (the session's real lesson)

7. **Never market on a commodity number.** Agent count is the enterprise-software equivalent of bragging about lines of code. (The same trap had caught the industry count and the moat count.)
8. **Sell the orchestration layer + the outcome — not the components.** Agents are the swappable edge, like Anduril's drones under Lattice OS.
9. **The "bigger drift":** we wandered from buyer-relevant moats into AI machinery (agent skills, workflows, Gödel-style learning loops). Great to build — but NOT what the buyer (Ari Hirvonen / Outokumpu: CBAM/CSRD/Scope 3) is buying as DWS IQ 6.
10. **Reframe every moat with the Ari test:** would he quote it in a procurement meeting? If it's "risk removed / leverage gained," yes. If it's engineering poetry, no. (Result: 19 moats + Layer 0, dual buyer×investor labels.)

## The Root Cause — and the Fix

11. **Things drift when they're not registered as canonical.** Agent count, prices, and industry count were drift-protected; the 21 moats — the actual competitive core — were never in the truth registry. So they drifted.
12. **Don't blend distinct facts — surface them.** Platform = 25 industries; Aegis tier = 21. A blanket "fix" nearly merged them. Lock the distinction; never average two truths.
13. **Lock it or lose it:** register the fact, deprecate the wrong phrasings, log the drift. That is what stops the next person re-introducing it.
