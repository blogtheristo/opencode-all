# 42CAP — Investment Principles & DWS Fit Map

42CAP (Thomas Wilke, GP) frames itself as **peers amongst entrepreneurs** — operators who built and exited (eCircle → €250M to Teradata), backing the brave with conviction. This note records their principles verbatim-in-summary and maps DWS IQ 6 to each, so the Wilke pitch leads with **their** language, not ours.

## 1. Their principles (summary)

**Focus principles**

- **Agility drivers** — verticalized solutions: Consumerized, XaaS, Point Solutions, Open APIs → simpler, better-connected business tools-as-a-service.
- **Seductive solutions** — must solve a burning need / staggering daily pain; create a "wow"; satisfying to use.
- **Whale & deer hunters** — both enterprise B2B AI _and_ SME/Prosumer markets.
- **Data-driven & technology-enabled** — AI-first _and_ AI-enabled; invest in both application and infrastructure layers.

**General principles**

- **Seed stage** — post-product pre/early-revenue (PMF bets) _or_ scaling bets with initial revenue; sometimes pre-seed.
- **Critical market potential** — only companies that can reach **€500m+ valuation in 7–8 years**; addressable market **€1b+ in recurring revenue at high gross margins**.
- **Europe focus, global ambition.**
- **Product first** — reflected in the founders.
- **Holistic, complementary teams** — determined, entrepreneurial, global ambition.
- **Significant ownership stakes** — concise portfolio, support each team fully (not spray-and-pray).
- **Sustainable round size** — fund rounds that grant **~18 months runway**; founder focus on building, not fundraising.
- **Solid entrepreneurship** — build a great company, not paper money.

## 3. Bars we must clear (be honest, prepare answers)

1. **€1b+ recurring TAM / €500m+ in 7–8 yrs.** This is their hard gate. We must show the math credibly: 25 EU-regulated industries × mandatory CSRD/CBAM/EPBD filing × recurring SaaS, at 95% margin. Prepare a defensible TAM build — post-Omnibus, fewer than ~5,000 large CSRD filers (high ACV) + the value-chain/Scope-3 pull + CBAM/EPBD (see `TAM_BUILD.md`) — not a hand-wave, and not the stale "49,000" figure.
2. **~18-month runway / sustainable round size.** €650K SAFE alone likely under-funds 18 months at their bar. This is _why_ the **upsize to a €1–2M priced seed with 42CAP leading** is the right ask — it satisfies their own "sustainable round size" principle. Lead with that, not the €650K floor.
3. **Significant ownership stake.** They want a concise portfolio and a meaningful stake. At ~€3.8M cap a €1–2M lead implies ~20–35% — be ready for that dilution; it aligns with their model, not against it.
4. **Product-first founder signal.** He evaluates the moat and the product himself, operator-to-operator. The architecture deep-dive must be ready.
