## What Happened

On 30 March 2026, the European Commission adopted a **EUR 1.5 billion work programme** under the European Defence Industry Programme (EDIP). First calls for proposals opened on the EU Funding & Tenders Portal on 31 March 2026.

### Funding Breakdown

| Allocation | Amount | Aegis Relevance |
|------------|--------|-----------------|
| Production ramp-up (counter-drone, missiles, ammunition) | EUR 700M+ | Supply chain monitoring for production scale-up — Aegis cross-vertical signal detection |
| Ukraine Support Instrument (missiles, ammunition, drones) | EUR 260M | Dual-use export control compliance — Aegis ComplianceAgent (EU Reg. 2021/821) |
| Defence Projects of Common Interest | EUR 325M | Large-scale collaborative projects — Aegis multi-tenant coordination layer |
| Joint procurement (counter-drone, air/missile defence, ground/naval) | EUR 240M | Procurement compliance across member states — sovereign AI requirement |
| FAST equity for defence SMEs and start-ups | EUR 100M | **Direct funding opportunity for Lifetime Oy** |
| BraveTech EU initiative (Ukraine + EU innovation) | EUR 35.3M | Innovation programme for defence start-ups — secondary opportunity |

## Directions for Aegis

### 1. Apply to FAST (EUR 100M equity fund) — PRIORITY

**What:** Fund Accelerating Defence Supply Chains Transformation — equity support for defence start-ups, SMEs, and small mid-caps.

**Why Aegis qualifies:**
- Sovereign AI platform (Finnish-hosted, zero CLOUD Act exposure)
- Defence supply chain intelligence across 21 EU-regulated industries
- Existing dual-use capability proven (Nordkalk lime cascade = steel → cement → defence industrial base)
- EU AI Act Article 12 native compliance — no competitor has this
- Price point (EUR 72K Year 1) accessible to defence SMEs who are the FAST target

**Action items:**
1. Monitor EU Funding & Tenders Portal for FAST-specific call text
2. Prepare Aegis as "defence supply chain transformation" tool — reframe from compliance to resilience
3. Align investor memo language with EDIP terminology: "production capacity," "supply chain resilience," "technological advancement"
4. Highlight cross-vertical signal propagation as EDIP-relevant: one disruption affects multiple defence supply chains simultaneously

### 2. Position Aegis for Production Ramp-Up Monitoring (EUR 700M+)

**The opportunity:** EUR 700M+ is going to scale up counter-drone systems, missiles, and ammunition production. Every production facility receiving EDIP funds will need:

- **CBAM compliance** — Imported steel, aluminium, and chemicals face carbon border tariffs. Aegis VulcanAgent and GaiaAgent calculate this automatically
- **ETS exposure tracking** — Production scale-up = more emissions = higher ETS costs. Aegis tracks this across the full supply chain
- **NIS2 compliance** — Defence production facilities are critical infrastructure under NIS2. Aegis monitors compliance posture
- **Cross-vertical supply chain visibility** — When one raw material supplier fails, Aegis traces the cascade across all affected industries

**Action items:**
1. Build a "defence production ramp-up" use case document showing how Aegis monitors EDIP-funded production
2. Map counter-drone / missile / ammunition supply chains to existing Aegis industry agents
3. Add a defence production monitoring dashboard template to the Firehorse Report Engine

### 3. Target EDIP Joint Procurement Participants (EUR 240M)

**The opportunity:** Member states + Norway jointly procuring counter-drone, air defence, and combat systems. Joint procurement = multiple national regulations applying simultaneously = exactly Aegis's cross-jurisdictional strength.

**Action items:**
1. Identify which member states are leading procurement consortia (public EDIP documentation)
2. Prepare national regulatory overlay capability — Aegis already handles EU-level regulations, add member state-specific defence procurement rules
3. Pitch Aegis as the compliance backbone for multi-nation procurement consortia

### 4. Ukraine Track — Export Control Compliance (EUR 260M + EUR 35.3M)

**The opportunity:** For the first time, Ukrainian companies gain direct access to EU defence programme funding. This creates a massive dual-use export control compliance requirement:

- EU Regulation 2021/821 (dual-use export control) applies to every EU↔Ukraine defence transfer
- Aegis ComplianceAgent already covers dual-use export control scoring
- Sovereignty credentials matter: EU defence agencies need EU-sovereign tools to manage Ukraine cooperation

**Action items:**
1. Add Ukraine-specific export control rules to ComplianceAgent (Regulation 2021/821 Annex I categories)
2. Prepare a "EDIP Ukraine Support Instrument — Compliance Guide" positioning Aegis as the compliance tool for EU-Ukraine defence cooperation
3. Contact DEFINE Network about EDIP-Ukraine alignment
