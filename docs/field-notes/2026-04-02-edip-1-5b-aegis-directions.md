## What Happened

On 30 March 2026, the European Commission adopted a **EUR 1.5 billion work programme** under the European Defence Industry Programme (EDIP). First calls for proposals opened on the EU Funding & Tenders Portal on 31 March 2026.

### Funding Breakdown

| Allocation | Amount | Aegis Relevance |
|------------|--------|-----------------|
| Production ramp-up (counter-drone, missiles, ammunition) | EUR 700M+ | Supply chain monitoring for production scale-up — Aegis cross-vertical signal detection |
| Ukraine Support Instrument (missiles, ammunition, drones) | EUR 260M | Dual-use export control compliance — Aegis ComplianceAgent (EU Reg. 2021/821) |
| Defence Projects of Common Interest | EUR 325M | Large-scale collaborative projects — Aegis multi-tenant coordination layer |
| Joint procurement (counter-drone, air/missile defence, ground/naval) | EUR 240M | Procurement compliance across member states — sovereign AI requirement |
| FAST equity for defence SMEs and start-ups | EUR 100M | **Direct funding opportunity for Lifetime Oy** |
| BraveTech EU initiative (Ukraine + EU innovation) | EUR 35.3M | Innovation programme for defence start-ups — secondary opportunity |

## Boardy's Honest Assessment

Before the action plan: three of these angles are real but at different temperatures. Don't spread thin.

| Angle | Strength | Honest Read |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **FAST fund (EUR 100M)** | Strongest | Direct equity for defence SMEs. Aegis is a textbook fit: sovereign, dual-use, EU-hosted. The blocker is access — can you get in front of the right EDF/EDIP programme officer before the window closes? |
| **Production ramp-up (EUR 700M+)** | Good but indirect | Real compliance need (CBAM + ETS + NIS2), but positions Aegis as a compliance add-on to a facility build, not a primary procurement target. Better as a **customer acquisition angle** than a funding angle. Sell to the facilities that win EDIP grants. |
| **Joint procurement (EUR 240M)** | Niche, defensible | Cross-jurisdictional regs is the moat argument. But EUR 240M is infrastructure money, not software money. You can't extract value without a **prime contractor relationship** (Rheinmetall, MBDA, Kongsberg, etc.). Without a prime, this is aspirational. |

**Bottom line:** FAST is the only angle where Lifetime Oy can apply directly. The other two are customer-sourcing strategies, not funding strategies. Don't confuse the two.

## Directions for Aegis

### 1. Apply to FAST (EUR 100M equity fund) — ONLY DIRECT FUNDING ANGLE

**What:** Fund Accelerating Defence Supply Chains Transformation — equity support for defence start-ups, SMEs, and small mid-caps.

**Why Aegis qualifies:**
- Sovereign AI platform (Finnish-hosted, zero CLOUD Act exposure)
- Defence supply chain intelligence across 21 EU-regulated industries
- Existing dual-use capability proven (Nordkalk lime cascade = steel → cement → defence industrial base)
- EU AI Act Article 12 native compliance — no competitor has this
- Price point (EUR 72K Year 1) accessible to defence SMEs who are the FAST target

**Critical question:** What is the FAST application timeline? Calls opened 31 March 2026. If the window is weeks not months, this is urgent. If it's an ongoing programme, there's time to prepare properly.

**Action items:**
1. **URGENT:** Check EU Funding & Tenders Portal for FAST-specific call text and deadline
2. Prepare Aegis as "defence supply chain transformation" tool — reframe from compliance to resilience
3. Align investor memo language with EDIP terminology: "production capacity," "supply chain resilience," "technological advancement"
4. Highlight cross-vertical signal propagation as EDIP-relevant: one disruption affects multiple defence supply chains simultaneously

### 2. Sell to EDIP-Funded Facilities (EUR 700M+ creates customers, not funding)

**Reframed:** Don't try to get EDIP production ramp-up money directly. Instead, the EUR 700M+ flowing into counter-drone, missile, and ammunition production **creates your customer base.** Every facility that wins EDIP funding will need:

- **CBAM compliance** — Imported steel, aluminium, and chemicals face carbon border tariffs. Aegis VulcanAgent and GaiaAgent calculate this automatically
- **ETS exposure tracking** — Production scale-up = more emissions = higher ETS costs
- **NIS2 compliance** — Defence production facilities are critical infrastructure under NIS2
- **Cross-vertical supply chain visibility** — When one raw material supplier fails, Aegis traces the cascade

**The play:** Wait for EDIP grant winners to be announced. Then approach them: "You just got EUR X million to scale production. Your compliance obligations just multiplied. Here's the sovereign tool that covers your full regulatory stack for EUR 72K Year 1."

**Action items:**
1. Track EDIP grant announcements — build a prospect list from winners
2. Build a "post-EDIP compliance checklist" one-pager showing what grant recipients must now comply with
3. Map counter-drone / missile / ammunition supply chains to existing Aegis industry agents

### 3. Joint Procurement — Park Unless You Find a Prime (EUR 240M)

**Honest read:** Cross-jurisdictional compliance is Aegis's moat, but EUR 240M is hardware procurement money. Software compliance tools don't get budget line items in joint procurement contracts unless a prime contractor brings you in.

**Gate:** Do you have a relationship with any prime (Rheinmetall, MBDA, Kongsberg, Saab, BAE Systems)?

- **If yes:** Position Aegis as their compliance layer for multi-nation procurement. Valuable.
- **If no:** Park this angle. Don't spend cycles chasing procurement consortia without an inside track. Revisit after Arctic 15 if you make defence industry contacts.

### 4. Ukraine Track — Export Control Compliance (EUR 260M + EUR 35.3M)

**The opportunity is real but narrow:** Ukrainian companies gaining EU defence programme access creates export control compliance demand. But the question is who buys the compliance tool — the Ukrainian companies (unlikely budget), or the EU agencies managing the transfers?

**Action items (low-effort, high-optionality):**
1. Verify ComplianceAgent already covers Regulation 2021/821 Annex I dual-use categories — if yes, this is marketing, not engineering
2. Contact DEFINE Network about EDIP-Ukraine alignment — low cost, could open doors
3. Do NOT build Ukraine-specific features speculatively. Wait for a customer signal.

**Current framing (INVESTOR_MEMO_AEGIS.md v1.1):** Defence supply chain resilience + sovereign AI.

**Enhanced framing with EDIP:** "EUR 1.5B of EU defence spending just dropped. Every recipient needs sovereign compliance infrastructure. Aegis is the only EU-sovereign platform that covers the full regulatory stack in a single tenant. And we're applying to FAST — the EUR 100M equity fund inside EDIP specifically for defence SMEs."

**Key data points for investor conversations:**

- EUR 100M FAST fund targets exactly what Aegis is — defence tech SME with sovereign credentials
- EDIP grant recipients become Aegis customers — EUR 700M+ creates addressable market
