## How to read this dossier

- **PhD relevance** = why this connection matters for the LUT PhD's agentic/sovereign-EDA line (§14.41), as strategic analysis — not a claim of any existing relationship.

## 1 · Workshop leadership (organizers)

### Meng Li — Peking University · *Workshop Chair, Lunch presenter*
- **Field:** Efficient and secure machine learning, hardware-aware neural architecture, privacy-preserving ML, and ML-driven EDA. Trained in the UT Austin EDA lineage (David Z. Pan group); industry background in large-scale ML systems.
- **PhD relevance:** Bridges efficient-ML-hardware and EDA — the exact seam the §14.41 "agentic EDA" argument sits on. Peking University tie also matters for the non-EU/EU sovereignty framing (§14.23). **Priority A.**

### Jiaqi Gu — Arizona State University · *Co-Chair*
- **Field:** Photonic computing, ML-for-EDA, differentiable / automated design flows, and optical neural networks; hardware–AI co-design. UT Austin EDA lineage.
- **PhD relevance:** ML-for-EDA and automated design-space exploration are the mechanism behind "from tools to agents"; photonic/quantum-adjacent design ties to the open quantum-EDA tutorial thread (§14.41). **Priority A.**

### Nan Wu — George Washington University · *Co-Chair; organizer of the DAC tutorial "From Vibe Coding to Silicon"*
- **Field:** ML-for-EDA, high-level synthesis (HLS), and **agentic systems for hardware design** — the C→synthesis-ready-RTL agent refinement flow analysed directly in §14.41.
- **PhD relevance:** The single most direct thesis anchor in this cohort — the "From Vibe Coding to Silicon" tutorial *is* the §14.41 load-bearing citation. First-choice academic contact for the agentic-EDA line. **Priority A.**

## 2 · Featured speakers

### Ulf Schlichtmann — Technical University of Munich (TUM)
- **Field:** EDA methodology, robust and reliable chip design, timing, and design automation for emerging technologies; long-standing chair of EDA at TUM and a senior European figure in the field.
- **PhD relevance:** Anchor **EU** connection — a European EDA authority for the sovereign-EDA framing (§14.23 / §14.41) and a credible LUT-adjacent academic bridge. **Priority B.**

### Sachin S. Sapatnekar — University of Minnesota
- **Field:** Physical design, timing and statistical analysis, EDA algorithms, thermal/reliability, and ML-for-EDA; ACM/IEEE Fellow and one of the field's most-cited methodologists.
- **PhD relevance:** Foundational EDA network hub — a citation cornerstone whose framing legitimises the verification/timing-agent claims in the EDA business case. **Priority B.**

### David Z. Pan — University of Texas at Austin
- **Field:** Physical design, design-for-manufacturability, ML-for-EDA, and photonic design automation; ACM/IEEE Fellow and doctoral advisor to several rising stars in this very cohort (incl. Meng Li, Jiaqi Gu).
- **PhD relevance:** Central network node — connecting here reaches a whole sub-tree of the agentic/ML-EDA community at once. **Priority A.**

### Yingyan (Celine) Lin — Georgia Institute of Technology
- **Field:** Efficient AI, hardware-efficient ML, and accelerator–algorithm co-design (EfficientML).
- **PhD relevance:** Efficiency-per-watt framing mirrors the DGX Spark "frontier reasoning under ~1 kW" argument in the EDA business case. **Priority B.**

### Hai (Helen) Li — Duke University
- **Field:** Neuromorphic and in-memory computing, ML hardware/systems, and model compression; IEEE Fellow and department leader.
- **PhD relevance:** Senior ML-hardware authority; strong for the "Body/compute" layer of the Brain–Memory–Body–Hands architecture (§14.35). **Priority B.**

### Yuan Xie — Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)
- **Field:** Computer architecture, 3D IC, near-/in-memory computing, and EDA; IEEE Fellow, very senior.

### Weiwen Jiang — George Mason University
- **Field:** Quantum computing + ML co-design, quantum machine learning, and EDA for quantum circuits; hardware–software co-design.

### Puneet Gupta — University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA)
