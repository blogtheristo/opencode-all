## Jurisdiction 3 — what the SG-entity condition actually means

The condition activates **only if shortlisted AND selected**, with a
3-month clock from programme commencement (prior cohort pattern: ~Jan
start → incorporation by ~Apr 2027). Implications worth having on paper
before we're standing in them:

1. **Sovereignty model alignment — this is not a foreign concept to DWS.**
   The three-pillar sovereignty model (`/sovereignty-pillars-assess`)
   already scores architectures across **EU, US, and SG** — Singapore is
   the designed-in third jurisdiction. A Lifetime SG entity would make the
   corporate structure match the product thesis: FI (Lifetime Oy, core
   R&D + IP), SE (Lifetime AB, Nordic expansion), SG (market entity, APAC).
2. **IP stays home.** Any SG entity must be a market/sales vehicle. Core
   R&D and IP remain with Lifetime Oy in Finland — same rule already locked
   for the Cubet/9series framing and the EIC narrative. This must be
   explicit in any programme agreement we sign.
3. **Cost (runway-relevant, but deferred):** SG incorporation + registered
   address + corporate secretary ≈ **S$2–4k setup, S$2–3k/yr running**
   (EST, to verify if selected). Lands ~Q1–Q2 2027 at the earliest — after
   the potential Taler/EIC cash window, so it does not touch 2026 runway.
4. **Equity caveat carried over:** prior cohort gave NUS Enterprise an
   option on up to S$50k equity for grant awardees. Read the 2026 terms
   before accepting any grant component.
