## What we learned

1. **The competitive baseline moved today.** NVIDIA's Open Agent Safety Platform (OpenShell + Sentry) reached GA on 28.09.2026. EQTY Lab and Diagrid already sell signed audit trails. Kill authority and a signed log are now table stakes. Aegis's defensible difference is EU ownership, the severed-uplink acceptance test on the customer's site, and an Article 12 record with no gap during a disruption.
2. **The Gemini input was about 60 % accurate.** It contained six factual errors: Ficolo was never renamed "Lumi"; Granlund makes no control-room software; Summa Equity was confused with Summa Defence; Vitosha is a Bulgarian fund; Atman is Polish; atNorth never belonged to EQT. Two claims overstated the product: "industry's only" and "verified from the host kernel log". External-model input must be checked name by name before it reaches outreach. This repeats the Phase 2 lesson in the pipeline file.
3. **The schedule conflicts.** "1 paid pilot delivered and accepted by 31.12.2026" cannot be met with a 13-week pilot unless it is signed now. The corrected target is a signature plus the Phase 1 gate by year end, with acceptance in Q1/2027.
4. **Two new wedges.** First, AI Act Art. 2(3): dual-use factory AI is not exempt when a production line also serves civil buyers. Second, Indian integrators now run agentic AI at the IT/OT boundary (Neste–HCLTech, Metsä–Infosys, Valmet–Infosys). That creates demand for an independent governance layer.
