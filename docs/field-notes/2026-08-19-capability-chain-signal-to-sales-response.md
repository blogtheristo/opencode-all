# Capability chain: signal to executable sales response

Observed period: 2026-05-25 to 2026-08-19

An X-originated sales signal identified a relevant dual-use account, but Lifetime did not yet have a matching commercial response package. By 2026-08-19, the response capability existed as a repeatable chain:

1. X signal
2. Dual-use account case
3. Aegis sales funnel package
4. Account-specific buying-centre and entry-point analysis
5. Sisu-specific validation offer
6. Next commercial conversation

Learning: Opportunity recognition existed before response capability. The capability leap was the conversion of a market signal into a bounded, account-specific sales path with an offer, funnel stages and validation logic.

Evidence boundary: This proves a sales-response process exists. It does not prove customer interest, a meeting, a pilot, revenue or product readiness.

Operational rule: Future signals should be routed through the same chain before broad product or investor claims are made.
