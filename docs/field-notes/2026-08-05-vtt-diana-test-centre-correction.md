## Summary

Risto approached Markus Häyhtiö (VTT, `natodiana@vtt.fi`) directly via LinkedIn, per the DIANA
door-opening plan's action 2.1. Häyhtiö replied the same day with two facts:

1. **Test-centre use is tied to being in the NATO DIANA programme** — it is not a door open to
   companies outside a cohort.
2. **The next application process opens spring 2027.**
3. Challenge areas are set by NATO DIANA centrally and reset annually, independent of VTT as host.

This directly contradicted a standing assumption written into two repo files: that test-centre
access was "separate from cohort membership" / "independent of the annual challenge cycle." Both
were corrected in this same session.

## Why it matters

The EIC dual-use application rule (`DIANA_DOOR_OPENING_PLAN.md` §4) is that nothing DIANA-related
may be claimed until a logged exchange exists. One now does — but it must be characterised
correctly: this is an informational reply to our outreach, not an invitation, request, or
relationship commitment from DIANA. The corrected test-centre framing also changes the practical
strategy: pre-cohort VTT engagement is worth doing for the warm-contact and positioning value
ahead of the spring 2027 call, but it cannot be pitched (internally or externally) as securing
early test-centre access.
