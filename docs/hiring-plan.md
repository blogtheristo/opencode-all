## Overview

Lean, investor-credible hiring plan. Five roles max — more than that is wishful thinking without revenue to back it.

## Hiring Timeline

### Immediate — Q2 2026

| Role | Focus | Type |
|------|-------|------|
| **Head of Sales / BD** | Nordic industrial markets, compliance software background | Full-time |
| **Senior Full-Stack Engineer** | React/TypeScript, agent systems experience | Full-time |

**Rationale:** Revenue generation and product velocity are the two bottlenecks. Sales opens pipeline; engineering ships faster.

### Growth — Q3 2026

| Role | Focus | Type |
|------|-------|------|
| **Customer Success Lead** | Onboarding and retention for SaaS compliance products | Full-time |
| **Compliance Domain Specialist** | CSRD/NIS2 subject matter expert for product and sales support | Full-time |

**Rationale:** With pipeline active, retention and domain credibility become critical. CSM reduces churn; compliance specialist strengthens both product and sales conversations.

### Strategic — Q4 2026

| Role | Focus | Type |
|------|-------|------|
| **CTO / Co-Founder** | Reduce founder concentration risk | Full-time / Equity |

**Rationale:** Signals maturity to investors. Distributes technical leadership and de-risks the company.

## Principles

1. **Revenue before headcount** — no hire without a clear revenue or retention case
2. **5 roles max** — credibility over ambition
3. **Nordic-first** — Helsinki hybrid, with remote for engineering
4. **Compliance DNA** — every hire understands EU regulatory landscape (CSRD, NIS2, EU AI Act)
