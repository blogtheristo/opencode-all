# DWS Liquidity Engine — Revenue Architecture
## Investor & Partner Briefing | DWS IQ 6 · 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary

DWS IQ 6 is not a pure SaaS company. It operates a **Liquidity Engine** — a two-stream revenue architecture that generates income through direct product sales and through a tiered seat model that converts domain expertise and distribution capacity into paid positions on the platform.

The two streams are designed to reach **€900k ARR by Q4 2026** without requiring institutional capital as a prerequisite. The engine is designed to print money, not dilute equity.

**Revenue Stream 1 — Product Sales:** 32 active products, €4,999–€59,990, sold direct B2B through dws10.com and the Arctic 15 GTM motion.

**Revenue Stream 2 — Liquidity Engine (Seat Sales):** One Fleet Admiral seat (€250k), nine Industry Captain seats (€50k SAFE each), and a three-tier partnership model with active distribution leverage.

## 3. Three-Tier Partnership Model

Beyond seat sales, DWS operates a three-tier reseller and referral partnership model that creates downstream distribution without headcount:

| Tier | Name | Qualification | Revenue Share | Product Access |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Certified DWS Partner | €5,000 onboarding + annual review | 20% on referred product sales | Full product catalogue |
| Tier 2 | DWS Reseller | Trained, 2 closed deals | 30% on resold products + 15% renewals | Full catalogue + white-label option |
| Tier 3 | DWS OEM / Integrator | Technical certification + €10k integration fee | 40% on integrated revenue | API + SDK access |

**Distribution math (conservative):**
- 5 Tier 1 partners × 2 deals/year × €15k avg deal = €150k partner-sourced ARR
- 2 Tier 2 resellers × 3 deals/year × €20k avg deal = €120k reseller ARR
- 1 Tier 3 OEM × embedded platform = €80k OEM ARR
- **Total partner-channel ARR contribution: ~€350k**

## 5. Why the Engine Model Works

**For the Fleet Admiral and Captains:**
- They buy distribution rights and sector intelligence, not just equity
- They bring domain expertise that accelerates sales in their vertical
- Their network + DWS platform = faster deal cycles than pure SaaS outbound

**For DWS:**
- Non-dilutive revenue from seat sales reduces equity pressure
- Captain distribution multiplies GTM capacity without hiring salespeople
- Partner channel creates a recurring referral engine that compounds quarterly

**For investors who see this model:**
- This is not a company asking for money to build — it is a company with paying seats and a structured path to €900k ARR
- The SAFE note structure aligns Captains financially while giving them a reason to close deals
- The combination of product ARR + seat revenue is more defensible than pure SaaS
