## Why gap 3 matters for the EIC

EIC Accelerator funds **scalable product innovation**, not services businesses. Lifetime Oy currently shows a mixed picture an evaluator will read the same way RTOY did:

- **Product-led:** DWS IQ platform, Aegis tier, KYA engine, priced SKUs in `products.json`
- **Project/services-led:** consulting engagements (e.g. website work), the ORK framework bid (subcontracted expert services), transport operations

Left unresolved, this invites the exact question that sank the RTOY application: _what is this company actually scaling?_ The EIC answer needs to be unambiguous — **the product is the KYA trust layer and the Aegis tier that packages it; services revenue funds the company but is not the scale story.** That framing should appear explicitly in F9 (business model) rather than being left for the evaluator to infer.
