### Slide 1 — Title
- **AEGIS** — Sovereign, on-premise AI agents that **act and transact** for regulated Europe.

### Slide 2 — The problem: who's in control?
- AI agents are starting to **act and pay** on behalf of enterprises.
- Europe regulates the **humans** (KYC, eIDAS) and the **models** (AI Act) — but **nothing regulates the agent**: no standard identity, no bounded mandate, no revocation, no audit with legal weight.
- Every current answer (Coinbase x402, Google AP2, Stripe agent toolkit) is **US-custodial, card-rail, binds the human — not the agent.**
- From **Aug 2027**, AI Act high-risk enforcement makes wrong answers enforceable violations (up to 7% of global turnover).

### Slide 3 — The solution (live today)
- **DWS IQ Aegis** — a live EU-sovereign platform; **50 production agents**, 25 regulated verticals.
- Runs on the **customer's own NVIDIA DGX (S2)** — inference, data, model weights **never leave the building**. Sovereignty by physics, not policy.
- **KYA engine in production:** agent identity, live trust score, TC-4 "Leash Snap" revocation, Article-12 append-only audit.
- Proof: **6 paying customers, €7k MRR, 100% retention since first paid deployment (Dec 2025).**

### Slide 4 — The innovation we're asking EIC to fund
- From agents that **gate actions** → agents that **act and transact under mandate**: a signed **mandate credential** (caps, scope, validity) + a **selective-disclosure proof** ("within mandate" without revealing mandate or principal).
- **Tier-aware Sovereignty Profile** (S1 cloud / S2 on-prem DGX / S3 hybrid) + **Signed Sovereignty Attestation** — machine-verifiable {jurisdiction, residency, weight-ownership, air-gap}.
- **TRL 6→8.** The differentiator no other agent-governance framework has (lead patent claim).

- **AI Act** Articles 12/14/72 enforce for high-risk systems **Aug 2027**.
- **EU sovereign rails arriving:** EUDI Wallet law from 12/2026; **ECB Digital Euro** pilot H2 2027.
