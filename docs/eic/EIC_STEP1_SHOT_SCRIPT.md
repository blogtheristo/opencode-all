## How Screencast works (so the columns make sense)

Screencast records the **whole screen + a webcam bubble** at once, and saves a `.webm` to **Downloads** (local, not cloud). So you don't "switch camera on/off" — the **webcam bubble stays on the whole time**; what changes is **what's on the main screen** behind it. Enable **"show mouse clicks"** in Screencast settings for the demo beats.

**Two things open before you hit record:**

1. **Pitch deck** — `EIC_STEP1_PITCH_DECK.html` in Chrome, fullscreen (press **F**). Slide 1 showing.
2. **Aegis UI** — the live DWS IQ Aegis window (trust score + Leash Snap + Article-12 view ready). **Blur/close any confidential customer data first.**

Alt-Tab (or Chrome tabs) to move between the deck and Aegis. One continuous take is fine.

## Pre-record checklist

- **Lighting:** face a window; light from the front, not behind.
- **Audio:** quiet, non-echoey room; Chromebook Plus mic is good — no headset needed.
- **Screencast:** "show mouse clicks" ON; record the whole screen + webcam bubble; save `.webm` to Downloads.
- **Dry run once** without recording. Hit the pace: ~150 wpm; ~15 s of buffer exists.
- **Edit on the ZBook** if needed (trim ends, captions) — record on the Acer (1080p), transfer, edit on the ZBook.
- Watch the [1:45] paragraph — slow down, and pause before "That makes EU AI Act compliance machine-verifiable."
