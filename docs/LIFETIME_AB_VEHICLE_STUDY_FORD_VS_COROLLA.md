## 0. TL;DR

- **Recommendation: Toyota Corolla Hybrid stays the floor and the default buy.** For a
  full-time Uber cash engine, nothing beats its total cost of ownership: full hybrid
  (~4.5 L/100 km real), proven 300k+ km reliability, strong resale, UberX **and** Comfort
  eligible. New campaign **from ≈ 299,900 kr**.
- **Ford alternative worth it only if** you want to **minimise upfront capital** via the
  **Ford 100** financing (2.95% loan; Arval leasing; 25% service discount through 2026) —
  then the pick is **Kuga FHEV** (full hybrid, roomy, Comfort-tier). **Puma** is a cheaper
  UberX runabout but mild-hybrid + small.
- **CRITICAL CAVEAT:** the headline **Ford 100 privatleasing** offers are *private* leases
  that **typically forbid taxi / ride-hailing use.** For Uber you must **buy** (cash/loan)
  or get an explicit **commercial/taxi lease (företagsleasing/operationell)**. Verify the
  contract permits taxi use before signing — this can void the Ford-lease cost advantage.
- **Net:** Corolla Hybrid (buy in Sweden, in the AB) unless a Ford dealer offers a
  **taxi-eligible** Kuga FHEV finance/lease that beats Corolla TCO. Decision grid in §5.

## 1. Use case — what the vehicle must do

It is a **productive commercial asset** for the Uber cash engine (§1.4 of the business
plan), not a personal car:

- **High annual mileage** (full-time/part-time ride-hailing) → fuel economy + reliability
  dominate TCO.
- **Uber Sweden eligibility:** max **8 years** from registration (10 for EV); 4 doors, 5
  seats. **Uber Comfort** = newer + more legroom (higher fares).
- **Taxi-legal:** commercial insurance, taxi registration, and any finance/lease must
  **permit taxi use**.
- **Buy in Sweden (Sverige):** registration, taxi-eligibility, warranty, service in the AB's
  jurisdiction.

## 2. Toyota Corolla Hybrid — the benchmark

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Powertrain | **Full hybrid** (self-charging), ~4.5 L/100 km real-world |
| Reliability | Toyota hybrid drivetrain — the global ride-hailing standard; routinely 300k+ km |
| Body options | Hatchback / **Touring Sports** (estate — best boot for Comfort/XL feel) / Sedan |
| Uber tiers | UberX **and** Comfort eligible (newer model years) |
| New price (SE, 2026) | Touring Sports 1.8 Hybrid Active **from ≈ 299,900 kr** (campaign; ord. 334,900). Corolla Cross SUV from 361,900 kr |
| Resale | Strong — high demand, low depreciation |
| Why it wins for Uber | Lowest fuel + maintenance + depreciation per km; minimal downtime |

**This is the floor.** Any alternative must beat it on *total cost per km incl. downtime*,
not on sticker price or financing gimmicks.

## 3. Ford alternative — options in Sweden (2026)

Ford has **exited the C-segment hatch/sedan in Europe** (Focus production ended; Mondeo
gone), so there is **no direct Corolla-hatch equivalent** from Ford. The hybrid passenger
line is now SUV/crossover:

| Ford model | Powertrain | Fit for Uber | New price (SE, indic.) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Puma** EcoBoost | **Mild hybrid** (48V) | UberX ok; tight rear for Comfort | ~357,000 kr (avg new) | B-SUV; mild hybrid ≠ full hybrid → higher fuel use than Corolla; small |
| **Puma Gen-E** | **Full EV** (44 kWh, ~360 km) | UberX/Green; 10-yr Uber age allowance | privatleasing fr. ~3,995 kr/mån (Arval) | EV running-cost low, but range/charging limits full-time shifts |
| **Kuga FHEV** | **Full hybrid** | **UberX + Comfort** (roomy) | (confirm at dealer) | **Closest true Corolla rival** — full hybrid, C-SUV space |
| **Kuga PHEV** | Plug-in hybrid (243 hp, ~69 km EV) | Comfort; cheap city km on electric | ST-Line X ~490,000 kr; privatleasing fr. ~3,795–4,995 kr/mån | Higher price/lease; needs charging discipline to realise savings |

### 3.1 The Ford 100 campaign (anniversary, extended into 2026)
- **2.95%** campaign car-loan interest on all passenger cars (Feb 2026).
- **25%** jubilee discount on **service & repairs** through **31 Dec 2026** (code "Ford25").
- **Arval privatleasing**: Puma fr. ~2,795–3,995 kr/mån; Kuga PHEV fr. ~3,795–4,995 kr/mån
  (service + winter tyres included on Kuga).

**The campaign's real value is the financing + service discount, not the cars** — useful for
an AB that wants OPEX over CAPEX and predictable service cost.

## 5. Decision grid

| If the priority is… | Choose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Lowest cost per km + reliability (default) | **Corolla Hybrid (buy, in AB)** | Unbeatable TCO; taxi-proven; strong resale |
| Minimise upfront capital, OPEX, predictable service | **Kuga FHEV via Ford 100** *(only if lease permits taxi use)* | 2.95% loan / Arval + 25% service; roomy Comfort-tier |
| Comfort/XL space is the earner | Kuga FHEV/PHEV | C-SUV space lifts fare tier |
| Cheapest Ford entry, light use | Puma | Low lease, but mild-hybrid + small |
| Mostly city, can charge, EV age-allowance | Puma Gen-E / Kuga PHEV | Low electric running cost; range/charging discipline required |

**Recommendation:** **buy a Toyota Corolla Hybrid in Sweden, in the AB.** Pursue the Ford
route only if a Ford dealer puts a **taxi-eligible** Kuga FHEV finance/lease (Ford 100 terms)
on the table that beats Corolla's total cost per km — get the taxi-use permission **in
writing** first.
