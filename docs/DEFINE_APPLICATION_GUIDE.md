# DEFINE Application Guide — Defence Tech Hub Espoo & DEFINE Accelerator

## 1. Overview

Finland's defence innovation ecosystem includes two key entry points for startups and SMEs:

| Entity | Location | Type | Coordinator |
|--------|----------|------|-------------|
| **DEFINE Accelerator** | Riihimäki (nationwide) | 3-month startup accelerator | Redstone VC + City of Riihimäki |
| **Defence Tech Hub** | Espoo (Otaniemi) | Membership-based innovation hub | Enter Espoo |

Both signed a collaboration agreement in October 2025 — member companies can access each other's resources. The **NATO DIANA Accelerator** site in Espoo (opened January 2026) adds a third layer to Finland's defence innovation infrastructure.

## 2. DEFINE Accelerator

**Website:** https://www.definefinland.fi/en/accelerator/

### What You Get (Free — No Equity Taken)

- 3-month structured program in three phases:
  1. Validation & Strategy Development — mentors refine business idea and strategy
  2. Technology Development & Prototyping — access to facilities
  3. Go-to-Market & Investment Readiness — prepare for market entry and investment
- One-on-one coaching from industry experts
- Direct connections to investors and customers within NATO and EU ecosystems
- Access to facilities and infrastructure in Riihimäki
- Co-funded by EU, OP Häme, and City of Riihimäki

### Eligibility

- Startups and SMEs from **NATO countries**
- Focus: defence, security, and dual-use technologies
- Past participants from 13 countries (Germany, Sweden, Finland, and more)

### How to Apply

1. Go to https://www.definefinland.fi/en/accelerator/
2. Fill in the online application form
3. No participation fee, no equity taken — startups cover own travel and direct costs

### Timeline

| Autumn 2026 | Expected April–May 2026 | TBD |

## 3. Defence Tech Hub (Espoo)

**Website:** https://www.enterespoo.fi/innovation-ecosystem/defence-tech-hub

### What You Get

- Access to 60+ member companies in defence tech ecosystem
- Connections to Finnish deep tech leaders: ICEYE, IQM, Kuva Space, Varjo, Elfys
- Collaboration with VTT and Aalto University
- Bi-monthly member lunches with defence industry speakers
- Trade missions and exclusive opportunities (MoU with Digital Defence Ecosystem)
- Newsletter with events and opportunities
- Development laboratories, platforms, and infrastructure

### Eligibility

- Startups, companies, investors, and research organisations
- Finnish and international defence players
- Especially relevant for non-traditional defence tech companies entering the defence market

### How to Apply

1. Contact **Ossi Ritvos** at Enter Espoo
2. Visit https://www.enterespoo.fi/innovation-ecosystem/defence-tech-hub
3. Membership-based — open to new companies at any time
