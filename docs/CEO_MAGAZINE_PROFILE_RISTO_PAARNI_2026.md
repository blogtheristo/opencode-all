## Page 1 — The Opening

### When Compliance Becomes Intelligence

In a continent racing to cut emissions by 55% before 2030, most industrial companies are drowning in regulation. The EU's Fit for 55 package — spanning ETS, CBAM, CSRD, EPBD, and a dozen more acronyms — has created a compliance burden that threatens to consume the operational capacity of companies across 16 regulated industries.

Risto Paarni saw something different. Where others saw bureaucratic overhead, he saw an architectural problem begging for an engineering solution.

"Every regulation is a data contract," says Paarni from his base in Finland. "ETS tells you what to measure. CBAM tells you what to report at the border. CSRD tells you how to disclose. These aren't separate problems — they're one system with 15,000 rules. And systems are what engineers build."

That insight became DWS IQ 6, the industrial climate intelligence platform that Paarni and his company Lifetime Oy have spent years engineering. It is not a dashboard. It is not a consulting framework wrapped in software. It is a full-spectrum AI platform that thinks, remembers, executes, and physically deploys — and it is already generating revenue.

## Page 2 — The Founder

### From Industrial Management to Industrial Intelligence

Risto Anton Paarni holds an M.Sc. in Industrial Management, a degree that straddles the boundary between engineering and business — a combination that defines his approach to everything he builds.

Born and raised in Finland, fluent in Finnish, English, and Swedish, Paarni operates at the intersection of Nordic engineering discipline and European regulatory ambition. He founded Lifetime Oy (formerly Lifetime Consulting) to address a gap he observed firsthand: industrial companies had no shortage of climate data, but they had no system capable of turning that data into auditable, compliant, actionable intelligence.

"I didn't start with AI," Paarni explains. "I started with the problem. Industrial companies in the EU face a regulatory timeline that is accelerating — CBAM full implementation in 2026, ETS2 for road transport and buildings in 2027, EPBD embodied carbon reporting in 2028. The question was never whether they need automation. The question was whether anyone would build the right kind."

Paarni is that rare founder who maintains deep technical involvement. He manages the GitHub repository directly, reviews architecture decisions, and writes code alongside his AI-assisted development workflow. His philosophy: the CEO of an AI company should understand the models, the infrastructure, and the deployment constraints — not just the market opportunity.

## Page 3 — The Platform

### DWS IQ 6: The Brain-Memory-Body-Hands Ecosystem

DWS IQ 6 is not a single product. It is an ecosystem designed around a biological metaphor that Paarni calls the Brain-Memory-Body-Hands architecture:

**The Brain** is the DWS IQ 6 platform itself — a multi-agent AI orchestration layer that coordinates real-time decision-making across compliance domains. Built on Google Cloud Run with Groq LPU acceleration, it processes regulatory signals, emissions data, and supply chain inputs simultaneously.

**The Memory** is the Lifetime World Model (LWM), a 55-billion parameter Climate LLM specialized for EU regulations. It has indexed over 15,000 EU regulatory documents and has been pre-trained across 16 industry verticals. This is not a general-purpose language model repurposed for compliance; it is a purpose-built regulatory intelligence system.

**The Body** comprises the Firehorse product suite — a family of applications for reporting, automation, content management, and omnichannel communication. Firehorse handles the operational layer: generating reports, automating workflows, managing documents, and coordinating outreach across channels.

**The Hands** are the Lifetime Fleet — physical robots, vehicles, drones, and IoT devices that extend the platform's reach into the physical world. This is the component that most competitors cannot replicate. While other compliance platforms stop at software, Lifetime's Fleet division is already operational, generating EUR 7,000-14,000 in monthly recurring revenue through partnerships including Uber and Taksi Helsinki.

"Most climate tech companies build a brain without hands," says Paarni. "They can analyze, but they can't act. We built the entire organism."

## Page 4 — The Market

### 16 Industries, One Regulatory Stack

DWS IQ 6 covers 16 EU-regulated industries, organized by emissions criticality:

**Tier 1 — Critical Emissions Industries:**
Power and heat generation (25-30% of EU emissions), iron and steel production, cement and lime production, chemicals and petrochemicals, aviation, maritime shipping, road transport and logistics, and construction and buildings (36-40% of lifecycle emissions).

**Tier 2 — Important Emissions Industries:**
Agriculture, waste management, aluminium, pulp and paper, petroleum refining, food and beverage, glass and ceramics, and mining.

For each industry, DWS IQ 6 provides tailored compliance modules covering the full regulatory stack:

| Regulation | What DWS IQ 6 Automates |
|------------|------------------------|
| ETS | Allowance tracking, trading, and surrender |
| CBAM | Import carbon calculations and certificate management |
| CSRD | ESRS E1 climate disclosures, audit-ready data |
| EPBD | Embodied carbon assessment (EN 15978), lifecycle analysis |
| ESR | National target tracking, non-ETS emissions |
| ReFuelEU | Sustainable aviation fuel mandates, fuel intensity tracking |
| NIS2 | Cybersecurity compliance for critical entities |

The construction industry has emerged as a primary beachhead. With EPBD mandating embodied carbon reporting for buildings over 1,000 square meters by 2028, construction companies face an urgent compliance deadline that current manual processes cannot meet. DWS IQ 6's Firehorse V2 module — positioned as a "forensic situation room" — uses Physical AI to reconstruct site events and generate audit-ready compliance documentation.

## Page 5 — The Technology

### Edge AI and the EUR 333,000 Question

One of Paarni's most consequential technical decisions was the adoption of edge AI infrastructure built on NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano devices. While competitors route all inference through cloud APIs — paying per token, accepting latency, and depending on connectivity — Lifetime deploys intelligence at the edge.

The numbers tell the story:

- **Inference latency:** Under 100 milliseconds (25 times faster than cloud alternatives)
- **Cloud cost avoidance:** EUR 27,750 per month
- **Annual savings:** EUR 333,000 versus cloud-only competitors
- **Hardware ROI payback:** Under 2 months
- **Power consumption:** 7-15 watts per device (solar-capable for remote sites)

"Edge AI isn't a nice-to-have in industrial settings," Paarni argues. "Construction sites don't have reliable internet. Factories have air-gapped OT networks. Maritime vessels operate mid-ocean. If your compliance platform requires a cloud connection to function, you've already failed the use case."

The infrastructure stack reflects this edge-first philosophy:

- **Edge layer:** AWS IoT Greengrass combined with NVIDIA Jetson across 50 deployment sites
- **Core platform:** Google Cloud Run with Groq API and LlamaStack orchestration
- **Client access:** Chromebook Plus with Progressive Web App
- **Data storage:** Supabase for hot data, AWS S3 for cold archive
- **Monthly infrastructure cost:** USD 180 (86% cheaper than AWS-only architecture)

The AI stack itself is multi-model, leveraging Claude from Anthropic, Google Gemini, and Groq API, orchestrated through Model Context Protocol (MCP) — an open standard for agent coordination that Paarni's team has embraced as a core architectural principle.

## Page 8 — The Security Architecture

### Enterprise-Grade by Design, Not by Afterthought

In an era of increasing cyber threats and regulatory scrutiny, Paarni has made security a foundational element rather than a bolt-on concern. DWS IQ 6's security architecture is designed for industries where data sovereignty is not optional:

**Data Residency:** 100% European Union — servers in Finland, Germany, and the Netherlands. No customer data leaves EU jurisdiction.

**Deployment Options:** Customer VPC, Dedicated Private Cloud, Hybrid Edge, and OT-Isolated Edge. Industrial customers in sectors like chemicals and energy often require air-gapped deployments that most SaaS platforms cannot support. DWS IQ 6 can.

**Encryption:** AES-256-GCM at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, with Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) and Customer-Managed Encryption Keys (CMEK) supported.

**Audit Logging:** Immutable logs with 7-year retention and SIEM export capability — designed to meet Article 12 of the EU AI Act, which mandates automatic logging of all AI system events for traceability.

**AI Data Policy:** No customer data is used to train public models. Ever.

**Certification Roadmap:**
- ISO 27001: Q3 2026
- SOC 2 Type II: Q4 2026
- IEC 62443 (manufacturing security): Q4 2026
- EU AI Act Article 12 compliance: Q2 2026

"We serve industries that handle critical infrastructure," says Paarni. "Power generation, chemicals, maritime — these companies cannot use a platform that stores their emissions data in a US data center. EU data residency is not a feature. It is a prerequisite."

## Page 9 — The Vision

### The Lifetime Equation

Behind the technology, the sales discipline, and the regulatory expertise is a personal philosophy that Paarni calls the Lifetime Equation:

**Lifetime Happiness = Livelihood Independence + Prosperity Creation - Carbon Footprint - Erosion Expansion - Droughts Mitigation**

It is a formula that reads like an engineer's attempt to quantify what matters — and that is precisely the point. Paarni does not approach climate as an activist or a moralist. He approaches it as a systems engineer who has identified that carbon reduction, economic prosperity, and technological advancement are not competing objectives. They are interdependent variables in a single equation.

The company's tagline — "Turning 2050 goals into 2026 insights" — captures the operational philosophy. The Paris Agreement's 2050 net-zero target is a constraint, not an aspiration. The job is to work backward from that constraint and build the systems that make compliance achievable today, not theoretically possible tomorrow.

Paarni describes his transformation framework in three levels:

**Level 1 — Survival:** Manual compliance, reactive scramble, and the risk of fines and market exclusion. This is where most industrial companies operate today.

**Level 2 — Efficiency:** Automated tracking, real-time monitoring, and EUR 50,000-100,000 in annual savings. This is what DWS IQ 6 delivers immediately upon deployment.

**Level 3 and Beyond — Leadership to Transformation:** Carbon-optimized decision-making that creates first-mover positioning, shapes market standards, and defines what good looks like. This is where Paarni believes the long-term value lies — not in avoiding penalties, but in leading the transition.
