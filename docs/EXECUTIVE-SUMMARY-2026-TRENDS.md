## Strategic Context

### The Market Opportunity

**EU Regulatory Mandate = €45B TAM by 2030**

The EU's Fit for 55 package creates a **mandatory** compliance market:

- **ETS (Emissions Trading System):** 10,000+ companies must trade carbon allowances
- **CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment):** 100,000+ importers must calculate embodied carbon
- **CSRD (Sustainability Reporting):** post-Omnibus, fewer than ~5,000 large EU companies (>1,000 employees AND >€450M turnover) file directly — with a much larger addressable population pulled in via value-chain / Scope-3 supplier-data requirements
- **EPBD (Building Performance):** All buildings >1,000 m² must track embodied carbon by 2028

**This isn't optional—it's regulatory compliance with €20M+ GDPR-level fines.**

## Risk Analysis

### Technical Risks

**Risk 1: Quantum Hype (Technology Not Ready)**

- **Likelihood:** Medium (current quantum hardware has 50-1000 noisy qubits)
- **Mitigation:** Phase 1 (PQC) is software-only, zero quantum hardware needed
- **Mitigation:** Phase 2 (QAOA) uses hybrid quantum-classical (degrades gracefully)
- **Go/No-Go:** Phase 2 only proceeds if Phase 1 generates measurable sales lift

**Risk 2: Synthetic Data Doesn't Generalize**

- **Likelihood:** Low (differential privacy + statistical validation)
- **Mitigation:** Validate on held-out real data (20% withheld from training)
- **Success Metric:** Synthetic-trained model must match real-trained model within 5% MAPE

**Risk 3: EU Regulation Changes Invalidate Compliance Logic**

- **Likelihood:** High (CBAM transitional → definitive in Jan 2026)
- **Mitigation:** Synthetic regulatory scenarios (stress test compliance logic)
- **Mitigation:** Modular architecture (regulation changes affect 1 module, not entire platform)
