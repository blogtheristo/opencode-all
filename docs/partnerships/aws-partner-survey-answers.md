## Free-text answers

### 1. What are your top business priorities for the next 6–12 months?

List **DWS IQ** on AWS Marketplace as a procurement channel for enterprise buyers; secure funding accessible to a small EU ISV (POC credits, build/MDF); and understand what partner-tier movement unlocks. Grow enterprise pipeline in EU regulatory-compliance software without compromising our EU data-sovereignty commitment.

### 2. What are your biggest challenges in growing your AWS business?

Clarity on which funding and co-sell benefits a small EU ISV can actually access, and at what qualifying effort. Reconciling an AWS Marketplace / infrastructure motion with the EU data-residency and sovereignty guarantees our customers require (CLOUD Act sensitivity). Limited team bandwidth for partner-program administration.

### 3. What would you most like this webinar to cover? (agenda-shaping)

1. **Funding:** the concrete tier→funding map for an EU ISV — type, amount, and qualifying effort.
2. **Marketplace + residency:** how to list DWS IQ on Marketplace while contractually guaranteeing EU-region data residency (AWS European Sovereign Cloud / EU regions) auditable by our customers.
3. **Distributor vs. direct:** whether an EMEA distributor reduces net effort for a small team.
4. **Reselling:** whether a resale motion shortens enterprise procurement for compliance software.

### 4. Which growth motion interests you most? (select if multiple-choice)

Primary: **AWS Marketplace transacting.** Secondary: **Partner-tier advancement + funding.** Exploring: **Distributor relationship.** Lower priority for now: **Reselling** (pending value case).

### 5. What is your current go-to-market approach?

Direct enterprise sales of DWS IQ into EU compliance buyers. Evaluating AWS Marketplace and co-sell as additional procurement paths to reduce enterprise buying friction (committed-spend drawdown, approved-vendor listing).

### 6. Anything else the team should know?

We market EU data sovereignty and flag US CLOUD Act exposure. We are not opposed to AWS — we need the AWS path to be compatible with EU-region residency and an auditable sovereignty posture. Guidance on the European Sovereign Cloud and EU-region Marketplace mechanics would make this webinar directly actionable for us.
