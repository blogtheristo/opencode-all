# Qantic Call Agenda — 26.03.2026

**Call with:** Thomas Kalitzky, Managing Director, Qantic GmbH
**Duration:** 20-30 minutes
**Goal:** Confirm one pilot client. Send NDA same day.

---

## Opening (2 min)

- Thank Thomas for the time
- Reference the NDA discussion (already in progress)
- Frame the call: "I want to understand your clients' compliance needs so we can scope a concrete first project"

---

## 1. Discovery — Understand His World (8 min)

**Ask these questions. Listen more than you talk.**

> "How many of your current clients have active CSRD reporting obligations this year?"

This one question sizes the pipeline. If he names specific clients with deadlines, the deal is real. If he speaks in generalities, the pipeline is smaller.

> "When your clients evaluate Q-System, does compliance cost come up in their investment models?"

If yes — you solve a real pain point. If no — you're creating demand, which is harder.

> "Who is the ideal first client for a pilot? Someone with both BESS assets and an upcoming CSRD deadline?"

Let him name the client. Don't suggest MAN or Naturstrom unless he doesn't have an answer.

> "What does Q-System export today? JSON, CSV, API?"

Technical question that unlocks Phase 2 planning.

---

## 2. Present — What We Built (5 min)

**Keep it tight. Three points only.**

1. **"We built a BESS compliance cost API."**
   - Input: dispatch data from any energy management system
   - Output: EUR/year compliance cost per asset (CSRD, ETS, Taxonomy, Scope 2)
   - Already built, 19 tests passing, EU-hosted in Finland

2. **"Your clients' investors are missing 5-15% of costs."**
   - Example: 50MW/200MWh BESS in Germany = €21,693/year compliance cost
   - Without this number, revenue models overstate returns
   - Qantic gives the revenue side, we give the cost side — together it's the complete picture

---

## 3. The Ask (5 min)

**Be direct.**

> "I'd like to start with one pilot client. We deliver a complete compliance cost report for their BESS portfolio — CSRD, ETS, EU Taxonomy. You see the output, the client sees the value, and we go from there."

**If he asks about pricing:**
- Setup + workshop: "Depends on scope — ranges from €7k to €16k depending on what the client needs"
- API ongoing: "€99 per asset per month after setup"
- Don't anchor to €15,990 unless he's clearly ready for the full package

**If he asks about reseller commission:**
- "30% on clients you refer. We deliver everything — you carry zero delivery risk."
- Make clear: referred clients only, not direct

**If he pushes back on setup price:**
- "We can start smaller — a compliance assessment report for one BESS site. That's €2,900 and takes a week. If the client sees value, we expand."
- Entry point: Firehorse Reports + Data Services = €2,898

---

## 4. Credibility Point (1 min)

> "Your clients will research both of us. Our dws10.com gives them the technical depth and compliance credibility to feel confident. We're not just a backend — we're part of your pitch to your own clients."

Only say this if the conversation is going well and Thomas is engaged. Skip if the call is running short.

---

## 5. Close and Next Steps (3 min)

> "Can we agree on a next step today?"

**Ideal outcome:** Thomas names a pilot client. You send NDA same day.

**Good outcome:** Thomas needs to check with a client first. Set a follow-up call for the following week.

**Minimum outcome:** Thomas is interested but vague. Ask: "What would you need to see to move forward?" Then deliver that specific thing.

**Only if he asks about scope beyond energy:**
> "Our platform covers 20 EU-regulated industries — as your client base grows beyond energy, you don't need a new vendor."

Don't volunteer this. It plants a bigger decision than he needs to make right now. If he doesn't ask, save it for month 2-3.

**After the call:**
1. Send NDA via DocuSign within 1 hour (template ready, digital signature)
2. Send thank-you email summarizing what was agreed
3. If pilot client named: begin scoping immediately
4. If LOI appropriate: fill in template and send for review

---

## What NOT to Do

- Don't show the full deal prospectus document
- Don't mention LWM, QLoRA, NVIDIA Cosmos, or 15,000 regulations
- Don't discuss the expansion strategy or platform licensing
- Don't send the LOI before he agrees to terms
- Don't push the full €15,990 if he hasn't signaled budget appetite
- Don't talk more than 40% of the time — this is a discovery call
