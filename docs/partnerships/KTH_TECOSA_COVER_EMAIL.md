Subject: DWS IQ × TECoSA — one-pager, scope points and a basis for discussion

Hi Martin,

Thank you — attached are three things: a one-pager on Lifetime and DWS IQ, the scope points
I think are worth covering, and a basis for discussion towards an LoI.

The third one is deliberately not a draft agreement. Before we finalise anything, I would
appreciate your view on the partnership structure rather than assuming the model from our
side — so the sections on funding route, entity and co-funding are left open rather than
pre-filled.

1. Which Vinnova route would best fit this work: a TECoSA partnership, a joint call, or a
   separate application involving Lifetime and KTH? Who would normally coordinate it?

2. In that structure, what would be the appropriate role for Lifetime Oy, and would a Swedish
   entity be required as the applicant or funded partner? If so, Lifetime AB could be
   established on an accelerated schedule as the Swedish research and financing entity. Would
   a Swedish subsidiary, a branch, or participation alongside another Swedish funded party be
   the most workable construction?

3. How should Lifetime's co-funding contribution be defined and valued? Possible contributions
   include cash, engineering time, and scoped access to the licensed operating environment and
   platform. Would TECoSA accept an in-kind regulated-edge testbed contribution, and what
   evidence or valuation would be expected?

4. What level of commitment and time horizon would TECoSA expect from an industrial partner in
   a competence-centre model?

5. Which researcher pathway would be most realistic for this topic: an industrial doctoral
   student, a licentiate route, or a postdoc hosted at KTH?

6. Are there specific TECoSA contacts, decision points, or upcoming calls that should shape the
   material before we take it further?

The technical scope would remain centred on assurance cases for sovereign edge AI,
safety–performance trade-offs, and validation methodology. We would define fleet
instrumentation and data access separately, within GDPR, safety, and operator-licence
boundaries.

A written reply is entirely sufficient; a call only if it is easier for you.
