# Towards a Letter of Intent — a basis for discussion

**KTH Royal Institute of Technology / TECoSA and Lifetime Oy (Business ID 0772407-9), Espoo, Finland**

> **What this is.** Not a draft agreement and not a document for signature — **a structured way of putting our thinking on the table so you can correct it.** Each section gives where we currently stand and what we would want your view on. The numbering is deliberately the shape a Letter of Intent would take, so that once the open questions are settled this converts into one rather than being rewritten.
>
> Three things are **not** proposed here on purpose: the **applicant entity**, the **coordinating party** and the **co-funding model**. Those depend on how TECoSA's model actually works, and we would rather have your answer than our assumption.

---

## 1. What we would like to work on

How an autonomous decision taken at the edge can be shown, **on evidence**, to be safe — and what that assurance costs in system performance.

That second half matters to us as much as the first. We are not looking for a compliance narrative; we are looking for a defensible engineering result about where the operating point sits.

## 2. Who is asking

**Lifetime Oy** builds DWS IQ, a governed outcome layer for AI agents in EU-regulated industries. The KYA engine — identity binding, session trust scoring, containment, EU AI Act Article 12 reasoning-lineage capture — is at TRL 5 completed, entering TRL 6.

Lifetime also operates a **licensed passenger-transport business** under Traficom operator licence 00010089, valid to 24 October 2034. That is the part we think is genuinely unusual to offer: a regulated, insured, operating edge environment, not a demonstrator.

## 3. Scope we would propose — and what we would leave out

Our current thinking:

1. **Assurance cases for sovereign edge AI** — what claim structure is appropriate, what the KYA evidence is and is not sufficient to support, and how such an argument is validated rather than asserted.
2. **Safety–performance trade-offs** — what containment, trust scoring and lineage capture cost in latency, throughput and availability, and where a defensible operating point lies.
3. **Validation methodology** applied to an industrial use case.

**Open:** which of TECoSA's trustworthiness concerns this should actually target — and, more usefully, which it should explicitly exclude. We would rather agree a short exclusion list than a long inclusion list.

## 4. What each side would bring

**KTH / TECoSA**, as we understand it: research leadership on assurance argumentation and validation methodology, supervision of any researcher engaged under §6, and academic quality assurance.

**Lifetime:** the industrial use case, the DWS IQ platform and KYA engine as the system under study, and scoped access to a licensed operating environment — subject to §7.

We should say plainly that Lifetime is a small company. What we bring is the operating environment and the platform, not headcount.

## 5. Funding route, entity and co-funding — **open, and the main thing we are asking you about**

We have deliberately not filled this in.

- **Which route** — a TECoSA partnership, a joint call, or a separate application?
- **Who would normally coordinate** in that route?
- **Which entity would need to be the applicant or funded party?** Lifetime Oy is a Finnish company. Our reading is that a Swedish construction may be required — a subsidiary, a `filial`, or participation alongside a Swedish funded party. **We make no claim to be eligible today; no Swedish entity is currently registered.** One can be established on an accelerated schedule if the track becomes concrete, but we would rather build the right construction than the fast one.
- **How would our co-funding contribution be defined and valued?** Cash, engineering time, or scoped in-kind access to the licensed operating environment and platform. Would an in-kind regulated-edge testbed contribution be accepted, and what evidence or valuation would be expected?

## 6. Researcher pathway — three options, no preference yet

An **industrial doctoral student** employed by the Lifetime entity and enrolled at KTH; a **licentiate** route; or a **postdoc** hosted at KTH and part-funded by the project.

We would rather hear which is realistic for this topic and for TECoSA than argue for one.

## 7. Instrumentation, data and data protection

Nothing is being offered or committed here on data access.

Instrumentation and data access would be **defined separately and afterwards**, within safety, GDPR and operator-licence-holder boundaries. Operational fleet data involves personal data of passengers and drivers, so GDPR roles, what is anonymised or aggregated before leaving the operational system, and what never leaves it at all, all need settling first. Our position is that those limits are set **before** instrumentation begins, not negotiated once someone wants the data.

## 8. Intellectual property

Our starting assumption, open to discussion: background IP stays with its owner — DWS IQ, the KYA engine and DWS IQ Aegis are Lifetime background IP — and foreground IP is allocated per category (research results, software, data) in whatever definitive agreement follows.

## 9. Publication

KTH publishing freely is a premise, not a concession. We would want a defined review window before submission — 30 days is what we have seen elsewhere — to flag confidential material and file where appropriate. If TECoSA has a standard practice here, we would simply adopt it.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each side keeps the other's non-public information confidential and uses it only for this purpose. If KTH has a standard mutual NDA, we would prefer to use yours rather than introduce ours.

## 11. If this becomes a Letter of Intent

When there is enough agreement to draft one, we would expect it to commit no funds and to be **non-binding except as to data protection, publication review, confidentiality and any IP provisions expressly agreed** — and to be superseded by a consortium or Vinnova project agreement.

Flagging that now so it is not a surprise later, not asking for it today.

## 12. What would help most

1. Your view on §5 — route, coordinator, entity, co-funding.
2. Your view on §6 — which researcher pathway is realistic.
3. Anything in §3 that is the wrong scope for TECoSA, and anything obvious we have missed.
4. Any upcoming calls or decision points that should shape the timing.

A written reply is entirely sufficient. A call only if that is easier for you.
