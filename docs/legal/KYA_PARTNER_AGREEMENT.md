## Purpose

This agreement governs the KYA Certified Partner Program. Partners are technology vendors whose products integrate with or are certified under the KYA (Know Your Agent) framework. Lifetime Oy is the licensor of the KYA certification mark.

## Partner Tiers

| Tier | Annual Fee | Target Partners | Rights |
|------|-----------|----------------|--------|
| **Tier 1 — Integration Partner** | EUR 2,500/year | Sub-processors (KYC, biometrics, identity providers) | Listed on dws10.com/kya-standard, KYA-certified badge, annual recertification |
| **Tier 2 — Strategic Partner** | EUR 7,500/year | ERP vendors, cloud providers (Visma, IFS, Azure) | Co-marketing rights, joint case studies, priority integration support |
| **Tier 3 — Enterprise Partner** | EUR 15,000/year + revenue share | System integrators, defence primes | Custom SLA, dedicated support channel, revenue share on sourced deals |

## Agreement Outline

### 1. Definitions
- KYA Framework, KYA Certified Partner, Certification Mark
- Lifetime Oy as Licensor, Partner as Licensee

### 2. Certification Requirements
- Pass annual KYA compliance review
- Maintain technical integration with DWS IQ 6 platform
- Comply with DPA and data processing obligations
- Meet security standards (ISO 27001 or equivalent, or remediation plan)

### 3. Badge Usage Rights
- KYA Certified Partner mark for marketing materials
- Listed on dws10.com/kya-standard partner directory
- Badge variants per tier (Integration, Strategic, Enterprise)
- Usage guidelines provided by Lifetime Oy
- Badge removal within 7 days of agreement termination

### 4. Data Processing Obligations
- Flow-down from Lifetime Oy's DPA (docs/DPA_DATA_PROCESSING_AGREEMENT.md)
- Sub-processor obligations per GDPR Article 28
- EU data residency requirements where applicable
- Breach notification within 48 hours

### 5. Annual Fee and Payment
- Fee per tier (see table above)
- Annual billing, Net 30
- Revenue share (Tier 3): 10-15% of deals sourced through partner, paid quarterly

### 6. Annual Recertification
- Annual compliance review by Lifetime Oy
- Technical integration verification
- Security posture assessment
- Recertification fee included in annual fee

### 7. Termination
- Either party: 30 days written notice
- Immediate termination for material breach
- Badge removal within 7 days of termination
- No refund for partial year upon termination by Partner
- Pro-rata refund upon termination by Lifetime Oy without cause

### 8. Liability
- Proportional to annual fee
- No consequential damages
- Standard Finnish law indemnification

### 9. Governing Law
- Finnish law
- Espoon käräjäoikeus (Espoo District Court)
