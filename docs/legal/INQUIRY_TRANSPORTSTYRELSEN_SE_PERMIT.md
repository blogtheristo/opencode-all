## Context

Traficom (Finnish Transport and Communications Agency) replied that it is **not** the
competent authority for a transport permit covering operations conducted **in Sweden**,
and directed us to ask the Swedish authorities (**Transportstyrelsen**) how the required
permit is applied for. The driving-rights (ajo-oikeus) part of the original inquiry was
forwarded by Traficom to its own driving-licence advisory and will be answered separately.

This document is the inquiry to Transportstyrelsen. Send the Swedish version to
Transportstyrelsen's contact / e-service (kontakt.transportstyrelsen.se). The English
version is included for internal reference and as a fallback.

**Before sending:** replace `[ANGE VERKSAMHET …]` / `[OPERATION TYPE …]` with your actual
intended operation (e.g. godstransport på väg / persontransport / taxitrafik), or leave it
as a question if you want Transportstyrelsen to confirm the category for you.

Suggested channels: Transportstyrelsen "Yrkestrafik" — https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/yrkestrafik/ — or their contact form / e-service. Keep any personal data to their secure channel.

## English (internal reference / fallback)

**Subject:** Inquiry on operator permit for transport operations in Sweden — Finnish company

Hello,

Lifetime Oy (Business ID 0772407-9, VAT FI07724079), a Finnish company, is planning to
start transport operations in Sweden. We have been in contact with Traficom (the Finnish
Transport and Communications Agency), which directed us to the Swedish authorities regarding
the permit required for operations conducted in Sweden.

We therefore ask Transportstyrelsen for guidance on the following:

1. **Permit type.** Which permit is required for [OPERATION TYPE — e.g. road goods
   transport / passenger transport / taxi]? Please confirm the correct permit category.

2. **Application procedure.** How do we apply — forms, e-service, and required attachments?

3. **Requirements.** What are the requirements regarding
   - establishment / fixed place of business in Sweden,
   - a transport manager and professional competence (CPC),
   - financial standing (capital requirement), and
   - good repute?

4. **Recognition of Finnish qualifications.** Can Finnish professional competence
   (transport-manager CPC) and a Finnish EU Community licence be used in Sweden, or is a
   separate Swedish operator permit with establishment in Sweden required? Please clarify
   the line between international transport / cabotage under a Community licence and
   established operations in Sweden.

5. **Driving rights and driver qualification.** How are Finnish driving licences and
   professional driver qualification (YKB / Certificate of Professional Competence)
   recognised for drivers operating in Sweden?

6. **Processing time and fees.**
