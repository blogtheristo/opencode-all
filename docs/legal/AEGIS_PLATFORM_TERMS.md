## Purpose

This document will contain the Terms of Service for DWS IQ Aegis, the single-tenant sovereign AI platform (EUR 59,990 one-time + EUR 999/mo). These terms are separate from the standard DWS IQ 6 SaaS terms because Aegis involves dedicated infrastructure, sovereign data residency guarantees, and defence/dual-use considerations.

## Sections Required

### 1. Service Description
- Single-tenant sovereign AI platform
- Deployed on customer-designated EU infrastructure (UpCloud Helsinki or customer cloud)
- KYA framework with full agent governance
- EU-only data processing guarantee

### 2. Pricing and Payment
- One-time setup fee: EUR 59,990
- Monthly subscription: EUR 999/mo (standard), EUR 1,499–1,999/mo (GPU), EUR 3,000–5,000/mo (enterprise)
- Government tier: cost-plus model
- Payment terms: Net 30

### 3. Data Sovereignty
- All processing within EU/EEA
- No CLOUD Act exposure (no US-incorporated infrastructure for Aegis tier)
- Customer data never leaves designated jurisdiction
- Data residency certificate provided upon request

### 4. Infrastructure Ownership
- Infrastructure provisioned by Lifetime Oy on behalf of Customer
- Customer may request infrastructure transfer upon contract termination
- Lifetime Oy manages infrastructure during contract term
- Customer retains data ownership at all times

### 5. Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Uptime: 99.9% (standard), 99.95% (enterprise)
- Response time: 4h critical, 8h high, 24h medium
- Service credits for SLA breaches
- Planned maintenance windows with 48h advance notice

### 6. Security and Compliance
- ISO 27001 (Q3 2026), SOC 2 Type II (Q4 2026)
- PiTuKri audited (v1.1, transitioning to national criteria library autumn 2026)
- EU AI Act Article 12 logging
- KYA agent governance framework
- Annual security assessment report

### 7. Decommissioning
- 90-day transition assistance period
- Data export in machine-readable format
- Verified data destruction certificate
- Infrastructure deprovisioning confirmation

### 8. Dual-Use Considerations
- EU Dual-Use Regulation (EU 2021/821) compliance
- Export control screening for defence customers
- See separate Dual-Use Export Notice

### 9. Limitation of Liability
- TBD — Finnish law standards
- Proportional to annual contract value

### 10. Governing Law
- Finnish law
- Espoon käräjäoikeus (Espoo District Court)
