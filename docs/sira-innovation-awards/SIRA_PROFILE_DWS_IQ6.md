## 1 — Future-Shaping Index _(lead with this)_

Europe is putting **15,000+ Fit for 55 rules** — ETS, CBAM, CSRD, ETS2, EPBD, ESR, ReFuelEU, NIS2, EU AI Act — onto **25 regulated industries** on a compressed 2026–2030 timeline. Today they comply with spreadsheets, consultants, and 3–6 weeks per report. There is no platform that treats every regulation as a data contract and automates it end-to-end **without sending Europe's critical-infrastructure data to US clouds**.

DWS IQ 6 is that platform, and the future-shaping claim is **sovereignty at continental scale**: a three-pillar model — **Data / Technology / Operational sovereignty** — across the EU jurisdiction, 100% EU data residency (Finland, Germany, Netherlands), no customer data used to train public models. This is the infrastructure layer for how an entire regulated continent stays compliant _and_ sovereign.
