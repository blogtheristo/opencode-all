## Short (deck slide 1 / submission headline)

> **Sovereign EU compliance infrastructure.** One AI platform that turns **15,000+ Fit for 55 rules** into automated, audit-ready compliance for **25 EU-regulated industries** — built on a three-pillar sovereignty model (Data / Technology / Operational), 100% EU data residency, no customer data used to train public models.

## Full (first paragraph of a submission / pitch)

> Europe is loading **15,000+ Fit for 55 rules** — ETS, CBAM, CSRD, ETS2, EPBD, ESR, ReFuelEU, NIS2, EU AI Act — onto **25 regulated industries** on a compressed 2026–2030 timeline, and the only tools available are spreadsheets, consultants, and 3–6 weeks per report. There is no platform that treats every regulation as a data contract and automates it end-to-end **without sending Europe's critical-infrastructure data to US clouds**. DWS IQ 6 is that platform: a three-pillar sovereignty model (Data / Technology / Operational), 100% EU data residency (Finland, Germany, Netherlands), and one regulatory stack across all 25 industries. This is the infrastructure layer for how an entire regulated continent stays compliant _and_ sovereign.

## Proof point (place _under_ the story, not as the headline)

> Edge deployment on NVIDIA Jetson cuts inference cost by **EUR 333,000/year** vs. cloud-only at sub-100ms latency — sovereignty that is also cheaper.
