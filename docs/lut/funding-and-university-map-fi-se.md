## 2. Research-question staging (candidate angles targeting LUT × KTH)

The EV/Uber fleet at the Stockholm driftställe is simultaneously **(a)** Vinnova evidence,
**(b)** a live research testbed, and **(c)** an edge-control-room-capital loop source. The RQs
should exploit that triple use:

- **RQ-A (KTH/TECoSA angle — trustworthy edge):** How can agentic AI orchestration be made
  trustworthy and verifiable at the edge for regulated transport/fleet operations (real-time
  constraints, KYA-style trust scoring, evidence chains)?
- **RQ-B (LUT angle — sovereignty & compliance):** What architecture patterns let EU
  organisations retain sovereign control (data, models, evidence) while adopting agentic AI
  under NIS2 / GDPR / EU AI Act — and how is compliance evidence generated as a by-product of
  operations rather than as overhead?
- **RQ-C (bridge — learning loop / token capital):** Do proprietary real-world traces from an
  owned EV fleet (edge control-room capital) measurably improve an orchestration platform's
  private evals and learning loop versus synthetic/benchmark data — i.e. does physical
  operation compound into owned AI capability?

Each RQ maps to: LUT scientific framing (PhD) · KTH/TECoSA collaboration scope (MoU) · Vinnova
Offering-1 verification activities (funded work at the driftställe). One fleet, three uses.
