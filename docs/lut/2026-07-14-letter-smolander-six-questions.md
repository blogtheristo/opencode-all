## Send-ready draft (v1.1 — superseded by the as-sent version; retained per formulation-preservation rule)

**Subject:** PhD research direction — six questions grounded in deployed sovereign AI systems

Hi Kari,

Quick update since we last spoke. I've been in touch with KTH/TECoSA (via Martin Törngren) and have reached out to the Stockholm Resilience Centre (Victor Galaz); that work has sharpened six research questions grounded in our live production systems rather than pure theory.

**Q1. How can sovereign edge AI orchestration be formally verified for trustworthiness in regulated industrial environments?**
Anchor: KTH/TECoSA (Törngren). Testbed: DGX-based Aegis + KYA edge control room. EU hook: AI Act high-risk, NIS2.

**Q2. What governance structures are required for multi-agent AI systems to produce legally defensible environmental compliance outputs under EU regulation?**
Anchor: Stockholm Resilience Centre (Galaz). Testbed: Article 12 logging pipeline + KYA evidence chain over CBAM/CSRD workloads. EU hook: AI Act Art. 12, CBAM/CSRD.

**Q3. How can sovereign AI system transparency be operationalized for public legibility under EU AI Act Article 50?**
Anchor: science communication via Universeum/Chalmers. Testbed: DWS IQ transparency/disclosure surface. EU hook: AI Act Art. 50.

**Q4. How can safety-performance trade-offs in sovereign edge AI systems be formally modeled and validated under real-time operational constraints?**
Anchor: Törngren's SAFECOMP safety-argument methodology. Testbed: Aegis edge deployment (safety evidence vs. latency/compute). EU hook: AI Act Art. 12, safety-critical CPS.

**Q5. How can a Know-Your-Agent (KYA) trust-scoring model be designed and validated as an open conformance standard for agentic AI in EU-regulated industries?**
Anchor: LUT-native (Design Science). Testbed: KYA reference implementation in production. EU hook: AI Act Art. 12/14/72. This is the technical governance spine that Q1, Q2 and Q4 instantiate and Q3 exposes.

**Q6. How can industrial enterprises govern the transition from voluntary ESG reporting to mandatory sovereign-AI compliance infrastructure without disrupting existing management systems?**
Anchor: LUT Industrial Management — your department's territory (software-engineering management, digital transformation). Testbed: Outokumpu + construction-sector pilots. EU hook: CSRD/CBAM mandatory-reporting transition, AI Act. This is the organizational capstone — the management-layer counterpart to Q5's technical spine.

A note on where the external anchors stand, so you have the honest picture: the KTH/TECoSA thread (Q1, Q4) is live, and Q5 (KYA) and Q6 are grounded in our own production system and Finnish pilots. For Q2 and Q3, I've contacted the anchors — Galaz at the Stockholm Resilience Centre, and Universeum/Chalmers respectively — but haven't yet had a reply, so those two are prospective rather than confirmed.

What makes LUT the stronger home for this work is the Finnish industrial enterprise ties. Questions 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6 all have live testbeds through existing customers and pilot targets (Outokumpu, construction sector), which gives the doctoral research empirical grounding that KTH or SRC alone cannot provide.

In parallel I'm establishing a Swedish entity (Lifetime AB — incorporation being finalized now) and pursuing a Vinnova/TECoSA funding track alongside the KTH collaboration, so the PhD would sit inside an emerging cross-border Nordic research infrastructure rather than a purely Finnish thesis.

I've attached a 10-page memo mapping our production system to these research questions, including an explicit note on what the current KYA work does not yet cover. Happy to discuss which direction aligns best with LUT's doctoral program strengths and your own research interests. Would 20 minutes work in the next couple of weeks?

Best,
Risto
