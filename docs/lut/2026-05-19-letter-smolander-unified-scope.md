Dear Kari,

Two industry announcements in the past 48 hours have, in my view, named the category my doctoral work has been pursuing — and given me a reason to consolidate the six research questions I sent earlier (PHD_FIREHORSE §1.2) into a single, more defendable spine. I am writing to share the consolidation and to ask for your guidance before I commit it to the thesis.

### 1. The two industrial signals

- **18 May 2026 — Dell + Palantir announce an On-Premises AI Operating System** (Dell Newsroom, "Dell and Palantir Introduce an On-Premises AI Operating System", byline Rajbir Panag). Dell hardware (AI Factory with NVIDIA) + Palantir Foundry / AIP as semantic and agent layer. Targets regulated industries and governments. Both primary vendors US-domiciled; CLOUD Act applies to the vendor relationship.
- **19 May 2026 — Google presents the *Antigravity Agent Operating System*** publicly with a benchmark demonstration: 93 subagents, 12 hours, 15,000+ model requests, 2.6 billion tokens, under USD 1,000 API credits. Cloud-native, US-domiciled, hyperscaler-bundled.

Two announcements, two opposite ends of a spectrum (on-premises co-branded vs. cloud hyperscaler), one shared category claim: the **Agent / AI Operating System**. The category is being publicly defined right now. This timing window — 2026–2027 — is when the open, EU-sovereign variant must be named and published if it is to be cited as a peer rather than a follower.

### 2. The unified doctoral spine

I propose to reframe the doctoral work around a single artefactual object:

> **DWS OS** — the NVIDIA-based, EU-sovereign on-premises AI operating system. Already operating in 24/7 production on NVIDIA GB10 Grace Blackwell (Acer Veriton GN100), running Nemotron 3 Super (123.6B / 12.7B-active MoE) and Qwen3-Coder 30B via Ollama, under the OpenClaw agent runtime, with KYA (Know-Your-Agent) governance and Firecracker MicroVM hardware isolation (< 125 ms kill, AWS benchmark). Operator: Lifetime Oy, Finland.

The six prior research questions (Ontologies, KYA, Standardization, Agentic Commerce, EU eWallet, Transactions — PHD_FIREHORSE §1.2) are not abandoned. They become **workloads on, and instantiations of, DWS OS**. KYA remains the governance standard; the carbon balance sheet (§1.4) becomes the flagship workload; the agentic-commerce / eWallet / transaction primitives become a single forward-looking chapter rather than separate articles. This collapses six loosely related streams into one coherent dissertation with a named, defendable artefact and one named comparison set (Dell + Palantir on-prem, Google Antigravity cloud, Palantir Foundry standalone, pure-cloud frontier).

### 4. Recommended dissertation structure

If the consolidation has merit, the publication phasing changes as follows:

- **Article 1 (2026, Q3 target).** *KYA Standard — An Open-Source Governance Framework for Agentic AI in EU-Regulated Industries.* Already drafted; unchanged from the prior plan. Open-licensed conformance suite mapped to EU AI Act Art. 12 / 14 / 72.
- **Article 2 (2027).** *Sovereign by Construction — An Architectural Pattern for the EU On-Premises AI Operating System.* This is the new article. Named comparators: Dell + Palantir on-prem AI OS (18.05.2026), Google Antigravity Agent OS (19.05.2026), Palantir Foundry standalone. 8-dimension sovereignty rubric (DWS Sovereignty Standard) as the evaluation instrument.
- **Articles 3–4 (2028).** Empirical 20-vertical case base. *Two named verticals as flagships:* (i) carbon balance sheet under CBAM / ETS / CSRD; (ii) dual-use / defence under NIS2 + national security legal regimes.
- **Monograph theoretical chapter (2028–2029).** *The Reasoning–Behaviour Gap and Hardware-Attested Falsifiability.* Bridges the 2026 multi-agent alignment literature (Shapira *Agents of Chaos*, NDSS 2026; Emergence AI *World*, 2026; Smitsman et al. EARTHwise EAB, 2026) to KYA hardware attestation as the falsifiability locus. Highest theoretical originality.
- **Forward-looking chapter (monograph only).** Agentic Commerce, EU eWallet, and Transaction primitives folded together. Real but premature for separate articles at thesis horizon (eIDAS 2.0 wallets not yet operational at industrial scale).

What this drops from the prior plan: scenarios E (eWallet) and F (Transactions) as standalone articles, and C (Standardization pathway) as a separate RQ — both absorb into Article 1's dissemination strategy.

### 5. Out-of-box directions I considered but did not pick (and why I want your view)

- **Formal-verification track.** Specify the 8-dim sovereignty rubric in TLA+ / Alloy and prove DWS OS satisfies it under stated assumptions; run the same proof against Dell + Palantir from public information. High prestige ceiling, narrow venue (IEEE S&P workshop / USENIX Security). I judged this **outside the IS-research zone** where your supervision is strongest. If you disagree, I will pursue it as Article 2's methodological backbone.
- **Behavioural-benchmark track.** Submit DWS OS into the EARTHwise EAB / ASB benchmark and publish a behavioural conformance paper. Real, but the benchmark is still maturing and would put a moving target inside the thesis schedule. I kept this as a side-channel publication, not an Article.

### 6. Five open questions for you

These are the points where I would benefit most from your guidance before writing the v3.58 thesis revision.

1. **Venue strategy for Article 1.** Information-systems conference (ECIS / ICIS family), security-and-governance venue (NDSS / IEEE S&P workshop), or DSR venue (DESRIST)? Each frames KYA differently.
2. **Naming live commercial products in comparative tables.** Is the LUT convention to anonymize (Vendor A / Vendor B / Vendor C), or are named comparisons acceptable where the data source is a public announcement (here, Dell + Palantir 18.05.2026 and Google Antigravity 19.05.2026)?
3. **Ethics envelope.** Empirical validation runs in paying-customer industrial environments under DPA. Does LUT's doctoral school require additional ethics submission given that the agent under study acts with real industrial consequences?
4. **Article-led vs monograph-led balance.** Current phasing is article-led (four articles → synthesis). Would you prefer a heavier monograph earlier (2027) with articles as derivatives?
5. **Co-authorship policy.** The KYA reference implementation is engineered with named collaborators. What is the LUT policy on co-authored articles in the doctoral portfolio, and what proportion of single-authored work is expected?
