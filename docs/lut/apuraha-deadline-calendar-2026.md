## Requirements at a glance — what each live application needs from you

**Liikesivistysrahasto (deadline 15 Aug — ~1 month):**

1. Research + financing plan, 2–3 pages (background, research questions, methods) — draw
   from `docs/lut/tutkimussuunnitelma-lut-2026-fi.md`, trimmed to LSR length.
2. 1-page project description: contribution/relevance to **Finnish business and economic
   growth** (this is the kauppatiede framing lever — lead with agentic commerce,
   sovereignty as a market, EU compliance cost reduction).
3. CV.
4. Professor's statement filed **by the professor** by 31 Aug (after your own deadline).

**Nokia Foundation (opens 3 Aug, deadline 15 Sep):**

1. Doctoral research plan, ICT-framed (lead with the Aegis architecture + KYA governance —
   the ICT/engineering core, not the economics angle).
2. Principal supervisor's **commitment statement** filed by the supervisor in the e-tool.
3. Doctoral studies at a Finnish university (LUT) — enrolment is the gating requirement.
