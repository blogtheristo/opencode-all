## One sentence

The LUT doctorate (4 years, 2027–2030) and the DWS IQ version roadmap
(7 → 8 → 9, 2026–2031) are the same programme viewed from two sides: each DWS
version supplies the production testbed that makes the research questions
falsifiable, and each dissertation article can supply independent,
peer-reviewed evidence relevant to the product's Level 4 claims. The Product
Gate—not academic status—determines whether a product claim passes.

## Why the interlock wins on both causes

**Product → PhD (the testbed direction):**

- DWS 7's paying industrial customers (target: 16 by Q4 2027) are the empirical
  case base no competing doctoral candidate has — real MGD/CRC data, Article 12
  lineage, audit-grade decision records from regulated EU industry.
- The Strategy Loop's own discipline ("independently reproduce Level 4–5 claims
  annually", `Strategy_Loop.md` §6) _is_ an academic validation cycle — the
  doctoral research subjects the evidence to external academic scrutiny.
- KYA's four falsifiability gates (`standards/kya-open/SPEC.md` §0.6) cannot be
  cleared by Lifetime Oy alone by design. The university channel is the realistic
  source of gate #1 (independent implementations built by parties with no
  commercial relationship) and gate #2 (a conformance suite an implementation
  can fail, built as reviewed research instrumentation).

**PhD → Product (the proof direction):**

- Peer-reviewed articles become first-class Evidence Room artifacts
  (`Strategy_Loop.md` §7): published, citable evidence relevant to the Level 4 evaluation that any enterprise buyer — R03/R04 research leaders,
  R13/R15 technical approvers — can verify without trusting our own materials.
- The §4 intelligence maturity model's Level 4 requires independent
  reproduction, adversarial review, and an expert baseline. Doctoral research can
  contribute evidence to those tests, but it does not certify the product or
  replace an independent Product Gate review.
- Enrolled doctoral status unlocks funding lanes closed to a plain company:
  Finnish foundations requiring a supervisor statement, Business Finland
  research tracks, EU instruments with academic-partner requirements
  (`docs/lut/fundraising-roadmap-2026-2027.md`, `docs/lut/fi-se-lanes-research-2026-07.md`).

## Success criteria — both tracks, one scoreboard

The strategy works when each row is true on _both_ sides; a row true on one
side only is a drift warning:

| Signal                                     | PhD side                                           | Product side                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Falsifiable evidence                       | Article accepted at peer review                    | Same evidence pack passes a customer/auditor review (MGD counted, Article 12 lineage intact)        |
| Independent validation                     | External co-authors / reviewers reproduce results  | KYA gate #1–#2 progress: an implementation not built by Lifetime, a conformance suite that can fail |
| Knowledge discovery (post-doctorate level) | A novel validated finding per dissertation article | ≥1 validated discovery per quarter changes an enterprise decision (`Strategy_Loop.md` §11)          |
| Funding                                    | Foundation grants + doctoral funding secured       | MRR growth carries the company without diluting for research time                                   |

## Risks and their controls

1. **Over-scoping** — the dominant failure mode named by the supervisory review
   (PR #1712). Control: articles map 1:1 to already-planned DWS version
   deliverables; no article requires work the product roadmap doesn't fund.
2. **Founder bandwidth** (CEO + doctorate). Control: the interlock is designed
   so the same artifact serves both sides once — evidence packs, evaluation
   runs, and gate audits are produced for customers and _reused_ in articles,
   never duplicated.
3. **Funding window misses.** Both live 2026 windows require the supervisor
   statement — the critical path is the Smolander working session, not the
   writing. Next windows otherwise fall to 2027
   (`docs/lut/apuraha-deadline-calendar-2026.md` — recovered from unmerged
   PR #1663 on 05.08.2026; also `docs/lut/fi-se-lanes-research-2026-07.md`).
4. **Thesis-lock discipline.** The RQ set sits in the supervisor's inbox;
   revisions are RED-zone (HIC + external act). This roadmap deliberately cites
   RQs by role (assurance / ontology / evaluation) rather than renumbering them.
5. **DWS 9 slippage is acceptable; Y4 is not coupled to it.** The defense
   depends on Articles I–III evidence from versions 7–8 only; DWS 9 timing is
   q-day-gated by design and the dissertation must not wait on it.
