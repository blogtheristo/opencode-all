## 1. Apuraha lane (FI research grants) — the personal-grant lane

**Recipient:** Risto Anton Päärni, doctoral candidate at LUT — field: AI governance / EU
regulatory compliance / agent-audit / sovereign AI (DWS IQ).

### 1.1 BOTTOM LINE — the negative-equity gate does NOT bite here

**The Lifetime Oy negative-equity / GBER UiD classification does not touch personal doctoral
apurahat. The apuraha lane is clean, and _apuraha → stay Finland_ is fully viable.** Why:

1. **Finnish foundation apurahat are _henkilöapurahat_ — awarded to the individual researcher
   (or a named working group of individuals), not to a company.** Every foundation checked
   (SKR, Wihuri, TES, LSR, KAUTE, Emil Aaltonen, TSR) pays the natural person. The recipient
   of record is Risto, not Lifetime Oy.
2. **Private foundation grants are not state aid.** GBER — including the Art. 2(18) UiD test
   Business Finland applies — governs _state aid to undertakings_. A private säätiö disbursing
   its own endowment to an individual is outside the state-aid regime, so the firm-in-difficulty
   test has no hook. Finnish tax law treats these as personal scholarship income to the
   individual (tax-free up to the state artist-grant level, ~€2,300/mo in 2026), explicitly not
   a public/state grant — confirming the recipient is the person. [vero.fi / suomi.fi]
3. **The Oy's balance sheet is not an eligibility input.** Foundations assess the research plan,
   the researcher's merit, and field fit — there is no equity/solvency gate in any application
   instruction reviewed.
4. **Where the gate _does_ bite: Business Finland only.** BF is the state-aid channel; its
   published rule is that it "cannot grant funding to a company in difficulty," assessing equity
   even mid-financial-period. Lifetime Oy's negative equity blocks BF until cured — the one
   Finnish instrument to treat as closed. [businessfinland.fi]

only to Swedish-registered companies (see §3.4).

### 3.2 How to apply + regional structure

- **No fixed deadlines — rolling.** Normal decision ~3–4 weeks on a complete application.
- Apply online at almi.se; an adviser reviews situation, finances, model, plans ("not only
  figures" — potential/market/goals count).
- **Regional office model.** For a **Västra Götaland HQ → Almi Företagspartner Väst AB (Almi
  Väst)**; equity via **Almi Invest Västsverige**. National line 0771-55 85 00.

### 3.3 Credit-gate analysis (the load-bearing question)

1. **Repayment capacity is the primary test.** Almi's own language: a loan needs "a reasonable
   likelihood the company can repay." A pre-revenue / thin-equity <3y AB has weak demonstrable
   repayment capacity → the central obstacle for the larger loans (Business/Growth/Green).
2. **Company age is NOT a bar.** New businesses are welcome; you can apply pre-registration.
   "Under 3 years" does not disqualify — the balance-sheet story does.
3. **Co-financing gate compounds it.** Business/Growth/Green loans normally require a bank/owner
   match (Almi ~30–50%). A young AB that cannot bring a match effectively cannot pass — the bank
   must also say yes.
4. **Mitigants that fit Lifetime AB:** **Mikrolån** (no co-finance gate, ≤SEK 250k — lowest
   friction) and **Innovationslån** (50% project financing with own-work counting as co-finance,
   stage-matched to a pre-revenue deep-tech build).
5. **Does the Finnish Oy's negative equity bite Almi's group view?** Not stated on any primary
   Almi page — Almi assesses the **Swedish applicant**, not a foreign affiliate. **However
   (verify):** Growth/Green loans run under the **EIF InvestEU guarantee**, which carries the EU
   **"undertaking in difficulty" (UiD)** test assessed at the **single-economic-entity/group**
   level — so a commonly-controlled affiliate with negative equity could _in principle_ taint the
   group's UiD status and block a guaranteed loan. Standard InvestEU/GBER logic, not spelled out
   by Almi — **confirm with Almi Väst + the InvestEU guarantee terms before relying on
   Growth/Green.** Mikrolån and plain Företagslån are **not** under that guarantee, so the
   group-UiD screen is less likely to reach them (verify).

### 3.4 Lifetime Oy — out of scope

Almi lends to Swedish-registered companies only; Lifetime Oy is Finnish → **not eligible to
borrow**. Its only relevance is as a possible affiliate in the UiD/group screen for EU-guaranteed
products (§3.3 pt 5).

### 3.5 Bottom line — can Lifetime AB realistically draw Almi financing?

- **Yes, narrowly — via the small, low-gate products:** **(1) Mikrolån (≤SEK 250k)** — no
  co-finance gate, built for this profile (most realistic); **(2) Innovationslån (SEK 50k–500k)**
  — stage-matched, own-work counts toward the 50% (second).
- **Larger debt (Företagslån / Tillväxtlån / Grönt lån) is gated** by repayment capacity the
  young AB likely lacks and a bank/owner co-finance match it must secure first — and Growth/Green
  additionally carry the **EIF InvestEU UiD group screen (verify)** the negative-equity Finnish
  affiliate could complicate.
- **Almi Invest** offers a real AI/deep-tech **equity** path (SEK 1–4M) but is dilutive, needs
  co-investors, and **excludes single-founder companies** — a different track.

**Decision-rule check ("apuraha → stay Finland"):** Almi is **debt, not grant** (Verifieringsbidrag
aside), a **Swedish-AB-only** lever that exists _because_ Lifetime AB exists in Sweden. It is **not**
a substitute for Finnish non-dilutive grants and does not violate "grants stay in Finland."
Position it as: **Mikrolån/Innovationslån as small early debt for the Swedish vehicle**, Almi Invest
held as an optional equity track, Growth/Green deferred until the AB has revenue, a bank co-finance
partner, and a clean group UiD position.

_"Support can be granted to companies that have a workplace in the support area in Västra
Götaland,"_ and for the seed instrument _"Företaget ska vara registrerat i Västra Götaland och
avser att driva verksamheten här."_

- **Lifetime AB** — a Swedish AB, but unless it has a **registered säte/workplace in Västra
  Götaland**, it **does not qualify today**. A Swedish registration elsewhere (e.g. Stockholm) is
  not sufficient. The fix is concrete but has a cost: register a workplace/branch in the Göteborg
  region and run real operations there.
- **<3-years status is an _advantage_ here, not a barrier.** The "Nystartade innovativa företag"
  seed instrument **explicitly prioritises companies under 3 years** (max 5). This is the opposite
  of the EU-instrument concern — the GBER under-3-years exemption does **not** bite the VGR lane.
  The binding constraint is **territorial registration**, plus (for the established-company
  checkar) a **≥2 annual reports / turnover threshold** a very young AB may not yet meet.

### 4.3 Most-relevant instrument detail

- **#5 Nystartade innovativa företag (best profile match).** A **conditional seed loan
  (villkorslån)** — repaid once the innovation generates revenue, **not a grant**. Max **SEK
  850,000 (verify)**, ≤65% of development cost. Eligibility: ≤10 employees, business plan, **under
  5 years (prioritised under 3)**, innovation height assessed by a **third party (Almi innovation
  advisor or an incubator)**, and **registered in Västra Götaland**. DWS IQ fits "teknik- och
  kunskapsbaserade företag" precisely and the under-3-years status is a _priority_. **Blocked
  solely by the VG registration gate + the Almi/incubator innovation-height assessment.**
- **#1 Digitaliseringscheck** is strong on product fit (AI/digitalisation) but requires **≥2 filed
  annual reports** — a <3y AB likely lacks them, so it is likely **not qualified today** on two
  counts (registration + annual-report history).
- **#9 Projektstöd** and **#10 ERUF** are realistically **indirect** — access is as an SME partner
  inside a science-park / Business Region Göteborg / ERUF innovation-hub project, not as lead
  grantee.

### 4.4 Adjacent actors (cross-reference — not VGR grants)

- **Almi Väst** is **51% Almi AB / 36.75% VGR / 12.25% Business Region Göteborg** — offers loans,
  advisory, and (via **Almi Invest Västsverige**) early-stage VC, and is the standard **third-party
  innovation-height assessor** the seed loan (#5) requires. It is the natural **first contact once
  VG-registered** (ties this lane to §3 Almi).
- **Science parks / innovation milieux (VGR co-funded):** Lindholmen (mobility/AI), Johanneberg
  (Chalmers), Sahlgrenska (life science), Science Park Borås, Gothia, Innovatum. These are the
  entry points to ERUF/projektstöd consortia and to **incubator status** (which satisfies the
  seed-loan innovation-height gate). **Business Region Göteborg** is the practical front door.

### 4.5 Bottom line — does Lifetime AB qualify today?

**Not today** — no VGR money is reachable without a **registered workplace/operation in Västra
Götaland**. Quantify the cost of establishing VG presence before pursuing any instrument. Once
gated, the **Nystartade innovativa företag seed loan** (SEK 850,000 (verify), 65%, under-3-years
prioritised) is the best-fit instrument — but it is a **conditional loan** needing Almi/incubator
sign-off. An **indirect route is available even before heavy VG footprint:** join a science-park /
Business Region Göteborg / ERUF innovation-hub project as an SME partner.

### 5.3 What this means for the roadmap

1. **Finland carries the near-term non-dilutive load.** Two of the three soonest FI/SE deadlines
   are personal apurahat (LSR 15 Aug, TSR/SKR Oct), untouched by the Oy's GBER problem. This is
   why _apuraha → stay Finland_ is not just a preference but the structurally-cleanest lane.
2. **Sweden carries the company-grant load via the AB.** Vinnova is the real Swedish grant door,
   and the AB's <3y status is an asset (Innovativa Startups <10y cap; de minimis basis). Eurostars
   Call 11 (10 Sep) is the one live company window and doubles as the roadmap's anchor instrument
   (§3–§4 of the master roadmap).
3. **Almi and VGR are conditional/second-order.** Almi is **debt** (only Mikrolån/Innovationslån
   are realistically reachable for a thin-balance-sheet AB); VGR is **grant-shaped but gated behind
   a Västra Götaland registration** the AB does not yet hold. Neither is a near-term non-dilutive
   win without either taking on debt or standing up VG presence.
4. **The Oy stays a partner, never a Swedish applicant.** Across Vinnova/Almi/VGR the Oy's negative
   equity is only a risk if it applies or co-finances on the Swedish side. Its clean role is a
   BF-funded foreign consortium partner (Eurostars / planning grant), with a minimal funded line.
