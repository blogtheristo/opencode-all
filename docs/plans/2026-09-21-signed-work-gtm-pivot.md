## 2. Diagnosis — the Trophy Effect

A government cheque plus a standing task force closes the category they were funded to "solve". They must announce. They will copy a six-to-eight-month-ahead evangelist to fill the announcement. Payday continues. The issue is _politically_ solved.

The founder already did what a compliance officer believes AI can do to compliance. That is why they can copy the _advice_. That is also why **selling that advice as a product is over.** They do not need another vendor to complete a trophy they already hold.

This is the same mechanic in industry: a CSO who hired a Big Four to "do CSRD" also has a trophy. The plant still cannot produce the signed artifact that lets a shipment leave or a customer pay.

June 2026 already said "don't sell fine-avoidance; sell tender velocity" (`docs/field-notes/2026-06-10-offensive-positioning-dws10.md`). It still sold the noun _compliance_. The trophy-holder owns that noun. First-touch must drop it.
