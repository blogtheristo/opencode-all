## Page 2 — Origin Story: The Shrimp Boat Captain

### The Narrative

There's a line in Forrest Gump that most people remember wrong. Bubba doesn't sell Forrest on the economics of shrimping. He doesn't pitch the TAM or the unit economics. He makes Forrest feel like he belongs on that boat. He gives him a role, a purpose, and a deck to stand on.

That's the feeling Risto Paarni wants his investors to have. Not "I backed a company." But "I'm crew on this ship."

Paarni spent 35 years in industrial IT. Factory floors in Finland. ERP migrations across Scandinavia. Cloud architecture for manufacturers who still ran systems built in the 1990s. He holds an M.Sc. in Industrial Management — the degree that sits at the intersection of engineering and business, the exact boundary where compliance problems live.

Two years ago he looked at the EU's Fit for 55 package — ETS, CBAM, CSRD, EPBD, and a dozen more regulations hitting 25+ industries simultaneously — and saw what no one was building: a single platform that treats every regulation as a data contract, maps them to one system, and automates compliance end-to-end.

Every regulation tells you what to measure. CBAM tells you what to report at the border. CSRD tells you how to disclose. They're not separate problems. They're 15,000 rules in one system. And systems are what engineers build.

So he built it.

He still writes code daily. He manages the GitHub repo. He reviews architecture decisions. When a demo breaks, he fixes it himself. The CEO of an AI company should understand the models, the infrastructure, and the deployment constraints — not just the market opportunity.

That's the origin story. Not a pivot from fintech. Not a Stanford MBA chasing a trend. An industrial engineer who spent three decades watching factories struggle with compliance — and finally built the tool they needed.

## Page 3 — The Problem: 15,000 Rules, Zero Tools

### The compliance tsunami

The EU's Fit for 55 package is the most ambitious climate regulation in history. It covers 25+ industries and spans regulations that are arriving on a compressed timeline:

| Regulation | What it demands | Deadline |
|------------|----------------|----------|
| **ETS** | Emissions allowance tracking, trading, surrender | Active now |
| **CBAM** | Carbon calculations on every import at the EU border | Full implementation 2026 |
| **CSRD** | ESRS E1 climate disclosures, audit-ready data | Expanding 2026 |
| **ETS2** | Road transport + buildings emissions trading | 2027 |
| **EPBD** | Embodied carbon reporting for buildings >1,000 sqm | 2028 |
| **ESR** | National target tracking, non-ETS sectors | Active now |
| **ReFuelEU** | Sustainable aviation fuel mandates | Active now |
| **NIS2** | Cybersecurity compliance for critical infrastructure | Active now |
| **EU AI Act** | Automatic logging of AI system events (Article 12) | 2026 |

### What companies use today

Spreadsheets. Consultants. Manual data entry. Time to produce a compliance report: 3-6 weeks.

### Why existing tools fail

- General-purpose ESG dashboards don't handle regulation-specific logic
- Consulting firms don't scale — they sell hours, not automation
- Cloud-only platforms can't serve air-gapped factories or mid-ocean vessels
- No existing platform covers all 25+ industries with one regulatory stack

### The gap DWS IQ 6 fills

One platform. **16 captain hats. 25+ EU industries.** Every Fit for 55 regulation. Edge-deployed. Revenue-generating today.

## Page 4 — Edge AI Economics: The EUR 333,000 Story

### This is the headline

Every press pitch, every investor conversation, every LinkedIn post should lead with this number. It's concrete, differentiated, and verifiable.

### The decision

Instead of routing all AI inference through cloud APIs — paying per token, accepting latency, depending on connectivity — Lifetime deploys NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano devices at the edge. Construction sites. Factories. Maritime vessels.

### The numbers

| Metric | Cloud-only competitor | Lifetime (edge AI) |
|--------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Monthly inference cost | EUR 27,750+ | EUR 0 (on-device) |
| Monthly infrastructure | EUR 2,000-5,000 | EUR 15-50 |
| Inference latency | 2-5 seconds | Under 100ms |
| Works offline | No | Yes |
| Annual total cost | EUR 350,000+ | EUR 17,000 (incl. hardware) |
| **Annual savings** | — | **EUR 333,000** |

### The hardware

- **Device:** NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano (~EUR 500/unit)
- **Power:** 7-15 watts (solar-capable for remote sites)
- **Hardware ROI payback:** Under 2 months
- **Planned deployment:** 50 sites

### Why edge AI isn't optional for heavy industry

Construction sites don't have reliable internet. Factories have air-gapped OT networks. Maritime vessels operate mid-ocean. A compliance platform that requires a cloud connection has already failed the use case.

### The infrastructure stack

- **Edge:** NVIDIA Jetson + AWS IoT Greengrass across deployment sites
- **Core:** Google Cloud Run + Groq API + LlamaStack orchestration
- **Client:** Chromebook Plus with Progressive Web App
- **Data:** Supabase (hot) + AWS S3 (cold archive)
- **AI models:** Claude (Anthropic) + Google Gemini + Groq, via MCP
- **Monthly infrastructure cost:** EUR 15-50

## Page 5 — Platform Architecture

### What DWS IQ 6 actually is

A multi-agent AI platform that automates climate compliance across **16 captain hats covering 25+ EU industries**. Four layers:

**Brain — Multi-Agent Orchestration**
Coordinates real-time decision-making across compliance domains. Built on Google Cloud Run with Groq LPU acceleration. Processes regulatory signals, emissions data, and supply chain inputs simultaneously.

**Memory — Lifetime World Model**
Climate-specialized knowledge base indexed across 15,000+ EU regulatory documents and 16 industry verticals. Purpose-built for regulatory intelligence, not a general-purpose LLM repurposed for compliance.

**Body — Firehorse Product Suite**
Operational layer: report generation, workflow automation, document management, omnichannel outreach. The Firehorse V2 module for construction uses Physical AI to reconstruct site events and generate audit-ready compliance documentation.

**Hands — Lifetime Fleet**
Physical execution: vehicles, IoT devices, and drones that extend the platform into the real world. Already operational, generating EUR 7-14k MRR through Uber, Taksi Helsinki, and Kela National Care transportation.

### The 16 captain hats (covering 25+ industries)

The platform serves 25+ EU regulated industries. The captain framework clusters them into **16 captain hats** — each hat covers one or more related industries with shared regulatory exposure.

**Tier 1 — Critical emissions (8 hats):** Power/heat generation, iron/steel, cement/lime, chemicals, aviation, maritime shipping, road transport/logistics, construction/buildings.

**Tier 2 — Important emissions (8 hats):** Agriculture, waste management, aluminium, pulp/paper, petroleum refining, food/beverage, glass/ceramics, mining.

### Construction beachhead

EPBD mandates embodied carbon reporting for buildings over 1,000 sqm by 2028. Current manual processes can't meet this deadline. DWS IQ 6 can.

A Deputy who doesn't engage isn't crew — they're cargo. The structure self-selects for people who actually want to work.

A Platinum Captain of Construction understands building sites. A Platinum Captain of Maritime knows what happens mid-ocean. A Platinum Captain of Chemicals has the Rolodex that opens procurement doors. By matching investors to industries, every Captain brings domain knowledge — not just capital.

The Deputy tier gives rising stars a path in at EUR 25k — accessible, but with real duties. They earn their way to Platinum. No free rides.
