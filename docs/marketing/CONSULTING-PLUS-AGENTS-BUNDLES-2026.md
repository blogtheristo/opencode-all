## 1. The Motion

> **"The consultant leaves. The agent stays."**

A consulting engagement or training course produces a deliverable — a CSRD readiness report, a safety audit, an AI literacy cohort. Today, when the consultant leaves, the work has to be re-bought next quarter. With DWS, the **same Skilled Agent** that the consultant used during the engagement stays installed at 15–30% of an FTE cost. The customer doesn't re-buy — they keep the worker.

This is the natural bridge from advisory-spend to per-agent-month spend, and from per-deployment to recurring. It also resolves two buyer objections in one move:

1. *"Can your AI really do this?"* — Answered by the engagement itself.
2. *"How do we operationalize what you produced?"* — The agent that produced it is the operating answer.

### Tier definitions

- **T1 — Lead motions (drive 70% of sales effort).** These four bundles match: regulatory pain that is already on the buyer's desk, an obvious named buyer with budget today, fast close, and the cleanest "land with consult / keep the agent" story. Every Q1/26 manifesto account gets one of T1 as the opening offer.
- **T2 — Second wave inside landed accounts.** Run only after T1 lands or in accounts with an explicit data-residency, carbon, or construction-ops trigger.
- **T3 — Channel-led / Salesforce-attached.** Lower-touch, faster-closing, smaller ACV. Use for AppExchange listing and inbound sales.
- **T4 — Strategic / transformation accounts only.** Don't push; only pursue when the buyer initiates a programmatic conversation (e.g., a CFO/CEO-led automation programme).

## 5. Operating Rules

1. **No engagement is sold without an agent attached** for at least 12 months. Pure consulting is offered only as an exception, priced 25% above the bundle rate (so the bundle is always the rational choice).
2. **The agent SKU is the recurring product.** Year-2 quotes drop the engagement line and renew only the agent subscription (per `DWS_DYNAMIC_DEPLOYMENT_PRICING_STANDARD.md` — monthly subscription is a required separate product).
3. **The engagement deliverable is the agent's first output**, not a parallel artifact. The consultant runs the agent; the customer signs off on the agent's work.
4. **Senior Specialist day rate floor: €1,500/day direct, €1,200/day contractor** (Blue/Green split per Dynamic Deployment Pricing Standard §11). Training day rate floor: €999/seat for a half-day, €499/seat for the existing EU AI Act Literacy SKU.
5. **Marketplace-listed agents always carry the gross-up** (+3% MSFT, +18% SF, per Skilled Agents Price List §5–6). Bundles sold direct are at sticker.
6. **Every bundle quote shows the human-baseline anchor line** ("Replaces N FTEs + advisory retainer at €X/yr today"). No SaaS comparison appears on the quote.
