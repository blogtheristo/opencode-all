## 0. Headline

**The entire funding plan needs almost no cash.** Application fees across all
four tracks: €0. The only real out-of-pocket item is patent drafting
(~€1.5–5k net after the EUIPO voucher), and it is deferrable. The scarce
resource is **founder time**, and the plan must not cannibalize selling time —
€7k MRR is the actual runway extender until any grant pays out (earliest
realistic grant cash: **late 2026 Taler milestone 1; everything else 2027**).
