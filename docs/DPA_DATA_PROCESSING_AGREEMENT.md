# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

**DWS IQ 6 Platform — Customer Implementation**

Version 1.1 — April 2, 2026

---

This Data Processing Agreement (hereinafter "DPA") forms an integral part of the contract relating to the provision of DWS IQ 6 Services concluded between DWS IQ Oü and the Customer (hereinafter "the Contract").

The purpose of this Data Processing Agreement is to define the conditions under which DWS IQ Oü undertakes to carry out, on behalf of the Customer and for the sole purpose of the strict execution of the Contract, Personal Data Processing operations. The Parties undertake henceforth to comply with the Data Protection Regulations.

This DPA shall apply to the Services covered by the Contract for which the Customer acts as Data Controller or Data Processor and DWS IQ Oü acts as Data Processor or Sub-processor, within the meaning of the GDPR. The Data Processing carried out by DWS IQ Oü as Controller is managed within the framework of its Privacy Policy.

The Customer has assured itself, based on the information provided by DWS IQ Oü and on other information at its disposal, that DWS IQ Oü offers sufficient guarantees, particularly in terms of experience, resources, capacities and reliability, to implement the necessary technical and organisational measures for the Processing of Personal Data specified in the Contract to be carried out in accordance with the Data Protection Regulations.

## ARTICLE 2. DESCRIPTION OF THE DATA PROCESSING

**2.1** The Data Processing carried out by DWS IQ Oü for the purposes of this Data Processing Agreement relates only to the types of Personal Data and the categories of Data Subjects defined by the Customer and under its sole responsibility.

**2.2** The Customer is also responsible for the choice of Services used and in particular for their compatibility with the data processing it implements. When DWS IQ Oü offers Services adapted to particular types of processing, the Customer must use the Services concerned, in particular when these processing operations are subject to specific regulations or standards (including but not limited to industrial emissions data, environmental compliance data, or ESG reporting data).

**2.3** The Customer undertakes to provide DWS IQ Oü with the data referred to in this Data Processing Agreement for the purposes of the execution of the Contract and, if the Customer is Data Controller, to document in writing any instructions concerning the Data Processing operated by DWS IQ Oü. Furthermore, the Customer remains solely responsible for selecting Services that are adapted to its needs and to implement sufficient technical and organizational measures to protect its Personal Data, particularly in the event of Data Processing likely to result in a high risk for the rights and freedoms of the Data Subjects.

**2.4** DWS IQ Oü undertakes to (i) guarantee the confidentiality of Personal Data and (ii) to ensure that all Employees and Sub-processors authorized to process Personal Data under this Data Processing Agreement respect the confidentiality of Personal Data. The obligation of confidentiality of Personal Data will remain in force for five (5) years following the expiration of the Contract.

**2.5 Description of Data Processing**

- **Subject**: Customer climate compliance, industrial emissions, and operational data;
- **Duration**: The duration of data retention depends on the Customer's choice except in the event of a legitimate request in particular from a judicial or administrative authority, in which case the duration of data retention will depend on the legal limitation period;
- **Nature and purpose of the processing**: The Service provided may include:
  - Climate compliance analysis and reporting (Fit for 55, ETS, CBAM, CSRD)
  - Industrial emissions data processing and monitoring
  - AI-powered document analysis and automated compliance checks
  - ESG data aggregation and reporting
  - ERP integration data synchronization
  - Multi-channel communication and notification services (Firehorse Omnichannel)
  - Content management and document generation
  - Automated workflow orchestration
- **Categories of data**: Any categories of data uploaded in the context of the use of a Service subscribed through the Customer's DWS IQ 6 account, which may include:
  - Employee contact information (names, email addresses, phone numbers)
  - Industrial facility operational data
  - Environmental compliance and emissions data
  - ERP system identifiers and transaction references
  - User authentication and access log data
  - Communication metadata (email addresses, notification preferences)
- **Categories of Data Subjects**: Customers, end users, employees of the Customer, employees of the Customer's business partners, Employees or Sub-processors of DWS IQ Oü, depending on the situation.

**2.6 AI-Specific Processing**

Where AI Processing is performed as part of the Services:

- **2.6.1** DWS IQ Oü shall maintain transparency regarding the AI models used for processing, including their purpose and general operational logic;
- **2.6.2** Personal Data processed through AI models shall not be used for model training without the explicit prior written consent of the Customer;
- **2.6.3** DWS IQ Oü shall maintain automatic logging of all AI system events in accordance with Article 12 of the EU AI Act;
- **2.6.4** DWS IQ Oü shall provide documentation of the reasoning lineage for AI-assisted decisions that impact the Customer's compliance obligations;
- **2.6.5** The Customer retains full ownership of all outputs generated by AI Processing using the Customer's data.

> **9.1.2** in collaboration with the Customer, shall implement without undue delay any measure that may be necessary in order to minimize risks of any kind weighing on Personal Data, to mitigate the possible harmful effects and to contribute, within the scope of the Services, to the identification of the cause thereof.

**9.3** DWS IQ Oü undertakes to keep a register listing Personal Data Violations relating to the Personal Data subject to this Data Processing Agreement, the associated circumstances, their consequences, the remedial measures implemented as well as any breach committed under this Data Processing Agreement.

## ANNEX B: TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES (TOM)

### B.1 Access Control

- Role-based access control (RBAC) with principle of least privilege
- JWT-based authentication with token expiration
- Multi-factor authentication available for administrative access
- Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) policies enforced at database level

### B.2 Encryption

- Data at rest: AES-256 encryption (Supabase/PostgreSQL)
- Data in transit: TLS 1.3 for all API communications
- Database connections encrypted via SSL/TLS
- Secrets managed via environment variables, never stored in source code

### B.3 Network Security

- Helmet.js security headers on all Express endpoints
- Cloudflare WAF and DDoS protection
- VPC network isolation for database services
- API rate limiting and input validation (express-validator)

### B.4 Data Minimization and Pseudonymization

- Personal Data is pseudonymized before AI model API calls where technically feasible
- Data collection limited to what is strictly necessary for the Services
- Automated data retention policies with configurable expiry

### B.5 Monitoring and Logging

- Comprehensive event logging for all platform operations
- AI system decision logging per EU AI Act Article 12 requirements
- Security incident monitoring and alerting
- Audit trail for all data access and modifications

### B.6 Business Continuity

- Automated database backups with point-in-time recovery
- Multi-region deployment capability (Finland/Germany)
- Disaster recovery procedures with documented RTOs and RPOs

### B.7 Staff Measures

- Confidentiality agreements for all employees and contractors
- Regular data protection training
- Background checks for personnel with access to Personal Data
- Access revocation upon termination of employment

### B.8 Physical Security

- Data centers operated by certified providers (Supabase, Google Cloud)
- ISO 27001 certified infrastructure providers
- Physical access controls managed by Sub-processors per their security certifications
