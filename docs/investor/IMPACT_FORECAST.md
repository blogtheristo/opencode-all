## 1. Headline

> Re-framing investor conversations from "AI platform" to **DWS 365 Skilled Agents priced like labor** is projected to lift the SAFE valuation cap **+30–60%** in thesis-fit funds, lift cold-INTRO meeting conversion **+30–50%**, compress time-to-term-sheet roughly **2× faster**, and let us close the round **Q4 2026 vs the Q1 2027 baseline**. Downside scenarios and assumptions are explicit below.

---

## 2. Five Quantified Predictions (With Assumptions)

### Prediction 1 — Valuation Cap Lift

**Forecast:** SAFE cap moves from the **€3.8M current target** toward **€4.9M – €6.1M** in thesis-fit conversations (deep-tech EU, industrial AI, defense-adjacent funds). Mid-case: **€5.5M cap (+~45%)**.

**Reasoning:** The right comp set shifts from "EU AI SaaS" (10–15× forward ARR) to "vertical AI labor companies" (recent Cognition / Sierra / Harvey rounds priced 20–30× ARR on similar revenue stages). Even partial multiple expansion lifts the cap meaningfully against the same forward-revenue plan.

**Quantitative model.** Take €1.07M Q4/26 booked ACV target as the revenue anchor visible to investors at close. SaaS-comp cap at 4× forward ACV (a typical post-seed European deep-tech cap on a not-yet-recurring ACV) ≈ €4.3M; labor-AI-comp cap at 6× forward ACV ≈ €6.4M. Discounting both for the 2026 Q2 entry timing and a Finnish-anchored team factor gives a current target of €3.8M (SaaS lens) → €4.9M–€6.1M (labor lens) mid-case **€5.5M**.

**Downside scenarios.**

- _Funds reject the labor comp framing._ Cap holds at €3.8M; SAFE still closes. Net zero impact.
- _Comp prints (Cognition / Sierra / Harvey) compress in H2 2026._ Cap retraces toward €4.5M; still +18% vs current.
- _Liquidation-preference / pro-rata / MFN terms tighten._ Cap nominally rises but economic terms cancel the lift. **Mitigation: require clean SAFE terms in every conversation; flag any rights/preferences negotiations to Risto immediately.**

**EUR impact at current round size.** At €650k SAFE: cap rising from €3.8M to €5.5M shifts dilution from 17.1% to **11.8%** — _Risto retains 5.3 percentage points of equity._ On a €1M oversubscription, dilution at €5.5M is 18.2% vs 26.3% at €3.8M — Risto retains 8.1 percentage points.

#### 2.1.1 The 4P Capital Classes as the cap-lift mechanism

The multiple shift (SaaS ~4× → labor-AI ~6×) is not asserted — it is _earned_ by four durable capital classes the HIL phase produced. Each does specific work in the cap math (canon: `DWS_BRAND_STANDARD.md` §23):

| Capital Class (4P)     | Cap-math role                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Prompt Capital**     | The codified intelligence (30+ skills, 50-agent foundry, evals) that makes "labor company, not SaaS" credible — earns the 6× comp.                                                                                                                  |
| **People Capital**     | Calibrated 7-month HIL judgment lowers the **execution / key-person discount**, lifting the realized cap within the range.                                                                                                                          |
| **Plant Capital**      | EU-resident, Article-12-logged, deployable two-room stack makes the forward ACV **durable** enough to underwrite the multiple.                                                                                                                      |
| **Perception Capital** | Governed world model + closed sense→act loop = data-network effect + physical-AI comp. **This is the re-rate lever** — it defends the **€5.5M mid-case** and is what justifies naming **€6.1M** as the upper bound rather than the SaaS-lens €4.3M. |

Read against §2's quantitative model: Prompt + People move the comp set onto the labor curve (the €4.3M → €6.4M gross bracket); Plant holds it there; **Perception is why the mid-case sits at €5.5M and not at the SaaS floor.** In a downside where funds reject the labor framing, Prompt + Plant still defend €3.8M on SaaS terms — Perception is upside, not load-bearing for the floor.

**Diligence framing (the 4P as an M&A ontology).** Funds increasingly score agentic targets on exactly these four axes. DWS's acute internal risk — the one to name before a fund does — is **People + Perception walking out together**: the calibrated judgment and the world model it built are tacit, paired, and off the balance sheet (the Kaukopää case, `docs/PHD_FIREHORSE_EN.md` §14.27). Mitigation is the Prompt-Capital ratchet: every codified skill and lineage-logged observation converts tacit People/Perception capital into transferable Prompt/Plant capital, shrinking the key-person discount over time.

### Prediction 2 — Cold INTRO Conversion Uplift

**Forecast:** Cold-INTRO subject line + opening paragraph leading with the labor anchor lifts response and meeting-booking conversion by **+30–50%** vs prior "AI platform" framing.

**Reasoning:** Subject-line A/B priors from B2B SaaS show that concrete labor / cost framings outperform abstract "AI platform" framings by 30–80% on open / response rates. Investor inboxes are saturated with "AI platform" pitches; "EU regulation is hiring — DWS 365 Skilled Agents supplies the workforce" is differentiated language. The master template enforces the differentiation across every INTRO.

**Model.** Prior baseline: ~6% INTRO → meeting conversion across the 33-INTRO set. New baseline at +40% mid-case: ~8.4% conversion. Across 33 INTROs in the active set, that lifts booked first-meetings from ~2 to ~3 incrementally per sprint cadence.

**Downside scenarios.**

- _Investor fatigue on the "AI labor" framing if it becomes saturated by Q4 2026._ Mitigation: lean harder into the **regulation-as-demand-engine** sub-frame, which is uniquely European and not crowded.
- _Conversion uplift is real but the booked meetings convert to "no" faster._ Net effect on term sheets is muted. Mitigation: pair every cold INTRO with the Q&A bank rehearsal so live conversations defend the frame.

---

### Prediction 3 — Time-to-Term-Sheet Compression

**Forecast:** Target compression from a typical EU deep-tech post-seed timeline of **16–24 weeks** to **8–14 weeks** for thesis-fit funds.

**Reasoning:** The compression comes from three sources: (a) the labor-comp framing replaces 2–3 weeks of "what category are you in?" diligence; (b) the data-room delta (PRICING_MODEL, TAM_REFRAMED, DISTRIBUTION_MOAT, REGULATORY_TAILWIND, PROOF_POINTS) is pre-built and answers the first 80% of due-diligence questions on day one; (c) the Q&A bank pre-empts the most common LP-protective questions partners must ask.

**Model.** 33-INTRO set × 8.4% meeting conversion = ~2.8 first-meetings per sprint. Of those, historical second-meeting conversion ~50% → ~1.4 second-meetings. Term-sheet conversion ~25% → ~0.35 term sheets per sprint at compressed cycle, vs ~0.15 at prior cycle. **Roughly 2× term-sheet velocity.**

**Downside scenarios.**

- _Funds require operational diligence (customer references, security audit) that takes its own calendar time independent of framing._ Compression is bounded to the conversational stages, not the operational stages. Mitigation: pre-stage two customer references and a recent security audit before the round opens.
- _The Finnish-anchored team factor stretches geographic-bias diligence._ Mitigation: lean into the Lifetime Oy EU-jurisdiction story as a feature, not an apology — it is the sovereignty moat.

### Prediction 4 — Messe Stand Pickups

**Forecast:** **12–20 qualified investor conversations** over the Messe Germany 3-day window (Stuttgart, Sep 17–19, 2026), using the refreshed one-pager + DE talk track.

**Reasoning:** Messe construction trade shows draw deep-pocketed industrial buyers; the DACH PE and family-office crowd that scouts those shows is dense and accessible at a stand. Prior Messe / industry-trade-show pickup baselines for a clean labor-AI pitch with a one-page leave-behind are in the 10–25 conversation range per booth over a 3-day window.

**Model.** 3 days × ~8 hours / day = 24 booth-hours. At ~1 qualified investor conversation per 1.5 hours (realistic with Risto leading and a screener present), that's **~16 qualified conversations**. Bracket: 12 (downside) – 20 (upside).

**Downside scenarios.**

- _DACH investor traffic skews to industrial / strategic buyers, not financial investors._ Mitigation: this is a feature, not a bug — strategic / industrial conversations seed Tier-1 customer pipeline that funds use as proof points.
- _Stand quality / location compromises foot-traffic._ Mitigation: secure a known partner co-stand or industry-association adjacency for the Messe slot.

---

### Prediction 5 — SAFE Close Timing

**Forecast:** SAFE round closes **Q4 2026** (mid-case Nov 2026) vs the **Q1 2027** baseline.

**Reasoning:** Stacks of:

- INTRO conversion uplift (Pred 2)
- Cycle compression (Pred 3)
- Messe Germany live-conversation funnel (Pred 4)
- The post-launch Arctic 15 V1 announcement (June 15) and Messe Germany (Sep 17–19) function as natural investor close-forcing events; calendaring the close to immediately follow Messe is the operational play.

**Model.** Baseline assumption: 1 term-sheet → 1 close (typical). Pred 3 gives ~0.35 term sheets per 6-week sprint × 4 sprints = 1.4 term sheets through Q3 2026. With 4 sprints between June and late September, the round closes by Nov 2026.

**Downside scenarios.**

- _Q3 2026 macro shock (rate hikes, EU geopolitical event) delays close calendars across funds._ Mitigation: maintain optionality — bridge structure ready, multiple parallel conversations.
- _Customer pilot data delays publishable Q3 outcome numbers._ Mitigation: pre-stage the Messe Germany customer-attended slot to act as the proof event in lieu of public outcome data.

## 4. Net Effect Statement (For the Board / Operating Plan)

The labor-pricing thesis refresh is forecast to:

1. Hand Risto roughly **5 percentage points of retained equity** at €650k SAFE through cap expansion.
2. **Roughly double** term-sheet velocity through the Q3–Q4 2026 fundraising window.
3. Close the SAFE round **a full quarter earlier**, freeing Q1 2027 for Series A prep on a labor-comp valuation curve.
4. Convert Messe Germany 2026 from a brand event into a **funding-funnel event** with measurable pickup count.

Risks are bounded, downside scenarios preserve the existing plan rather than damaging it. The refresh is asymmetric upside.
