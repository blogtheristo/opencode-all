## 0. Brand Architecture (use this consistently)

- **Buyer / investor-facing offer (the thing they buy):** **DWS 365 Skilled Agents** (Microsoft-anchored / industrial customers) and **Firehorse 365 Skilled Agents** (Python data / research-anchored customers).
- **Platform underneath (the thing it runs on):** **DWS IQ** (current product line: DWS IQ 6 release train).
- Investor pitches lead with the offer-layer name. The DWS IQ platform is mentioned once as "powered by DWS IQ" — do not lead with the platform name; investors buy labor, not platforms.

> **Labor-anchored pricing · regulation drives demand · MACC + AppExchange drive distribution.**

Every opening paragraph, subject line, follow-up, and Messe pitch resolves to that line. If a draft loses the spine, rewrite the draft.

## 3. The Opening Three Paragraphs — Required Spine

The first three paragraphs of every refreshed INTRO must follow this shape. Investor-specific context (firm thesis, prior meetings, portfolio overlap, language preference) gets layered into Paragraph 3 and the body below — never above the spine.

### Paragraph 1 — Category reframe (tool → labor), @dhaber attribution

> Most founders, buyers, and investors still look at AI through a SaaS lens —
> copilots, seats, "X% faster." But when software stops helping humans do the
> work and starts **doing the work itself**, that is not a feature upgrade; it
> is a business-model shift, and the price ceiling stops being other software
> and starts being the loaded cost of a billable hour. _(Frame credit:
> conversation with @dhaber on what happens when software stops helping humans
> do the work and starts doing the work itself.)_
>
> DWS 365 Skilled Agents (on DWS IQ) is on the labor side of that line.

### Paragraph 2 — DWS-specific evidence

> 16 Skilled Agents are already producing the signed artifact — the EN 15978
> LCA report, the CSRD / CBAM gap analysis, the site-safety assessment, the
> CSRD double-materiality study, the EU AI Act conformity pack, the Themis-
> coordinated multi-agent case. Pricing is anchored to the **human cost of
> producing that work**, not to inference cost: per-outcome (≈10–20% of
> consultancy day-rate baseline) or per-agent-month (≈15–30% of fully loaded
> FTE cost). The bundle replaces 6–8 EU FTE for €199,990/yr.
>
> Gross margin sits at ~85% at list. As foundation models commoditize, the
> marginal cost falls and **margin expands — because the anchor is labor, not
> compute.**

### Paragraph 3 — Investor-specific wedge

Pick ONE wedge per fund, based on their declared thesis. Wedge wording is provided in §4. Always close the paragraph with the same line:

> The spine in one line: **labor-anchored pricing, regulation drives demand,
> MACC + AppExchange drive distribution.** That is the line we lead with at
> the Messe round and in PE meetings this quarter.

## 5. Closing Ask — Required Shape

Last paragraph of every INTRO email must contain, in this order:

1. **The ask.** A 20-minute PE meeting _or_ a Messe stand visit (Q3/26).
2. **Two calendar options.** Concrete dates within the next 14 days; example:
   - _"Two slots that work: Thu 22 May 09:00 CET (30 min) or Tue 27 May 14:00 CET (30 min). Messe Germany visit (Stuttgart, Sep 17–19, 2026) is also open if you'd rather see the stand."_
3. **A clean unsubscribe.** Plain text, single sentence:
   - _"If this isn't a fit, reply STOP and you won't hear from me again."_
