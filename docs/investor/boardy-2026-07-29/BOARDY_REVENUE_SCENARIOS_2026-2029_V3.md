## 2026 landing cadence stress test [M]

- Base €600k ending ARR needs ~12 new logos @ €46k net-new across Aug-Dec (V1 launched 15 Jun; Messe 17-19 Sep).
- Required closings: ~2-3 logos/mo over 5 mo; summer slowdown loads this into Oct-Dec (post-Messe pickups convert in Q4).
- **Downside:** if the Aug-Sep ramp slips, 2026 lands toward Conservative €400k (~8 logos). Plan Q4 closing capacity accordingly.
- **Mitigant:** at cash break-even, a slower ramp is not a survival risk — it moves the SAFE close and Series-A timing, not the runway.
