## 1. How to Read This Document

Five regulations, one snapshot, 24-month enforcement horizon. Each entry covers (a) what the regulation mandates, (b) when each enforcement step lands, (c) what artifact a Skilled Agent produces against that step, and (d) what the Tier-1 EU industrial buyer must have on the desk by that date.

The investor takeaway: **regulation is the demand engine.** Every enforcement step adds buyers who must produce a signed artifact they cannot produce with a copilot.

## 2. CSRD — Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive

### What it mandates

Mandatory sustainability reporting under European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) — double materiality, climate, social, governance — for large undertakings and listed SMEs across the EU. Reports are audited by a statutory assurance provider; signed by a natural person in the undertaking.

### Enforcement calendar (24-mo horizon)

| Step                                                          | Date / period   | DWS-addressable artifact                                  |
|---------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Wave 1 reporting (NFRD scope) — FY2024 reports filed          | Calendar 2025 (continuing) | CSRD double-materiality + ESRS pack (Compliance + Material Oracle + Emissions Data) |
| Wave 2 — large undertakings (FY2025 reports filed)            | 2026 H1 → H2    | Same artifact, larger scope; co-issue with Big-Four assurance       |
| Wave 3 — listed SMEs, simplified ESRS                         | 2027 (FY2026)   | SME-shaped CSRD pack — Material Oracle + Compliance attach          |
| ESRS sector-specific standards (delegated acts)               | Rolling 2026–28 | Sector overlays on the same artifact                                |
| EFRAG / Commission revisions, simplification track            | 2026 H2 ongoing | Live monitoring — `/regulatory-radar` refresh quarterly             |

### Demand size

50,000+ EU undertakings in scope by 2028. Average reporting effort: 2–4 ESG / compliance FTE per undertaking. **The labor pool the regulation creates demand for is the labor pool we sell into.**

## 3. CBAM — Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism

### What it mandates

Importers into the EU of cement, iron and steel, aluminium, fertiliser, electricity, hydrogen must report embedded emissions; from 1 Jan 2026 they buy CBAM certificates to cover the gap between EU ETS-priced carbon and the carbon priced in the country of origin.

### Enforcement calendar (24-mo horizon)

| Step                                                                                | Date            | DWS-addressable artifact                                            |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Transitional reporting (quarterly, no financial liability)                          | Until 31 Dec 2025 | CBAM quarterly report (Compliance + Emissions Data + Material Oracle) |
| Definitive period — CBAM certificate purchase obligation begins                     | 1 Jan 2026      | CBAM declaration + certificate-purchase plan (Compliance Enterprise) |
| First definitive CBAM declarations due                                              | 31 May 2027     | Annual CBAM declaration pack, audit-trailed                          |
| Sectoral scope review (potentially additional product categories)                   | 2026 H2 review  | New sector overlays                                                  |
| CBAM + EU ETS price convergence (free-allocation phase-out)                         | 2026 → 2034     | Material Oracle EN 15978 LCA tightens the boundary on supplier emissions |

### Demand size

Tens of thousands of EU importers in scope. EU steel + cement + aluminium + fertiliser industries are dependent on this declaration to defend their EU market access. The artifact is high-stakes and high-frequency. **CBAM closes a multi-billion EUR import flow on quarterly reporting cadence — that is recurring labor demand by definition.**

## 4. EU AI Act

### What it mandates

Risk-based regime for AI systems placed on the EU market: prohibited, high-risk, limited-risk, minimal-risk. High-risk systems (Annex III — includes safety components, employment, essential services, critical infrastructure) trigger conformity assessment, technical documentation, post-market monitoring, registration, human oversight.

### Enforcement calendar (24-mo horizon)

| Step                                                                              | Date              | DWS-addressable artifact                                       |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| General-purpose AI obligations live                                               | 2 Aug 2025        | Provider documentation, training-data summary (Compliance)     |
| **High-risk obligations enter into application**                                  | **2 Aug 2026**    | **Annex IV technical documentation, conformity assessment pack, post-market monitoring plan (Compliance Enterprise)** |
| AI Office sandbox, notified-body designations, harmonised standards               | Rolling 2025–26   | Live monitoring; Compliance Enterprise co-signed with notified body target Q4 2026 |
| Annex III sectoral review and updates                                             | 2026–27           | New sector overlays                                            |
| Full applicability of remaining provisions                                        | 2 Aug 2027        | Steady-state conformity-assessment cadence                     |

### Demand size

High-risk systems are pervasive in our Tier-1 customer base — construction safety (Annex III), employment screening, critical infrastructure (energy, water), credit scoring. Conformity assessment is a **signed regulatory deliverable** — exactly what a Skilled Agent + Themis + human-on-loop produces.

## 5. NIS2 — Network and Information Security Directive 2

### What it mandates

Cybersecurity obligations on essential and important entities across 18 sectors (energy, transport, banking, health, water, digital infrastructure, public administration, manufacturing, food, chemicals, postal, waste, and more). Incident reporting within 24 / 72 hours, supply-chain risk management, governance accountability, technical and organizational measures.

### Enforcement calendar (24-mo horizon)

| Step                                                          | Date              | DWS-addressable artifact                                         |
|---------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Member State transposition deadline                           | 17 Oct 2024       | Transposition status by Member State (continuing) — Sovereignty Monitor reports |
| Operational readiness (essential / important entity registration) | Continuing through 2025 | Sovereignty Monitor + Compliance pack — registration, governance attestation |
| **NIS2 fully operational across EU**                          | **Q4 2025 (achieved)** | **Live incident-reporting and audit-pack workload — recurring demand** |
| Sectoral implementing acts and Commission guidance            | Rolling 2025–27   | Live monitoring                                                  |
| First Member State enforcement actions                        | 2026 H2 onward    | Incident report and remediation pack (Sovereignty Monitor + Themis)  |

### Demand size

EU-wide essential / important entities are in the tens of thousands. Each one needs a Sovereignty / DPO / CISO function with **continuous monitoring** and **incident-reporting cadence** — perfectly shaped for the Sovereignty Monitor Skilled Agent.

## 6. Fit for 55

### What it mandates

The EU's 55%-by-2030 emissions-reduction package — EU ETS Phase IV expansion, ETS2 (buildings, road transport), Effort Sharing Regulation, LULUCF, CBAM (covered separately above), Renewable Energy Directive III, Energy Efficiency Directive, CO₂ standards for vehicles, ReFuelEU Aviation, FuelEU Maritime, social climate fund.

### Enforcement calendar (24-mo horizon)

| Step                                                                       | Date              | DWS-addressable artifact                                   |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| ETS Phase IV — free-allocation phase-down (overlap with CBAM)              | 2026 → 2034       | Material Oracle EN 15978 LCA + Emissions Data reporting   |
| **ETS2 — fuels for buildings & road transport enters operation**           | **2027 onward**   | New compliance scope for building owners and fleet operators (Compliance + Emissions Data) |
| Renewable Energy Directive III transposition                               | 2025–26 transposition | RED III compliance pack (Compliance + Emissions Data)  |
| Energy Efficiency Directive recast                                         | 2026 transposition    | Energy audit and reporting pack                        |
| CO₂ vehicle standards / ReFuelEU / FuelEU Maritime                         | Rolling 2025–30   | Sectoral compliance overlays                              |

### Demand size

Fit for 55 expands the scope of the EU emissions-pricing labor pool by roughly an order of magnitude over the 2026–2030 horizon. Every undertaking with a building, fleet, or energy footprint becomes an Emissions Data and Material Oracle buyer.

| Month       | Regulation step                                            | Net effect on DWS demand                          |
|-------------|------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|

| Jan 2027    | CBAM definitive period — financial liability live           | CBAM declaration recurring demand                  |
| May 2027    | First CBAM annual declarations due                          | Spike in CBAM-pack demand                         |
| Aug 2027    | EU AI Act remaining provisions fully applicable             | Steady-state conformity work                      |

The pattern: **enforcement stacks every ~6 months for two years.** No buyer can hire fast enough; the labor pool is the constraint. DWS 365 Skilled Agents are the workforce.
