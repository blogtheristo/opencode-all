## Purpose

One earned press hit in a credible Nordic tech publication. The story is the EUR 333k edge AI cost advantage — a real headline, not a paid placement.

## The Pitch Email

**Subject:** Finnish startup runs industrial AI for EUR 50/month — saves EUR 333k/year vs. cloud

---

Hi [journalist name],

I'm writing because I think you'd find this cost story interesting.

A Finnish company called Lifetime Oy runs a climate compliance AI platform organized around **16 captain hats covering 25+ EU industries**. Their infrastructure cost is EUR 15-50 per month. That's the full production platform, not a demo.

The economics: they deploy NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano devices at the edge — construction sites, factories, maritime vessels — instead of routing inference through cloud APIs. The result:

- **EUR 27,750/month** in cloud cost avoidance
- **EUR 333,000/year** savings versus cloud-only competitors
- **Under 100ms** inference latency (25x faster than cloud)
- **7-15 watts** per device (runs on solar at remote sites)
- **Hardware ROI payback:** under 2 months

The founder, Risto Paarni (M.Sc. Industrial Management, 35 years in industrial IT), argues that cloud-dependent AI platforms fundamentally don't work for heavy industry — construction sites don't have reliable internet, factories have air-gapped networks, ships operate mid-ocean.

The company is already generating EUR 7-14k MRR from its Fleet division and has signed EUR 59,900 in Q1 2026 pilot contracts. They're raising EUR 150k via SAFE at a EUR 3.8M valuation.

Happy to connect you with Risto for a 20-minute call. He's technical — he'll talk architecture, not marketing.

Best,
[your name / Risto directly]

## Key Facts for the Journalist (fact sheet)

### The Cost Comparison

| | Cloud-only competitor | Lifetime (edge AI) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly inference cost | EUR 27,750+ | EUR 0 (on-device) |
| Monthly infrastructure | EUR 2,000-5,000 | EUR 15-50 |
| Inference latency | 2-5 seconds | Under 100ms |
| Works offline | No | Yes |
| Annual cost | EUR 350,000+ | EUR 17,000 (incl. hardware amortization) |

### The Hardware

- **Device:** NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano
- **Cost per unit:** ~EUR 500
- **Power:** 7-15 watts
- **Deployment sites:** 50 (planned)
- **Solar-capable:** Yes — critical for remote construction and maritime

### The Platform

- **Name:** DWS IQ 6
- **What it does:** Automates EU Fit for 55 climate compliance (ETS, CBAM, CSRD, EPBD, +7 more regulations)
- **Industries:** 16 EU-regulated verticals
- **Data residency:** 100% EU (Finland, Germany, Netherlands)
- **AI stack:** Claude (Anthropic) + Google Gemini + Groq, orchestrated via MCP

### The Company

- **Name:** Lifetime Oy (Finland)
- **Founded by:** Risto Paarni
- **Revenue:** EUR 7-14k MRR (Fleet division) + EUR 59,900 Q1 pilots
- **Raising:** EUR 150k SAFE at EUR 3.8M valuation
- **Cloud credits secured:** USD 135,000+ (Google, AWS, Groq)
- **Accelerator:** Peachscore Cohort 25
- **InvestEU:** Project INPO-20254383

### Why This Is a Story Now

1. **CBAM** reached full implementation in 2026 — every EU importer of steel, cement, aluminium, etc. now needs carbon accounting at the border
2. **CSRD** reporting scope expanded — thousands more companies must disclose
3. **ETS2** (road transport + buildings) launches 2027 — the next compliance wave
4. **EPBD** embodied carbon mandates hit construction in 2028 — Lifetime's beachhead market
5. **EU AI Act** Article 12 (automatic logging) deadline approaching — Lifetime claims compliance readiness

The regulatory pressure is creating a market. The question is who builds the infrastructure. This company argues cloud-only platforms can't serve heavy industry, and they have the cost numbers to prove it.

## What NOT to Say to Press

- No "most innovative CEO" framing — that's pay-to-play language
- No "operating system for climate compliance" — too grandiose
- No unverifiable claims about the LLM (55B parameters is a design target, not a deployed model journalists can test)
- No competitor bashing by name
- No revenue projections beyond what's signed

**Stick to facts. Let the journalist write the narrative.**

---

## Follow-Up Strategy

1. **Send pitch email** to top 2-3 publications simultaneously
2. **Wait 5 business days** before follow-up
3. **Follow-up once** — if no response, move to next publication
4. **If a journalist bites:** offer a 20-min call + access to see the Jetson hardware live (video call demo)
5. **After publication:** share on LinkedIn with a "thank you to [journalist]" post — builds relationship for future coverage
