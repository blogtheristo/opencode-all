## When to use this

You're at Bauma, SMM, Hannover Messe, Slush, an EU industry-association AGM, or in a warm-intro DM with a sold founder / ex-CRO / family-office principal / association chairman. They've signaled buyer intent. You have 30–45 minutes.

This playbook gets the conversation from cold introduction to signed term sheet in under a week.

## The buyer

Captain seats are not for VCs. Four archetypes write €50k:

1. **Sold founders** — exited their industrial company in the last 3–7 years.
2. **Retired-but-restless C-suite** — ex-CRO/COO of a Tier-1 in the industry, wants legacy not salary.
3. **Family-office principals** whose money came from the industry — they back what they understand.
4. **Industry-association chairmen / ex-chairmen** — they bring the Rolodex you're selling access to.

Fleet Admiral chairs (€250k) target the same archetypes, but typically people with portfolio thinking across 4–6 adjacent industries.

## The funnel — five (sometimes six) questions, fixed order

A real Captain conversation runs through these in this order. If the questions arrive in a different order, you're either talking to someone unusually domain-expert (good — let them lead) or to a script (verify identity before answering Q3+).

### Q1 — The product: *"How does it work?"*

> Think of DWS IQ as a team of specialist EU-sovereign AI agents you can hire by the task, not by the seat.
>
> 1. **You tell it the outcome** in plain language (FI, SV, EN, DE, FR).
> 2. **The right agent picks up the job** — Themis routes to the specialist (SiteSense, Material Oracle, Compliance, etc.).
> 3. **You get a scored, audit-ready answer in minutes** — structured result, the rule it was measured against, what's missing, what to do next. Every run is logged.
>
> **Why it's different:** Data stays in the EU. No CLOUD Act exposure. Built on the regulations *before* they hit.
>
> **What it costs:** Pennies per query instead of a five-figure consultant invoice.

### Q2 — The business: *"How much do you make per month?"*

> Today: low five figures monthly, deal-by-deal. Trajectory: **€900k ARR by end of 2027**.
>
> Pricing isn't speculative — €4,999 per deployment, €6,500 per bundle. Roughly 12–15 customers a month at steady state.
>
> Every customer saves more in a quarter than they pay us in a year. That's the only reason the curve works.

**Pivot move:** flip "what *I* make" to "what *you* make" — avoided CSRD findings, tenders won, FTE freed. €4,999 pays back the first time it stops one finding.

### Q3 — The ticket: *"How much money to get in?"*

> Two ways in. Pick the altitude you want to fly at.
>
> - **Captain — €50k.** One industry, one seat. Yours. Quarterly briefing, industry vote, customer-intro rights, path upward.
> - **Fleet Admiral — €250k.** Sits above the Captains. Owns a cluster of 6 Captain seats — shapes the portfolio, shares in the upside of every Captain seat anchored. Five Captains report into one Admiral.
>
> The real question isn't *"can I invest"* — it's:
> **€50k and own [Industry],** or **€250k and own the cluster [Industry] sits inside?**

### Q4 — The return: *"What do I get back?"*

> Three streams. They stack.
>
> 1. **SAFE equity upside** — €50k Captain converts at next priced round, capped. €250k Admiral, same mechanics, larger position, anchor-investor rights. At €900k ARR / 2027 valuation: ~10–20× on paper (illustrative).
> 2. **Industry carry** — Captain earns **5%** of every deal closed in your industry, life of the customer. Fleet Admiral earns **2% on top**, across all 5 Captain industries in the cluster.
> 3. **Strategic seat** — customer-intro rights, industry vote on roadmap, co-branded launch, path to Fleet Admiral → CEO Council.
>
> Cash back (carry, monthly), paper back (equity, at exit), influence back (vote, roadmap, brand). **The €50k is the smallest of the three.**

### Q5 — The path: *"How do I start this week?"*

> Three steps:
>
> 1. **Tonight** — one-pager + SAFE terms by email.
> 2. **Within 48h** — 30-min call with the CEO. Camera on, ID confirmed. No deck. Just questions.
> 3. **By Friday** — sign + wire to Lifetime Oy (Finland). Seat is yours. First quarterly briefing in three weeks.
>
> You're not late. You're early.

### Q6 (optional) — The seat: *"Why is scarcity real?"*

> 30 industries. 30 Captains. **One per seat.** When Steel is taken, no second Steel Captain exists. The scarcity is structural, not marketing. Construction is filling. Maritime is in conversation.

## The Boardy — one-screen written summary to send after the call

Send this within 4 hours of the call ending. Plain text, no attachments yet (attachments come post-KYC).

```
SAFE
€50k or €250k. Converts at the next priced round; cap and discount set in
final docs. Illustrative until signed.

Carry
• Captain (€50k): 5% on every qualifying deal in your industry, life of customer.

Strategic rights
Customer-intro rights, an industry vote on roadmap, co-branded launch — and a
path from Captain → Fleet Admiral → CEO Council.

Final terms in the legal docs. This is the shape.
```

## The KYC gate (added after the live "Madonna" incident)

A prospect impersonating a public figure ran the funnel cleanly enough to make us articulate it on a public page. The pitch is real; the verification step is non-negotiable.

**Before any SAFE doc, bank detail, or wire instruction leaves your hands:**

1. **Camera on**, full name, ID on camera. 30 minutes minimum.
2. **LinkedIn URL** matches the name + the industry.
3. **Independent corroboration** — a mutual contact, a company they actually run, a sold-asset record. Take 10 minutes to check.
4. If anything dodges (no camera, no LinkedIn, "send the docs first, I'll wire tomorrow"), **stop**. Treat as the celebrity-impersonation romance/investment-scam pattern.

The line to use: *"Standard step on our side — camera on, ID confirmed before SAFE docs land. Protects both of us."*

## Red flags from the live incident — keep these in mind

- Account name = global celebrity, but follower ratio is *seeker* (high following / low followers).
- Five questions in textbook order, no rambling, no personal small talk.
- Pivots from "let me get to know you" to "where do I wire" inside 30 minutes.
- Cannot or will not do a 20-minute video call.
- Romance-adjacent posts on profile (Valentine's, "old but gold", lifestyle).

Real prospects ramble, joke, ask about your kids, get the question order wrong. Scripts don't.

## Test for whether this playbook is working

After every Captain or Fleet Admiral conversation, ask:

- Did the prospect's questions track Q1 → Q5 in order? (If not, why not — and which Q answer should be sharpened?)
- Did the prospect KYC clean? (If not, what flag was missed?)
- Did we send the Boardy within 4 hours? (If not, what blocked it?)
- Did the prospect open the SAFE docs within 48h? (If not, the Q4 return story didn't land — re-tune it.)

Update this file when an answer above is "no" twice in a row. The playbook stays alive by absorbing every failed close.
