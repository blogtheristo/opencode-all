## Decision Paths — the Buyer's Numbers and Who Signs (2026-09-29)

The pipeline is a set of decision paths, not a funnel (HIC 29.09.2026). Accounts do not "leak" through stages; each one sits at one decision, owned by one person, and that decision has a fixed set of exits. The states and exits are the Workflow v2 sales graph (`src/agents/workflow-v2/examples/sales-outreach.graph.json`, node ids = `OutreachStatus` in `src/outreach/types.ts`), so this table and a graph run use the same vocabulary. Every path ends at `CONVERTED`, `PASSED` or `PARKED`; `PASSED` and `PARKED` are decisions, not losses.

**How to read a row.** _Node_: where the account is now, **derived from the last dated event** (sent letter, reply, HIC approval pending), never typed as a stage. _Decision_: the one question the next person must answer yes or no. _Decider_: who answers it; for `NEGOTIATION → CONVERTED` that is the person who signs. _Evidence the decider needs_: the problem in the buyer's own numbers — **sourced** figures are cited in this file or in the offer file; **missing** figures are not estimated, they are the questions for the next call. _Exits_: the allowed next nodes. No decider below is confirmed by the buyer; each is a hypothesis to verify in the first call.

**Public-sector rule:** under Hankintalaki (1397/2016) §25 the national threshold for services is €60,000 (ex VAT). Below it an agency buys directly under its own rules; above it the purchase needs a tender or an existing framework (e.g. Hansel). The next public-sector contract should therefore be a pilot under €60,000 or enter through a framework holder.
