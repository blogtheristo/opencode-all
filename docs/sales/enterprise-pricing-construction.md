## Pricing Tiers

### Enterprise Navigator (Pilot) — EUR 24,900

**Scope:** 1 country, 3 reference projects, 4 weeks

**Deliverables:**
- Full data audit of existing ERP + BIM data sources
- ERP connector setup (Visma or IFS, read-only)
- EN 15978 calculation on 3 reference projects (historical data)
- ESRS E1 disclosure draft
- EPBD-ready building carbon assessment
- EU Taxonomy alignment calculation (CapEx/OpEx)
- Team training session (2 hours)
- Data gap analysis report

**Cost basis:** ~140 hours senior engineer + platform setup + emission factor validation. Comparable consulting engagement: EUR 60-80K from Big 4.

---

### Enterprise Premium (Annual) — EUR 49,900/year

**Scope:** All 3 jurisdictions (NO/DK/FI), unlimited projects

**Includes everything in Navigator, plus:**
- Multi-jurisdiction ERP connectors (all instances)
- Real-time dashboard access for sustainability team
- Quarterly compliant reports (ESRS E1, EPBD, EU Taxonomy)
- Automated alerts at carbon budget thresholds
- Regulatory update service (emission factor refresh, new directive implementation)
- Dedicated support channel (email, 24h response SLA)

**Cost basis:** Replaces 1-2 FTE sustainability analyst positions (EUR 70-120K/year each) + One Click LCA licenses across 3 countries (~EUR 15-30K/year). Net saving for customer: EUR 100-200K/year.

---

### Enterprise Nordic (Full Deployment) — EUR 89,900/year

**Scope:** Custom enterprise deployment

**Includes everything in Premium, plus:**
- Custom SLA (4h response, 99.9% uptime)
- Private cloud option (EU-hosted, single-tenant)
- Dedicated integration engineer (named resource)
- BIM/IFC direct integration (Tekla Structures, Revit)
- Custom emission factor validation for proprietary materials
- Board-level reporting package (quarterly ESG summary)
- Priority access to new regulatory modules

**Cost basis:** Comparable enterprise sustainability platform contracts: EUR 200-400K/year (Sphera, Normative, Watershed at enterprise tier). We're 55-75% cheaper with deeper Nordic construction specialization.
