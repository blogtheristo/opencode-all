0. Why Industrial Intelligence Over 25 Industries Benefits Customers

DWS IQ 6 is not a single-industry compliance tool. It is cross-industry intelligence — the Lifetime World Model (LWM) connects 25 EU-regulated industries into a single persistent knowledge graph. This is the core competitive moat and the primary reason enterprises choose DWS over point solutions.

The compound intelligence effect: When one industry's regulation changes, every connected industry sees the ripple within hours — not months. A steel tariff adjustment propagates to construction costs, maritime shipping rates, and chemical feedstock prices simultaneously. No single-industry tool can do this.

1. LWM Base + Enterprise Own Training

Every customer receives the Lifetime World Model base layer — 55 billion parameters trained on 15,000+ EU regulation documents across 20 industry ontologies. This is the shared foundation. But the real power is what happens next:

  • Train Model SaaS (€1,490 / €2,990 / €7,490): Customers train their own industry-specific model layer on top of LWM, using their own facts, their own supply chain data, their own process knowledge.
  • Enterprise ontology ownership: The customer's trained model belongs to them. Their trade secrets, process IP, and supplier relationships never leave their control.
  • Cross-industry signal propagation: When Outokumpu trains their steel ontology, it enriches — but never exposes — the base layer that NCC's construction ontology draws from. Intelligence compounds; secrets stay sovereign.
  • 5,000-enterprise ontology network (LWM V1, Q4 2026): Each new enterprise that joins the network makes the base layer smarter for everyone, while keeping proprietary data isolated.
Customer value: "You don't start from zero. You start from 15,000 regulations and 20 industry ontologies. Then you add your own facts, and the model becomes yours — but it stands on the shoulders of every industry we serve."

2. Sovereignty on Everything

In a world where US CLOUD Act compels American providers to hand over data regardless of where it is stored, sovereignty is not a feature — it is a requirement.

  • Data sovereignty: All customer data stays in UpCloud Helsinki. No US cloud provider touches production data. Zero CLOUD Act exposure.
  • Model sovereignty: The customer's trained model layer is theirs. Not ours. Not a cloud provider's. Not subject to foreign government subpoena.
  • Agent sovereignty: KYA (Know Your Agent) gives cryptographic identity to every AI action. The customer controls what agents can do, can kill any agent in <125ms, and owns the complete audit trail.
  • Infrastructure sovereignty: Aegis Standard runs on Finnish sovereign infrastructure. Aegis Defence runs air-gapped, on-premises, with NVIDIA DGX Spark — completely disconnected from any external network.
  • Regulatory sovereignty: EU AI Act Article 12 audit trails are hash-chained, immutable, and retained for 7 years. The customer's compliance record is legally defensible in EU courts.
One sentence for the boardroom: "Your data, your models, your agents, your infrastructure, your audit trail — all sovereign, all in EU jurisdiction, all under your control."

3. Defence in Tough Times Ahead

Europe faces converging threats: geopolitical instability, supply chain fragility, energy transition pressure, and regulatory acceleration. Industrial intelligence is not a luxury — it is a defence mechanism.

  • Supply chain resilience: When conflict disrupts one supply route, 25-industry intelligence shows alternative paths within hours. A steel company knows immediately which port is still operational, which chemical supplier has capacity, which construction project needs rescheduling.
  • Regulatory preparedness: Six major EU regulations hit between 2026–2028 (CBAM, EU AI Act, ETS2, CSRD Wave 2, EPBD, CRA). Companies without automated compliance will face fines, market exclusion, or both. DWS IQ turns regulatory pressure into competitive advantage.
  • Dual-use readiness (Aegis Defence): SAR orbital target detection (ICEYE), export control screening (Reg. 2021/821), EU regulatory 24h incident reconstruction (NIS2, EU AI Act, GDPR, CER, CRA), sovereign climate compliance in dual-use industry, critical supply chain monitoring — ready now, not when the threat arrives.
  • Energy transition intelligence: As carbon pricing rises (ETS €60–100/tonne by 2028), cross-industry intelligence predicts cost cascades before they hit the balance sheet.
  • Critical infrastructure protection: NIS2 and CER directives require organisations in critical sectors to demonstrate resilience. DWS Aegis provides the architecture; DWS agents provide the continuous compliance evidence.
The cost of not having industrial intelligence: A single CBAM filing error can trigger €10K–50K in penalties. A NIS2 incident without 24-hour reconstruction capability can trigger board-level liability. The question is not whether you can afford DWS IQ — it is whether you can afford not to have it.
