## FAQs

### Q: Is this a loan or equity investment?

**A**: This is an **equity-style investment** via SAFE. It's **not a loan** - there is no repayment obligation. Your return comes from valuation growth when the SAFE converts to equity.

### Q: Can I lose my entire investment?

**A**: **Yes**. This is a high-risk, early-stage investment. If the company fails, your €15k investment can go to zero. Only invest what you can afford to lose.

### Q: When will I see returns?

**A**: This is an **illiquid investment**. You'll see "paper" returns when the SAFE converts (typically at Series A), but actual cash returns only come at an exit event (acquisition or IPO), typically 5-7+ years from now.

### Q: What happens if the company is acquired before Series A?

**A**: The SAFE converts at the valuation cap (€3.8M) and you receive your pro-rata share of the acquisition proceeds.

### Q: What if the Series A valuation is lower than €3.8M?

**A**: The SAFE converts at the actual Series A valuation (not the cap), which would reduce your returns. However, we're targeting a €15M+ Series A valuation.

### Q: How much dilution should I expect?

**A**: Typical dilution path:

- Series A: 20-25%
- Series B: 20-25%
- Series C+: 15-20% per round
- By exit, expect 50-70% total dilution from Series A onwards

### Q: What are my rights as a SAFE holder?

**A**: SAFE holders typically have:

- Conversion rights (at qualified financing)
- Information rights (monthly updates)
- Pro-rata rights (optional, in some SAFEs)
- MFN rights (if better terms offered later)

### Q: Can I sell my SAFE before it converts?

**A**: Generally **no** - SAFEs are illiquid until they convert. Secondary sales are rare and require company approval.

### Q: What's the expected timeline to exit?

**A**: Typical timeline:

- 2025: Build to €900k ARR
- 2026: Series A
- 2027-2028: Series B, scale operations
- 2029-2031: Exit event (acquisition or IPO)
- **Total timeline**: 5-7 years from investment
