## Detailed Changes by Article

### Article 1: Definitions (NEW - Expanded)

**Original (2019):**
```
1. For the purposes of this Agreement confidential information includes, 
without limitation, computer programs, code, algorithms, names and expertise 
of employees and consultants, know-how, formulas, processes, ideas, inventions, 
schematics, product development plans, forecasts, strategies, existence of a 
work or client relationship and other technical, business, financial information...
```

**Updated (2026):**
- **6 comprehensive categories:** Technical, Business, Commercial, Personnel, IP, Client Information
- **Trade Secret definition** per EU Directive 2016/943 Article 2:
  - Secret (not generally known)
  - Commercial value because secret
  - Subject to reasonable protective steps
- **Authorized Representatives** definition with binding requirements
- **Purpose** clearly defined

**Why Changed:** EU Trade Secrets Directive requires specific definition criteria for legal protection.

---

### Article 2: Obligations (ENHANCED)

**Original (2019):**
```
2. The Recipient agrees:
a. to keep and hold any and all Lifetime's and/or Lifetime's clients' 
   Confidential Information in whatever form in strict confidence,
b. not disclose or divulge any Confidential Information to any third party,
c. not to make any use whatsoever of Confidential Information except for 
   the purpose of the discussions...
d. not to copy or reverse engineer, reverse compile or attempt to derive 
   the composition or underlying information...
e. to comply with security rules of Lifetime or the business customer.
```

**Updated (2026):**
- **2.1 Confidentiality Obligation:** 5 specific duties
- **2.2 Prohibited Actions:** 5 explicit prohibitions including EU Directive Article 4 unlawful acquisition
- **2.3 Security Measures:** Technical and organizational requirements
- **2.4 Binding of Personnel:** Written confidentiality obligations for all recipients

**Why Changed:** EU Directive requires "reasonable steps" to be documented; modern security practices require explicit measures.

---

### Article 3: Exceptions (ENHANCED)

**Original (2019):**
```
3. The confidentiality obligations shall not extend to information:
- which the Recipient can clearly show was already in his/her possession...
- which is public or becomes public because no fault of the Recipient.
- which is rightfully received by the Recipient from third parties...
```

**Updated (2026):**
- **4 standard exceptions** with "clear and convincing evidence" standard
- **NEW: Legally Compelled Disclosure procedure:**
  - Prompt written notice to Lifetime
  - Cooperation in seeking protective order
  - Minimum necessary disclosure
  - Seek confidential treatment

**Why Changed:** Legal compelled disclosure is common; procedure protects both parties.

---

### Article 4: Intellectual Property (ENHANCED)

**Original (2019):**
```
6. The Confidential Information remains as the sole property of Lifetime. 
Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any rights or licenses...
7. No warranties of any kind are given by Lifetime...
```

**Updated (2026):**
- **4.1 Ownership:** Explicit sole and exclusive property statement
- **4.2 No License:** No implied license by implication, estoppel, or otherwise
- **4.3 No Warranty:** "AS IS" provision with explicit disclaimer

**Why Changed:** Clearer IP protection language; prevents implied license claims.

---

### Article 5: Return/Destruction (ENHANCED)

**Original (2019):**
```
4. At any time, upon Lifetime's request, the Recipient has to immediately 
return all the Confidential Information and copies...
```

**Updated (2026):**
- **Trigger events:** Request, termination, or completion of discussions
- **Options:** Return OR destroy with written certification
- **Retention exceptions:**
  - One archival copy for compliance verification
  - Legally required copies (remain confidential)

**Why Changed:** Practical reality that some retention is necessary; certification provides evidence.

---

### Article 6: Term and Duration (NEW - Critical)

**Original (2019):**
```
8. The confidentiality obligations shall survive the termination of this 
Agreement or any subsequent business relationship entered into by the Parties.
```

**Updated (2026):**
- **Agreement Term:** 5 years from Effective Date
- **Survival:**
  - General Confidential Information: 5 years from disclosure
  - Trade Secrets: **Indefinitely** (as long as qualifies under law)
- **Termination:** 30 days written notice

**Why Changed:** EU courts require reasonable duration; indefinite for trade secrets aligns with Directive.

---

### Article 7: Remedies (NEW - Critical)

**Original (2019):**
*No specific remedies clause*

**Updated (2026):**
- **7.1 Acknowledgment of Harm:** Irreparable harm, inadequate monetary damages
- **7.2 Injunctive Relief:** Without proving damages or posting bond
- **7.3 Damages:** Direct, indirect, consequential, attorneys' fees
- **7.4 Contractual Penalty:** EUR [AMOUNT] per breach (customizable)

**Why Changed:** EU Directive requires effective remedies; contractual penalty common in EU practice.

---

### Article 8: Data Protection (NEW - GDPR)

**Original (2019):**
*Not addressed*

**Updated (2026):**
- Processing only per Lifetime's instructions
- Appropriate security measures
- No third-country transfers without consent
- Data subject rights assistance
- Breach notification without undue delay
- Personnel confidentiality obligations

**Why Changed:** GDPR mandatory since 2018; personal data often included in business discussions.

---

### Article 9: General Provisions (ENHANCED)

**Original (2019):**
```
10. This Agreement shall be executive after both parties have signed it.
```

**Updated (2026):**
- **9.1 Entire Agreement**
- **9.2 Amendments:** Written and signed
- **9.3 Severability**
- **9.4 Waiver:** Written requirement
- **9.5 Assignment:** Consent required
- **9.6 No Partnership**
- **9.7 Notices:** Multiple delivery methods including email
- **9.8 Counterparts:** Electronic signatures valid

**Why Changed:** Standard boilerplate for enforceability; electronic signature validity important.

---

### Article 10: Governing Law & Disputes (UPDATED)

**Original (2019):**
```
9. This Agreement is constructed and governed under the laws of Finland. 
Any dispute... shall be finally settled by arbitration in accordance with 
the Rules of the arbitration Institute of the Helsinki Chamber of Commerce 
by one (1) arbitrator... The Proceedings shall be conducted in the Finnish 
language in the city of Helsinki, Finland.
```

**Updated (2026):**
- **10.1 Governing Law:** Finnish law + explicit EU Directive 2016/943 reference
- **10.2 Amicable Resolution:** Good faith negotiations first
- **10.3 Arbitration:**
  - Finland Chamber of Commerce (updated name)
  - One arbitrator
  - Helsinki seat
  - **English language** (or Finnish by agreement)
  - Confidential proceedings
- **10.4 Interim Relief:** Court access for urgent injunctions

**Why Changed:** Institution name updated; English default for international partners; interim relief important.

## Usage Recommendations

### For Certified Partners Pre-Phase:

1. **Contractual Penalty:** Set at EUR 100,000 - 250,000
2. **Duration:** Keep 5-year term
3. **Schedules:** Complete both A and B before signing
4. **Legal Review:** Recommended for each new partner

### For Standard Subcontractors:

1. **Contractual Penalty:** EUR 50,000 - 100,000
2. **Simplified Schedules:** May omit if not needed
3. **Language:** Confirm English acceptable

### For Strategic Partners:

1. **Contractual Penalty:** EUR 250,000 - 500,000
2. **Additional Provisions:** Consider mutual NDA format
3. **Extended Term:** May negotiate longer duration
