## 1. Program Overview

The **Lifetime Certified Partners Program** enables qualified partners to resell, implement, and deliver training on Lifetime's core products:

- **Firehorse ESG Report** - Sustainability reporting & carbon accounting
- **DWS IQ 6** - Industrial AI platform for regulated collaboration
- **Lifetime Agent Foundry** - AI agent development services

### Partner Types

| Type | Focus | Revenue Model |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **Reseller Partner** | Product sales (JM-oikeus) | Commission on sales |
| **Training Partner** | Workshops & certification | Revenue share on training |
| **Implementation Partner** | Customer deployments | Project-based fees |
| **Strategic Partner** | Co-development & market expansion | Equity + revenue share |

### 2.1 Product Overview

**Firehorse ESG Report** is a SaaS platform for:
- EU CSRD/ESRS compliance reporting
- Carbon footprint calculation (Scope 1, 2, 3)
- Fit for 55 readiness assessment

## 4. Partner Certification Requirements

### 4.1 Firehorse Certified Trainer

**Requirements:**
- [ ] Complete Firehorse Platform Training (8 hours)
- [ ] Pass Certification Exam (80% minimum)
- [ ] Deliver 2 supervised workshops
- [ ] Maintain annual recertification

**Benefits:**
- Listed in Partner Directory
- Access to training materials
- Co-marketing support
- Priority support channel

### 4.2 Firehorse Certified Consultant

**Requirements:**
- [ ] Firehorse Certified Trainer status
- [ ] Complete Implementation Training (16 hours)
- [ ] Deliver 3 customer implementations
- [ ] Customer satisfaction score >4.0/5.0

**Benefits:**
- Implementation project referrals
- Advanced technical support
- Beta access to new features
- Quarterly partner briefings
