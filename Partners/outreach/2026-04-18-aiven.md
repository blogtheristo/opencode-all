### 5.2 Partnership Read & Negotiation Stance (Risto + analysis, 2026-07-06)

**Risto's read of the call:** Leevi was specifically hunting **upsell directly to Lifetime's customers** and **downplayed the dws10 platform's significance**. Aiven's reseller agreements are **volume-based**.

**Analysis — three signals, one coherent picture:** the pre-NDA customer-data ask + platform downplaying + volume-based reseller terms all point the same way: the account manager's model is _Lifetime as a channel into Lifetime's customers_ (his consumption quota), the inverse of our §5.1 model. Not malicious — structural.

**Consequences for §5.1:** stream 4 (reseller margin) is effectively dead near-term — volume tiers are unreachable at our consumption. Streams 1–2 (own product + consulting) remain the money, and are precisely the parts the AM has no incentive to promote.

**Stance:**

1. **Negotiate referral / deal registration, not volume reseller.** Our value to Aiven is compliance-driven consumption they can't originate; price it per registered workload. Consulting-partner paper (no volume threshold, services billed to customer) beats reseller paper for us.
2. **Ecosystem/ISV validation is not the AM's call** — route the dws10/platform track to Aiven partnerships org (partnerships@aiven.io), separate from Leevi's account track; the signed NDA is the bridge.
3. **Structural customer protection:** any co-sell touching Lifetime customers goes through us with deal-reg protection; NDA → DPA sequencing stays mandatory regardless of urgency from their side.
