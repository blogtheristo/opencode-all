## 7. Expansion and cross-sell orientation
Aegis may be the entry platform for a broader solution. Each addition must address a named problem, owner, budget and measurable acceptance output.

Potential additions include Firehorse for bounded reporting, document and evidence workflows; Climate Intelligence for climate, emissions and sustainability evidence; DWS Control Room for a defined single-node local workload; DWS Situation Room for multi-node or multi-workflow deployment; DWS Data Room for controlled data and evidence handling; and Lifetime.fi services for architecture, data preparation, integration, installation, training, deployment and support.

For each addition, complete: product/service, customer problem, additional data or systems, deployment or hardware requirement, buyer and budget owner, acceptance output, price, dependencies and exclusions.

A broader package may combine Aegis, Firehorse or another DWS IQ product, implementation, integration, hardware and support. Approximately €150,000 may be used as a package-planning target only after scope and deliverables are defined. It is not a standard price, guaranteed outcome or automatic bundle.

## 8. Customer-specific orientation

### Kemira: governed process evidence and decision support for water treatment, chemical use or process optimisation
Start with one plant, one workflow and one traceable output. Do not promise chemical savings before establishing a baseline.

### Nokia Defence: controlled AI operation in a defence or dual-use manufacturing environment
With defined data boundaries, human approval, local-first execution and evidence of operational decisions. Confirm export-control, security and procurement requirements.

### NCC: controlled evidence workflows across construction, project delivery or critical infrastructure
Using agreed BIM, ERP, document or site data. Define the buyer-owned process and acceptance output first.

### Outokumpu: installation-level emissions evidence for a defined material and site workflow
Such as 304L from Tornio, producing structured, citable evidence for procurement, customer reporting or CBAM-related workflows.
