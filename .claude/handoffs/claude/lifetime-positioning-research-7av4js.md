## What Was Decided

Lifetime Oy is repositioning from **trust-as-the-product** to **outcomes-made-dependable-by-control** in response to the OpenAI/Hugging Face agent incident (July 2026).

**Message hierarchy (canonical, locked in field note):**

1. Outcome — compliance problem solved (CBAM, CSRD, ETS, NIS2)
2. Proof — inspectable reasoning, full audit trails
3. Mechanism — controlled workflows (automation under customer authority)
4. Foundation — governance as verification (Article 55 disclosure, external audit, sovereignty)

**Key principle:** "Governance earns belief in the outcome rather than competing with it as the headline."
