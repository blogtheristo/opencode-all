## Executive Summary

The EU's **Fit for 55** package mandates a 55% reduction in net GHG emissions by 2030. This creates an unprecedented compliance burden across 25 industries — requiring real-time carbon tracking, automated reporting, supply chain transparency, and continuous regulatory alignment.

**DWS IQ 6 automates this entire compliance stack.** From embodied carbon calculations (EN 15978) to ETS reporting, CBAM border adjustments, and CSRD sustainability disclosures — one platform, one source of truth.

## Industry-Specific Capabilities

### 🏗️ Construction & Buildings (Priority Focus)

**Why Construction is Ground Zero for Fit for 55:**
- 36-40% of EU lifecycle emissions
- EPBD mandates embodied carbon reporting for buildings >1,000 m² by 2028
- ETS2 adds carbon costs to heating fuels from 2027
- Supply chain pressure from CBAM-affected materials (cement, steel, aluminium)

**DWS IQ 6 Delivers:**
- ✅ **EN 15978 Lifecycle Assessment** — Automated A1-A5, B, C stage calculations
- ✅ **EN 15804 EPD Integration** — Material carbon data from European databases
- ✅ **BIM Integration** — Direct carbon data from Revit, IFC models
- ✅ **Supply Chain Tracking** — Subcontractor emissions, CBAM-affected materials
- ✅ **CSRD Ready** — Sustainability disclosures aligned with ESRS E1

**Target Customers:** NCC, Skanska, Vinci, Strabag, Bouygues, Eiffage, BAM
