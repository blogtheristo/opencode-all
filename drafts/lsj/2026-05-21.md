## Agentic Lens

The week-21 signal is not just a procurement story. It is a *standards* story.

The EU AI Act Article 14 requires human oversight to remain effective throughout deployment. But if a deployer's AI budget collapses mid-year and oversight is cut to fit the residual envelope, the system is no longer running in the configuration on which conformity was assessed. The cost-surface of an AI deployment is now a conformity-stability variable that Article 14 does not currently capture.

We are extending the KYA Standard candidate with a third conformance track:

- **ASB-track** — Operational safety (OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications, governs what agents *do* with tools and credentials)
- **EAB-track** — Decision quality (EAC1–EAC13, governs how agents *choose* under competitive pressure)
- **CSB-track** *(new)* — Cost-Surface Stability under deployment (extends Article 14 from a *quality* variable to an *affordability-stability* variable)

CSB is productionisable on owned-compute inference. It is not productionisable on metered-API inference. The frontier-lab pricing capitulation is therefore not just an enterprise budget event — it is the moment when a continuing-affordability assumption silently embedded in the EU AI Act high-risk regime becomes auditable failure surface.
