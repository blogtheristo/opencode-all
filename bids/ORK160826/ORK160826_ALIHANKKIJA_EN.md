## Security & Compliance Requirements

**Important:** This is a Finnish government authority contract. Security requirements are strict:

- Personnel: EU citizens preferred; background checks may be required
- **Data processing must remain within the EU** — no data transfer outside EU/EEA
- Work involving sensitive registry data must be conducted by Finland-based resources or via secure VPN with documented access controls
- An updated NDA/DPA (Data Processing Agreement) will be required before contract signing
- Compliance with Finnish information security standards (Liitteet 1.3.1–1.3.4)

**Recommended model:** Your team handles development and coding tasks; EU-based team members handle any direct data access or client-facing work.
