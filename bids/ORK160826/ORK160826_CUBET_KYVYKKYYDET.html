Purpose

This annex explains how Cubet's consultants work inside the DWS IQ capability platform on the ORK delivery. Your people are not replaced by agents and they do not work around our platform — they are augmented by it. Each Cubet AI Engineer and Software Developer operates with the relevant DWS agent capabilities, skills, workflows and learning (Gödel) loops at hand, which makes the work faster, more consistent, and audit-ready under EU AI Act Article 12 from the first commit.

Ethical and contractual principle: agents augment, they do not replace. The role stays with your consultant; the agent removes routine and accelerates delivery. Personnel decisions remain yours.

EU-sovereign working model (mandatory for this contract)

This is a Finnish government authority contract; security requirements are strict and non-negotiable:

  • Data stays in the EU/EEA. No transfer of registry data outside EU/EEA. Cubet handles development

and coding tasks; any direct access to sensitive registry data and client-facing work is performed by EU/Finland-based resources or via secure VPN with documented access controls.

  • KYA — Know Your Agent governance. Every agent session is cryptographically bound to a verified

person, isolated in a Firecracker MicroVM, with <125 ms kill authority and full traceability. This gives ORK an auditable record of every AI action your consultants' agents take.

  • Article 12 audit trail is produced automatically — hash-chained, immutable, 7-year retention.
  • An updated NDA/DPA will be in place before contract signing; compliance with Finnish information

security standards (Liitteet 1.3.1–1.3.4).
