# Risto Anton Paarni

**Founder & CEO of Lifetime Oy**

Risto Anton Paarni has held roles as an Industrial IT Engineer and Technology Consultant across Scandinavian manufacturing, construction, and logistics for over 35 years. Risto has founded Lifetime Oy, an enterprise climate intelligence company where he built DWS IQ 6 — an AI platform automating EU Fit for 55 compliance across **16 captain hats covering 25+ EU industries**. The platform has earned three successive listings on the European Commission's InvestEU Portal and serves customers including Nordic construction firms, Uber Finland, and Kela (Finnish national healthcare).
