# Risto Anton Paarni

**Founder & CEO of Lifetime Oy**

Risto Anton Paarni has held roles as an Industrial IT Engineer and Technology Consultant across Scandinavian manufacturing, construction, and logistics for over 35 years. Risto has founded Lifetime Oy, an enterprise climate intelligence company where he built DWS IQ 6 — an AI platform automating EU Fit for 55 compliance across **16 captain hats covering 25 EU industries**. The platform has earned three successive listings on the European Commission's InvestEU Portal. Two enterprise pilots were contracted in Q1/2026 at €29,950 each, alongside four smaller landed accounts. Uber Finland, Bolt, Kela and Taksi Helsinki are **dispatch operators** in the role defined by Traficom — commercial counterparties of Lifetime's licensed transport operation, never customers of DWS IQ or any Lifetime software product.
