## 📘 Author — *Tietosuojajohtaminen* (2026)

Co-author of ***Tietosuojajohtaminen*** ("Data Protection Management"), a practitioner
framework for leading privacy work as a managed, risk-based discipline rather than ad-hoc
firefighting.

- **Publisher**: Alma Insights
- **Year**: 2026 (1st edition)
- **ISBN**: 978-952-14-5353-3
- **Format**: Hardcover, 432 pages
- **Subject areas**: Juridiikka (Law) · Companies & organizations
- **Co-authors**: Miia Kataja, Titta Penttilä
- **Listing**: <https://kirjakauppa.almainsights.fi/sivu/tuote/tietosuojajohtaminen/5537565>

**Thesis**: Data protection requires *management*. A clear governance model, leadership
commitment, and continuous improvement create the basis for both effective risk
management and a higher level of data protection — embedding privacy into the
organization's everyday processes and culture. The book explicitly addresses current
challenges including continuously evolving regulation and the increasing use of AI,
offering practical tools to keep pace with change and manage privacy risk in a
business-positive way.
