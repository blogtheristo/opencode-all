## 🧭 Profile — "Trustworthy Cyber-Physical Systems"

Research spans mechatronics, cyber-physical systems (CPS), and systems engineering, with the load-bearing threads being **architecture, safety engineering, automation, and model-based engineering**. Stated interests:

- Architectures and design methodologies for **autonomous and connected CPS**
- **System and functional safety**, and trade-offs with other properties — extending to **trustworthiness of autonomous machines and systems of systems**
- Sustainability, circular economy, innovation eco-systems
- Distributed control systems; co-design of control and embedded computer systems
- Life-long learning and education in CPS

Key properties of interest and their trade-offs: **safety, robustness, performance, cost, evolvability, autonomy**. Research is typically run in cooperative projects with international research groups and industry. 150+ peer-reviewed publications. He explicitly invites contact re: internships, MSc thesis projects, research, evaluations, and problem discussions, and flags **upcoming postdoc positions on trustworthy CPS**.

## 🎯 DWS Relevance & Positioning

His work maps almost one-to-one onto the DWS trustworthy-edge / agentic thesis — but the **framing must be his**:

- **Lead with trustworthy edge computing and _safety evidence_, not regulatory compliance.** For him, regulation (EU AI Act, NIS2) is the _outcome_ of an engineering discipline, not the pitch. "We generate audit-grade safety evidence for agentic edge systems" reads as peer; "we help you comply" reads as vendor.
- **The concrete bridge:** map the DWS **Article-12 logging pipeline + KYA (Know-Your-Agent) trust scoring** onto his **safety-argumentation / assurance-case** framework — an evidence chain for _autonomous/agentic_ systems at the edge.
- **AD-EYE ↔ sovereign edge-AI verification:** what AD-EYE does for automated-driving _evaluation_, DWS wants for sovereign edge-AI _verification_.
- **EU-sovereignty congruence:** a Vinnova-funded, EU-based, Nordic consortium — no CLOUD Act tension.

**Do not** explain GDPR, the EU AI Act, or basic safety concepts to him — he wrote the courses.
