## 🎓 Admission model — different from KTH's, closer to the process the HIC already specified

University of Oulu Graduate School (**UniOGS**) works differently from KTH's advertised-post model:

- **Employment as a doctoral researcher and doctoral study rights are separate.** Study rights are applied for via Finland's Studyinfo portal; a funded position is a separate matter.
- **The process starts exactly as the HIC's 5-step model says:** find and contact a suitable senior scientist willing to act as Principal Supervisor, _then_ pursue admission — not apply to a pre-defined advertised slot as at KTH.
- **A research plan is required for admission** — i.e. a tutkimusaihio, already the established practice in this repo.
- **Application windows:** 1 Jan–30 Apr, and 1 Jul–31 Oct each year. **The 1.7–31.10.2026 window is open now** (05.08.2026) — live, but only relevant once step 2 (a willing supervisor) exists.
- **Admission requirement:** a completed relevant M.Sc. or equivalent — **the same eligibility question flagged for the KTH NSS application applies here too** (`docs/gtm/KTH_NSS_PAPADIMITRATOS_APPLICATION_LETTER_2026-08.md` — Metropolia thesis status).
