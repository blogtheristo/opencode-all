## 🧭 Research profile — Networked Systems Security (NSS) Group

**Group goal (verbatim framing):** making current and upcoming networked systems **trustworthy**, shielding them and their users from attacks and abuse.

- **Scope:** a broad security/privacy agenda with emphasis on **wireless and mobile systems** — cryptography, security & privacy in VANETs, smart cities, IoT and 5G, security & privacy in distributed systems and cloud computing, certificate revocation and pseudonymous-authentication systems.
- **Method:** strong systems character (implements and evaluates real solutions) **combined with** theoretical methods, including **formal protocol analysis** and information-theoretic results.
- **Editorial roles:** Associate Editor, IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing and ACM/IEEE Transactions on Networking.
