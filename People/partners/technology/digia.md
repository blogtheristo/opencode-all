## 🔗 What Digia Hub Is

Digia Hub is Digia's **asiantuntijaverkosto** — an IT expert network / freelancer &
partner-company marketplace ("*Kokoava voima*"). It connects clients with experts and
partner companies for project assignments, and handles the administrative layer
(contracts, invoicing, payment verification) so the expert can focus on delivery.

- ~5 000 freelancers + ~450 partner companies in Finland; EU-wide network.
- Nearshore reach across the EU (Baltics, Poland) via the Allies network.
