Loops

Loop Engineering landed in PR #1447 (commit d34876a), 8 June 2026. A Maker-Checker cycle: one Skilled Agent proposes; a second validates; the loop continues until the outcome is correct or the budget stops it. State is disk-persisted — loops survive restarts. Two gates guard every cycle: a budget gate (cost ceiling) and a safety gate (KYA trust score).

KYA Governance

Know Your Agent — runtime safety for every Skilled Agent.

KYA (Know Your Agent) is Lifetime Oy's runtime agent governance layer. Every Skilled Agent is subject to KYA controls before, during, and after execution. No agent acts without a verified trust score; no loop runs past its budget gate.

KYA Control Layer

Trust score validation. Each agent carries a KYA trust score. Low-trust agents operate in a restricted sandbox. Score degradation triggers automatic escalation to a human operator before the next cycle.

Budget constraints. Every Loop has a cost ceiling enforced by the budget gate. When the ceiling is approached, the loop suspends and emits a BLOCKED signal. A human approves continuation or terminates.

TC-4 Leash Snap. Chain termination protocol TC-4 can halt any agent chain immediately — propagating a STOP signal through every active Workflow. Designed for adversarial or runaway conditions. No bypasses in production.

EU AI Act Article 12 audit trail. The Auditor agent writes a hash-chained journal entry for every agent action. Board-readable. CFO-auditable. Admissible evidence for regulatory inspections.
