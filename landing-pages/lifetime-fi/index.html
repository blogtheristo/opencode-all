DWS IQ Adoption Framework

Microsoft’s AI Adoption Framework — accelerated.

Microsoft’s AI Adoption Framework — part of the Cloud Adoption Framework for Azure — is the blueprint EU enterprises use to move from ad-hoc AI experiments to secure, scaled, value-driven production. Engineers trust it and Microsoft is a primary supplier, so we meet you where you already are: Lifetime Consulting runs your DWS IQ deployment along the same four lifecycle phases. The difference is where you land — DWS IQ lets you skip the risky experimentation and arrive directly in a governed, sovereign production environment.

Phase 1

AI Strategy

Start with the business problem, not the technology. We translate regulatory and operational gaps — CBAM, CSRD, ETS, NIS2 — into concrete use cases, and pick generative or deterministic AI per task. Deterministic scoring wherever an audit demands it.

Phase 2

Plan & Ready

Microsoft calls it the AI Landing Zone. We stand up your sovereign one — EU-hosted in Finland or on-prem edge (NVIDIA Jetson, L40S) — organise data domains, and wire in governance and EU AI Act Article 12 logging before a single agent runs.

Phase 3

Adopt · Build

Integrate AI into the flow of work. Ready-to-use Skilled Agents through to custom agentic workflows inside your ERP (SAP, Visma, IFS, Monitor, Jeeves) — the sovereign counterpart to the M365 Copilot and Azure AI Foundry adoption paths.

Phase 4

Govern, Secure & Manage

Zero Trust: never trust, always verify. KYA agent governance, strict internal/external workload boundaries, prompt-injection and data-leakage controls, and Responsible-AI / EU AI Act compliance — grounded in the platform, not bolted on.
