Sovereignty · Field Note

The factory came home.

Europe just got 35 AI supercomputers. The edge still needs a box.

25 Jun 2026 · by Risto Anton Paarni

The note, in one line

NVIDIA says 35 new AI supercomputers are in development across 23 European countries — roughly 800 AI exaflops, anchored by EuroHPC AI Factories. The sovereign upstream just became real. The sovereign edge is still a box the customer owns.

This is the news we have been waiting for.

For two years we have said: train upstream, serve at the edge. The catch was that the European upstream was mostly a slide. Now it is concrete and pouring — Barcelona, Bavaria, Stuttgart, the Nordics, and more, on Grace Blackwell and Hopper silicon.

And because a good thing deserves a clear head, here is what it does and does not change.

What landed

NVIDIA's numbers, at the level it stated them:

  • 35 AI/HPC supercomputers in development across 23 European countries — the largest European buildout to date.
  • About 800 AI exaflops deployed or announced in the past year; NVIDIA puts itself behind the bulk of Europe's AI-factory buildout.
  • Anchored by EuroHPC AI Factories — Barcelona's MareNostrum 5 expansion (GB300 NVL72 + GB200 NVL4), plus BavariaAI, HLRS, NAISS and others.
  • Built on Blackwell and Hopper today — the generation before Vera Rubin — serving 3M+ researchers in climate, health, energy, and quantum.

What it changes for the edge

It fills in the half of our sentence that used to wave its hands. “Train on a sovereign POD” now has an address in the EU, under EU governance. When a foundation model is fine-tuned on MareNostrum 5 instead of a hyperscaler in another jurisdiction, the provenance question gets an easier answer.

Upstream · the EU factory

EuroHPC AI Factories train and refresh, at exaflop scale, on EU soil. Shared, research-grade, national. The right place for the heavy lifting.

Downstream · the sovereign edge

A box the customer owns, running the trained model on-site. Air-gapped, audited, single-tenant. The half a shared factory cannot be.

What a national supercomputer is not

A EuroHPC AI Factory is a shared, research-first facility. It is excellent at training. It is not a single-tenant, air-gapped, Article-12-per-customer inference box sitting inside a regulated operator's building.

CLOUD Act immunity, on-prem incident logs, NIS2 handoff, dual-use isolation — those still live at the edge, on hardware the customer holds. A bigger upstream does not move them. It makes the upstream trustworthy; the edge is where the workload lands.

Why we are cheering

Because it makes our pitch easier, not harder. We sit at the far end of this road. The closer the factory moves to home, the shorter and cleaner the provenance story for everyone who buys a box from us. We do not compete with EuroHPC. We finish the sentence it starts.

The short version

  • 35 AI supercomputers, 23 countries, ~800 AI exaflops. The EU upstream is real.
  • It fixes provenance: train on EU soil, under EU governance, then deploy down.
  • A shared national factory is not a single-tenant, air-gapped edge box. The regulated workload still lands at the edge.
  • We do not compete with EuroHPC. We finish the sentence it starts.
  • The factory came home. The edge still needs a box.
