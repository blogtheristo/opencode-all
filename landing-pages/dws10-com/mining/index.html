Business Case: Precision Extraction

Optimizing Haulage for Lower Emissions

Challenge: Mining operations are energy-intensive, with haul trucks consuming massive amounts of diesel. Inefficient routing and idling contribute significantly to emissions.

Solution: DWS IQ's MineOps Agents optimize the dispatch and routing of haul trucks in real-time. They calculate the most fuel-efficient paths and speeds, reducing idling time and synchronizing with crusher capacity.

Impact:

  • 10-15% reduction in diesel consumption
  • Lower maintenance costs due to reduced engine wear
  • Maximized ore recovery rates
