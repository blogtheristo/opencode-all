The Sovereign Infra tier

Tier 2 partners are EU-headquartered or EU-operated platforms whose data and governance paths never leave the European Economic Area. DWS IQ Sovereign Infra deployments run on Scaleway (Paris / Amsterdam), Aiven (Helsinki), UpCloud (Helsinki), and Mistral AI (Paris) — all EU-sovereign by design. Sovereign Infra partners extend that promise into building automation, energy, industrial control, and ESG data.

Pricing: €5,000 / year · up to 25 Enterprise accounts · full EU data-residency · CSRD / EPBD / Fit for 55 aligned integrations.

Target sectors: construction, energy, logistics, manufacturing, real estate, healthcare. See the full benefit matrix on the Partner Ecosystem page.

Building Automation · Germany

Siemens — SI Building Nordics

Siemens Smart Infrastructure Buildings, Nordic unit. Siemens is a European platform (Siemens AG, Munich); its digital-enterprise building stack — Building X, Desigo CC, Navigator, Enlighted — is sold to exactly the Nordic customers facing CSRD Wave 2 (FY2025), the EPBD 2024 recast, and EU Taxonomy real-estate reporting (activities 7.1–7.7).

Action, not dashboards. Siemens captures operational building data. DWS IQ turns it into signed, submission-ready CSRD / EPBD / Taxonomy output — one command, 90 seconds — with KYA-governed writeback into the same building systems: HVAC setpoints, maintenance work orders, renovation-passport triggers. The point is making buildings run more efficiently, not generating more reports.

  • Data layer: Building X cloud API, Desigo CC (BACnet/REST), Navigator exports, Enlighted IoT feeds
  • Regulatory output: CSRD ESRS E1, EPBD 2024 energy class + renovation passport, EU Taxonomy alignment
  • Target customers: Nordic real-estate owners, REITs, construction groups (YIT, SRV, NCC tier), municipal property companies
  • Deployment: edge (Jetson / DGX Spark) for air-gapped sites, or Sovereign Infra EU cloud for portfolios
Design Partner · pilot Q2–Q3 2026
