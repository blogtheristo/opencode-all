How Gold is produced

  1. Discovery sprint (week 1) — audit your existing brand, docs, tone, regulatory surface. Identify the ten standards that matter most.
  2. Standards authoring (weeks 2–4) — rewrite the DWS standards baseline as your Gold Standards. You sign them off; from that moment they are canon.
  3. Agent training (weeks 5–6) — Gold Skillful Agents are trained on your Gold Standards and validated via KYA test harness.
  4. Firehorse deployment (weeks 7–8) — Production engine is wired to your brand, ERP, and signing tools. First real assets ship.
  5. Handover + operating manual — your team takes the keys. Ongoing support via Senior Specialist retainer if you want it.
