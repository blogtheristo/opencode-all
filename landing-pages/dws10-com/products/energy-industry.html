Energy-specific compliance agents

Pre-built agents for every major EU energy regulation — no configuration required for standard frameworks.

ETS Agent

Tracks emission allowances, calculates verified emissions per installation, forecasts surrender positions, and generates ETS annual report.

CBAM Agent

Monitors carbon content of imported goods, calculates CBAM certificates needed, tracks embedded carbon per supplier and origin country.

EPBD Agent

Tracks energy performance certificates (EPC) across building portfolio, flags non-compliant assets, and generates renovation roadmaps.

SCADA Connector

Direct OPC-UA and Modbus integration with industrial SCADA — consumption, output, and efficiency data auto-ingested hourly.

Grid Carbon Agent

Real-time grid emission factor from ENTSO-E transparency platform. Used for accurate Scope 2 calculation and demand-shifting recommendations.

Renewable Certificate Agent

Tracks Guarantees of Origin (GoO), matches renewable production to consumption, supports PPA verification and REfit reporting.
