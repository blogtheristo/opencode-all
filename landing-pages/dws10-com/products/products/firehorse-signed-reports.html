Two layers of signing: Transaction + KYA

Every Firehorse Signed Report carries two independent signatures — one from the humans who approve the content, one from the AI agent that produced it.

Transaction signing (DocuSign)

Human signers apply a legally binding eSignature to the document. Identity verified at signing time. eIDAS-compliant (AES / QES tiers), court-defensible.

  • Author → reviewer → auditor in sequence
  • DocuSign envelope + audit certificate
  • Used for CSRD sign-off, investor packs, contracts

KYA signing (Know Your Agent)

The AI agent that produced the report cryptographically signs its own output — proving which agent ran, under which capability manifest, at what trust score. Required for EU AI Act Article 12 logging.

  • Agent identity + capability manifest hash
  • KYA trust score + Article 12 log reference
  • Machine-provenance for every generated number

Why both? DocuSign proves who signed it. KYA proves who produced it. Together you get human accountability and machine provenance on the same PDF — the standard EU regulators will expect under the AI Act from 2026.

  • Investor reporting benefits from verifiable author accountability and tamper-proof signing.
  • Internal governance — multi-party signing enforces the author/reviewer/auditor workflow without manual chase-ups.
