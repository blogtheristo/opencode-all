Why this product

AI literacy is now a legal requirement, not a nice-to-have. Artikel 4 der EU-KI-Verordnung obliges organisations to ensure their people can use AI systems competently. But literacy alone does not make a team productive — engineers need a discipline for working with an autonomous coding agent: how to scope it, govern it, review its output, and keep quality high. Claude Code Coaching delivers both: the literacy and the discipline, on your own codebase, without sending any IP to a US-hosted service.
