How an account climbs the ladder

The DWS Skilled Agents catalog is a five-tier upsell ladder. Every step is anchored to the human role replaced, not to a SaaS comparison.

Price AI like labor, not like a tool. Every move up the ladder replaces more of the role — and stays a fraction of the fully loaded cost of hiring it.
