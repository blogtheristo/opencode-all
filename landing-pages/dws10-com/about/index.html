About DWS IQ

Nordic industrial AI built in Finland. Mission-driven compliance automation for EU-regulated enterprises.

Mission & Vision

Mission: Eliminate regulatory friction for European industrial enterprises through autonomous AI agents that observe, think, and act on compliance requirements.

Vision: A Nordic-built AI platform where 50+ Skilled Agents orchestrate compliance workflows for 25 EU-regulated industries — delivering deterministic, auditable outcomes at scale.

Regulatory Reality

The Problem

50,000+ EU companies with 250+ employees must comply with CBAM, CSRD, ETS, EU AI Act, and NIS2 by 2028. Compliance consultants cost €200k+ per engagement. In-house teams can't keep pace with regulatory change.

Our Solution

Compliance Automation

DWS IQ is the factory that produces compliance outcomes. Industrial Specialist Agents know each industry's regulations, emission factors, reporting requirements, and audit standards. They deliver signed, auditable results — not recommendations.

The Platform

DWS IQ 6 is built on a four-layer architecture: Brain (multi-agent orchestration), Memory (EU regulatory model), Body (Firehorse distribution), and Hands (Lifetime Fleet edge execution). Three product lines serve the full spectrum: Aegis (dual-use/defence), Climate Intelligence (CSRD/CBAM), and Firehorse (ERP distribution).

Foundation

50 Production Skilled Agents

Each extends BaseAgent, emits ledger-native output contracts with audit trails, and runs under strict internal/external workload boundaries. Orchestrated by Atlas, Athena, and Apollo coordinators.

Autonomy

Loops & Workflows

Maker–Checker cycles with disk-persisted state, budget gates, and safety gates. Multi-agent workflows chain compliance actions end-to-end. Resumable, auditable, deterministic scoring.

Distribution

Firehorse V2

Omnichannel sales, report engine, content DMS. Puts Skilled Agents into every ERP store — IFS, Visma, SAP, Monitor, Jeeves — as embedded compliance workflows, not chatbots.

Sovereignty

Aegis Path

Dual-use platform for government agencies and military industry. 21 EU industries. CLOUD Act-free sovereignty. Orbital intelligence integration (ICEYE SAR). EU-only, software-only.
