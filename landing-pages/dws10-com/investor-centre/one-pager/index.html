DWS IQ: Sovereign AI for Regulated European Industry

Lifetime Oy is opening a focused EU/EEA investor structure around DWS IQ. One Admiral anchors the round. Eight Captains bring industrial capital, domain authority and access to customers.

Admiral

  • One EU/EEA-based Admiral who anchors the €650K SAFE
  • Institutional judgement; helps shape the company's next commercial phase
  • Anchor capital and strategic guidance on financing, governance and growth

Captains

  • Eight EU/EEA-based Captains bringing industrial capital and domain authority
  • Access to named buyers, plants and decision-makers (subject to verification)
  • Chemicals, defence, metals, construction, water, energy, critical infrastructure

The Value Proposition

  • Sovereign AI infrastructure: private cloud, EU-hosted; designed to support GDPR- and CSRD-related workflows, subject to deployment-specific assessment
  • Industrial AI expertise: understanding of industrial markets and compliance requirements
  • Product portfolio: 116-product industrial software portfolio, with 13 DWS IQ products forming the commercial core

Why This Structure

The Admiral-Captain structure is designed around one principle: the people financing industrial AI should understand how industrial AI gets bought, deployed and accepted.

  • Admiral: anchor capital and institutional confidence
  • Captains: industry-specific access, domain knowledge and commercial reach
  • Combined: intended to support more repeatable industrial deployment over time
