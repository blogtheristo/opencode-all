Why Siemens + DWS IQ

Siemens captures the operational data across the European built environment and industrial base. DWS IQ turns that data into action — signed, submission-ready EU compliance output and writeback into the same building and industrial systems so they run more efficiently. One command, 90 seconds. Frida-conversation framing: "We don't need dashboards, we need action from data." We agree. That's the product. Siemens delivers the building operational data; DWS IQ delivers the compliance-and-action layer on top.

Two partnership angles

Angle 1 — Smart Infrastructure (Buildings)

Nordic real-estate owners, REITs, construction groups, and municipal property companies now face CSRD Wave 2 (FY2025), the EPBD 2024 recast (Directive (EU) 2024/1275), and EU Taxonomy reporting on real-estate activities 7.1–7.7. Siemens captures the operational data they need. DWS IQ turns it into signed, submission-ready reports with KYA-governed writeback: HVAC setpoint adjustments, maintenance work orders, renovation-passport triggers.

Integrates with: Siemens Building X · Desigo CC · Navigator · Enlighted

Angle 2 — Digital Industries (Synthetic Data Wedge)

DWS IQ's SyntheticDataAgent — built on NVIDIA NeMo DataDesigner — generates structured compliance and production data that does not exist in real datasets: fault scenarios, tolerance deviations, process variations, edge-case building profiles, rare grid events. Natural plug-in across the Siemens Xcelerator stack for training quality-control models, digital twins, and compliance reasoning engines when real data is scarce, confidential, or regulated.

Integrates with: Siemens Xcelerator · MindSphere · NX (incl. NX AM) · Mobility telematics · Energy grid-event pipelines

Data flow

[INGEST] Siemens Building X API / Desigo CC BACnet / MindSphere / Navigator exports ↓ [RESIDENCY] DWS IQ EU-sovereign edge (Jetson / DGX Spark) or cloud (Aiven Helsinki, Scaleway Paris) ↓ [REASON] Lifetime World Model evaluates against live EU regulation set (CSRD ESRS, EPBD 2024, EU Taxonomy, Fit for 55, ETS, CBAM) ↓ [ACT] Bidirectional: HVAC setpoint writeback, work orders, renovation-passport triggers, CapEx tagging, automated ESRS datapoint submission ↓ [AUDIT] Aegis signed output + EU AI Act Article 12 audit log + KYA-attested operator identity

Siemens data Regulatory output Evidence type
