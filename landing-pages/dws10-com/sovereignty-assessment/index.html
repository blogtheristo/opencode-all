The Problem: EU Hosting Is Not EU Sovereignty

Your AI platform may run in an EU datacenter. But if the provider is a US company (Google, AWS, Azure, Palantir), US courts can compel them to hand over your data under the CLOUD Act (2018) — regardless of server location. This directly conflicts with:

  • EU AI Act — Article 12 requires audit trails and transparency that cannot be guaranteed if a foreign government can access the data
  • GDPR — Schrems II invalidated EU-US data transfers; CLOUD Act creates the same exposure through the back door
  • NIS2 Directive — Critical infrastructure operators must ensure supply chain security, including cloud providers
  • National security regulations — Defence and dual-use data on US-accessible infrastructure creates sovereign risk
