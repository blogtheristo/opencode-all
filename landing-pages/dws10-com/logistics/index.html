Solution: RouteGenius Agents analyze real-time traffic data, weather patterns, and vehicle load profiles. They dynamically update delivery routes to minimize acceleration/deceleration events and avoid congestion, optimizing for fuel efficiency rather than just speed.

Impact:

  • Extended vehicle lifespan due to smoother driving profiles
  • Improved on-time delivery reliability

Agents — RouteGenius Agent reconstructs per-leg fuel and CO2 from fleet telematics; the Compliance Agent maps the result onto ESRS E1 and runs a CountEmissionsEU transport-accounting consistency check and an ETS2 readiness pass for road fuels; Argus Agent streams runtime telemetry into the immutable decision log.

Outcome — An audit-ready ESRS E1 disclosure with an EU AI Act Article 12 decision log. The architecture is ALIGNED to ESRS E1 and CountEmissionsEU methodology; it is architected to satisfy the ETS2 road-fuel carbon cost (from 2027) before exposure begins.
