Key Fit for 55 Regulations

The Fit for 55 package includes 13 interconnected legislative proposals to transform the EU economy. Our platform provides industry-specific compliance solutions for all major regulations.

📊

CSRD

Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive requiring detailed climate disclosures aligned with ESRS standards.

Mandatory 2024-2028
🏗️

EPBD

Energy Performance of Buildings Directive with EN 15978 carbon calculations and renovation requirements.

Phased 2025-2033
💶

EU ETS

Emissions Trading System expansion to maritime, buildings, and road transport with tighter caps.

Effective 2024
🌍

CBAM

Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism for imports of cement, steel, aluminum, fertilizers, and electricity.

Active 2023-2026
⚡

RED III

Renewable Energy Directive with 42.5% renewable energy target and sustainability criteria.

Target 2030
🚗

Transport

CO2 standards for cars/vans, AFIR infrastructure, FuelEU Maritime, and ReFuelEU Aviation.

Multi-phase
🌳

LULUCF

Land Use, Land-Use Change and Forestry regulation for carbon removals and nature-based solutions.

Binding 2030
🏭

ESR

Effort Sharing Regulation with national emissions reduction targets across sectors.

Annual Targets
