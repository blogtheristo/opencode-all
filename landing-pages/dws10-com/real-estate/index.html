Business Case: Autonomous Building Management

Reducing Building Energy Use by 25%

Challenge: Commercial buildings waste massive amounts of energy heating, cooling, and lighting unoccupied spaces due to static schedules and lack of real-time awareness.

Solution: BuildingBrain Agents connect to BMS (Building Management Systems) and occupancy sensors. They learn usage patterns and dynamically adjust HVAC and lighting zone-by-zone, ensuring comfort only where people actually are.

Impact:

  • 25% average reduction in total building energy consumption
  • Lower operational costs and carbon footprint
  • Improved tenant comfort and air quality
