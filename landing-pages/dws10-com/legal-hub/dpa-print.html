This DPA governs the processing of Personal Data by Shufti Pro Limited on behalf of Lifetime Oy in connection with identity verification services (KYC/IDV) integrated into the DWS IQ 6 Platform.

Article 1. Definitions

Terms used in this DPA have the same meaning as defined in Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). Additionally:

  • “Biometric Data” — personal data resulting from specific technical processing relating to physical characteristics, including facial recognition data, as defined in GDPR Article 4(14).
  • “Identity Verification Services” — KYC/IDV services provided by Shufti Pro, including document verification, facial recognition, and liveness detection.
  • “KYA Framework” — the Know Your Agent identity and trust framework operated by Lifetime Oy.

Article 2. Scope and Purpose of Processing

2.1 Description of Processing

Subject matterIdentity verification for enterprise users and agents within the KYA framework
DurationFor the term of the service agreement between the parties
Nature and purposeKYC identity verification, document authentication, facial recognition matching, liveness detection
Categories of dataBiometric data (facial images), identity documents (passport, ID card, driver license), personal identifiers (name, date of birth, nationality), verification results and confidence scores
Categories of data subjectsEnterprise users, platform administrators, agent operators requiring identity verification under the KYA framework
Special categories (Art. 9)Biometric data for the purpose of uniquely identifying a natural person

2.2 EU-Only Processing Requirement

Shufti Pro shall process all Personal Data, including Biometric Data, exclusively within the European Union and European Economic Area (EU/EEA). This includes:

  • All biometric processing (facial recognition, liveness detection)
  • All document verification processing
  • All data storage, including temporary processing storage
  • All backup and disaster recovery storage

No Personal Data shall be transferred to, processed in, or stored in any jurisdiction outside the EU/EEA.

Article 3. Obligations of the Data Processor

Shufti Pro undertakes to:

3.1 Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Lifetime Oy.
3.2 Ensure persons authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations.
3.3 Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures per GDPR Article 32.
3.4 Not engage another processor without prior written authorization from Lifetime Oy.
3.5 Assist Lifetime Oy in responding to Data Subject rights requests (access, rectification, erasure, portability).
3.6 Assist Lifetime Oy in ensuring compliance with GDPR Articles 32-36 (security, breach notification, DPIA).
3.7 Delete or return all Personal Data upon termination of services, unless retention is required by law.
3.8 Make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allow for audits.

Article 4. Data Retention and Deletion

4.1 Biometric data (facial images, liveness data) shall be deleted within ___ days of verification completion.
4.2 Identity document images shall be deleted within ___ days of verification completion.
4.3 Verification results and confidence scores may be retained for the duration of the service agreement.
4.4 Upon termination, all Personal Data shall be securely deleted within thirty (30) days and a deletion certificate provided to Lifetime Oy.

Article 5. Security Measures

Shufti Pro shall implement at minimum:

  • Encryption at rest (AES-256 or equivalent) for all stored Personal Data
  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) for all API communications
  • Access controls with principle of least privilege
  • Regular security testing and vulnerability assessments
  • Incident detection and response procedures
  • Physical security controls at all data processing facilities
  • Staff training on data protection and security

Article 6. Data Breach Notification

6.1 Shufti Pro shall notify Lifetime Oy of any Personal Data Breach without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware.
6.2 Notification shall include: nature of the breach, categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, likely consequences, and measures taken or proposed to address the breach.
6.3 Shufti Pro shall cooperate with Lifetime Oy in investigating and remediating the breach.

Article 7. Sub-processors

7.1 Shufti Pro shall provide Lifetime Oy with a list of all sub-processors used in connection with the Identity Verification Services.
7.2 Shufti Pro shall notify Lifetime Oy at least thirty (30) days in advance of any intended addition or replacement of sub-processors.
7.3 All sub-processors must process data exclusively within the EU/EEA per Article 2.2.
7.4 Lifetime Oy has the right to object to any new sub-processor.

Article 8. Audit Rights

8.1 Lifetime Oy or its designated auditor may audit Shufti Pro’s compliance with this DPA once per year with 30 days written notice.
8.2 Shufti Pro shall provide access to relevant facilities, systems, and documentation.
8.3 Audit results shall be confidential.
8.4 Shufti Pro shall provide upon request: SOC 2 Type II report, ISO 27001 certificate, or equivalent certification evidence.

Article 9. Liability

9.1 Each party shall be liable for damages caused by processing that infringes the GDPR, in accordance with GDPR Article 82.
9.2 Shufti Pro shall indemnify Lifetime Oy for any fines, damages, or costs arising from Shufti Pro’s breach of this DPA or applicable data protection law.

Article 10. Term and Termination

10.1 This DPA shall remain in effect for the duration of the service agreement between the parties.
10.2 Either party may terminate this DPA upon material breach by the other party, subject to a 30-day cure period.
10.3 Data deletion obligations (Article 4) survive termination.

Article 11. Governing Law

This DPA is governed by Finnish law. The courts of Helsinki, Finland have exclusive jurisdiction.
