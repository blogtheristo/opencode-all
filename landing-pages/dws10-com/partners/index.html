Partner Requirements & Obligations

All partners must maintain the following baseline standards:

Security & Compliance

  • Security certification: ISO 27001, SOC 2, Cyber Essentials, or equivalent
  • GDPR compliance: Documented data processing procedures, DPO contact
  • Insurance: Professional indemnity (€1M minimum for Platinum/Gold)
  • Incident response: 24-hour notification of security incidents

Operational Requirements

  • Quarterly reporting: Delivery KPIs, project status, compliance attestation
  • Code of conduct: Ethics baseline, anti-corruption, conflict of interest disclosure
  • Subcontractor management: All subcontractors must meet same baseline
  • Audit rights: Customer and consortium right to audit compliance

Onboarding Process & Timeline

Total timeline: ~6 weeks from application to active partner status
