The Connect tier

Tier 3 partners access DWS IQ via the Connect product tier — built on global cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, NVIDIA) for market reach outside the EU sovereignty envelope. Typical Connect partners are startups, freelancers, SMEs, and global ISVs who want the DWS IQ agent framework and compliance primitives without requiring EU-only data residency.

Pricing: €600 / year · up to 5 partner accounts · online application · 48-hour onboarding · eligibility review for subcontracting into Aegis (€25K) and Sovereign (€5K) work packages.

Focus markets: US, Asia, Middle East, APAC.

Where Connect fits in the DWS IQ ecosystem

1. Global reach without EU sovereignty constraints

Connect is the right tier when your customers are outside the EU and don't have a hard data-residency requirement. Deploy on AWS, Azure, GCP, or NVIDIA cloud GPUs — wherever the workload makes sense commercially.

2. Fast onboarding for startups, freelancers, SMEs

Online application, click-to-accept terms, instant account provisioning. 48 hours from form submission to live account — no NDA round, no due diligence package, no discovery call required.

3. Subcontracting eligibility into Aegis and Sovereign work packages

Connect partners can apply for review to subcontract into larger consortium work packages. This is the recommended path for specialist freelancers and boutique studios who want exposure to defence and regulated-EU programs without taking on the €5K / €25K annual commitment up front.

4. Upgrade path to Sovereign or Aegis

Outgrow Connect? Upgrade to Sovereign Infra (€5K) once you have EU-resident customers, or to Aegis (€25K) once you need air-gapped sovereign deployment. Your Connect history counts as track record toward the higher tiers.
