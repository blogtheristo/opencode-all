Business Case: Predictive Energy Scheduling

Cutting Carbon Intensity by 20%

Challenge: Manufacturing plants often run energy-intensive processes during peak grid hours when electricity is dirtiest (highest carbon intensity) and most expensive.

Solution: DWS IQ's EnergyOracle Agents integrate with production schedules and local grid carbon data. They autonomously reschedule non-critical high-energy tasks (like heating or smelting) to times when renewable energy availability is highest.

Impact:

  • 20% reduction in Scope 2 carbon emissions
  • Significant reduction in electricity costs
  • Seamless integration with existing MES (Manufacturing Execution Systems)
