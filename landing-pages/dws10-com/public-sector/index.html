Agents — GovGreen Agent resolves Scope 1/2/3 across municipal building stock and procurement; the Compliance Agent maps results onto ESRS E1 and the Public Procurement Directive / GPP criteria; Sovereignty Monitor certifies EU data residency, RLS posture, and zero CLOUD Act exposure for the NIS2 dossier.

Outcome — A GPP-aligned tender pack and an audit-ready ESRS E1 disclosure, with an EU AI Act Article 12 decision log on sovereign infrastructure. The architecture is ALIGNED to ESRS E1, GPP criteria and NIS2 expectations; it is architected to satisfy EU AI Act obligations for public-sector AI as they phase in.
