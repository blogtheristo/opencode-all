DWS IQ integrates the best tools from NVIDIA, Google DeepMind, and the open-source ecosystem — curated per industry vertical. Each toolkit is agent-callable via NIM microservices or containerized pipelines on sovereign DGX Spark infrastructure.

NVIDIA Healthcare Stack

Clara

End-to-end AI platform for healthcare: imaging, genomics, and medical devices. The umbrella that connects BioNeMo, MONAI, Holoscan, and Parabricks into a unified development environment.

MONAI

Open-source medical imaging AI framework — 50+ pretrained models for CT, MR, pathology, and endoscopy. Supports 2D/3D segmentation, registration, reporting, and multimodal workflows. PyTorch-based.

Holoscan SDK

Real-time runtime for GPU-accelerated medical devices. Ingests high-bandwidth, multimodal sensor data, performs AI reasoning, and drives deterministic actions — surgical robotics, ultrasound, endoscopy.

FLARE

Federated Learning Application Runtime Environment. Trains AI models across hospital networks without sharing raw patient data — GDPR and EHDS compliant. Used by Mayo Clinic, Mass General, and NCI.

Parabricks

GPU-accelerated genomics pipeline. Alignment, variant calling, and annotation that runs 30–50× faster than CPU-only tools. GATK-compatible output for clinical and research workflows.

BioNeMo NIM

Agent-callable microservices for protein structure prediction, molecular docking, generative chemistry, and biomarker discovery. The agentic life sciences toolkit announced at BIO 2026.

Google DeepMind

AlphaFold 3

State-of-the-art protein structure prediction — proteins, nucleic acids, ligands, and their interactions. Foundation for understanding disease mechanisms and drug–target binding across oncology, autoimmune, and infectious disease.

AlphaProteo

AI-driven protein binder design. Generates novel high-strength binders for cancer targets (VEGF-A, PD-L1, TrkA), viral proteins (SARS-CoV-2), and autoimmune disease markers with surgical precision.

Isomorphic Labs

DeepMind drug discovery spin-off. Applying AlphaFold-class models to pharmaceutical drug design — advancing beyond structure prediction into end-to-end molecular design and optimization.

Open Source & Community

OpenFold 3

Open-source, trainable reimplementation of AlphaFold. Trained on 300K+ experimental structures and 13M+ synthetic structures. Allows custom fine-tuning on proprietary datasets — critical for sovereign deployment.

Nextflow + nf-core

Industry-standard workflow orchestration for bioinformatics. 83+ curated pipelines (nf-core) for genomics, transcriptomics, and proteomics. 43% citation share in bioinformatics WMS. Reproducible, scalable, publishable.

GATK + DeepVariant

Gold-standard variant calling. Broad Institute GATK for germline/somatic variants; Google DeepVariant (available as NVIDIA NIM) for deep-learning-based variant calling with higher accuracy on complex regions.

SAMtools / BCFtools

Foundational genomics utilities for reading, writing, editing, indexing, and viewing SAM/BAM/CRAM files. Core dependency of every modern sequencing pipeline from alignment to variant annotation.
