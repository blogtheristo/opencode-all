Business Case: Renewable Grid Balancing

Maximizing Solar & Wind Utilization

Challenge: The intermittency of renewable energy sources makes grid stability a challenge, often requiring carbon-heavy "peaker plants" to fill gaps.

Solution: DWS IQ's GridMind Agents manage Distributed Energy Resources (DERs) like solar panels and battery storage. They predict generation fluctuations and autonomously adjust storage charge/discharge cycles to smooth out supply, reducing the need for fossil-fuel backup.

Impact:

  • Increased utilization of renewable energy assets
  • Reduced reliance on fossil-fuel peaker plants
  • Improved grid stability and resilience
