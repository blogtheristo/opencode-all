The Myth

In Greek mythology, Argus Panoptes was the hundred-eyed giant set by Hera to guard Io. His eyes never all closed at once — while some slept, others watched. He was the perfect guardian, the ultimate sentinel.

In DWS IQ 6, Argus is reborn as the orbital perception layer. Powered by ICEYE's Finnish SAR constellation, he watches every steel mill, every port, every construction site on Earth — through monsoon, midnight, and smoke.

"A hundred eyes, and none of them blink. I see through clouds, darkness, and rain. Every steel mill, every port, every construction site — labeled, classified, governed. SAR doesn't need sunlight. Neither do I."

— Argus, to a client who thought fog was an excuse
