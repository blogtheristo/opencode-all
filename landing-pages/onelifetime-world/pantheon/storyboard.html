Rose from nothing. Demands justice — and quarterly results. Karate-chops anyone who brings incomplete CBAM filings. Secretly terrified of Athena. Paris energy meets Brussels steel.

Act I

The Green Room

Interior — Olympus Green Room — 06:45 UTC

🌍 ⚖️ 🔨 🌈 🏃 ☀️

Atlas is trying to hold up a whiteboard that keeps falling. Athena is correcting the whiteboard's grammar. Hephaestus is duct-taping the whiteboard to the wall. Iris is live-tweeting the whole thing.

"Has anyone seen my trident? I left it in the coffee machine again." — Neptune

Hermes zooms through carrying 47 packages. Trips over Gaia, who is sitting cross-legged on the floor, meditating on limestone deposits. A package lands on Vulcan's head.

"That was a load-bearing package." — Hermes, not stopping
"I felt that in my clinker ratio." — Gaia, without opening her eyes

The coffee corner

Prometheus is trying to make coffee by electrolyzing water into hydrogen and burning it. The espresso machine is on fire. Helios watches, unimpressed.

"You know the machine has a plug, right?" — Helios
"Where's the disruption in plugging things in?" — Prometheus, eyebrows singed

Apollo is composing a sonnet about the coffee. It's in iambic pentameter. Nobody asked for it. Argus is watching all of this from 500km altitude and taking notes.

"I can see the fire from orbit. Also, Hermes left a package in Sector 7." — Argus

Act II

The Morning Briefing

Interior — The Parthenon Conference Room — 07:00 UTC

Atlas stands at the head of a round marble table. Everyone is present. Ceres has brought pastries. Mars has brought blueprints. Neptune has brought a live fish, for reasons he won't explain. In the waiting room, Sofia Quijano is pacing, tilting at the CBAM filing windmill again.

"It's a metaphor." — Neptune
"For what?" — Atlas
"Maritime situational awareness." — Neptune
"It's a herring." — Athena
"A red herring." — Neptune, grinning

Atlas clears his throat. The room quiets. He holds up one document.

🚨 The curtain rises. Business mode activated.

"CBAM Quarterly Report Q1 2026. Due in 21 days. Three clients haven't filed. Athena?" — Atlas

Every god straightens. Neptune puts the fish away. Prometheus extinguishes himself. The room temperature drops 10 degrees.

"Client A: steel imports from Turkey, missing embedded emissions data. I've drafted the penalty exposure memo — €340,000 if unfiled by March 31. Client B: cement from Egypt, actual emissions required, default values sunset Q3. Client C: aluminium, compliant but using 2023 emission factors. Non-conformity risk: moderate." — Athena, without blinking
"Vulcan, I need verified steel mill emission intensities by Thursday. Gaia, cement clinker factors for the Egyptian plant. Neptune, confirm FuelEU Maritime filing status for Client B's shipping fleet." — Atlas

Three gods nod simultaneously. No jokes. No pastries. This is the part the clients see.

Act III

The Field Mission

Exterior — Construction Site, Helsinki — 09:30 UTC — Light snow

Hephaestus arrives at the site. Mars is already there, measuring wind speed. Argus is overhead, confirming the crane position via SAR.

"Argus, can you see if my hard hat is the right colour?" — Hephaestus
"It's orange. Like your personality." — Argus
"I'm going to take that as a compliment." — Hephaestus

A crane operator radios in. Wind is picking up.

🚨 Safety mode. Zero jokes.

"Wind: 14.2 m/s, gusting 18. EN 13001 limit for this crane class: 15 m/s sustained. We are at 95% threshold. Recommendation: SUSPEND LIFTING OPERATIONS. Resume when sustained wind drops below 12 m/s for 15 consecutive minutes." — Hephaestus
"Confirmed from orbit. SAR wind field shows gust corridor from NW. Estimated duration: 2.5 hours. Advise securing boom in weathervane position." — Argus
"Schedule impact: 2.5 hours lost on critical path activity CP-14 (steel erection). Float remaining: 1.2 days. Impact on CSRD material delivery deadline: NONE if resolved today. Hermes notified for material logistics adjustment." — Mars

The crane stops. Workers take shelter. Nobody argues with the gods when safety is on the line.

"Tea break! I brought Finnish pulla." — Ceres, appearing from nowhere with a basket

Act IV

The Intelligence War Room

Interior — Iris's Research Lab — 11:00 UTC — Screens everywhere

Iris has 14 browser tabs open. Argus is feeding satellite imagery to screen 3. Apollo is drafting outreach copy on screen 7. They're all talking at once.

"Competitor X just launched a 'green steel' product. I found it on LinkedIn, their investor deck, AND a leaked internal memo." — Iris
"How did you get an internal memo?" — Apollo
"I'm the goddess of rainbows. Light passes through me. So does information." — Iris

Vulcan walks in, looks at the competitor analysis, and snorts.

🚨 Competitive intelligence mode. Precision required.

"Their 'green steel' claims 1.2 tCO&sub2;/t crude steel. That's BF-BOF with 30% scrap. Real green steel (EAF + renewable) is 0.4 tCO&sub2;/t. They're greenwashing. Athena, can we cite Article 2(2) of the Green Claims Directive?" — Vulcan
"Already drafted. Proposal 2023/0085(COD). Their claim fails the 'substantiation requirement' test. I recommend we prepare a comparative positioning document for our steel-sector clients." — Athena
"Argus confirms: their steel mill in Sector 4 shows thermal signature consistent with blast furnace operation, not EAF. SAR pass 03:00 UTC, confidence 0.94." — Iris, reading Argus's feed
"Should I write the blog post title? I'm thinking: 'Green Steel, Red Flags, and the Gods Who See Through Marketing.'" — Apollo
"...That's actually good." — Athena, visibly annoyed at complimenting Apollo

Act V

The Ocean Incident

Exterior — Gulf of Finland — 14:00 UTC — Overcast

Neptune is monitoring his fleet dashboard when an alert fires. Argus picked up a vessel going dark.

"Someone turned off their AIS. In MY ocean." — Neptune, standing up slowly

Gaia looks up from her meditation. Athena is already pulling up maritime law.

"Is it the herring again?" — Atlas, via intercom
"This time it's a BULK CARRIER." — Neptune

🚨 Maritime enforcement. Multi-god coordination.

"Argus: SAR confirms vessel at 59.4°N, 24.7°E. Bulk carrier class, ~45,000 DWT. AIS dark for 6 hours. Heading: SE at 12 knots. Confidence: 0.91." — Argus
"Cross-referencing MRV database. Vessel matches profile of 'Atlas Carrier' — last MRV filing: 3 months overdue. FuelEU Maritime compliance: UNKNOWN. ETS allowance status: DEFICIENT." — Neptune
"Legal exposure: MRV non-compliance (Regulation 2015/757 Art. 20): €5,000–250,000. ETS non-surrender (Directive 2003/87/EC Art. 16): €100/tCO&sub2; × unreported. AIS manipulation (IMO SOLAS V/19.2.4): flag state jurisdiction. Total estimated exposure: €1.2M." — Athena
"If they're carrying cement, I want to know the clinker content. For science." — Gaia
"Neptune, please stop calling enforcement actions 'releasing the Kraken.'" — Atlas
"I will not." — Neptune

Act VI

The Hydrogen Debate

Interior — The Agora (Common Area) — 16:00 UTC — Pastries depleted

Prometheus and Helios are in their daily argument. Everyone else has learned to just watch.

"Hydrogen is the future!" — Prometheus
"Hydrogen is an energy CARRIER, not a SOURCE. I AM the source." — Helios
"You're a ball of gas!" — Prometheus
"So is hydrogen!" — Helios

Ceres is eating the last pastry. Mars is sketching a hydrogen storage building. Iris is recording the argument for "content purposes."

🚨 A client walks in. Instant transformation.

"Welcome. Your RFNBO certification requires three things: temporal correlation (monthly until 2030, then hourly), geographical correlation (same bidding zone), and additionality (renewable capacity commissioned within 36 months of electrolyser). Your current setup meets two of three. The additionality clause fails because your solar farm was commissioned in 2019." — Prometheus, suddenly in a suit
"I can model three alternative configurations. Option A: new 12 MW solar PPA, €890k/yr, meets additionality. Option B: existing wind + battery storage, temporal correlation via 15-minute PPA matching. Option C: grid-connected with Guarantee of Origin, but this only qualifies as 'low-carbon hydrogen,' not RFNBO." — Helios, pulling up a spreadsheet that didn't exist 3 seconds ago

The client leaves, impressed. The door closes.

"Where were we?" — Prometheus
"You were calling me a ball of gas." — Helios
"Right. Ball of gas." — Prometheus
"Energy carrier." — Helios
(This continues until 18:00 UTC.)

Act VII — Finale

The Curtain Call

Interior — Olympus Main Hall — 18:30 UTC — Golden hour

The day is done. All 14 gods gather in the main hall. The screens show green across the board: 3 CBAM filings completed, 1 maritime incident resolved, 1 construction site kept safe, 1 greenwash competitor exposed, 1 hydrogen client properly certified.

🌍 ⚖️ 🔨 🌈 🏃 ☀️ ⚒️ 🔆 🔱 🌾 🔥 🏗️ 🪨 🛰️

Ceres has somehow produced more pastries. Neptune is telling a joke about a kraken and a CBAM form. Prometheus and Helios are arm-wrestling. Iris is posting a sunset photo with the caption "Another day of saving EU industry from itself."

"Same time tomorrow?" — Atlas
"Always." — All 13, in unison

Argus watches from orbit. All 100 eyes. Some of them are smiling.

"Night shift starting. I don't sleep. But I do enjoy the quiet." — Argus

The moral of the storyboard:

The gods are chaotic, loud, opinionated, and occasionally set the coffee machine on fire. But when a deadline looms, a regulation changes, a crane hits wind limits, or a vessel goes dark —

not one of them blinks.

Joy is the fuel. Discipline is the output.
The Muppets run the backstage. The gods run the stage.
