Our Promise

A clear commitment to sustainable, sovereign infrastructure solutions

We commit to building European, Fit for 55‑aligned digital tools that help construction and infra leaders cut emissions, waste and water use. Our products are designed and hosted with EU data sovereignty in mind, and we prioritise measurable climate impact over vanity metrics.

Concrete Actions

Three commitments that drive real-world impact

Measurable Climate KPIs

Every new DWS IQ or Firehorse deployment must include at least one measurable climate/sustainability KPI: emissions, energy, waste, or water.

Customer Data Control

We avoid lock‑in: customers keep control of their data, cloud choice, and exit options, so long‑term sustainability and autonomy are possible.

Open Learnings

We share insights openly through Lifetime Scope Journal and Lifetime World Community, so others can reuse what works.
