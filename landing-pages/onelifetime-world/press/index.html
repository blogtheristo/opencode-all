DWS Launches "IQ Aegis" to Secure Global Dual-Use Industrial Trade

LONDON/HELSINKI — DWS today announced the formal release of IQ Aegis, a specialised security and compliance suite designed specifically for dual-use industries. As global regulations tighten around the export of sensitive technologies, IQ Aegis serves as a digital shield, ensuring that innovation remains secure and compliant across borders.

"IQ Aegis is the 'Firehorse' of our stable. It represents speed, power, and the reinforced protection required in today's geopolitical climate."

— The Team at OneLifetime

DWS IQ Aegis deploys six autonomous AI agents — including orbital intelligence via ICEYE SAR satellites, CLOUD Act sovereignty monitoring, EU AI Act Article 12 logging, and the KYA (Know Your Agent) governance framework — covering all 20 EU-regulated industries. The platform is available exclusively on European sovereign cloud or enterprise private cloud.

Aegis is designed for the Finnish Defence Tech Hub ecosystem including the NATO DIANA Accelerator (VTT, Espoo). Strategic partnerships with ICEYE (SAR satellites), NVIDIA (edge AI), and Shufti (agent identity) anchor the platform's capabilities.
