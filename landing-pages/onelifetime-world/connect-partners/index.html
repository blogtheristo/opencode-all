Why DWS IQ vs. Building Your Own Ecosystem

Building a multi-vendor industrial collaboration platform from scratch is expensive and slow. DWS IQ provides:

  • Pre-vetted partner network: faster procurement with reduced due diligence overhead
  • Shared compliance baseline: GDPR, NIS 2, ISO 27001 alignment across all partners
  • Consortium funding eligibility: structured for EU Horizon Europe, national innovation programs, and public-private partnerships
  • Multi-vendor delivery without reinventing governance: proven legal templates, data processing agreements, and IP frameworks
  • Reduced time-to-value: 6-week onboarding vs. 6-month custom procurement processes
  • Risk pooling: shared security standards, incident response, and insurance frameworks

Why Corporate Customers Can Join the Consortium (Strong Legal Positioning)

DWS IQ is structured to be procurement-ready and compliant-by-design. A typical contract + governance stack includes (as applicable):

  • NDA + due diligence: controlled entry, references, and risk screening.
  • Data governance: GDPR-ready processing roles (controller/processor), records of processing, and access controls.
  • Security baseline: least-privilege roles, audit trails, incident response expectations, supplier security commitments.
  • IP & licensing: background IP stays with its owner; project/foreground IP is defined per work package; clear open-source usage rules.
  • Liability & risk boundaries: clear scope, acceptance criteria, warranties, and subcontracting controls.
  • Consortium governance: steering group / product council, change control, and escalation paths.

This gives corporate customers a strong position to:

  • join the ecosystem with clear exit terms and no vendor lock-in
  • sponsor pilots and scale procurement through consistent legal templates
  • onboard multiple suppliers under one governance and compliance model
