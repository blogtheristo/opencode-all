Application Process

1

Apply via Email

Send CV and cover letter to risto@lifetime.fi. Tell us why you're interested, what you'd bring to the role, and include relevant portfolio/GitHub links.

2

Initial Call (30 min)

Video call with CEO. Get to know each other, discuss your background, understand role expectations and company vision.

3

Technical/Strategic Assessment

Role-specific: Technical interview, case study, or strategic discussion. We respect your time (3-4 hours max) and compensate for longer assessments.

4

Team Interviews (60 min)

Meet potential teammates. Discuss collaboration style, ask questions about culture, technology, and day-to-day work. Mutual evaluation.

5

Offer & Negotiation

If it's a mutual fit, we extend an offer with transparent terms. Typical timeline: 2-3 weeks from application to offer.
