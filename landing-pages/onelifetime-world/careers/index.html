Careers at DWS

DWS is building the industrial AI standard through people who understand industries from the inside.

This is not a generic AI hiring board. There is one career type here — Deputy Captain — and two investor roles, Captain and Admiral, which sit on a separate path. No mixed promises. No empty AI job listings.

Three roles, clearly separated

Deputy Captain

Hired or partnership role. Owns execution.

Captain

Invests and carries a defined industrial territory or sector.

Admiral

Invests and shapes several territories, sectors, or Captain networks.

How a role is named

Every role here follows one template — role family × industry × capability × jurisdiction, with economics and decision rights attached. The career type is the family. The industry and capability are the role identity. The jurisdiction is an operating qualifier.

Roles read like this

Deputy Captain, Steel CBAM Evidence, Finland/EU

Deputy Captain, Defence AI Safety, Sweden/EU

Deputy Captain, Sovereign Deployment, Arizona/Texas, US

Jurisdiction does not define a separate career type. It changes the deployment, legal, buyer and commercial conditions — not the person's fundamental role — so it belongs in the role title, mandate and requirements, not in a parallel ladder.

Deputy Captain

Deputy Captains own a specific industrial AI capability in a named industry. This could be sovereign deployment, compliance evidence, industrial sales, safety argumentation, implementation, architecture, or domain operations.

This is not a generic AI career ladder. You are taking responsibility for an industrial motion and earning authority through execution.

Every Deputy Captain role must state

  • the industry
  • the capability owned
  • the jurisdiction it operates in
  • the customer or revenue motion
  • decision rights
  • required evidence of competence
  • compensation and partnership terms

If a role we publish here is missing any of these six, it is not ready and you should tell us so.

The capabilities

Sovereign deployment

Standing the platform up on infrastructure the customer owns, in their jurisdiction, to their residency posture.

Compliance evidence

Turning industrial process reality into evidence that survives an auditor — CSRD, CBAM, ETS, EN 15978.

Industrial sales

Carrying a named revenue motion in one industry, from first conversation to cash collected.

Safety argumentation

Building the case that an autonomous agent is safe to run in a regulated industrial setting — and defending it.

Implementation

Getting a customer from signature to a system that actually runs their process, not a pilot that stalls.

Architecture

Designing how data, agents and audit trail fit an industry's real systems — ERP, BIM, plant historians.

Domain operations

Running the industrial motion day to day once it is live, and feeding what breaks back into the product.

Something we have not listed

If you own a capability your industry needs and we have not named it, propose it. Name the industry and the motion.

Investor roles

Investors sit on a separate path from the Deputy Captain career track.

CPT

Captain

Capital, relationships and market knowledge in a defined territory or sector

An industry-rooted operator who brings capital, relationships, market knowledge, and the ambition to make DWS the standard in a defined territory or sector.

Captain economics are carry-bearing and tied to named accounts they originate and materially advance.

Type: Investor role

ADM

Admiral

Shapes the wider adoption system across sectors and institutions

A higher-level industrial authority who can influence several sectors, territories, institutions, or Captain networks.

The Admiral role is for people who can shape the wider adoption system, not simply introduce individual accounts.
