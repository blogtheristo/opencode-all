Firehorse Amplify: 5 Workflows That Save 20 Hours Per Week

March 21, 2026 · Risto Anton Päärni · Lifetime Oy

Compliance teams at Nordic industrial companies spend an average of 25-30 hours per week on manual data collection, report formatting, and email coordination[1]. DWS Firehorse Amplify eliminates the most time-consuming workflows with five pre-built automations that deploy in days, not months. Here is what each one does, and the time it saves.

Workflow 1: Automated CSRD Data Collection (Saves 6 Hours/Week)

The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive requires data from across the organization: energy consumption from facilities management, employee diversity metrics from HR, supply chain emissions from procurement, waste volumes from operations. In most companies, this data lives in different systems with different owners, and collecting it involves emails, spreadsheets, and follow-up reminders.

Firehorse Amplify connects directly to source systems via API: ERP (SAP, IFS, Monitor), HR systems, energy monitoring platforms, and waste management providers. It pulls data on a configurable schedule (daily, weekly, monthly), validates it against ESRS (European Sustainability Reporting Standards)[2] requirements, and flags gaps before they become audit findings. The compliance team reviews a structured dashboard instead of chasing spreadsheet updates.

Workflow 2: Regulatory Change Monitoring (Saves 4 Hours/Week)

EU regulatory output is substantial. Between the Official Journal, EFRAG guidance, national transposition acts, and industry-specific delegated acts, a compliance team monitoring CSRD[3], EU Taxonomy[4], EPBD, CBAM, and the EU AI Act faces hundreds of pages of new material each month. Missing a change can mean non-compliance.

Firehorse Amplify monitors regulatory sources automatically. It ingests new publications from EUR-Lex, EFRAG, national legislator feeds, and industry body updates. AI-powered relevance scoring identifies changes that affect your specific industry and regulatory profile. The output is a weekly briefing with prioritized changes, impact assessment, and links to source documents. No more manual scanning of legislative databases.

Workflow 3: Compliance Report Generation (Saves 5 Hours/Week)

Most compliance reports follow predictable structures: header with company information and reporting period, data tables with metrics and year-over-year comparisons, narrative sections explaining methodology and deviations, and appendices with supporting documentation. Yet these reports are typically assembled manually in Word or PowerPoint, with data copied from various sources.

Firehorse Report generates compliance documents directly from validated data. It supports CSRD/ESRS format, EU Taxonomy disclosure tables, EPBD energy performance certificates, and custom management reports. Templates are configurable to match your corporate style guide. The system produces draft reports that the compliance team reviews and approves, rather than building from scratch each reporting period.

Workflow 4: Audit Trail Management (Saves 3 Hours/Week)

Every compliance assertion needs supporting evidence. When an auditor asks "how did you calculate Scope 2 emissions for Q3?", the answer should be immediately available, not reconstructed from email threads and spreadsheet version histories.

Firehorse maintains a court-defensible audit trail for every data point in the compliance system. Each value is linked to its source system, extraction timestamp, transformation logic, and validation status. When data is corrected or updated, the original value is preserved with the reason for change. This provides the evidence chain that external auditors require, without the compliance team manually maintaining audit documentation.

Workflow 5: Stakeholder Distribution (Saves 2 Hours/Week)

Compliance deliverables go to multiple stakeholders: management reports to the board, detailed disclosures to auditors, summary reports to investors, specific datasets to regulators, and internal briefings to department heads. Each audience requires a different format and level of detail.

Firehorse Omnichannel handles distribution automatically. From a single validated dataset, it generates audience-appropriate outputs: executive summaries for the board, full ESRS disclosures for auditors, investor-ready PDFs, and regulatory submission formats. Distribution schedules are configurable, and delivery confirmation is tracked. The compliance team approves content once; distribution happens automatically.

Total Impact: 20 Hours Returned to Strategy

Combined, these five workflows return approximately 20 hours per week to the compliance team. That is 20 hours that shift from data collection and formatting to analysis, strategy, and proactive compliance improvement. For a team of three compliance professionals, that represents a 33% increase in productive capacity without adding headcount.

The financial impact is equally clear. At a loaded cost of EUR 80-120 per hour for compliance professionals, 20 hours per week represents EUR 83,000-125,000 in annual savings. The Firehorse Amplify suite pays for itself within the first quarter of deployment.
