Lifetime Oy Launches DWS IQ Aegis — EU-Sovereign Dual-Use AI Platform for Defence and Regulated Industries

26 March 2026 · Risto Anton · Lifetime Oy
EU Data Residency EU AI Act Article 12 DEFINE / NATO DIANA Aligned

HELSINKI, 26 March 2026 — Lifetime Oy today announced DWS IQ Aegis, an EU-sovereign AI platform for defence organisations, dual-use manufacturers, and other regulated operators. The platform combines AI governance, compliance logging, sovereignty monitoring, and SAR-enabled operational intelligence in a deployment model covering 21 EU-regulated dual-use industries.

DWS IQ Aegis is designed for organisations that need to operate AI systems under export-control, data-residency, and sector-compliance requirements. It can be deployed on European sovereign cloud or enterprise private cloud infrastructure, depending on security, procurement, and operating model requirements.

Operational Scope

The platform is positioned for teams that must combine operational AI use with formal governance. This includes AI auditability under EU AI Act Article 12, sovereignty controls for infrastructure and data handling, and workflow support for operators working across dual-use, industrial, and other regulated environments.

In practical terms, Aegis brings together six specialised agents under one control framework so organisations can manage monitoring, logging, and compliance-sensitive automation without splitting those responsibilities across separate systems.

Six Autonomous Agents, One Platform

DWS IQ Aegis deploys six specialised AI agents, each governed by the KYA (Know Your Agent) framework:

ArgusAgent

AI orbital target labeling via SAR satellite data (e.g. ICEYE). All-weather, day/night industrial monitoring across 21 dual-use industry classifications from orbit.

SovereigntyMonitor

CLOUD Act exposure detection and EU data residency verification. Four sovereignty tiers: Sovereign, Compliant, Partial, Exposed.

Article12Logger

Every AI decision logged with risk classification, reasoning lineage, and audit-ready documentation for EU regulators.

KYA Engine

Know Your Agent — identity verification, Firecracker MicroVM isolation, kill authority, and forensic observability for every autonomous agent.

ComplianceAgent

Dual-use export control (EU Reg. 2021/821) combined with EU climate compliance — CSRD, CBAM, ETS, NIS2 — in a single scoring engine.

SiteSenseAgent

Defence construction site safety, logistics optimisation, and EN 13001 crane operation monitoring for field operations.

SAR Orbital Intelligence

ArgusAgent is designed for integration with SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) providers such as ICEYE. That enables all-weather, day/night monitoring of industrial assets and areas of interest, with AI-assisted classifications routed through KYA governance controls and Article 12 logging.

DEFINE / Defence Tech Hub Alignment

DWS IQ Aegis is designed for the Finnish defence and deep-tech environment, including organisations operating with DEFINE and NATO DIANA-adjacent requirements. Lifetime Oy positions Aegis as infrastructure for sovereign deployment, auditable AI use, and operational oversight rather than as a general-purpose analytics product.

"European defence and regulated operators need AI systems they can deploy under their own governance rules. Aegis was built to give them auditability, sovereignty controls, and operational visibility in one platform."

— Risto Anton Paarni, Founder & CEO, Lifetime Oy
