Lifetime Oy Launches DWS IQ Aegis — EU-Sovereign Dual-Use AI Platform for Defence and Regulated Industries

26 March 2026 · Risto Anton · Lifetime Oy
EU Data Residency EU AI Act Article 12 DEFINE / NATO DIANA Aligned

HELSINKI, 26 March 2026 — Lifetime Oy today announced the launch of DWS IQ Aegis, a standalone dual-use AI platform purpose-built for defence agencies, military industry enterprises, and dual-use manufacturing supply chains operating under EU regulation. Aegis covers all 20 EU-regulated industries and is available exclusively on European sovereign cloud or enterprise private cloud.

Named after Athena's shield in Greek mythology, DWS IQ Aegis provides protection through intelligence — not just walls. The platform packages orbital satellite intelligence, sovereignty monitoring, autonomous agent governance, and EU AI Act compliance into a single hardened deployment, addressing the unique intersection of defence requirements and EU regulatory obligations that no existing platform serves.

The Defence Compliance Gap

Defence and dual-use supply chains face the same EU compliance obligations as civilian industry — CSRD reporting, CBAM carbon border adjustments, NIS2 cybersecurity, and the EU AI Act — plus additional layers of export control regulation (EU Reg. 2021/821), data sovereignty requirements, and dual-use classification. Until now, no platform has addressed this intersection across all regulated industries.

Existing alternatives — Palantir AIP, Anduril Lattice, C3.ai Defence — are US-based, CLOUD Act exposed, and lack EU AI Act compliance infrastructure. DWS IQ Aegis is EU-sovereign by design: Finnish-hosted, Article 12 native, and built for the European defence ecosystem.

Six Autonomous Agents, One Platform

DWS IQ Aegis deploys six specialised AI agents, each governed by the KYA (Know Your Agent) framework:

ArgusAgent

AI orbital target labeling via SAR satellite data (e.g. ICEYE). All-weather, day/night industrial monitoring across 20 industry classifications from orbit.

SovereigntyMonitor

CLOUD Act exposure detection and EU data residency verification. Four sovereignty tiers: Sovereign, Compliant, Partial, Exposed.

Article12Logger

Every AI decision logged with risk classification, reasoning lineage, and audit-ready documentation for EU regulators.

KYA Engine

Know Your Agent — identity verification, Firecracker MicroVM isolation, kill authority, and forensic observability for every autonomous agent.

ComplianceAgent

Dual-use export control (EU Reg. 2021/821) combined with EU climate compliance — CSRD, CBAM, ETS, NIS2 — in a single scoring engine.

SiteSenseAgent

Defence construction site safety, logistics optimisation, and EN 13001 crane operation monitoring for field operations.

SAR Orbital Intelligence

The Argus agent is designed for integration with SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) satellite data, including providers such as ICEYE — the world's largest SAR constellation, headquartered in Espoo, Finland. SAR provides all-weather, day/night imaging capability, enabling industrial infrastructure monitoring regardless of cloud cover or lighting conditions. The pipeline flows from Area of Interest definition through SAR catalog API ingestion, AI labeling, KYA governance, and into the Article 12 audit trail.

DEFINE / Defence Tech Hub Alignment

DWS IQ Aegis is designed for the Finnish Defence Tech Hub ecosystem, including the NATO DIANA Accelerator operated by VTT in Espoo. The platform aligns with the DEFINE programme and the 60+ member companies of Finland's defence technology cluster — including ICEYE, IQM (quantum computing), Kuva Space, Varjo, and Elfys. Research collaboration with VTT and Aalto University supports continued platform development.

"Defence supply chains cannot afford to choose between compliance and capability. Aegis delivers both — sovereign AI agents that satisfy EU AI Act Article 12, dual-use export control, and climate regulation simultaneously. Every agent decision is logged, governed, and auditable. This is what responsible dual-use AI looks like."

— Risto Paarni, Founder & CEO, Lifetime Oy
