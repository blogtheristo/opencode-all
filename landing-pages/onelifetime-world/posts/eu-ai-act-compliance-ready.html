The Tradeoff You Shouldn't Have to Make

Most AI platforms treat compliance as a feature to add later — a reporting layer, a dashboard, a manual review step that slows everything down. That's the wrong architecture.

DWS IQ 6 was built from the ground up with EU AI Act, NIS2, CSRD, and Fit for 55 as design constraints, not afterthoughts. The result: agents that are both more capable and more trustworthy than systems that treat compliance as optional.

How It Works

Our agent harness architecture governs every agent action across your operations:

  • Regulatory thresholds loaded just-in-time, not memorised by the model
  • Every agent action logged with forensic-grade attribution (who authorised what, when)
  • Hardware-level isolation via KYA (Know Your Agent) — agents run in sandboxed MicroVMs, not shared containers
  • Human oversight built into the control flow, satisfying EU AI Act Article 14
