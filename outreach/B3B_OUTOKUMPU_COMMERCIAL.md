## Why this exists

First outreach to Outokumpu was sent to Heidi Peltonen, VP Sustainability. Polite decline: "We have all we need for CBAM reporting."

**Diagnosis (refined):** Sustainability owns _internal compliance hygiene_ — wrong door. Segment VPs / Business Area presidents are the _strategic_ commercial owners but they don't sponsor single-account experiments; they delegate down. The **sharpest** B2B2B target is the **Sales KAM** — the person who personally loses sleep over one named end-customer, owns the PO conversation, and can say yes to a no-cost pilot on **their own account** without escalation.

See `outreach/LESSONS.md` § L1 "Sales KAMs are the buyer in B2B2B".

## Target

- **Right role at Outokumpu:** Sales Key Account Manager (KAM) in a CBAM-exposed segment — Automotive, Wind, Process Industry, Construction.
- **Action item before sending:** for one named KAM, identify **one CBAM-exposed end-customer in their portfolio** (e.g. a named automotive OEM, wind OEM, or fabricator). The subject line names that customer. No customer name = no send.
- **Why not segment VPs:** they triage 200 emails/day, will forward to a KAM anyway, and add 2 weeks to time-to-pilot. Going direct to the KAM short-circuits that.
