## How to use this file

Per `outreach/LESSONS.md` L1, the cold note must name a **specific end-customer in a specific KAM's portfolio**. This file gives you two well-sourced end-customers and the segment a KAM would sit in. It does **not** name individual KAMs — LinkedIn employee search is gated and Outokumpu does not publish KAM names. To convert a candidate into a sendable note:

1. Pick one candidate below (see "Recommended first send").
2. Find the named KAM via **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** ("Outokumpu" + segment + "Key Account Manager" / "Account Manager" / "Senior Sales Manager"), a **warm referral**, or a **direct call to Outokumpu Krefeld switchboard** asking for the KAM on that account.
3. Substitute `[End-Customer]` and `[Name]` into `outreach/B3B_OUTOKUMPU_COMMERCIAL.md` and send.

If a named KAM can't be sourced within 48h, send to **Matthieu Jehl** (President, Stainless Europe) with the same KAM-frame note — he will route it down faster than going to Sustainability.

## Candidate 1 — Alfa Laval AB × Outokumpu Business Line Stainless Europe

- **End-customer:** Alfa Laval AB (Lund, Sweden).
  - Sourcing relationship with Outokumpu: Alfa Laval signed a multi-year contract with Outokumpu to move its main European heat-exchanger production units onto Circle Green stainless steel (Supra and Core grades, used in plate heat exchangers which are ~80% stainless). Source: https://www.outokumpu.com/en/news/2023/outokumpu-and-alfa-laval-join-forces-to-cut-carbon-emissions-with-towardszero-stainless-steel-3369136
  - CBAM-exposed because: heat exchangers are deployed across CBAM Annex I value chains (refining, chemicals, hydrogen, district energy); Alfa Laval has publicly stated Scope 3 = ~98% of its total footprint and has an SBTi-validated 50% Scope 3 cut by 2030, with stainless steel its single largest upstream emission driver. Source: https://www.alfalaval.com/about-us/sustainability/sustainability-strategy/carbon-neutrality-by-working-together/
- **Outokumpu segment:** Business line Stainless Europe (formerly Business Area Europe; #1 EU30 share at 31%, key customer segments include process industry and metal processing). Source: https://www.outokumpu.com/en/news/2025/change-in-outokumpu-leadership-team,-c-,-matthieu-jehl-appointed-as-president,-business-line-stainless-europe-3548743
- **KAM-level contact:**
  - Named individual KAM: not publicly discoverable without LinkedIn login.
  - Route-via fallback: Matthieu Jehl, President, business line Stainless Europe (Krefeld, DE; appointed by 26 May 2025). Source: https://www.outokumpu.com/en/news/2025/change-in-outokumpu-leadership-team,-c-,-matthieu-jehl-appointed-as-president,-business-line-stainless-europe-3548743
- **Why this is sharp right now:** In March 2025 Alfa Laval expanded the Outokumpu deal into a tri-party low-emission steel collaboration (with SSAB) supplying the Laakso Joint Hospital — meaning Alfa Laval's procurement is actively re-cutting POs around verified mill CO2 lines, exactly the data CBAM definitive-period reporting (Jan 2026) will demand. Source: https://www.alfalaval.com/media/news/investors/2025/alfa-laval-outokumpu-and-ssab-announce-new-collaboration-to-reduce-emissions-at-laakso-joint-hospital-in-helsinki/

## Candidate 2 — Fiskars Group × Outokumpu Business Line Stainless Europe (consumer goods)

- **End-customer:** Fiskars Group Oyj (Helsinki, Finland).
  - Sourcing relationship with Outokumpu: Fiskars was the first commercial recipient of Outokumpu Circle Green and uses it in cookware produced at its Sorsakoski plant in Finland. Source: https://fiskarsgroup.com/news/press-release/fiskars-group-and-outokumpu-join-forces-with-new-emission-minimized-stainless-steel-for-cookware/
  - CBAM-exposed because: stainless cookware bodies fall under CBAM iron & steel downstream goods (Annex I CN 73), and Fiskars has SBTi-approved targets including a public commitment that 80% of suppliers by spend (raw material, components, finished goods) must have science-based targets by 2029, and reached its 2024 60% milestone — meaning mill-level CO2 data is contractually material. Source: https://fiskarsgroup.com/news/press-release/fiskars-group-strengthens-climate-ambition-with-updated-science-based-targets-that-aim-for-more-significant-emissions-reductions-across-the-value-chain/

- **KAM-level contact:**
  - Named individual KAM: not publicly discoverable.
  - Route-via fallback: Matthieu Jehl, President, business line Stainless Europe (same as above). Source: https://www.outokumpu.com/en/news/2025/change-in-outokumpu-leadership-team,-c-,-matthieu-jehl-appointed-as-president,-business-line-stainless-europe-3548743
- **Why this is sharp right now:** Fiskars publicly hit its 2024 supplier-SBT milestone and has rolled the bar to 80% by 2029 — its category buyers are now scoring Outokumpu mill data on every renewal, and CBAM definitive period starting Jan 2026 makes verified embedded-emissions certificates a literal PO requirement, not a marketing nicety.
