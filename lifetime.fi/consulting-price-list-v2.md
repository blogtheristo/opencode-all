## Service Model

All consulting services follow the same outcome-focused approach:
- **Business Outcome:** What problem is solved?
- **Supported Product:** Which DWS IQ product does this support?
- **Agent/Workflow:** Which DWS IQ agents/workflows are used?
- **Deliverable:** What do you receive?
- **Customer Inputs:** What do we need from you?
- **Delivery Window:** When will it be delivered?
- **Acceptance Test:** How do we know it's complete?
- **Price:** What is the investment?
- **Exclusions:** What is not included?
