Lifetime Fleet

We operate a mobility fleet in the Helsinki region — Uber, Bolt, Taksi Helsinki and Kela-reimbursed special transport. These are real operating roles with real shifts, not AI-adjacent job titles.

🚘 VIP Driver

Premium and special-transport driving across the Helsinki region. Flexible shifts, full induction, and help getting the licences and professional qualification in place if you do not have them yet.

🗂️ Fleet Manager

Owns dispatch, scheduling, driver rota and vehicle availability. The person who keeps utilisation up and makes sure the cars and the people are where they need to be.

🔧 Fleet Engineer

Keeps the vehicles and the in-car technology running — maintenance planning, edge-device installs, and the telemetry that feeds our own systems.
