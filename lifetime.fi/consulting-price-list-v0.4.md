## Commercial Rules

- Prices exclude VAT and require written scope confirmation.
- Customer dependencies, unavailable data and third-party delays extend delivery windows.
- Work outside scope requires a separate quote.
- Recurring support must define hours, response times, availability and service boundaries.
- No package promises legal compliance, certification, specific ROI, accuracy percentages or audit outcomes unless separately evidenced, agreed and contracted.
- Travel and on-site expenses are quoted separately.
